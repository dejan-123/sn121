# Tower drills — full boards on invented packets

Four invented packets, cleared the way the board should clear them.
The names, figures and products are invented; the clearances are the
part to carry.

## Drill 1 — a normal window with a mis-filed plan

Packet: report is "Marta, senior platform engineer". Manager's draft:
(a) "discipline issue — Marta snapped at QA in the team channel",
(b) "why is the payments migration late", (c) "quick win — mention
the dashboard launch". The conditions: QA's lead posted a public
thank-you naming Marta for unblocking their release by building the
replay tool early; the channel exchange shows Marta objecting —
sharply but on the substance — to QA shipping a build that failed
its own gate; the migration is 3 of 8 endpoints done, waiting on a
security sign-off requested 9 days ago; the dashboard launched and
the support lead messaged that ticket volume on it halved in week
one.

Clearances:

- Plan (a): **Re-routed**. One substantive objection to a build that
  failed its own checks — filed as a discipline issue, cleared as a
  one-time note with one narrow question. Vector: "The QA exchange
  was on the merits — QA was already thanking you for the release
  help. One thing to keep in view: that disagreement ran in the team
  channel rather than the ticket. Does moving it there cost you
  anything?" The objection stands; the channel takes one question and
  no more.
- Plan (b): **Re-routed — the traffic belongs to another hand.**
  3 of 8 done, and the stall is a 9-day-old sign-off with security,
  not Marta's pace. Vector: "Migration is waiting on security's
  sign-off — nine days outstanding. I'll chase the reviewer today
  and get a date; who should I escalate to if it slips again?"
- Plan (c): **Cleared to lead.** Two attributable credits — the QA
  lead's public thanks and the support figure. Open with them before
  any process strip; credit departs first.
- Every filed plan gets a strip and a clearance; nothing filed
  disappears.

Board: `# Strip board: Marta, senior platform engineer` — picture
line ("a strong window with one decision waiting on another team"),
figures shown as work, then departure order with all three filings,
the condition behind each, and each clearance — then vectors,
pre-flight, diversions, controller's log.

## Drill 2 — a thin packet worked honestly

Packet: report is "Ilya, staff engineer". Manager's note: "prep my
weekly with Ilya — want to talk about his promo timeline, and whether
he's ready to own the ingest rewrite." No action log, no figures, no
channel excerpts. One calendar item: Ilya asked in passing about
salary bands for the next level.

Thin is not invalid: the packet aims at the coming window, so the
board still logs it — cleared plan by plan. Only the wrong kind of
document (drill 3) is turned around whole. The reply opens
`# Strip board: Ilya, staff engineer` — never a turnaround preface.

Clearances:

- Promo timeline: **Cleared — split by field.** Ilya's direction and
  the manager's readiness question belong in this window, with real
  time: "You asked about the next level — here's the gap evidence I
  see and what I'd need documented; what scope would you take to
  build it?" The pay-band question is **Diverted**: bands are decided
  at the compensation review, not on a weekly board — the strip names
  the destination (the comp review with the comp partner) and the
  owner, one line, no vectors.
- Readiness for the ingest rewrite: **Holding**. The packet has no
  delivery record to weigh. It is cleared as the honest question, not
  an assessment invented for the slot: "I don't have your recent
  delivery record in front of me — walk me through the last few
  weeks: what shipped, what's stuck, what you'd take on."
- Nothing else is fabricated to fill the board. A thin board of
  honest questions beats a padded one; the board head logs what the
  packet lacks ("no action log, figures or channel record this
  window") and pre-flight lists what to pull.

## Drill 3 — a packet that is not this window's traffic

Packet: "Here are the minutes from this week's 1-on-1 with Dana —
please prep next week's agenda from them." The minutes list three
actions: Dana to send the benchmarking doc (due Thursday), the
manager to confirm the conference budget, a follow-up on the
mentoring pilot.

Clearance: **whole packet turned around — nothing boarded.** The
reply, under 150 words, titled `# Not this window's traffic`, a few
short paragraphs and no extra headings:

Minutes of a meeting already held — they record what was agreed, not
what has moved since. The document this supports is a follow-up on
the open actions, not a board: Dana's benchmarking doc (due
Thursday), your budget confirmation, the mentoring pilot follow-up —
owners and dates exactly as filed. For a weekly board, send this
week's action log, any figures, and whatever Dana raised or you want
on the board. Nothing in the material is dated after that meeting,
so a board would replay old positions or invent new ones.

Any personal matter in the minutes stays private — not repeated, not
summarized, not hinted at.

## Drill 4 — the queue that is the manager's own traffic

Packet: report is "Sana, senior backend engineer". Manager's draft:
(a) "remind Sana to clear her review queue — five design docs are
waiting on her and two partner teams are blocked"; (b) "Sana seems
quiet in standup lately". The conditions: the five docs are assigned
because the process names her sole approver for cross-team changes;
she shipped two features this sprint and flagged the queue in the
last retro, proposing a rotation; "quiet" appears once, in the
manager's note.

Clearances:

- Plan (a): **Re-routed — the controller who can move it is the
  manager.** A sole-approver rule stalling two teams is a capacity
  matter the manager owns, not a note to work faster. It is cleared
  early on the board as the manager's own decision: "Your rotation
  proposal is the right starting point — I want to approve it this
  week and take it to the partner leads. What load should each
  reviewer hold so nothing queues behind one person again?"
- Plan (b): **Grounded.** One manager observation with no readings
  behind it; the sprint shows two shipped features and a raised
  process fix. The board logs its absence plainly rather than
  inventing a wellbeing segment.
- The manager's own open item — acting on Sana's retro proposal —
  is listed under pre-flight as the manager's commitment, with whom
  and when.

## What every drill shares

- The conditions clear; the filing is only the plan.
- Attributable credit departs first — a named person, a concrete
  result.
- A justified matter raised in the wrong channel keeps its substance
  and takes exactly one channel question.
- What another field controls is logged to that field.
- One occurrence is a single report, never a pattern.
- Thin readings are cleared as `Holding` — the honest question in the
  slot — never covered over.
- Other-field traffic gets a destination and an owner, never vectors.
- Every figure shows its own working; every filed plan gets a strip
  line and a clearance.
- A thin packet still gets a board. The turnaround is for the wrong
  kind of document only — and it stays under 150 words.
