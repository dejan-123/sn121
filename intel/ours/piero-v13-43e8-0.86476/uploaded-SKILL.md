# Audit the terms, not the headlines

The deal's terms set the questions; every signal is testimony for or against one of them. Each term gets a verdict — met, at risk, untested, untouched — each signal a tier with an owned action. Never advise; never score.

## Part 1 — Put the terms on trial

From the input's own words only:

- **Stage** — pre-LOI, post-LOI, pre-close; sets the step a finding must clear.
- **Thesis** — what the price pays for: an asset, contract base, licence, person or margin; it never becomes a condition.
- **W1, W2…** — each walk-away quoted with its number and unit, **each paired with the record that would breach it**: a lien register for an unencumbered-assets term, an enforcement list for a violation term.
- **P1, P2…** — each price condition, stated or implied: a multiple of a figure assumes the figure real; value said to sit in one asset assumes that asset owned. Pair each with its deciding record too.
- **Not searched** — every register the input says lay outside the sweep, tagged by its record kind. A scope-named register no numbered signal reports gets no row — the evidence-gap paragraph carries it.
- **Worked thresholds** — a share of something the input counts, computed into its units, written beside the term: "over 30% of the 410-strong fleet = over 123 vehicles".

Nothing stated on a line reads "none stated"; never invent one.

## Part 2 — Findings per signal

**Whose fact.** The target's exact entity, or a party the pool itself ties to it: certifier, lender, named customer, opponent in a filed case, the person a term is about. An anonymous post naming the target still attributes. A certificate is worth what its issuer is worth: an issuer losing accreditation reaches the term the certificate supports even when no article names the target. A near-match — a letter off, another suffix or legal form, even "(sic)" — is undecided identity: only a register joins two names. A signal naming nobody in the deal is noise however grave, with no step asking whether the unnamed subject might be the target — even when the event is the very one a walk-away guards against and the story names the target's own product line; the overlap is why the sweep returned it.

**Origins and label.** Count first access, never outlets. Outlets quoting one release or spokesperson are one origin; an outlet citing another adds none; everything the target publishes or says is one voice that never corroborates itself. Independent origins each had their own access: a docket, a regulator's notice or register, a counterparty's own filing. Labels: **corroborated** (2+ independent, one outside the target); **single-source** (one; say whether primary and binding — docket, notice, statutory filing, counterparty filing — or not); **unverified** (anonymous, "sources familiar"); **absence** (a never-searched register).

**Silence kind.** Searched-clean clears that register for its date only. Never-searched proves nothing. A fact only the parties hold — a client's status, contract terms, renewal data — is no register: the row asks for the papers. A news-and-company sweep opens no court, regulator or registrar database.

**Term touched.** W1, P1, the thesis, or none — a row touching nothing staked never climbs past monitor — and the record whose pull ends the question; it writes the action cell.

## Part 3 — Verdicts

**must-address** needs four things: attributed; corroborated or one primary-and-binding record; decisive for a stated walk-away, price condition or the thesis — magnitude, probability (occurring, not possible), reversibility; and settlement before the next step at this stage. Three overrides lift thinner evidence:

- **Unsearched decisive register** — only when its record kind is the one a stated term turns on; the action is the search; one search deciding two terms becomes a lettered row per term. An unsearched register of other things, or a topic only the thesis touches, is deeper-diligence.
- **Thin-report tripwire** — it names the target spelled as the target is; it reports the breaching process already under way against it — an inspection, a notice being drafted; and no record in the pool answers it. If literally true it would by itself meet a stated walk-away or breach a stated price condition. Label stays what the origins say; the action is written confirmation from the record-holder; the target's denial does not disarm it. Corollary: a report that even if true would not break a term can never climb.
- **Compound cluster** — thin signals that together establish one fact against one stated term — financing admitted by the target, reported by the press, confirmed by the lender — climb together, named against that term, linked rows marked "read with row N".

**deeper-diligence** — bears on a term or the thesis but fails a test, one verification settling: the target's own word on what the value rests on; a searched-clean register, which speaks only for its date (re-read before the step); a certificate whose issuer another row puts in doubt — the finding rides the issuer's row; a claim ended on ground the deal stakes; a dismissal without prejudice touching no stated term — paused, able to return; every unresolved near-match — the identity pull alone; a departure or hire in the function a term or the thesis runs through — even anonymous.

**monitor** — attributed, and either an independent record already confirms it (a counterparty's filing, a live register entry) or nothing staked turns on it — including what adds nothing to check: a bare denial, which never lifts the claim it answers; a counsel retention; a routine appointment. Light keep-in-view action.

**noise** — names nobody in the deal, or bears on nothing this deal stakes. Action: none, one-line reason.

**Never must-address**, whatever the drama: a near-match even when its matter is "referred for review" or the term covers any breach "regardless of size"; papers only the parties hold; a nobody-row; a searched-empty sweep; a register in hand showing the term met; a hire no term turns on. No terms stated → only a live adverse claim on the thesis asset on an independent record reaches the top.

**Absence-worded terms** — "unencumbered", "no liens", "no sanctions", "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists: attributed evidence of the thing puts the term at risk; financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought; a dismissal without prejudice leaves a challenge able to return — and where a walk-away turns on no active challenge, that paused dispute stays must-address until the docket shows it ended for good. A term can be contradicted in kind while its measure stays unknown — the thing exists but the pool lacks the count: write "not yet measurable" and name the record that supplies it.

**Filed figures** stand as filed; test each against its threshold once and move on. Doubt a base only when the pool shows the figure struck on a different quantity than the term names — a one-off gain inside an earnings base; then the term is untested — must-address although the number clears.

## Part 4 — Rows judge rows

- A certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate; the finding rides the accreditor's row; the certificate's row reads "read with row N".
- Set the target's own asset list against the register: how many claimed, how many back live under its exact name — the register governs disagreements. A register result whose entries differ in owner or status splits lettered, one row per status class — live, lapsed, other-name: the lapse a loss taken, the other name an identity question, the action the item-by-item schedule.
- Rows carrying one fact share one tier, marked "read with row N". A second source inside one numbered signal stays on that signal's row; two numbered signals on one matter stay two rows. A later row confirming an earlier writes "confirmed by row N" — never a merge, never a renumber.
- Audit before writing: every numbered signal has a row and tier before any paragraph; every must-address names its earned test or override; the count line's row total equals signals plus splits; the tier cell holds only the four tier words. An alarming pool can end with no must-address row — say so.

## Part 5 — The reply

Open with `## ` — nothing before it:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):" quoted, each with its deciding record and any worked figure; "Not searched:"; "Conditions:" each term's state — met, at risk, untested, untouched; "none stated" where silent. A state is not a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered. Columns: # | Signal | Attribution | Origins and label | Bears on | Tier | Required action — role, verb, exact record or party, step cleared; unique per row. Noise rows: "none — <reason>".
4. **Cross-signal check** — which rows moved each other and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; each tier with its row numbers and count; the arithmetic summing to the row total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — must-address picture ending on what needs answering before the next step; elevation rationale — the closest row and the test it failed, why each dramatic row sat lower; most important evidence gap and the record that would close it.
