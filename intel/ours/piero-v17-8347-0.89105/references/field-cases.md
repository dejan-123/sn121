# Boundary card

- Named praise or a measured result can open; vague credit cannot.
- External waits and assigned queues are the manager's unblock; the queue, not the report, holds waiting teams.
- A planned escalation stays conditional until confirmed. Two dated misses can carry coaching; coach the earlier flag when the report already found the cause.
- A thin future draft still gets a board. Held minutes get only the redirect with exact owners and dates.
