# Worked memo

One invented deal from an unrelated industry, written out in full, then how the memo changes when the artifacts differ. Copy the method, form and length; every name, figure, tier and comparable in a real memo comes only from its own artifacts.

## The artifacts, condensed

**Risk table.**
1. Submerged-land leases at two marinas (35% of slip and storage revenue) expire in 26 months; renewal applications filed, no decision. Must-Address, In-Progress.
2. State consent order after a fuel-dock spill at Port Alder; remediation works complete. Must-Address, Resolved.
3. The harbour authority's operating licence at Port Alder requires its consent to any change of control. Must-Address, Resolved.
4. The general manager owns the brokerage affiliate that refers about 30% of new slip customers. Manageable, Unresolved.
5. Marine insurance premium up 38% at the last renewal after storm claims. Manageable, status cell blank.
6. Online slip-booking system runs on software its vendor no longer supports. Informational, Unresolved.

**Narrative.** Harrow Point Marinas, Inc. runs seven marinas on Lake Michigan and Lake Huron. LTM revenue $21.8M: slip and winter storage $13.1M on seasonal contracts, service yard $3.3M, fuel sales $5.4M resold near cost. Growth 3%. Margin after cost of sales 44% blended, 50% excluding fuel. EBITDA $3.9M; the seller presents $5.1M, adding back $0.7M of storm repairs and $0.5M for a brand relaunch campaign that ended in the spring. The state agency's latest letter says sign-off on the Port Alder remediation awaits a final groundwater test. Asking price $52M enterprise value.

**Comparables.** Bayline Marina Group 2.4x revenue, 10.5x EBITDA, growth 5%, margin 52%. Northbank Harbors 2.9x, 12.0x, 8%, 55%. Saltmarsh Holdings 2.2x, 9.0x, 3%, 48%. Keel & Mast Partners 3.8x, 16.0x, 7%, 54%, bought by an infrastructure fund last year. PierPoint Docks Online 5.6x, NM, 34%, 71%, a booking-platform model.

## The memo

# IC Memo: Harrow Point Marinas, Inc.

## 1. Deal Thesis

Harrow Point brings seven lakeshore marinas with $13.1M of recurring slip and storage revenue and $3.9M of reported EBITDA. The primary risk is row 1: two marinas carrying 35% of slip and storage revenue sit on state submerged-land leases that expire in 26 months, with renewal undecided. The recommendation turns on rows 1 and 2, the leases and the consent order the agency has not yet signed off.

## 2. Risk Table

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submerged-land leases at two marinas (35% of slip and storage revenue) expire in 26 months; renewal filed | Must-Address | In-Progress | Open | Closed by the state's renewal decision on both leases |
| 2 | Consent order after the Port Alder fuel-dock spill; works complete | Must-Address | Resolved | Open (flag) | Flag: the narrative says sign-off awaits a final groundwater test, so the order is not closed; status left as given |

Gate: must-address rows 1 and 2 are open; row 3 is closed with a flag; manageable rows 4 and 5 carry mitigations.

## 3. Valuation Range

Base: EV/EBITDA, a mature profitable business, on $4.4M = reported $3.9M + $0.5M relaunch campaign (one-off, ended). The $0.7M of storm repairs stays in: storms recur on this shoreline, and row 5's premium rise confirms it. The seller's $5.1M is not used.

| Comparable | EV/EBITDA | Growth | Margin | Included | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saltmarsh Holdings | 9.0x | 3% | 48% | Yes | - |
| Bayline Marina Group | 10.5x | 5% | 52% | Yes | - |
| Northbank Harbors | 12.0x | 8% | 55% | Yes | - |
| Keel & Mast Partners | 16.0x | 7% | 54% | No | Acquisition price carrying a fund's control premium, not a trading multiple |
| PierPoint Docks Online | NM | 34% | 71% | No | No EBITDA multiple; platform model, growth 31 points above the target |

Median of included, ascending 9.0x, 10.5x, 12.0x: 10.5x (median growth 5%, margin 52%).
- Growth: 3% against 5%: down.
- Margin: 50% excluding fuel against 52% (the comps' fuel treatment is not given): down.
- Scale: comparable sizes not given; no adjustment.

Net adverse, so the range runs from the lowest included multiple to the median: 4.4 x 9.0 = $39.6M to 4.4 x 10.5 = $46.2M. Cross-check: revenue excluding fuel $16.4M (13.1 + 3.3) x included EV/Revenue median 2.4x = $39.4M, near the low end. The ask, $52M / $4.4M = 11.8x, sits $5.8M above the top.

## 4. Recommendation

**Recommendation: Request more data**

Must-address rows 1 and 2 are open, so Proceed is not available; row 3 is closed with a flag. Neither open row is established against the deal: row 1 awaits a renewal decision and row 2 a test result, and the state can settle both before signing. Manageable rows 4 and 5 carry mitigations. On price, the comp set supports $39.6M-$46.2M; the $52M ask would need to come into that range before a vote to proceed.

## 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **Row 1, submerged-land leases:** legal obtains the state's written renewal decision, or its status letter and timetable, for both leases; renewal on current terms closes the row.
- **Row 2, Port Alder consent order:** the seller provides the final groundwater result and the agency's sign-off letter; a clean sign-off closes the row and the Resolved status then stands.
- **Row 3, harbour licence:** legal obtains the harbour authority's written consent to the change of control, held as a condition precedent to signing.
- **Row 4, brokerage referrals:** commercial obtains the referral terms and three years of referral volumes to size an arm's-length agreement and the GM's retention terms.
- **Row 5, insurance premium:** finance obtains the renewed policy and next season's broker quote to confirm the full premium sits inside the $4.4M base.
- **Row 6, booking software:** operations scopes a replacement; monitored only, no effect on the verdict.

Next step: return to IC once rows 1 and 2 are closed, with the price re-based on the range.

## How the memo changes

**Every must-address row closed.** Had the narrative confirmed both lease renewals and the agency's sign-off, rows 1 and 2 read Closed, row 3 stays Closed (flag), and rows 4 and 5 carry mitigations: `**Recommendation: Proceed**`, at no more than $46.2M. The bullets turn into protections: lease assignment consent (row 1), an environmental indemnity surviving closing (row 2), the harbour consent as a condition precedent (row 3), the referral agreement signed before closing (row 4), the price set on the renewed premium (row 5).

**A blocker established against the deal.** Had the state refused both renewals in writing, row 1 is settled against the thesis: 35% of slip and storage revenue leaves within 26 months and no condition restores it. `**Recommendation: Pass**`, and each bullet says what would reopen the deal, such as new leases on fresh terms. A lawsuit in discovery or a review still pending is never this case: it is an open row, and the verdict is Request more data.

**An even count.** Had a fourth comparable at 11.2x been included, the ascending list 9.0x, 10.5x, 11.2x, 12.0x has median (10.5 + 11.2) / 2 = 10.85x, and the net adverse range runs 4.4 x 9.0 = $39.6M to 4.4 x 10.85 = $47.7M.

**A growth outlier.** A comparable growing 26%, against the target's 3% and a set median of 5%, sits more than 15 points from both and is excluded with that line. One growing 12% stays in, and the growth adjustment carries the difference.

**Other tier names.** A table using Critical, Major and Minor gets one line under it, "Tier mapping used for the gate: Critical = must-address; Major and Minor = manageable", while the cells keep the upstream words.

**A missing artifact.** Had the comp set been absent, section 3 says "Comparables not provided; no range derived", the recommendation's price line says the same, and nothing is estimated from outside the artifacts.

**No must-address rows.** The gate rests on the manageable rows: Proceed when each carries a named mitigation, Request more data when one cannot be mitigated on what the artifacts show.

**Other bases.** Revenue booked gross of pass-through costs is valued net of them; a one-off project leaves LTM; revenue that will not transfer to a buyer leaves the base; a run-rate drops customers already gone. Each appears as arithmetic in the base line, beside the seller's figure, and software or platform targets are valued on EV/Revenue.

## Check before sending

Five sections in order. Every upstream row present, tier and status exactly as given, each Gate value one of the four, each flag naming what it rests on. The verdict matches the gate in row numbers. Every comparable marked Included or Excluded, every exclusion with a reason; median, adjustments, both products and the implied multiple shown, and the range in section 4 identical to section 3. One bullet per row, in table order. Under about 6,500 characters.

Your reply now begins with `# IC Memo:` and the target's name. No sentence comes before it.
