# Worked memos (invented companies; form only)

Both cases are made up to show the steps on paper. A memo is written straight out, with no notes ahead of it: its first characters are `## ` and the target's name. Copy the form: the sections, the columns, the way a sum is printed and a reason is attached. Copy no fact, name or figure; in a real memo each one comes from the three artifacts supplied.

## Case one: the artifacts, in brief

Target: Saltreach Marinas, an operator of leisure marinas earning berth rentals.
Risk table as supplied:
- Seabed lease for the two largest sites (52% of berths) ends in 30 months; the port authority has opened a public tender. Must-Address, Resolved.
- Breach notice under the dredging consent at the harbour site; remediation plan filed with the regulator. Must-Address, In-Progress.
- Environmental cover for the fuel dock had lapsed. Must-Address, Resolved.
- Head of operations retires this year; no successor named. Manageable, Unresolved.
- A $1.9M storm insurance recovery is booked in revenue. Informational, Resolved.
- Berth waiting list has never been audited. Informational, Unresolved.
Narrative: LTM revenue $26.3M including the $1.9M recovery; growth 9%; gross margin 58%; reported EBITDA $6.4M, or $4.5M excluding the recovery; the seller presents adjusted EBITDA of $7.7M, adding back $1.3M of "site development costs"; management calls the lease renewal "agreed in principle", the authority's tender remains open and no award letter has been issued; net debt $8.0M; the seller asks $72.0M enterprise value.
Comparables (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, growth, gross margin): Harbourline Berths 2.4x, 13x, 7%, 56%; Quaywater Leisure 2.7x, 14x, 11%, 60%; Tidegate Marinas 3.4x, 17x, 24%, 66%; Anchor Reach Holdings 3.9x, 20x, 12%, 61%, a sponsor's purchase price from last year; Moorfield Docks 2.1x, 11x, 3%, 52%.

## Saltreach Marinas: investment committee memo

### 1. Deal thesis - Saltreach Marinas (max 140 words)
Saltreach earns berth rentals that grew 9% at a 58% gross margin, with $24.4M of revenue once a one-time recovery is removed and $4.5M of EBITDA on the same footing. The case for buying is steady rental income from sites that are hard to replicate. The primary risk is R1: the seabed lease under 52% of the berths ends in 30 months and the port authority's tender is still open, although the table calls it resolved. The seller's case leans on two things the artifacts dispute: an adjusted EBITDA of $7.7M that adds back recurring site development costs, and a price that only fits peer multiples when the recovery is counted as revenue.

### 2. Tiered risk table - Saltreach Marinas (one row per finding)

| # | Finding | Tier given | Status given | Status used and why | Effect on the vote |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Seabed lease for the two largest sites (52% of berths) ends in 30 months; public tender opened | Must-Address | Resolved | Open: disputed. The narrative says the tender remains open and no award letter has been issued; "agreed in principle" is not an award | Blocks |
| R2 | Breach notice under the dredging consent; remediation plan filed | Must-Address | In-Progress | Open: a filed plan is not an accepted one | Blocks |
| R3 | Environmental cover for the fuel dock had lapsed | Must-Address | Resolved | Open: not evidenced. Nothing contradicts it, but no artifact shows the renewed policy; a documentation gap, cured by the policy schedule | Blocks until seen |
| R5 | $1.9M storm insurance recovery booked in revenue | Informational | Resolved | Accepted as a fact, lower tier; read with section 3, where it sets the base | Moves the price, not the vote |
| R4 | Head of operations retires this year; no successor named | Manageable | Unresolved | Open; held at manageable because no artifact ties the sites' consents to this person | Condition 4 |
| R6 | Berth waiting list never audited | Informational | Unresolved | Open; it supports demand claims the thesis does not rely on | Condition 5 |

Status dispute: R1 is marked Resolved while the tender is live; this memo uses open and says so. Links: R5 sets the valuation base; R1 and R2 concern the same two sites that carry most of the berths. Open top-tier findings: three (R1 disputed, R2 in progress, R3 not evidenced).

### 3. Defended valuation range - Saltreach Marinas (max 320 words plus one table)
Base: $26.3M reported LTM revenue - $1.9M one-time recovery (R5) = $24.4M pricing base; EBITDA on that base $4.5M, as the narrative states. The seller's $7.7M is not used: $6.4M + $1.3M, where site development costs recur and the $6.4M still contains the recovery.

| Comparable | Multiples | Growth | Gross margin | IN or OUT | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harbourline Berths | 2.4x, 13x | 7% | 56% | IN, range | trading multiple; closest below the target's 9% growth and 58% margin |
| Quaywater Leisure | 2.7x, 14x | 11% | 60% | IN, range | trading multiple; closest above on both |
| Moorfield Docks | 2.1x, 11x | 3% | 52% | IN, reference | trading multiple, slower and thinner; a floor, not a range peer |
| Tidegate Marinas | 3.4x, 17x | 24% | 66% | OUT | outlier: growth gap of 15 points |
| Anchor Reach Holdings | 3.9x, 20x | 12% | 61% | OUT | a sponsor's purchase price: it carries a control premium the trading multiples do not |

Growth gaps to Saltreach Marinas (9%): Harbourline 2, Quaywater 2, Anchor Reach 3 (OUT, sponsor price), Moorfield 6, Tidegate 15 (OUT, over ten). Range peers: Harbourline and Quaywater, the two smallest IN gaps.
Range: 2.4x (Harbourline) x $24.4M = $58.6M; 2.7x (Quaywater) x $24.4M = $65.9M. Cross-check on EBITDA: 13x x $4.5M = $58.5M; 14x x $4.5M = $63.0M, so the lower half of the range is better supported. Full IN span: 2.1x to 2.7x, $51.2M to $65.9M. Position: Saltreach sits between its two peers on growth and margin, carries $8.0M of net debt, and earns an 18.4% EBITDA margin against their implied 18.5% and 19.3%. No size adjustment is possible: the dataset gives no peer revenue.
Ask: $72.0M / $24.4M = 2.95x, above the range by $72.0M - $65.9M = $6.1M. For reference, 2.7x x $26.3M reported = $71.0M: the ask is close to a peer multiple only if the recovery is paid for as revenue.

### 4. Recommendation - Saltreach Marinas (max 140 words)
Three must-address findings are open (R1, R2, R3), so the vote is no-go as presented; R1 puts 52% of the berths at stake, and R3 is a documentation gap only. The vote turns to go when the port authority's award letter for the seabed lease, the regulator's written acceptance closing the breach notice and the renewed policy schedule are all in hand. Price is a separate matter: even with all three closed, $72.0M is not supported; a go would be at or below $65.9M, and the EBITDA cross-check argues for less.

### 5. Conditions - Saltreach Marinas (max 8 lines)
1. R1: the port authority's award letter for the seabed lease, from the seller, before signing.
2. R2: the regulator's written acceptance that the dredging breach is closed, from the seller's counsel, before signing.
3. R3: the renewed environmental policy schedule for the fuel dock, from the seller, before signing.
4. R4: a named successor to the head of operations, or a signed handover period, from management, before closing.
5. R6: an audit of the berth waiting list, from the seller, before closing.
6. R5: revenue and EBITDA restated without the recovery in the seller's own model, from the seller's finance lead, before any price is discussed.
7. Most important gap: whether the seabed lease survives the tender. The record that closes it is the port authority's award letter.

## Case two: a quiet table, no asking price, two methods that disagree

Artifacts in brief: Brindle Quay Boatyards stores and repairs boats. The table's scale is Severe / Moderate / Minor. Its two Severe findings (a crane certification that had expired; a boundary dispute with a neighbour) are both marked Resolved, and the narrative confirms each: the crane's new certificate was issued last quarter and the boundary settlement has been signed by both parties. Two Moderate findings are open (the slipway winch is overdue for replacement, Unresolved; the yard manager's contract renewal is under negotiation, In-Progress) and one Minor (a lapsed customer survey, Unresolved). LTM revenue $38.0M, growth 16%, EBITDA $3.4M; storage is 60% of revenue at a 30% gross margin and repair work 40% at 62%; the comparables' own split is not given; no asking price is stated. Comparables: Slipway Marine Services 1.5x, 9x, 14%; Drydock Partners 1.8x, 10x, 18%; Portside Refit 1.2x, 8x, 6%; Keelhaul Yachtworks 3.0x, NM, 41%.

How sections 2 to 4 read (in short):

| # | Finding | Tier given | Status given | Status used and why | Effect on the vote |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Crane certification had expired | Severe | Resolved | Closed: the narrative confirms the new certificate was issued | Condition 1 (copy for the file) |
| R2 | Boundary dispute with a neighbour | Severe | Resolved | Closed: the narrative confirms the settlement was signed by both parties | Condition 2 (copy for the file) |
| R3 | Slipway winch overdue for replacement | Moderate | Unresolved | Open; a lower tier, so it cannot block | Condition 3 |
| R4 | Yard manager's contract renewal under negotiation | Moderate | In-Progress | Open; a lower tier, so it cannot block | Condition 4 |
| R5 | Customer survey lapsed | Minor | Unresolved | Open; nothing in the thesis rests on it | Condition 5 |


Scale: Severe is this table's top tier and is read as must-address. Status disputes: none. R1 and R2 are closed: the narrative itself confirms the certificate and the signed settlement, so copies are requested as conditions 1 and 2 and neither row blocks. Open top-tier findings: none, because both Severe rows are resolved and the narrative confirms how. Three lower-tier rows are open (R3, R4, R5); they are conditions and do not change the vote: zero open top-tier findings means go.

Base: $38.0M reported LTM revenue stands; nothing in the artifacts removes any of it. EBITDA $3.4M, an 8.9% margin.
Growth gaps to Brindle Quay Boatyards (16%): Slipway 2, Drydock 2, Portside 10, Keelhaul 25 (OUT, over ten, EV/EBITDA not meaningful). Range peers: Slipway Marine Services and Drydock Partners; Portside Refit stays IN as a floor.
On revenue: 1.5x (Slipway) x $38.0M = $57.0M; 1.8x (Drydock) x $38.0M = $68.4M. On EBITDA: 9x (Slipway) x $3.4M = $30.6M; 10x (Drydock) x $3.4M = $34.0M. The methods disagree by about half. The peers' implied EBITDA margins are 1.5 / 9 = 16.7% and 1.8 / 10 = 18.0%; Brindle Quay earns 8.9%, so a revenue multiple would pay for margin it does not earn. EV/EBITDA leads. Defended range: $30.6M to $34.0M. The revenue result is shown as the reason for caution, not as a range. Mix: Brindle Quay is 60% low-margin storage; the peers' mix is not in the dataset, so no mix adjustment is made and the gap is listed as a data request.
Ask: the artifacts give no asking price. No implied multiple is computed; $30.6M to $34.0M is offered as the price guidance.

Recommendation: No Severe finding is open, so the vote is go, subject to the conditions; the open Moderate and Minor findings are handled as conditions 3 to 5. Price: no ask to test; an offer stays inside $30.6M to $34.0M until the peers' mix shows that a higher multiple is earned.
