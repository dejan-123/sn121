# A worked memo, and how the last section changes with the verdict

Saltmere Coatings and every figure, party and comparable below are invented. Take the reasoning and the proportions; a real memo takes every fact from its own three artifacts.

## The memo

# Investment Committee Memo: Saltmere Coatings

## 1. Deal Thesis

Saltmere makes copper-free anti-fouling coatings: LTM revenue $36.0M, up 12%, 41% gross margin, reported EBITDA $5.4M (15%). Its lead copper-free formulation carries 58% of revenue, which is the reason to own it. The seller asks $81M, 15.0x reported EBITDA; its $6.7M adjusted figure adds back launch costs that recur yearly, so we value reported earnings. The primary risk is R1, the expiring EU authorisation for that formulation, compounded by R2, a 38% distributor without agreed renewal terms.

## 2. Risk Assessment

| ID | Finding | Upstream tier | Status | Gate effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Authorisation for 58%-of-revenue formulation expires in 9 months | Must-Address | In-Progress | Open: blocks proceed |
| R2 | Halyard Marine Supply is 38% of revenue, annual contract | Must-Address | Resolved (contradicted) | Treated as open |
| R3 | Rival's infringement claim on lead formulation, settled last year | Must-Address | Resolved | Resolved per table; confirm terms |
| R4 | Two wastewater notices in 24 months | Manageable | Not stated: treated as unresolved | Needs mitigation; flagged |
| R5 | Single specialty-pigment supplier | Manageable | Unresolved | Needs mitigation |
| R6 | Founder-CEO stepping back within two years | Informational | Resolved | None |

**Flagged observations**
- R2 is marked Resolved, but the narrative says Halyard's renewal is still under negotiation; the cell stands, and the memo treats R2 as open.
- R4's agency notice warns a further exceedance triggers a permit review; it may warrant must-address, for the committee to decide.

## 3. Valuation

EV/EBITDA is primary: Saltmere is profitable and the comps report EBITDA. Base: reported $5.4M; the $1.3M launch add-back is rejected as recurring.

- BlueHull Coatings 11x — included
- Tidewell Paints 12x — included
- Anchorline Chemical 9x — included
- Seaguard Polymers 13x — included
- Keelstone Marine 18x — excluded: control-premium acquisition
- NovaFoul Bio NM — excluded: NM on EV/EBITDA, and growing 46% against 6–15% for the rest

Sorted, the included multiples are 9x, 11x, 12x, 13x, so the median is (11 + 12) / 2 = 11.5x. Peers' growth sorted is 6%, 9%, 11%, 13% (median 10%) and gross margin 38%, 43%, 44%, 45% (median 43.5%). Saltmere grows a little faster (12%) but earns a thinner gross margin (41%), and is probably smaller than listed peers, though their scale is not given. On balance it deserves a discount, so the range runs from the lowest included peer to the median: 9x × $5.4M = $48.6M; 11.5x × $5.4M = **$62.1M**. The $81M ask is 15.0x reported EBITDA, above the top of the range.

## 4. Recommendation

**Request more data.** R1 is in progress and R2's resolution is contradicted, so both must-address rows are open and proceed is unavailable; documents can close both before signing, so this is not a pass. Any revisit should price inside $48.6M–$62.1M.

## 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1** — Regulatory counsel obtains the authority's acknowledgment and decision date; refusal, or a decision after signing, moves the verdict to pass.
- **R2** — Commercial lead obtains Halyard's signed renewal with volumes; unsigned terms keep R2 open.
- **R3** — Deal counsel confirms the settlement terms cover EU sales of the formulation; a confirmation, not a block.
- **R4** — Management supplies both notices and the agency's correspondence; the committee decides R4's band.
- **R5** — Operations lead provides the supply contract and a second-source qualification timeline.
- **R6** — Monitor; request the founder's transition plan, no action before the committee.

## Proportions

The memo above runs about 550 words: a thesis of 70–110 words, short table cells with at most three flagged observations, a valuation of at most 160 words plus one line per comparable, a recommendation of 40–80 words, and one condition of at most 30 words per risk row. Committees read memos of this length to the end; keep yours within about 750 words unless the request sets its own lengths.

## What the memo above does deliberately

Its shape is the committee's usual one: the Deal Thesis is one paragraph ending on the primary risk by row ID; the Risk Assessment table runs `ID | Finding | Upstream tier | Status | Gate effect`, must-address band first, followed by any flagged observations; the Valuation shows its working; the Recommendation states the verdict in bold with the rows that decide it and the price stance; Conditions and Next Steps gives one bullet per risk row, in table order, each a single sentence. The whole memo stays near 750 words so the committee reaches the conditions.

Its medians come from sorted lists: the multiples (9x, 11x, 12x, 13x, so (11 + 12) / 2 = 11.5x) and the peers' growth and margin, because a median read by eye from an unsorted table is the commonest slip a committee catches. With an odd count the median is the middle value; with an even count, the mean of the two middle values.

Its range is anchored on real peers: a target that deserves a discount runs from the lowest included multiple to the median, one that deserves a premium from the median to the highest included multiple, and a balanced case between the two middle multiples. Each end is written as its own product.

R3 carries no flag even though a settled IP claim on the lead product sounds serious: nothing in the artifacts says it is open, so its Resolved stands and the check sits in its condition. R2 is flagged because the narrative itself says the renewal is still being negotiated.

## How section 5 changes with the verdict

Under **Proceed**, each bullet is a condition precedent or post-close mitigation with an owner: "R4 — Seller funds an escrow equal to the quantified exposure until the appeal is decided." Under **Pass**, each says what would have to change to reopen: "R1 — Reopen only if the counterparty confirms in writing it will continue on our ownership." Either way, one bullet per risk row, in table order.

That is the whole shape. Write the memo now: its first characters are `# Investment Committee Memo:` and the target's name, with no notes or working before it.
