# IC Memo: Kestrel Cold Storage

## 1. Deal Thesis

Kestrel stores frozen and chilled food for regional distributors in four owned-equipment warehouses, with revenue growing 7% a year on multi-year storage contracts. It would give our logistics platform temperature-controlled capacity in a region where we turn away customers today. On a $7.5M EBITDA base and an included comp median of 10.75x, we defend $75.0M to $80.6M against a $92.0M ask. No must-address row is open; the primary risk is R2, a distributor worth 34% of revenue that can leave on 120 days' notice.

## 2. Risk Table

Labels mapped once: High = must-address, Medium = manageable, Low = informational.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| R2 | Anchor distributor contract (34% of revenue) terminable on 120 days' notice | High | Resolved | Closed (questioned) | No renewal or extension named in the artifacts |

Must-address rows: R1, R2; open: none.

**Flagged observations**
- R2 is recorded as Resolved, yet the finding still describes a 120-day exit right on 34% of revenue; a signed term extension would prove the resolution. Not re-tiered.
- R5 is labelled Low, but losing the third warehouse would remove about a quarter of capacity, so it reads as manageable at least. Covered by the R5 condition. Not re-tiered.

## 3. Valuation

Base: $36.9M revenue, $7.5M EBITDA (reported $38.5M and $7.2M; stripped a one-time recall surge and a vague add-back; kept the anchor distributor, at risk only).

Reported revenue includes $1.6M of one-time blast-freezing work during a product recall, so recurring revenue is $36.9M. Of the seller's add-backs, the $0.7M site-closure cost is documented as one-time and accepted; the $0.8M "platform build-out" has no support and is rejected. Removing the surge's $0.4M contribution gives `$7.2M + $0.7M - $0.4M = $7.5M`. The seller's $92.0M rests on an adjusted $8.7M EBITDA; it is not used.

Included (4, ascending): 9.0x, 10.0x, 11.5x, 12.5x; median `(10.0x + 11.5x) / 2 = 10.75x`.
- Size: one distributor is 34% of revenue (R2); no comp states a concentration. Down.
- Margin: gross margin 33% against a median of 32.5%. Up.
- Growth: 7% against a median of 7%. Neutral.
- Forced by the artifacts: the unrenewed third-warehouse lease (R5). Down.

Two adjustments point Down and one Up, so the range runs from 10.0x (the included value just below the median) to 10.75x (the median).

Range: low `10.0x × $7.5M = $75.0M`; high `10.75x × $7.5M = $80.6M`. Cross-check on EV/Revenue: included median 2.05x, and `2.05x × $36.9M = $75.6M`, inside the range. The $92.0M ask sits $11.4M above the high end.

## 4. Recommendation

**Proceed**, subject to the conditions below. R1 is closed by re-inspection and R2 is closed as recorded with its evidence requested; the open manageable rows R3 and R4 and the low-tier R5 each carry a mitigation. Our price is the defended $75.0M to $80.6M, not the $92.0M ask.

## 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1**: Deal counsel to attach the re-inspection report to the disclosure schedule; confirm at signing.
- **R2**: Corporate development to obtain a signed term extension from the distributor as a condition precedent, within 4 weeks; without it, the price moves to the $75.0M low end.
- **R3**: Tax counsel to set a specific indemnity for the $0.45M reassessment until the appeal closes.
- **R4**: Risk manager to obtain the insurer's written coverage position before signing; any uninsured amount goes into a specific indemnity.
- **R5**: Real-estate lead to secure the third-warehouse lease renewal as a condition precedent.

## When a must-address row is still open

**4. Recommendation.** **Request More Data.** R1 is an open must-address row that a document can settle. Proceed becomes available once R1 closes; the price position is unchanged.

**5. Conditions, R1.** Deal counsel to obtain the regulator's re-inspection report within 3 weeks. A pass closes R1 and moves the verdict to Proceed; a failed re-inspection that stops refrigeration moves it to Pass.
