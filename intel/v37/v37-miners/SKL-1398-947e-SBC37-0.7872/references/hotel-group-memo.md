# Worked memo: an invented hotel group

The company, its franchisor, landlord, comps and every figure below are invented to show the reasoning moves. None of them belongs in a real memo. Part A lists what the invented artifacts said; Part B is the memo written from them; Part C shows how single sections change when the input is different.

## Part A. What the invented artifacts said

Risk table:
- F1 Brand licences for 7 of Corvanne's 11 hotels terminate on change of control unless the franchisor, Penwick Brands, consents. Must-Address, Resolved.
- F2 Ground lease under the Quayside hotel expires in 30 months; the landlord has not agreed to extension talks. Must-Address, In-Progress.
- F3 Liquor licences at 5 hotels were held in the founder's personal name and must sit with the operating company; the table records the reissue as done. Must-Address, Resolved.
- F4 Corvanne manages 2 hotels for their owners under agreements each owner can cancel on 120 days' notice; they bring $2.8M of fee revenue. Must-Address, Resolved.
- F5 Three hotels scored below Penwick's brand-standard minimum at the last inspection. Manageable, Unresolved.
- F6 LTM includes a 9-month municipal shelter-housing block booking at 2 hotels; it ended 2 months ago. Informational, Unresolved.

Narrative: LTM revenue $66.4M, up 12%; excluding the block, revenue grew 6%. The block brought $4.7M of revenue and $1.3M of EBITDA; excluding it, EBITDA is $9.0M. Reported EBITDA $10.3M; the seller's "adjusted EBITDA" of $13.4M adds back $0.8M of pre-opening costs for one hotel reopened after refurbishment and $2.3M of "brand-standard catch-up" spending. Gross operating profit (GOP) margin 39%. Quayside contributes $5.7M of revenue. "Penwick's consent committee will consider the transfer after signing; no consent letter has been issued." The seller says corporate bookings in its pipeline will replace the block; no contracts are attached. Asking price $127M enterprise value.

Comps (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, growth, GOP margin): Ravel Suites 2.6x, 14.0x, 11%, 44%; Ostrand Resorts 3.4x, 19.0x, 9%, 43% (analyst note: multiple from its take-private by a PE fund 9 months ago); Brightwater Lodging 1.8x, 11.0x, 4%, 38%; Kestrel Urban Hotels 3.1x, NM, 34%, 35%; Tessaly Inns 2.1x, 12.5x, 7%, 41%.

## Part B. The memo

# Corvanne Hospitality Group: investment committee memo

## 1. Deal thesis for Corvanne Hospitality Group (up to 130 words)

Corvanne operates 11 select-service hotels at a 39% GOP margin, in line with its closest peers; revenue outside a one-off block booking grows 6%. The primary risk is F1: seven brand licences end on change of control, and the franchisor has issued no consent, although the table marks it Resolved. A second open must-address, F2, puts the Quayside lease and $5.7M of revenue in doubt. On the revenue that survives the close, the $127M ask sits above our EBITDA-led range. Call: NO-GO at this time, and not at the asking price.

## 2. Risk table for Corvanne Hospitality Group (one row per finding, notes up to 60 words)

| F# | Finding | Tier as given | Status as given | Status used | Why / bears on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | 7 brand licences end on change of control without Penwick consent | Must-Address | Resolved | OPEN (contradicted) | Narrative: "no consent letter has been issued"; Penwick decides after signing. The memo uses the narrative, not the table. |
| F2 | Quayside ground lease expires in 30 months; no extension talks | Must-Address | In-Progress | OPEN | Not closed; $5.7M of revenue rests on it. |
| F3 | Liquor licences at 5 hotels moved from the founder to the operating company | Must-Address | Resolved | resolved, not evidenced | No sentence says the reissue is still to come; no copies supplied. Not counted as open. |
| F4 | 2 management agreements cancellable by the owners on 120 days' notice | Must-Address | Resolved | resolved, not evidenced | A notice right is the contract's shape; the input is silent on notice, and silence is not a contradiction. Not counted as open, however much revenue rides on it. |
| F5 | 3 hotels below Penwick's brand-standard minimum | Manageable | Unresolved | OPEN | Bears on F1: gives Penwick a ground to refuse consent. |
| F6 | Ended 9-month shelter-housing block inside LTM | Informational | Unresolved | OPEN | Bears on the base: $4.7M of revenue and $1.3M of EBITDA will not recur. |

Open must-address findings: two of four (F1, contradicted Resolved; F2, In-Progress). F3 and F4 are resolved, not evidenced, and do not count.

## 3. Valuation for Corvanne Hospitality Group (up to 350 words plus tables)

Base bridge. Revenue: reported $66.4M - ended block $4.7M = $61.7M. EBITDA: $9.0M excluding the block, as the narrative states it (= $10.3M - $1.3M), + pre-opening $0.8M (one-time, accepted) = $9.8M. The $2.3M "brand-standard catch-up" is not added back: F5 shows the spending must continue. The seller's $13.4M fails on both counts. The seller's replacement pipeline has no contracts behind it: out of the base.

| Comp | EV/Rev | EV/EBITDA | Growth | GOP | Growth gap | Margin gap | IN/OUT and reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ravel Suites | 2.6x | 14.0x | 11% | 44% | 5 | 5 | IN: trading multiple, same select-service model |
| Ostrand Resorts | 3.4x | 19.0x | 9% | 43% | 3 | 4 | OUT: PE take-private price carries a control premium; the others are trading multiples |
| Brightwater Lodging | 1.8x | 11.0x | 4% | 38% | 2 | 1 | IN: trading multiple, same select-service model |
| Kestrel Urban Hotels | 3.1x | NM | 34% | 35% | 28 | 4 | OUT: growth 28 points above Corvanne's, from a reopening; EBITDA NM |
| Tessaly Inns | 2.1x | 12.5x | 7% | 41% | 1 | 2 | IN: trading multiple, same select-service model |

IN growth gaps, smallest first (sorted by gap, not by multiple): Tessaly 1, Brightwater 2, Ravel 5 (Ostrand, 3, is OUT). Closest two: Tessaly (2.1x revenue, 12.5x EBITDA) and Brightwater (1.8x, 11.0x). Ravel is an outer bound, not used.

EV/EBITDA (leads): 11.0x x $9.8M = $107.8M; 12.5x x $9.8M = $122.5M.
EV/Revenue (cross-check): 1.8x x $61.7M = $111.1M; 2.1x x $61.7M = $129.6M.
EBITDA leads: positive on the base, reported by both closest peers. Peers' implied EBITDA margin is 1.8 / 11.0 = 16.4% and 2.1 / 12.5 = 16.8%; Corvanne earns $9.8M / $61.7M = 15.9%, so a revenue multiple overstates it.
Reconciliation: the same revenue multiples on reported LTM $66.4M give $119.5M to $139.4M, inflated by the ended block.
Position: growth (6%) and margin (39%) sit between the two closest peers; F2 and F5 pull Corvanne to the low half.
Defended range: $107.8M to $122.5M.
Ask: $127M / $9.8M = 13.0x EBITDA and $127M / $61.7M = 2.06x revenue; $4.5M above the top of the range.
Not in the artifacts: the cost of the brand work at the three F5 hotels; it cannot be priced and is requested below.

## 4. Recommendation for Corvanne Hospitality Group (up to 170 words)

Gate: two must-address findings are open. F1, because the narrative says Penwick has issued no consent whatever the table's status says; F2, because the Quayside lease is still In-Progress. F3 and F4 are resolved, not evidenced: their documents are conditions, not blockers.
Call: NO-GO at this time.
What turns this into a go: Penwick's written consent for all seven licences, and an executed Quayside extension or the landlord's written terms.
Price: not at the asking price; our ceiling is $122.5M, reached only when F1 and F2 close.

## 5. Conditions for Corvanne Hospitality Group (one line per finding, up to 250 words)

1. F1 brand licences: Penwick's written change-of-control consent for all seven hotels, naming the buyer. Seller, from Penwick, before signing. Blocking.
2. F2 Quayside lease: executed extension or the landlord's written terms. Seller, before signing. If refused, re-value Corvanne without Quayside's $5.7M revenue. Blocking.
3. F3 liquor licences (resolved, not evidenced): copies of the five reissued licences. Seller's counsel, before signing.
4. F4 management agreements (resolved, not evidenced): each owner's written confirmation that no notice has been served. Seller, before signing.
5. F5 brand standards (bears on F1): Penwick's inspection report for the three hotels and a cost estimate for the work. Seller, before signing.
6. F6 ended block: monthly revenue by hotel with the block shown separately. Seller, before signing.
7. Data request: signed corporate contracts behind the seller's replacement claim; without them the pipeline adds no value.
Most important open gap for Corvanne Hospitality Group: Penwick's written consent to the change of control of the seven brand licences.

## Part C. Variants for inputs the memo does not show

Go case (zero open). Suppose Penwick's consent letter were in the data room and the Quayside extension signed. The count line would read: "Open must-address findings: zero of four. F1 is closed by Penwick's consent letter, F2 by the executed extension; F3 and F4 are resolved, not evidenced." Section 4 would read:
"Gate: zero must-address findings are open, because the consent letter and the executed extension close F1 and F2, and F3 and F4 are resolved, not evidenced. F5 stays open but is Manageable: it shapes the conditions and the price, not the gate. Call: GO with conditions. Conditions before signing: F3 licence copies, F4 owners' confirmations, F5 inspection report. Price: not at the asking price; ceiling $122.5M."
A table with no Must-Address finding at all reaches the same place: "Gate: zero must-address findings are open; the table holds no top-tier finding", then "Call: GO with conditions". A go never lifts the price ceiling.

No asking price. The ask line in section 3 becomes: "No asking price is given in the artifacts. The defended range is our offer ceiling: do not offer above $122.5M." No implied multiple is computed, and the thesis says the price is still to be set.

Different tier words. Above the risk table: "Tier words as given: Critical / Moderate / Low. Read Critical as must-address, Moderate as manageable, Low as informational; the same gate applies."

Comp mix not given. Under the comp table: "The comps' split between rooms and food and beverage is not given; that mix moves margin, so EV/EBITDA leads and EV/Revenue is a cross-check only."

Reported EBITDA negative. Had Corvanne reported EBITDA of -$0.5M while the seller added items back to reach a positive figure, the memo would recompute the add-backs only to test the seller's claim, then write "EV/EBITDA: NM, reported EBITDA is negative" and lead with EV/Revenue. A recomputed add-back figure is never a valuation base.

Every peer grows faster. If all IN peers had grown faster than Corvanne: "Corvanne trails every IN peer on growth, so the lowest IN multiple, 1.8x, is the top of its range; a further 0.2x discount for the growth gap gives 1.6x x $61.7M = $98.7M at the low end." The discount is stated in turns, with its reason.

Own filing awaiting grant. Had F3 instead read "Corvanne's application to register its own brand name is pending; a rival chain filed a similar mark earlier", marked Resolved, it would stay resolved, not evidenced: a filing of the target's own awaiting grant is the asset's ordinary state, not a dispute against it, and no sentence says the rival filing is being contested. Counsel's written opinion on both filings becomes a condition before signing; the gate stays clear.

Contradicted at a lower tier. Had F5 been marked Resolved while the narrative said Penwick's re-inspection is still to come, its row would read OPEN (contradicted), tier still Manageable: it does not block the gate, and the re-inspection result becomes a condition.
