# Authoring an IC decision memo

Produce a decision the committee can act on from the supplied record. Keep the
source record intact; distinguish observations, calculations and proposed actions.
Before drafting, open `decision-ledger.md` with read_reference for the ledger and
calculation checks. Use that method privately, then write the memo itself.

## Establish the decision record

Inventory the three artifacts and any explicit section-length requirements. If
an artifact or field is absent, identify exactly what is missing. Retain all five
sections, but say when a risk conclusion or comp-derived valuation is unavailable;
never invent the missing artifact or substitute market facts from memory.

Give every supplied risk a short stable ID. Keep its original tier and status in
separate, clearly labeled source columns. In another column record its economic
consequence, any contrary narrative evidence, and the IC treatment. A row marked
Resolved stays visibly Resolved even if closure is disputed. Flag apparent
mis-tiering and explain any proposed escalation as an IC assessment, not a new
upstream classification. If tiers use another vocabulary, show a contextual
mapping to Must-Address / Manageable / Informational while retaining the original
terms; explain uncertainty rather than silently equating labels.

Cross-read the financial narrative against every row. A live process, missing
status, deferral or management expectation is not a completed resolution. An
explicit contradiction to a Resolved label makes closure disputed for this
decision; identify the conflicting statements and evidence needed to reconcile
them. Conversely, a risk description or the absence of an attached closure
document alone does not prove that a supplied Resolved status is false. Accept
that status absent specific contrary evidence, and describe residual exposure
without inventing a fresh unresolved finding. Any material new issue from the
narrative belongs in the table as an explicitly IC-added row with no invented
source tier/status. The thesis's primary risk must have a named table row.

## Reconcile the economics before pricing

Record currency, units, period, definition and source for each financial base.
Keep revenue, ARR, transaction volume, gross billings and net revenue distinct.
Cross-foot segment sums, weighted margins and any reported-to-adjusted earnings
bridge. Preserve inconsistent supplied figures and show the unreconciled amount;
do not silently average them or substitute your own result as management's fact.
Historical growth is not automatically the post-churn growth rate. Do not deduct
a historical restatement from a different period without a bridge.

Choose a denominator compatible with the supplied comp metric. Remove known
nontransferable revenue and separate nonrecurring projects or lost customers
where relevant to the acquired business; label retained-run-rate versus LTM
comparisons and show the historical sensitivity when useful. Do not assume that
removing revenue removes the same amount of EBITDA. Identify actual costs,
contribution or an unresolved bridge. Use positive sustainable EBITDA for an
earnings-based method; negative/NM EBITDA is not a numeric comp observation.
Unverified seller add-backs are a sensitivity, not established recurring earnings.

Name **every** provided comparable in a compact inventory with its relevant
multiple and an explicit Include / Exclude / Sensitivity decision. Explain each
exclusion and premium/outlier treatment from the supplied facts. Do not discard
a comp merely because its multiple is inconvenient, or discard its usable revenue
multiple merely because EBITDA is NM. Distinguish an excluded comp from one
retained with lower weight.

For the selected pool, calculate the relevant multiple median and the supplied
growth and margin medians. State the target's relative growth and margin and the
direction of their effect on the selected multiple. Discuss size separately: if
comp scale is absent, say a size comparison is unavailable; do not fabricate peer
revenues or a precise size discount. Also discuss business mix or transaction
premiums when evidenced. Explain the final multiple interval as underwriting
judgment anchored to the retained comps. There is no universal haircut. If two
methods diverge, select and defend a primary method, show the cross-check, and
explain the gap rather than averaging incompatible answers.

Show each endpoint as **base × selected multiple = EV** with currency, units and
rounding consistent. Keep headline, normalized and downside bases visibly
separate. Price a known risk only once; a discount does not resolve a legal or
operating blocker. If cash and debt are supplied, show EV − debt + eligible cash
as a separate indicative equity bridge. If incomplete, state equity value cannot
be established. Do not invent a tax FX rate, financing, cost savings or return
hurdle. Label proposed mitigations, sensitivities and negotiating ranges as such.

## Set one operative recommendation

Apply this gate after reading the whole record, then check it again after drafting:

- **Request More Data** when a Must-Address finding is Unresolved, In-Progress,
  missing/ambiguous in status, or credibly disputed by contrary evidence. Specify
  what evidence changes the decision. This is a hold on acquisition approval,
  even if limited diligence or negotiation continues.
- **Proceed** is available only when all Must-Address findings are resolved and
  every Manageable finding has a named, credible mitigation. State proposed owners,
  actions and timing without implying those actions are already complete. Open
  manageable work can be controlled through a credible closing/operating plan;
  it does not automatically require Pass or endless new diligence.
- **Pass** when the supplied evidence demonstrates a thesis-breaking exposure,
  an infeasible remedy, or unattractive economics on stated terms. Explain why
  more information or a workable price/structure does not presently cure it.
  Uncertainty alone is not proof of permanent impairment.

Use one of these verdicts clearly. Do not call an unresolved Must-Address a
"conditional Proceed" by moving its resolution to post-close conditions. Where
information is missing, prefer an evidence request to an invented favorable
assumption. Keep the recommendation consistent with the IC treatment column.

## Write exactly these five sections, in order

1. **Deal Thesis** — a concise, company-specific investment case, key economics,
   and the primary risk that could invalidate it. No generic market essay.
2. **Tiered Risk Table** — all supplied findings, stable IDs, source tier/status,
   and concise IC assessment/mitigation. Make contradictions and added rows visible.
3. **Defended Valuation Range** — eligible base, complete comp inventory, median
   comparisons, directional adjustments, endpoint arithmetic, cross-checks and
   EV/equity distinction. If unsupported, state what prevents a range.
4. **Recommendation** — one explicit verdict and its causal link to the risk
   state and valuation. Identify the exact blocker or basis for proceeding.
5. **Conditions and Next Steps** — each bullet names one table row ID and finding,
   a proposed responsible role, a concrete action/deliverable, and a deadline or
   decision milestone. Separate bullets for different findings. Add an IC financial
   row if a necessary valuation/data request otherwise has no row to reference.
   For Proceed, specify enforceable closing protections and operational handoffs;
   for Request More Data, specify questions and evidence before IC reconsideration;
   for Pass, give the reason to stop and concrete reopening criteria if feasible.

Honor numerical section limits if supplied. Otherwise keep sections proportionate:
one thesis paragraph, a complete compact table, a transparent valuation analysis,
one recommendation paragraph and focused row-linked actions. Integrate financial
analysis into these sections; do not add an executive-summary or financial-review
section. Write for this deal, not a reusable letter with names exchanged.

Before returning, trace thesis risk → table row → operative verdict → action.
Verify every source label, comp decision, median, selected base and arithmetic
endpoint. Remove unsupported specifics and claims of work already completed.
