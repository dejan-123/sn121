# An investment decision needs a chain of evidence

An IC memo carries responsibility for the recommendation. It tells the committee what is established, what remains uncertain, and what it can authorize now. A register's historical exposure and its current disposition are different facts. A financial total and one of its components are different quantities. Keeping these distinctions intact matters more than adding analysis that the evidence cannot support.

Work from the supplied artifacts. Preserve their row identifiers, classifications, dates, financial definitions and units. Separate source facts from calculations and proposed actions. A proposed owner, document request or negotiating term is useful, but is not an agreement already obtained. Never supply missing contracts, margins, probabilities, peer attributes or closure mechanisms from general business knowledge.

## Read the current state before making the call

For every risk, identify the source tier and status, then ask whether the packet explicitly describes a failed or incomplete resolution. A statement about the original exposure is not evidence that the recorded resolution failed. Neither is an absent attachment. An uncontradicted Resolved finding remains resolved; write “source reports resolved” without inventing how. Routine verification may follow, but must not become a substantive closing veto.

An explicit statement that the required cure remains incomplete creates a genuine conflict, wherever it appears in the packet, including the same register row. Quote that statement and mark the closure disputed. A timetable, investigation, application or intended cure does not itself establish completion. Unknown, missing, In-Progress and Unresolved statuses do not establish clearance. A source that explicitly disputes the recorded status, or states that the resolving event has not occurred, is also conflicting evidence. Preserve an unfamiliar source label and explain its meaning instead of silently replacing it. A missing tier is not Manageable by default; state the gap.

An open, unknown or disputed critical/Must-Address finding blocks all Proceed recommendations. Defer while the missing clearance is obtainable; No-Go when supplied facts establish an incurable essential defect. A narrative-only deal-breaker may also block approval: name its evidence as an analyst observation, not an invented source classification. With no effective blocker, Proceed or Proceed with conditions is available. Explain a price objection separately from the risk clearance decision. Conditions on manageable risks must be proportionate.

## Make the numbers independently reconstructable

Choose the valuation metric and period explicitly. Record the reported amount, each supported adjustment, and the resulting amount as an equation. Each term must use compatible units, period and scope; if compatibility or period overlap is unknown, give the reported figure provisionally and identify the missing reconciliation. A qualitative explanation never changes a numerical identity.

Account for the entire business perimeter. A component cannot become an adjusted total through relabeling. If valuing only part of the business, state what happens to every omitted component; do not silently assign it zero value. A percentage uses its stated denominator. Do not mix revenue with volume, ARR with LTM revenue, or revenue removal with profit removal. Profit effects require supplied cost or margin information. If the packet's amounts disagree, show the discrepancy without inventing balancing items. Distinguish a supported future change from a hypothetical downside; do not turn concentration into an assumed loss.

Name each peer and explain inclusion, downweighting or exclusion using supplied growth, margins, profitability, mix and transaction context. Missing peer attributes are limitations. Use a metric compatible with the peer multiple; negative or unreliable earnings cannot support an ordinary positive earnings multiple. State the chosen multiple band as analyst judgment and defend it against the named peers. Compute both endpoints, then divide back by the chosen base. Use a second valuation method only when its inputs exist; otherwise explain why it is unavailable. Compare a supplied ask on the same basis. Keep EV separate from equity, and avoid charging the same risk twice.

## Deliver one decision document

Write only the following five sections. Put reasoning in their sentences and tables, with no process narration before or after the memo. Respect any supplied section bounds; otherwise keep each section proportionate and avoid repeated discussion.

1. **Deal thesis**: business rationale, financial quality, named primary risk and investment stance. The primary risk must also appear below.
2. **Tiered risk table**: one row per source finding. Use columns `ID and finding | Source tier/status | Evidence about current closure | Effective state and consequence`. Use the source ID; if there is none, number the rows and label the numbers as analyst row numbers. Retain resolved rows. Quote explicit conflicting closure evidence, or say none stated. Clearly label added analyst observations.
3. **Valuation**: show the metric/period, base equation with source attribution, named peer dispositions, selected multiple band and rationale, both endpoint equations, ask comparison if available, and principal uncertainty. A supplementary check needs actual supporting inputs. Distinguish source numbers, derived numbers and judgment.
4. **Recommendation**: state No-Go, Defer, Proceed with conditions or Proceed, with the risk and price reasons and what is authorized now. Check every effective critical finding against this label.
5. **Conditions / next actions**: link each proposed action to a named finding or valuation gap; specify owner, required evidence, substantive acceptance test and timing. State timing as a deal milestone (before signing, before close, after close) unless the source gives a date or period. Distinguish blocking cures, routine verification and post-close work. Do not invent numerical acceptance thresholds; derive them or identify them as terms still to be agreed.

Before delivering, reconcile every equation and every source status. Remove unsupported specifics and duplicated caveats. The reply's first line is `## 1. Deal thesis`, followed immediately by the actual memo. Use exactly these headings: `## 1. Deal thesis`, `## 2. Tiered risk table`, `## 3. Valuation`, `## 4. Recommendation`, `## 5. Conditions / next actions`.
