# IC memo casebook: default reading, the flip, the deciding test

Open `references/worked-memos.md` with read_reference before writing (two complete worked memos). Use only the input's names and figures. The reply is the memo alone. Its first line is `# Investment committee memo: <Target> acquisition`, with no opening sentence or walk-through before it.

## The rules
1. Status. Default: carry it as stated; an accepted "Resolved" gets its resolving record as a condition. Flips to OPEN when the finding, the narrative or a note says a process is still running: a decision deferred, a re-bid or review under way, counsel's comfort with no closure letter, claims pending, auditors not opined, management unable to document. Keep the tier; the cell reads table / evidence / used. Not a flip: a record absent from the pack, a standing term (notice period, consent requirement, application on file, concentration), silence about a waiver.
2. Blocking. Only Must-Address (or Critical, High) blocks. Manageable, Moderate, Informational and Low never block, even reopened; no must-address row means zero blockers. Count open blockers in words, by row. Zero: "Proceed, with conditions". One or more: "Conditional proceed": the deal does not proceed until conditions precedent close each open finding with its record, owner and timing; the IC approves signing only then, and nothing closes before.
3. Price, its own axis. Ask above the range: "not at this price". Inside: supported on value. None given: "No asking price is given; none is assumed"; the range is the ceiling.
4. Tiers and cross-links. Print a tier key; map by meaning (Critical = must-address), never by rank. A lower row raises another's weight only when the input states the tie: a ratio breaching a standard a blocker depends on, an Informational item that sets the base, contracts or a lease ending soon after close with no renewal. Alarming rows held lower get a reason.
5. Base. Default: reported LTM. Subtract, printed as "reported - item (reason) = base": a one-time project; gross volume where net revenue is the metric; revenue the buyer cannot inherit; an upward restatement auditors have not opined on (the restated amount); customers already gone; add-backs with no named one-time event. Not subtracted: revenue only at risk (re-bid, remediation, a billing fix, renewal pending), named as a risk or a downside on the input's own figures; unwon prospects are not priced. A figure the narrative states is used as stated, never recomputed or rescaled. Base EBITDA at or below zero: EV/EBITDA is NM.
6. Comps. OUT: a takeover price with a control, PE or strategic premium, or a gap score above 15 (growth gap + gross-margin gap, in points). Print every score in the input's order; of the IN peers the two lowest set the range, the others are bounds. "No exclusions flagged" still gets a reason per comp; unknown comp mix is a named gap.
7. Arithmetic, one line per step, each figure from the input or the line above. Lead with EV/EBITDA when base EBITDA is positive and the range-setting peers report it, else EV/Revenue: "low x x EBITDA $B = $L; high x x EBITDA $B = $H". One cross-check, no percentages. Ask / base = implied multiple; also / LTM. Position in words (lower or upper half) against the lowest-score peer. That one range is quoted unchanged in the recommendation; the thesis carries none.
8. Gaps and claims. Name missing records and conflicting opinions; never adopt the favourable one. Recompute seller claims from the input. Name the most important gap and the record that closes it. Never invent an ask, consent, cure date, period, amount or comp mix.
9. Shape. Five sections, each heading with the target's name and bound: Deal thesis (max 130 words; ends "Must-address rows open: <count>" and the call); Risk table (one row per finding; notes max 150 words); Valuation (max 450 words); Recommendation (max 120 words); Conditions (max 45 words per item; timing is a deal event or a period the input prints).

## Casebook: one mini-case per flip, all from quarrying

Status flips. A gritstone quarry's extension row says Resolved; the narrative says the planning vote was adjourned. Adjourned is not granted: "Resolved (table) / vote adjourned, no consent (narrative) / OPEN (used)".
Status holds. A sand pit's landowner consent and a haulage contract cancellable on 60 days' notice are both Resolved and nothing says a process is running: both accepted, and the consent and the contract are conditions to signing. "Nothing says it was waived" does not reopen either.

Blocking with value intact. A basalt quarry's discharge permit has lapsed (must-address, Unresolved) and the ask sits inside the range: "Conditional proceed; value supports the ask; no signing until the reissued permit is in hand."
Zero blockers. A pack tiers every row Manageable or Informational, some still open: no row is Must-Address, so zero blockers and "Proceed, with conditions", the open rows becoming conditions.

Cross-link. Dust readings over the permit limit (Manageable) while the extension waits on an inquiry where neighbours cite dust: the dust row raises the weight of the planning row. A haulage renewal date that nothing ties to a blocker gets no cross-link.

Base. A crushed-rock producer's LTM holds a finished one-off rail-ballast order and a customer that has served notice: both come out, and the ask is shown against LTM and the base. A supply framework up for retender stays in, named as a risk.
Seller framing. Plant hire added back as one-off appears in each of three years: the add-back is refused and adjusted EBITDA restated. Churn called "low-margin" whose share of net revenue equals its share of volume fails the same test.

Comps. Target 6% growth, 33% margin. Scores: A 5%/34% = 2; B 12%/40% = 13; C 8%/31% = 4; D 25%/44% = 30; E, bought by an infrastructure fund, OUT on premium. Two lowest: A and C set the range; B is a bound; D is OUT. The position line gives both figures for growth, margin and retention against A; retention below 100% argues for the lower half.

Arithmetic. Each line names its base: "9.0x x EBITDA $8.1M = $72.9M", never a revenue multiple against EBITDA. An unknown comp mix does not change the lead: EBITDA already carries the margin. The cross-check gets one sentence on why when its gap exceeds 15%, usually the target's margin against the peers'. With no comp EV/EBITDA there is no cross-check, and one line says so.

Shape. The risk table runs # | finding | tier | status in table | status used, and why | bears on, then the blocker count in words, cross-links and the reasons rows are held lower. Never a bare "proceed" over an open blocker, and never "conditional proceed" with zero blockers.
Conditions. Order: conditions precedent (row, record, owner, timing), then purchase-agreement protections, post-signing items, data requests. Owner: the party the input names, else the seller. Timing: before signing, at close, before the IC re-votes, or a period the pack prints; never "within 90 days of close", "12 months post-close" or a retention length the pack does not give. A consent or letter the pack does not show is "not in the pack", never "obtained".
