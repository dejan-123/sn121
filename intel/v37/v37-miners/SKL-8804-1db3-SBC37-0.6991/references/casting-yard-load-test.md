## The memo as built

# IC Memo: Corbel Precast Holdings

### 1. Deal Thesis

Corbel is a precast-concrete manufacturer whose two casting yards produce hollow-core slabs, bridge beams and drainage sections, with $56.0M of LTM revenue growing 9% and $9.2M of reported EBITDA. The case is a regional producer growing faster than its listed peers, with last spring's bridge-beam warranty claim already settled. The primary risk is R1: the hollow-core slab licence, $14.0M of revenue and $2.8M of EBITDA, ends on the change of control unless the system owner consents in writing, and consent has not been requested. The $75.0M ask is 8.15x reported EBITDA, which still includes that line.

### 2. Risk Assessment

| Row | Finding in brief | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Gate reading | Flag and deal impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Hollow-core slab licence ends on change of control without written consent | Must-Address | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | $2.8M of EBITDA ($14.0M of revenue, 25% × $56.0M) kept out of the base until consent is signed |
| R2 | Bridge-beam load-test failure and warranty claim | Must-Address | Resolved | Resolved | Consistent: claim settled in July within the warranty reserve; settlement in the data room |
| R3 | Two of three gantry cranes past rated service life, replacement unbudgeted | Manageable | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | Replacement cost not provided, so nothing is deducted from the range |
| R4 | Single cement mill; next year's contract terms in negotiation | Manageable | In-Progress | Open: In-Progress | Cost exposure on the main raw material |
| R5 | Plant manager and chief estimator retiring within three years | Informational | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | Succession; not gating |

R1 keeps its upstream status and is the only must-address row that reads Open. The system owner has refused nothing: the licence requires its consent, which has not yet been sought, so the gap can still be closed.

### 3. Valuation

Primary multiple: EV/EBITDA, because Corbel is profitable and mainly a manufacturer. Base: $6.4M, that is reported EBITDA of $9.2M less the $2.8M contributed by the hollow-core line, whose licence ends on the change of control unless consent is given. The seller's base is the full $9.2M.

| Comparable | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Ashlar Concrete Products (8.5x) | Included | Regional precast peer with similar growth and margin |
| Plinth Industries (7.5x) | Included | Precast peer |
| Keyway Castings (9.0x) | Included | Highest included margin (26%), still within the set's spread |
| Lintel Building Materials (8.0x) | Included | Precast peer |
| Modulus Building Systems (13.0x) | Excluded | Gross margin of 40% is 14 points above the next-highest comp (26%), so it sits over 10 points clear of all the others |

Included multiples sorted: 7.5x, 8.0x, 8.5x, 9.0x; median (8.0x + 8.5x) ÷ 2 = 8.25x. Included medians: growth 4.5%, gross margin 22.5%. An EBITDA step is 1.0x:
- growth, 9% against 4.5%: 4.5 points above, which clears the 4-point line, so one step up;
- gross margin, 24% against 22.5%: 1.5 points above, no step;
- concentration: no customer above $4.5M, and the top three ($13.0M) are at most 31% of the $42.0M of revenue that transfers, so no step;
- size: comp scale not provided, no step.

Running sum: 8.25x + 1.0x growth = 9.25x. Range, 9.25x ± 1.0x: 8.25x × $6.4M = $52.8M and 10.25x × $6.4M = $65.6M, so **$52.8M–$65.6M**.

The ask of $75.0M sits $9.4M above the high end and implies 11.70x the $6.4M base (8.15x on the seller's $9.2M). If the system owner consents, the same multiples on $9.2M give $75.9M–$94.3M, and the ask would sit below that range.

### 4. Recommendation

**Request more data.** The gate blocks Proceed because R1, a must-address row, reads Open: Unresolved. This is not a Pass: no artifact says the system owner will refuse, and a consent requirement is a condition to satisfy rather than a refusal. On the transferable base the comps support at most $65.6M, $9.4M below the $75.0M ask; a signed consent would lift the range to $75.9M–$94.3M.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1:** Deal lead to get the licensor's written change-of-control consent before signing. Signed: revalue on $9.2M and re-run the gate. Refused: value stays on $6.4M and the committee revisits the thesis.
- **R2:** Legal diligence to confirm before signing that the July settlement releases every claim tied to the failed beams, and to list it in the disclosure schedule.
- **R3:** Operations to obtain replacement quotes for both gantry cranes before signing; the quoted cost comes off both ends of the range as a debt-like item.
- **R4:** Procurement to secure signed cement terms for next year, or a quote from a second mill, before signing.
- **R5:** CEO to name successors for the plant manager and chief estimator within the first 100 days.

## How section 5 changes with the verdict

- **Proceed**: each bullet is a closing condition with its instrument (warranty, indemnity, covenant, consent or closure letter); an escrow or holdback appears only where the artifacts size the exposure, and day-one steps follow. Manageable rows keep their named mitigations.
- **Request more data**: each open row is given the single document that would close it, the person responsible, the due point, and the effect of a yes or a no on both the verdict and the range.
- **Pass**: the bullets state why the defect is beyond repair inside this deal and the change that would justify the committee taking a second look.

## One-fact notes

- **Tier words.** If the upstream table grades rows Critical / Moderate / Low, those grades go into the memo unchanged, with one line saying Critical is read as must-address for the gate.
- **Words that read Open.** In-Progress, "substantially resolved", "deferred" and an empty status cell all read Open in the Gate reading column.
- **Rights versus refusals.** A termination right, a notice period or a pending review is a load the deal must be designed to carry, so it becomes a condition. A Pass needs a sentence you can quote in which the counterparty refuses, or the terms forbid, for good.
- **Manageable rows only.** When no must-address row reads Open, open or flagged manageable rows lead to Proceed with conditions once each has a mitigation; an apparent mis-tier flag becomes a pre-signing condition.
- **Check the seller's footing.** Before comparing a banker's multiple with the range, rebuild it on the right base: net revenue where the industry prices net, recurring revenue where the quoted line includes one-time items.
- **Missing inputs.** When a metric or an artifact is absent, the memo says "not provided", values on what exists, and asks for the gap in section 5.

The test ends here. The memo's very first characters are `# IC Memo: ` and the target's name. An opening sentence of narration (for example "Now I will draft the IC memo") above the title is the most common failure; all working stays out of the reply.
