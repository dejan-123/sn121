Two invented deals written up in full, then closing sections from two others. The companies and figures exist only here. What carries over to the memo you write is the layout, the density and the way each call is tied to its evidence; every fact in your memo comes from the inputs in front of you, none from here.

# Deal one: blocked, and curable by evidence

What the three artifacts held. The risk table used a must-address, manageable, informational scale and listed five findings; one status cell was empty, and one finding marked resolved was described in the financial narrative as "filed, decision awaited". The narrative gave revenue of $264.0M growing 4%, a 41% gross margin, reported EBITDA of $38.4M and a seller-adjusted figure of $45.9M. The comparables table listed five companies, one of them a consumer brand and one a company acquired last year. The seller asked $560M.

The memo:

## Ardwell Dental Laboratories, dental laboratory network: IC memo

### 1. Deal Thesis

Ardwell is a network of dental laboratories with $264.0M of revenue, growing 4% a year at a 41% gross margin; owning it would give us scale in a trade where laboratories still sell locally. That case holds only once three top-tier findings close, and the $560M ask sits about 27% above the top of the range the comparables support. The primary risk is R1, the regulator's open quality-system finding at the main milling site: until re-inspection clears it, the site that produces most of the output operates under an unresolved compliance finding.

### 2. Tiered Risk Table: five findings, three blocking

| # | Finding | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Read for this memo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Regulator found the main milling site's quality system out of compliance; remediation plan filed, re-inspection not scheduled | Must-address | In progress | Blocking: in progress is not resolved |
| R2 | Wastewater discharge permit for the main site expired | Must-address | Resolved | Blocking. Flag: the narrative says the renewal is "filed, decision awaited"; label kept, treated as open |
| R3 | Collective agreement with laboratory technicians expired | Must-address | Not stated | Blocking: no status given, treated as open |
| R4 | Order-management system leaves vendor support next year; migration not started | Manageable | Unresolved | Open. Mitigation: migration cost deducted from price |
| R5 | Aligner line (about a fifth of revenue) relies on one resin supplier with no contract | Informational | Unresolved | Flag: appears under-tiered, a supply stop would halt a fifth of revenue |

Scale as supplied: must-address is the top tier. Two flags for the committee: R2's status and R5's tier are carried as written and are not changed here.

### 3. Valuation Range: $403M to $442M

The base is reported EBITDA of $38.4M. The seller's adjusted figure of $45.9M adds back $7.5M of "integration costs" that the narrative does not evidence as one-time, so the add-back is not accepted.

Three comparables are included: Calder Orthodontic Labs (10.5x), Norbrook Dental Supply (11.5x) and Fenwick Prosthetics (12.5x). Sable Clear Aligners (24.0x) is excluded because it sells direct to consumers at a 71% margin, growing 38%, which is a different business from a laboratory network. Tavish Dental Group (15.0x) is excluded because it is an acquisition multiple carrying a control premium. With EBITDA positive and every included comp reporting it, EV/EBITDA leads.

In order the included multiples are 10.5x, 11.5x and 12.5x, median 11.5x. Ardwell grows 4% against a 7% median and earns a 41% gross margin against 45%, below on both; comp revenue is not in the table, so size is not measurable and is treated as neutral. The range therefore runs from the low end to the median:

- 10.5x × $38.4M = $403.2M
- 11.5x × $38.4M = $441.6M

That gives $403M to $442M. On the same comps, 1.7x to 1.9x revenue × $264.0M gives $449M to $502M, which sits above it: Ardwell's EBITDA margin of 14.5% is below the 16% to 18% the comps' own multiples imply, so the range rests on EBITDA. The $560M ask implies 14.6x and is above the range.

### 4. Recommendation: Request more data

Request more data. R1, R2 and R3 are top-tier and open, R2 on the facts and R3 for want of a status, so the committee cannot approve the deal today; each can be closed by a document a regulator or the seller can supply. Any later bid is capped at $442M, the top of the range, against a $560M ask.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- R1: Regulatory counsel to obtain the re-inspection date and the regulator's written outcome before re-review; a clearance letter closes it.
- R2: Environmental counsel to obtain the regulator's decision on the discharge permit before re-review; the granted renewal closes it, and a refusal would leave the main site unable to operate.
- R3: Employment counsel to obtain the signed collective agreement, or the state of talks in writing from both sides, before re-review.
- R4: Finance diligence to cost the system migration from vendor quotes; the amount is deducted from price at signing.
- R5: Risk analyst to confirm or revise the tier; operations diligence to request a supply contract or a qualified second resin source before signing.

Next step: the seller is asked for the documents on R1 to R3, and the committee re-reviews when all three are in.

Why it reads this way. Three rows block for three different reasons (a status that is not resolved, a resolved label the narrative contradicts, a status never given) and the memo says which is which. The seller's add-back has a name but no evidence, so the base is the reported figure. Two comps leave the set, each for its own stated reason, and the two multiples disagree for a reason the memo gives.

# Deal two: proceeds, with every open row mitigated

What the three artifacts held. The risk table used a high, medium, low scale and listed five findings. The narrative gave revenue of $86.5M growing 12%, a 34% gross margin and EBITDA of $10.6M with no seller adjustments, and put the mark-to-market loss on forward coffee purchases at $2.3M. The comparables table listed five listed companies, one of them in a lender-led restructuring. The seller asked $112M.

The memo:

## Pellam Roasters, specialty coffee roaster: IC memo

### 1. Deal Thesis

Pellam roasts and supplies specialty coffee to grocers and cafés: $86.5M of revenue growing 12% at a 34% gross margin, with $10.6M of EBITDA. It gives us a branded supplier whose growth and margin match listed peers, at a $112M ask that sits inside the range the comparables support. The primary risk is R3, forward green-coffee purchases contracted above today's market, which the narrative values at a $2.3M loss.

### 2. Tiered Risk Table: five findings, none blocking

| # | Finding | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Read for this memo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Recall of one blend last year; regulator's file | High | Resolved | Closed: the narrative cites the regulator's closure notice |
| R2 | Trademark opposition to the main brand in one export market | Medium | Resolved | Closed on the label; the inputs do not show what closed it, so the evidence is a condition |
| R3 | Forward green-coffee purchases sit above market; mark-to-market loss of $2.3M | Medium | Unresolved | Open. Mitigation: $2.3M deducted from price |
| R4 | Customer rebate accruals under-recorded; correction under way | Medium | In progress | Open. Mitigation: seller indemnity for rebates earned before closing |
| R5 | Organic certification for one product line due for re-audit | Low | Unresolved | Open. Mitigation: re-audit passed as a condition of closing, owned by the quality lead |

Scale as supplied: high is the top tier, and its one row is closed. No tier or status is disputed; R2 carries an evidence condition.

### 3. Valuation Range: $106M to $117M

The base is reported EBITDA of $10.6M; the narrative shows no seller adjustments, so none are made.

Four comparables are included: Harrow Tea & Coffee (9.0x), Linden Beverage (10.0x), Castell Foods (11.0x) and Orrin Provisions (12.0x), each a listed trading multiple from the same trade. Dunmore Mills (5.5x) is excluded: it is in a lender-led restructuring with revenue falling 9%, and a distressed multiple prices survival, not the trade. EV/EBITDA leads.

Sorted, the four multiples are 9.0x, 10.0x, 11.0x and 12.0x, with a median of 10.5x. Pellam's 12% growth equals the set's 12% median and its 34% gross margin is within a point of the 34.5% median. The table gives no comp revenue, so size cannot be measured and counts as neutral. In line on both, the range is the band one comp either side of the median:

- 10.0x × $10.6M = $106.0M
- 11.0x × $10.6M = $116.6M

That gives $106M to $117M. On the same comps, 1.2x to 1.4x revenue × $86.5M gives $104M to $121M, which contains it. The $112M ask implies 10.6x and sits inside the range.

### 4. Recommendation: Proceed

Proceed. The one top-tier row, R1, is resolved and the narrative cites the regulator's closure notice. R3, R4 and R5 are open lower-tier rows and each carries a named mitigation; R2 carries an evidence condition. The $112M ask sits inside the $106M to $117M range, so a bid at the ask less the $2.3M in R3 is supported.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- R2: Deal counsel to see the withdrawal or settlement of the trademark opposition before signing; if it cannot be produced, the risk analyst reopens the row.
- R3: $2.3M, the loss the narrative states, is deducted from the purchase price at signing; finance diligence to re-mark the purchase book at that date.
- R4: Seller to give a specific indemnity for customer rebates earned before closing; finance diligence to agree the corrected accrual before signing.
- R5: Quality lead to obtain the re-audit certificate as a condition of closing.

Next step: deal lead to confirm the bid at the ask less the R3 deduction and open the confirmatory items on R2 to R5.

Why it reads this way. Nothing blocks, so the work moves to the open lower-tier rows: each gets an instrument, and the only number attached to a mitigation is one the inputs supplied. The exclusion here is at the low end, for a reason of its own. Had the inputs given no asking price, the valuation section would end "Asking price not provided, so no implied multiple is stated" and the recommendation would give the range as the basis for a bid, without naming a figure the seller never gave.

# Closing sections of a deal that is passed

The top-tier row here was a planning authority's refusal to extend the extraction permit for the target's main quarry, with the appeal period already ended according to the narrative. No document could close it.

### 4. Recommendation: Pass

Pass. R1 is top-tier and open, and nothing we could request would close it: the authority has already refused the permit extension for the main quarry and the appeal period has ended, so the reserves the thesis rests on cannot be worked once the current permit lapses. R2 is also open but does not decide this. The ask is not tested against the range, because the base it would multiply will not exist in its present form.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- R1: We reopen only if the authority grants a new permit for the main quarry, evidenced by its decision notice; that application is the seller's to make.
- R2: A reopened process would still need the haulage contract re-signed on a fixed term before any bid.

Next step: deal lead to tell the seller's adviser the decision and what would reopen it.

# A valuation section when the comparables are missing

Had the comparables table not been supplied for deal two, the section would read:

### 3. Valuation Range: none stated, comparables not provided

No comparables dataset was provided, so no multiple can be selected and no range is stated. The narrative fixes the base a range would use: reported EBITDA of $10.6M on $86.5M of revenue. A multiple recalled from elsewhere would put an unsourced number in front of the committee.

The verdict then becomes Request more data whatever the risk table shows, and the first data request is the missing artifact itself: the comparables set with each company's multiples, growth and margin, from the analyst who owns it, before re-review.
