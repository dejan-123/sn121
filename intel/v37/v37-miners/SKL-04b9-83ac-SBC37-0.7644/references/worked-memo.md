# Worked memo (invented deal)

Every name and figure below is invented; only the shape carries over. The deal: a hospital-linen laundry group, Ostrander Linen Services. Its risk table has a must-address row marked Resolved that the narrative contradicts, a must-address row with no status at all, and a tier that looks low. Its comp set holds a sponsor-premium transaction and a growth outlier. The seller quotes a multiple on revenue that includes a one-off contract and on an EBITDA figure with a vague add-back.

# IC Memo: Ostrander Linen Services

## 1. Deal Thesis

Ostrander washes and delivers surgical and bed linen for 31 hospitals from two plants, on contracts that renew yearly and have grown 6% a year. Buying it gives us a regional route network we can fill with our own healthcare accounts. On a recurring EBITDA base of $3.9M and an included comp median of 8.75x, we defend $31.2M to $34.1M against a $36.0M ask. The primary risk is R1: the hospital group behind 38% of revenue can terminate on change of control, and its consent is still a draft.

## 2. Risk Table

| Row | Finding | Tier (as recorded) | Status (as recorded) | Gate | Evidence or mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Hospital group master agreement (38% of revenue) terminable on change of control | Must-Address | Resolved | Open | Narrative: consent letter exists only as an unsigned draft |
| R2 | Wastewater discharge permit at the main plant expires in 5 months; renewal filed | Must-Address | (none given) | Open | No status recorded; read as unresolved |
| R3 | Wage-and-hour claim by four route drivers | Manageable | In-Progress | Open | Mediation scheduled; exposure not quantified |
| R4 | Main-plant boiler at end of life; $1.9M replacement unbudgeted | Manageable | Unresolved | Open | Mitigation: price adjustment or seller-funded capex |
| R5 | Linen loss rate rose from 9% to 13% of stock | Informational | Unresolved | Open | Track in the first 100 days |

**Flagged observations**
- R1 is recorded as Resolved, but the financial narrative says the consent letter is unsigned. The gate reads it Open. Not re-tiered.
- R2 has no status. A missing status is read as unresolved, never as cleared. Not re-tiered.
- R4 is filed as Manageable, yet a boiler failure would stop the plant that does 70% of volume; it reads closer to must-address. Covered by the R4 condition. Not re-tiered.

## 3. Valuation

**Base.** Reported revenue of $26.0M includes a $2.2M one-off emergency contract that has ended, so the recurring base is $23.8M. Reported EBITDA is $3.6M. Of the seller's two add-backs, the $0.8M plant-relocation cost is documented as one-time and is accepted; the $0.7M "growth initiatives" has no support and is rejected. Removing the one-off contract's $0.5M contribution gives `$3.6M + $0.8M - $0.5M = $3.9M`. The seller's $36.0M rests on the $26.0M revenue and an adjusted $4.9M EBITDA; neither is used.

**Multiple.** EV/EBITDA, since every included comp reports it; EV/Revenue is the cross-check.

| Comp | EV/EBITDA | Growth | Gross margin | In / Out | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brightwash Group | 9.5x | 7% | 30% | In | Same service, similar mix |
| Halden Textile Care | 8.0x | 5% | 27% | In | Same service |
| Corrie Hygiene | 11.0x | 9% | 33% | In | Same service, higher margin |
| Pellow Laundries | 7.5x | 4% | 26% | In | Same service, slower |
| Varn Healthcare Linen (sale 2 years ago) | 14.5x | 8% | 31% | Out | Transaction price with a stated sponsor premium, not a trading level |
| Quillon Uniform Rental | 10.0x | 31% | 35% | Out | Growth far apart from every other peer (next highest 9%) |

Included values in order: 7.5x, 8.0x, 9.5x, 11.0x. Median `(8.0x + 9.5x) / 2 = 8.75x`.

**Adjustments against the median**
- Size: one customer is 38% of revenue, against no stated concentration in the comps. Down.
- Margin: gross margin 31% against a median of 28.5%. Slightly up.
- Growth: 6% against a median of 6%. Neutral.
- Forced by the artifacts: the unbudgeted $1.9M boiler (R4). Down.

**Range.** Two adjustments point down and one up, so the range runs from the included value just below the median to the median. Low `8.0x × $3.9M = $31.2M`; high `8.75x × $3.9M = $34.1M`. Cross-check on EV/Revenue: included median 1.3x, and `1.3x × $23.8M = $30.9M`, just under the low end. The $36.0M ask sits $1.9M above the high end.

## 4. Recommendation

**Request More Data.** Two must-address rows are Open (R1, R2), and both can be settled by documents rather than being decided against us. Proceed is not available until both close. At the ask, the price is also $1.9M above the defended range; any later Proceed carries a price condition.

## 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1**: Deal counsel to obtain the hospital group's signed consent to change of control within 3 weeks. Signed and unconditional moves R1 to Closed; a refusal moves the verdict to Pass.
- **R2**: Environmental adviser to obtain the regulator's receipt and expected decision date for the discharge permit renewal within 2 weeks. A renewal or a written extension closes R2.
- **R3**: Employment counsel to quantify the drivers' claim after mediation; size a specific indemnity against it before signing.
- **R4**: Corporate development to cut the price by the $1.9M replacement cost or require seller-funded replacement before close.
- **R5**: Operations lead to set a loss-rate target in the 100-day plan; no pre-signing action.

## The same sections when the gate allows Proceed

**4. Recommendation.** **Proceed**, subject to the conditions below. No must-address row is Open (R1, R2 Closed), and each open manageable row has a named mitigation. Our offer is the defended $31.2M to $34.1M, not the $36.0M ask.

**5. Conditions and Next Steps**
- **R1**: Signed consent attached to the purchase agreement as a condition precedent.
- **R2**: Renewed permit delivered at signing; no further action.
- **R3**: Specific indemnity for the drivers' claim, with a holdback sized once counsel quantifies the claim.
- **R4**: Price reduced by the $1.9M boiler cost, or replacement funded by the seller before close.
- **R5**: Loss-rate target in the 100-day plan.
