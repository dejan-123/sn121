**Financial narrative.** Merrowgate runs five passenger and vehicle ferries on four short-sea routes. LTM revenue is $33.5M, of which $2.5M came from a one-off charter carrying survey crews for an offshore cable project; the charter ended during the year and will not repeat. Recurring revenue of $31.0M grew 4%. Gross margin is 34%. Reported EBITDA is $6.6M, of which the charter contributed $1.0M. No customer exceeds 8% of revenue. ## The memo

# IC Memo: Merrowgate Ferries

### 1. Deal Thesis

Merrowgate runs five ferries on four short-sea routes, with $33.5M of LTM revenue, $31.0M of it recurring once a one-off charter is removed. The case is a defensible crossing network: growth is slow at 4%, but no customer exceeds 8% of revenue. The primary risk is the terminal berth lease (R1). The upstream table marks it Resolved, yet the narrative says the new lease is not yet signed, and the main terminal cannot be worked without it. The $59.4M ask is 10.6x recurring EBITDA, well above the $33.6M–$44.8M range derived below.

### 2. Risk Assessment

| Row | Finding | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Gate reading | Memo flag | Deal impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Terminal berth lease expires within the year; renewal with the harbour trust | Must-Address | Resolved | Open: contradicted by "the new lease is not yet signed" | Narrative contradicts the status; upstream status kept | Sailings from the main terminal depend on the lease |
| R2 | Flagship passenger certificate suspended after a bow-door fault | Must-Address | Resolved | Resolved | Consistent: re-certified, valid to 2029 | None beyond monitoring |
| R3 | Two vessels aged 31 and 34; dry-dock surveys not costed | Manageable | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | — | Capex not quantified in the artifacts |
| R4 | Crew agreement expires this year; pay talks under way | Manageable | In-Progress | Open: In-Progress | — | Wage reset not quantified |
| R5 | Weather cancellations average 5% of winter sailings | Informational | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | — | Seasonal revenue swing |

R1's tier and status are left as the upstream table gave them; the narrative's words stand against the status, so the gate counts the row as open. R2 describes a past suspension that the narrative shows was cured, so Resolved stands.

### 3. Valuation

Primary multiple: EV/EBITDA, because Merrowgate is a profitable transport-services business and every comparable reports a meaningful EBITDA multiple.
Base: reported EBITDA of $6.6M less the $1.0M the one-off charter contributed, so $5.6M. The seller's banker values the full $6.6M.

| Comparable | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Wrenfield Marine (6.5x) | Included | Short-sea ferry peer |
| Harbourline Ferries (7.5x) | Included | Short-sea ferry peer |
| Northcape Crossings (8.5x) | Included | Short-sea ferry peer |
| Tidewater Links (10.5x) | Included | Highest multiple, but its growth and margin sit within 10 points of the others |
| Brackwater Ferry Holdings (12.5x) | Excluded | Precedent deal priced with a control premium, not a trading multiple |

Included multiples, low to high: 6.5x, 7.5x, 8.5x, 10.5x; median (7.5x + 8.5x) ÷ 2 = 8.0x. Step size for EV/EBITDA, 1.0x:
- growth: 4% against a 4.5% median (2%, 3%, 6%, 7%), no step;
- gross margin: 34% against a 37% median (31%, 36%, 38%, 40%), 3 points below, one step down;
- concentration: no customer above 8%, no step;
- size: comps' scale not given, no step.

Running sum: 8.0x − 1.0x margin = 7.0x. Range, 7.0x ± 1.0x: 6.0x × $5.6M = $33.6M and 8.0x × $5.6M = $44.8M, so **$33.6M–$44.8M**. The artifacts quantify no debt-like item.

The $59.4M ask sits $14.6M above the high end and implies 10.6x the $5.6M base. It reaches 9.0x only on the seller's $6.6M, which counts the charter.

### 4. Recommendation

**Request more data.** The berth lease (R1) is a must-address row that reads Open: "the new lease is not yet signed" stands against its Resolved status, so the gate rules out proceeding. No artifact sentence shows the lease cannot be signed, so this is not a Pass. The comparables support $33.6M–$44.8M; the $59.4M ask is $14.6M above the high end, and any bid should stay within the range.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1:** Deal counsel to obtain the executed berth lease from the harbour trust before signing; if its terms differ from those approved in principle, revisit the range.
- **R2:** Technical adviser to place the re-issued certificate in the data room and confirm it carries no open conditions; no further action.
- **R3:** Marine surveyor to cost the next dry-dock surveys for both older vessels before signing; deduct any agreed figure from both ends of the range.
- **R4:** HR diligence to obtain the latest pay offer and the union's response; price the settled wage cost into the EBITDA base.
- **R5:** Operations lead to report winter cancellation rates each quarter against the three-year average.

## How section 5 changes with the verdict

The rows and their order never change; what each bullet asks for does.

- **Proceed:** every bullet is a closing condition or a protection. Name the instrument for the row (a warranty, an indemnity, a covenant, a consent), size any escrow or holdback to the exposure the artifacts quantify, and add the day-one step. Manageable rows each carry their mitigation.
- **Request more data:** every open row gets the one document or answer that would close it, who fetches it, by when, and how each possible answer would shift the verdict and the range. Rows already settled get a filing or monitoring step.
- **Pass:** the bullets chart what would need to change for the committee to take a fresh bearing: the counterparty reversing its refusal, the transfer bar being lifted, the seller remedying the breach before any deal. Price, if any, stays in the Recommendation.

## One-fact notes

- **Tier words.** When the upstream table says Critical, Moderate and Low, the memo table says the same, and one sentence states that Critical gates like must-address.
- **Soft status words.** "Substantially resolved", "deferred", "in principle" and an empty cell each read Open, with one clause saying why.
- **Banker's multiple on the wrong base.** Restate it on the memo's base before comparing it with the range: gross volume where the business is valued on net revenue, or total revenue that still holds a one-off item.
- **Revenue that cannot transfer.** It leaves the base, the row stays open, and the first bullet in section 5 asks for the document that answers whether any of it can move to the buyer.
- **A Pass needs its sentence.** Quote the artifact line that makes the block permanent. If no such line exists, however grave the finding, the verdict is Request more data.
- **Absent inputs.** The memo says "not provided", values only what the artifacts give, and puts the request into section 5.
- **Running long.** Cut adjectives and repeated context; rows, exclusion reasons and arithmetic lines always stay.

Last check before you write: the reply starts with `# IC Memo: ` and the target's name. Nothing sits above that line, least of all a preamble like "Now I will draft the IC memo", which is the commonest slip.
