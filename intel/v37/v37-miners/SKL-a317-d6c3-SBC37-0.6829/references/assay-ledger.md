## The finished memo

# IC Memo: Marrowgate Refining

## 1. Deal Thesis

Marrowgate is a fee-based precious-metal refiner serving jewellers, dental labs and small mines, and never takes title to customer metal: $26.4M of recurring fee revenue growing 6%, and $5.9M of reported EBITDA once a one-off mint contract is set aside. Its largest customer has waived its change-of-control termination right and extended for four years. The primary risk is R1, the responsible-sourcing audit finding: marked Resolved, but the auditor's close-out has not been seen and must be evidenced before signing. The $56.0M ask is 9.50x that EBITDA, $4.4M above the range the comps support.

## 2. Risk Assessment

| # | Risk | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Gate reading | Flag | Deal impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Responsible-sourcing audit found weak diligence on two doré suppliers; corrective action required | Must-Address | Resolved | Resolved | No other artifact addresses it; auditor's close-out not yet seen | Accreditation underpins the core service; proof needed before signing |
| R2 | Largest customer (23% of revenue) held a change-of-control termination right | Must-Address | Resolved | Resolved | Consistent: narrative records a signed waiver and four-year extension | About $6.5M of revenue (23% of $28.4M) retained; concentration remains |
| R3 | Customs dispute over classification of imported scrap | Manageable | In-Progress | Open: In-Progress | None | Up to $0.8M of duty if lost |
| R4 | Vault insurance capped below peak customer-metal value | Manageable | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | None | Uninsured loss exposure; shortfall not quantified |
| R5 | Chief assayer to retire within two years | Informational | Unresolved | Open: Unresolved | None | Technical continuity |

Nothing in the narrative or the comparables can be quoted against R1's Resolved status, so its gate reading stays Resolved although the finding describes a real lapse; the auditor's close-out becomes a pre-signing condition. No manageable row carries injunction, regulatory or licence weight, so none is flagged as a mis-tier.

## 3. Valuation

Multiple: EV/EBITDA, because Marrowgate is a profitable refining service and every comp reports a meaningful EBITDA multiple.
Base: reported EBITDA of $5.9M, which is $6.6M less the $0.7M earned on the one-off mint contract. The seller's base is the full $6.6M.

| Comparable | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Aurelian Metals Recovery (9.0x) | Included | Fee-based refining peer |
| Sterling Bar Refiners (10.5x) | Included | Fee-based refining peer |
| Northcote Precious Metals (8.0x) | Included | Fee-based refining peer |
| Ingot & Vane Refining (11.0x) | Included | Fee-based refining peer |
| Bullionmark Group (15.0x) | Excluded | Precedent acquisition at a strategic premium, not a trading multiple |

Included multiples in order: 8.0x, 9.0x, 10.5x, 11.0x; median (9.0x + 10.5x) ÷ 2 = 9.75x. Included medians: growth 7%, gross margin 42.5%. One step is 1.0x:
- growth, 6% against 7%: no step;
- gross margin, 38% against 42.5%, 4.5 points below: one step down;
- concentration, the largest customer at 23% of revenue (above 20%): one step down;
- size: no step, as the artifacts give no scale for the comps.

Running sum: 9.75x − 1.0x margin − 1.0x concentration = 7.75x. Range, 7.75x ± 1.0x: 6.75x × $5.9M = $39.8M and 8.75x × $5.9M = $51.6M, so **$39.8M–$51.6M**. No debt-like items are quantified; the R3 duty exposure is contingent and is covered by escrow in section 5.

The $56.0M ask sits $4.4M above the high end: 9.50x the $5.9M base, or 8.50x the seller's $6.6M.

## 4. Recommendation

**Proceed with conditions.** R1 and R2, the two must-address rows, are Resolved, and nothing in the narrative or comps disputes either: the narrative confirms R2's waiver and extension, and R1 needs only the auditor's close-out before signing. Each manageable row has a named mitigation (R3 escrow, R4 insurance top-up). The range supports at most $51.6M; the $56.0M ask is $4.4M above it, so the bid should sit within $39.8M–$51.6M.

## 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1:** Deal lead to obtain the auditor's close-out letter and confirmation that the accreditation stands before signing; seller to warrant supplier due diligence, backed by a specific indemnity.
- **R2:** Legal to place the signed waiver and four-year extension in the disclosure schedule before signing.
- **R3:** Deal counsel to set a $0.8M escrow, sized to the duty exposure, held until the customs dispute closes; seller indemnity for any assessment above it.
- **R4:** CFO to bind vault cover at or above peak customer-metal value, effective at closing, as a closing condition.
- **R5:** Management to name and train a successor chief assayer in the 100-day plan.

## How the verdict re-marks section 5

- **Proceed:** every bullet is a protection that must be in place at signing or closing. That means the warranty or indemnity answering the row, an escrow or holdback matched to an exposure the artifacts put a number on, any covenant, consent or closure letter, then the day-one steps. Manageable rows keep their mitigations.
- **Request more data:** every open row is paired with the precise document or answer that would settle it, the person who obtains it, when, and how each possible answer would move the verdict and the range. When the terms bar some revenue from transferring, that question leads the list.
- **Pass:** the bullets explain why the blocker cannot be cured inside this deal and what would have to change before the committee would reopen it.

## Single-fact entries

- **Stamped labels.** Where the upstream table grades rows Critical / Moderate / Low, those labels go into the memo untouched, and one sentence records that Critical gates like must-address.
- **Open under another name.** In-Progress, "substantially resolved", "deferred" and an empty cell all count as open for the gate; a clause in the memo gives the reason.
- **Wrong base, wrong price.** A banker's multiple struck on gross volume, or on revenue that still holds one-time items, is restated on the valued base and only then set against the range.
- **Gaps stay gaps.** A missing metric or artifact is written "not provided"; value on what is present and put the request into section 5.
- **Over the word limit.** Cut adjectives and repeated context first. Rows, exclusion reasons and lines of arithmetic stay.

The ledger closes here. The reply opens with `# IC Memo: ` and the target's name, and nothing comes before it; the slip made most often is an opening line like "Now I will draft the IC memo" ahead of the title. All the testing stays out of the reply.
