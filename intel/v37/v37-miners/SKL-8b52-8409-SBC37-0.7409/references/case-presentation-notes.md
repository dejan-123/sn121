# Case presentation notes

One invented case, taken from its three artifacts to a finished memo. After it: how the treatment plan in section 5 shifts with the verdict, a handful of one-fact notes, a short second case, and the last check before writing. Learn the structure, the lengths and the reasoning behind each call. Use none of the names or numbers; every memo is built from its own artifacts alone.

## The memo

# IC Memo: Larkspur Veterinary Partners

### 1. Deal Thesis

Larkspur runs nine general-practice veterinary hospitals and a specialist referral centre, with $52.0M of LTM revenue, $50.0M of it continuing once a cancelled shelter contract is removed. The case is a practice network growing 14%, with a referral centre that feeds and is fed by the general hospitals. Both must-address rows are Resolved and confirmed by the narrative. The primary risk is the insurer referral agreement (R3): 18% of revenue the insurer may end on six months' notice, a right rather than a refusal. The $100.0M ask sits inside the $89.1M–$105.3M range.

### 2. Risk Assessment

| ID | Finding | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Second source | Flag | Effect on deal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Premises licences must be re-issued to a new owner | Must-Address | Resolved | Narrative: transfer of all ten approved, effective at completion | Consistent | None once completion occurs |
| R2 | Lead orthopaedic surgeon (11% of revenue) resigned | Must-Address | Resolved | Narrative: resignation withdrawn; five-year contract and non-compete signed | Consistent | Key-person dependence remains |
| R3 | Insurer referral agreement (18% of revenue) can end on six months' notice | Manageable | Unresolved | None | Notice right, not a stated refusal | Up to 18% of revenue at risk if exercised |
| R4 | Nurses' overtime claim, estimated at $0.8M, in mediation | Manageable | In-Progress | None | — | Contingent cost, estimated at $0.8M |
| R5 | Imaging equipment at two hospitals past its support date | Informational | Unresolved | None | — | Replacement cost not quantified |

Both must-address findings read like live problems, but the narrative shows each was cured, so Resolved stands. No row looks mis-tiered.

### 3. Valuation

Primary multiple: EV/EBITDA. Larkspur is a profitable veterinary services business and every comparable reports an EBITDA multiple.
Base: reported EBITDA of $8.6M less the $0.5M from the cancelled shelter contract, revenue already lost, gives $8.1M.

| Comparable | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Hollin Pet Hospitals (10.5x) | Included | Multi-site veterinary operator |
| Fernbrook Animal Health (11.5x) | Included | Multi-site veterinary operator |
| Kestrel Vet Partners (12.5x) | Included | Multi-site veterinary operator |
| Ashgrove Veterinary Group (13.5x) | Included | Multi-site veterinary operator |
| PawLine Telehealth (24.0x) | Excluded | Growth (34%) and gross margin (71%) each clear every other comp by more than 10 points; excluded on those, not on its multiple |

Included multiples, ranked: 10.5x, 11.5x, 12.5x, 13.5x; median (11.5x + 12.5x) ÷ 2 = 12.0x. A step on EV/EBITDA is 1.0x:
- growth: 14% against an 8.5% median (6%, 8%, 9%, 10%), 5.5 points above, one step up;
- gross margin: 42% against a 45% median (41%, 44%, 46%, 47%), 3 points below, one step down;
- concentration: the largest source, the insurer channel, is 18%, under 20%, no step;
- size: no scale is given for the comps, no step.

Running sum: 12.0x + 1.0x growth − 1.0x margin = 12.0x. Range, 12.0x ± 1.0x: 11.0x × $8.1M = $89.1M and 13.0x × $8.1M = $105.3M, so **$89.1M–$105.3M**. No debt-like item is quantified; the R4 estimate is covered by escrow in section 5.

The $100.0M ask sits inside the range, $5.3M below the high end, at 12.35x the $8.1M base (11.65x on the reported $8.6M).

### 4. Recommendation

**Proceed with conditions.** Both must-address rows are Resolved and the narrative confirms each, and both manageable rows carry named mitigations, so the gate allows Proceed. R3 is a notice right, not a stated refusal, so it becomes a pre-signing condition rather than a block. The $100.0M ask is inside the $89.1M–$105.3M range, $5.3M below the high end; the committee can sign at or below the ask.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1:** Legal counsel to file the council's licence-transfer approvals for all ten hospitals in the disclosure schedule; their taking effect at completion is a closing condition.
- **R2:** HR lead to place the surgeon's signed five-year contract and non-compete in the data room before signing; the purchase agreement carries a key-person warranty.
- **R3:** Deal lead to obtain the insurer's written confirmation, before signing, that the referral agreement continues after the deal; if it is withheld, revalue without that 18% of revenue.
- **R4:** Seller to give a specific indemnity for the overtime claim, backed by a $0.8M escrow released when the mediation concludes.
- **R5:** Operations director to add imaging replacement at both hospitals to the 100-day capital plan and report on it quarterly.

## How the treatment plan changes with the verdict

Each bullet still answers one row, in row order. What changes is the kind of order being written.

- **Proceed:** prescriptions for closing. Name the protection for each row (warranty, specific indemnity, covenant, consent letter), set any escrow or holdback at the amount of exposure the artifacts put a number on, and add the day-one step. Every manageable row receives its mitigation here.
- **Request more data:** tests to order. For each open row, the document that would settle it (an executed contract, a closure letter, a filed response, an audit opinion), who obtains it, by when, and which way each result would move the verdict and the range.
- **Pass:** the reasons the block cannot be treated inside this deal, and the change that would bring the committee back to it; any price at which it would return belongs in the Recommendation.

## One-fact notes

- **Critical, Moderate, Low.** These upstream words stay in the memo table, and one sentence says that Critical carries must-address weight at the gate.
- **Status words that sound settled.** "Substantially resolved", "deferred" and an empty status cell are all open; say so in a clause.
- **A seller's multiple on the wrong base.** Re-state it on the memo's base first, for example on net revenue where the seller quoted gross volume, or on revenue without a one-off item.
- **Notice rights.** A counterparty that may end an agreement on notice has not refused anything; the row gets a condition, such as written confirmation that the agreement continues after the deal.
- **Non-transferable revenue.** Remove it from the base. When it is a material share the thesis needs and the artifacts show no way around the bar, the verdict is Pass.
- **Missing inputs.** Write "not provided", value on what the artifacts do give, and put the request in section 5.
- **Space.** When a section overruns, adjectives and restated context go first; rows, exclusion reasons and arithmetic lines stay.

## A shorter second case: rows that sound live but are Resolved

An invented regional bakery group brings two must-address rows, both marked Resolved:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| The main plant's last food-safety audit recorded a major non-conformity | Must-Address | Resolved |
| The brand's recipe trademarks are registered to the founder personally | Must-Address | Resolved |

The narrative covers volumes and margins and says nothing about either row, and each manageable row has a mitigation. Each finding describes the complaint; no other artifact says the non-conformity is still open or that the trademarks remain unassigned. With nothing contradicting Resolved, both rows stay Resolved and the gate allows Proceed. The missing proof goes into section 5 as pre-signing conditions (R1, the clean re-audit certificate; R2, the executed assignment of the trademarks to the company), and the verdict is **Proceed with conditions**.

Before the first word goes out: the reply begins with `# IC Memo: ` and the target's name. An opening line that announces the work ("Now I will draft the IC memo") is the commonest slip; start with the title.
