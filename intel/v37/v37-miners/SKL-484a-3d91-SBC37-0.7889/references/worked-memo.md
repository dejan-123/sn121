## What this pack required

The first row's Resolved status is contradicted by the narrative (a tender is running, nothing is signed), so it is printed as received and read as open. The blank status is read as unresolved. The Informational row sets the base the price is built on, so it is flagged and returned to its analyst with its tier untouched. Two comps do not belong in the set. All of that is shown inside the memo; none of it is written as notes around it.

## The memo

## IC memo: Brindlemoor Veterinary Labs

### 1. Deal thesis

Brindlemoor is a veterinary reference-laboratory network with $22.4M of revenue growing 12% at a 44% gross margin, once a one-time $3.2M surveillance contract is set aside. The case rests on that base and on the clinic-group volume inside it. The seller asks $64M, a price built on the headline figures. The primary risk is R1: the largest clinic group, 33% of test volume, is out to tender and has not renewed.

### 2. Risk table

| ID | Finding | Upstream tier | Upstream status | Author's read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Largest clinic group, 33% of volume; agreement ends in 7 months | Must-Address | Resolved | Blocks. Disputed: narrative says the group "has issued a tender to two national laboratories and has signed nothing" |
| R2 | State licence for main site; inspector's report not issued | Must-Address | In-Progress | Blocks. No report, no clearance |
| R3 | Sole courier on a 60-day rolling contract | Manageable | Unresolved | Open - needs mitigation |
| R4 | Information system loses vendor support in 2 years; migration unbudgeted | Manageable | (blank) | Open - blank status read as unresolved; cost not supplied |
| R5 | One-time $3.2M contract inside LTM revenue | Informational | Unresolved | Noted. Apparent mis-tier: it sets the valuation base |

Blocking rows: R1, R2.

### 3. Valuation range

- Base: LTM revenue less the one-time contract the narrative sizes, 25.6 - 3.2 = $22.4M. Nothing else is removed.
- Multiple: EV/Revenue leads; EV/EBITDA is the cross-check.
- Included (4), lowest first: Tollard 1.9x, Wexcombe 2.1x, Calder 2.4x, Pinemarsh 2.8x. Median, midpoint of 2nd and 3rd: (2.1 + 2.4) / 2 = 2.25x.
- Excluded: Aurelian PetGenomics, 5.6x on 52% growth with no meaningful EBITDA multiple, detached from the pack. Fennick Vet Labs, a take-private price two years old carrying a control premium.
- Adjustments against the included medians: growth lowest first 8%, 10%, 14%, 18%, median (10 + 14) / 2 = 12%, target 12%, in line; gross margin 41%, 43%, 46%, 49%, median (43 + 46) / 2 = 44.5%, target 44%, in line; size, no scale figures in the set, so no adjustment is taken.
- Range: in line, so the values either side of the median. 2.1 x 22.4 = $47.0M; 2.4 x 22.4 = $53.8M.
- Cross-check: EBITDA without the one-time contract, 5.0 - (3.2 x 50%) = $3.4M; EBITDA multiples lowest first 12x, 13x, 15x, 17x, median (13 + 15) / 2 = 14x; 14 x 3.4 = $47.6M, inside the range.
- Ask: 64 / 22.4 = 2.9x, above the range and above every included comp.

### 4. Recommendation: Request more data

Proceed is unavailable while R1 and R2 are open: both are blocking-tier rows, R1's Resolved status is contradicted by the narrative and R2 is still in progress. No price adjustment substitutes for either. A signed renewal and a clean inspection report would open the way to Proceed inside the range above; a lost tender removes a third of volume and would need a new base before any vote.

### 5. Conditions and next steps

- **R1:** Seller to supply the signed renewal or the clinic group's written tender outcome before the vote. A signed renewal clears the row; anything less keeps it open.
- **R2:** Deal counsel to obtain the inspector's report from the licensing authority through the seller. A report with no conditions clears the row.
- **R3:** Operations diligence lead to obtain the courier contract and one alternative carrier's written terms, to show the mitigation is real before signing.
- **R4:** Finance diligence lead to request the vendor's migration quote; the figure is not supplied and is needed to size a price adjustment.
- **R5:** Risk analyst to confirm or revise the tier before the vote, since the row changes the base from $25.6M to $22.4M.

## IC memo: Corvane Learning

### 1. Deal thesis

Corvane is a subscription language-learning app with $15.5M of LTM revenue, all of it subscription, growing 36% at a 78% gross margin and not yet profitable (EBITDA -$0.8M). The case rests on that growth and margin holding, which in turn depends on the lesson catalogue staying complete. No asking price is supplied. The primary risk is R3: a publisher licence covering 20% of lessons renews one year at a time.

Scale read as: red the blocking tier, amber manageable, green informational. Blocking rows: none.

### 3. Valuation range

- Base: LTM revenue $15.5M; nothing in the narrative is one-off, non-transferable or cancelled.
- Multiple: EV/Revenue leads; reported EBITDA is negative, so there is no EBITDA cross-check.
- Included (5), lowest first: Halberd 3.9x, Lexwick 4.4x, Pontoon 5.0x, Sorrel 5.7x, Vantry 6.3x. Median, the 3rd of five: 5.0x.
- Excluded: none. Vantry is highest on every measure but only one step above Sorrel and its 39% growth is close to the target's 36%; no comp is a deal price.
- Adjustments against the included medians: growth lowest first 22%, 27%, 30%, 33%, 39%, median the 3rd, 30%, target 36%, above; gross margin 70%, 73%, 75%, 77%, 80%, median 75%, target 78%, above; size, no scale figures in the set, so no adjustment is taken.
- Range: above on both, so median to highest. 5.0 x 15.5 = $77.5M; 6.3 x 15.5 = $97.7M.
- Ask: asking price not supplied; no implied multiple.

### 4. Recommendation: Proceed

No row makes Proceed unavailable: both red rows are Resolved and the narrative disputes neither, however live R2's clause sounds. The recommendation is to proceed subject to the three conditions in section 5. R3 and R4 are open amber rows and each leaves with a mitigation below; R2 adds a document condition because its resolution is asserted, not shown. A bid inside the range above is supportable. The verdict would move if the R2 document does not exist.

### 5. Conditions and next steps

- **R2:** Deal counsel to receive the document that resolved the reseller's notice right (waiver or amended term) as a condition of signing; without it the row reopens as a blocker.
- **R3:** Publisher's multi-year renewal as a closing deliverable, or a holdback against the 20% of lessons it covers; owner, deal counsel.
- **R4:** Seller to complete the migration before closing under a funded plan with a named completion test; owner, operations diligence lead.

## Two cases the packs above do not show

**No document can cure the blocker (Pass).** A franchise operator whose franchisor has refused in writing to assign the licence to any buyer. The recommendation says which row cannot be cured and why price does not help. The bullets are reasons with a reopening trigger: "**R1:** Franchisor refuses assignment; the licence is the business. Reopen only on the franchisor's written consent." No data requests are written under Pass: nothing requested would change the answer.

**The cross-check disagrees.** If the EV/EBITDA cross-check lands well below the low end of the range, say so in one line and call the low end the defensible one. Do not explain the gap with figures the pack does not give; a missing breakdown (for example the comps' revenue mix) is a request against the row it touches.
