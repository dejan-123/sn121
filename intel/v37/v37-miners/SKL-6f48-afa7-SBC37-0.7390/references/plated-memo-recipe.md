# Plated memo: one dish from prep to plate

Here is a single invented deal cooked all the way from its three raw artifacts to the plated memo. After it come a short note on how section 5 changes with the call, a few kitchen notes, and the sign-off. Learn the plating, the portion sizes and how each call is reasoned; the ingredients of any real memo come only from that scenario's own artifacts.

## The plated memo

# IC Memo: Tamsin Hill Roasters

### 1. Deal Thesis

Tamsin Hill roasts specialty coffee for wholesale accounts, which make up 85% of its $42.0M LTM revenue (coffee bars, grocers and offices), and sells the remainder online. Revenue is growing 6% at a 34% gross margin, with reported EBITDA of $4.6M. The appeal is a loyal wholesale book and a gross margin slightly ahead of peers. The primary risk is the organic certification audit (R1): two major non-conformities remain open at the roasting plant, and certified blends carry 38% of revenue. At $52.0M, the ask is 11.3x reported EBITDA, above what the comps support.

### 2. Risk Assessment

| # | Risk | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | What the other artifacts say | Deal impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Organic audit: two major non-conformities at the plant; certified blends 38% of revenue | Must-Address | In-Progress | Narrative: corrective actions filed in August, no closure issued | About $16.0M of revenue (38% of $42.0M) depends on the certificate |
| R2 | Larkspur (23% of revenue) may terminate on 60 days' notice after a change of control | Must-Address | Resolved | Consistent: renewed for three years in April; nothing disputes the status | $9.7M of revenue (23% of $42.0M) exposed if the right is used |
| R3 | Green coffee unhedged beyond two months | Manageable | Unresolved | — | Margin exposure to coffee prices |
| R4 | Plant lease ends in 20 months; renewal under negotiation | Manageable | In-Progress | — | Relocation cost not provided |

R2 reads like a live risk, yet no artifact disputes its Resolved status, so it stays Resolved and its termination right becomes a pre-signing condition rather than a reason to stop. R1 is the row that sets the verdict.

### 3. Valuation

Primary multiple: EV/EBITDA, because Tamsin Hill is a profitable roasting and distribution business rather than software.
Base: reported EBITDA of $4.6M. The seller's $5.3M adds back $0.7M for a "one-off" price spike with no support, so it is shown here but not used.

| Comparable | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Kettlebrook Coffee (9.5x) | Included | Wholesale roaster peer |
| Roastworks Partners (8.0x) | Included | Wholesale roaster peer |
| Ferncastle Roasting (11.5x) | Included | Highest multiple, but its growth (12%) and margin (36%) sit inside the peer band |
| Morrow Bean Co. (8.5x) | Included | Wholesale roaster peer |
| Brightcup Cold Brew (21.0x) | Excluded | Growth of 34% is 22 points beyond the next comp (12%): an outlier profile |

Kept multiples in order: 8.0x, 8.5x, 9.5x, 11.5x; median (8.5x + 9.5x) ÷ 2 = 9.0x. Kept medians: growth 10%, gross margin 32.5%. Step size on an EBITDA multiple: 1.0x.
- growth: 6% versus 10%, 4 points short, one step down;
- gross margin: 34% versus 32.5%, 1.5 points ahead, no step;
- concentration: Larkspur at 23% and the top three at 47% sit under the 25% and 60% marks, no step;
- size: no comp scale given, no step.

Running sum: 9.0x median − 1.0x for growth = 8.0x. Range, 8.0x ± 1.0x: 7.0x × $4.6M = $32.2M and 9.0x × $4.6M = $41.4M, so **$32.2M–$41.4M**. No debt-like items are quantified.

The $52.0M ask sits $10.6M above the high end: 11.3x reported EBITDA, and still 9.8x on the seller's unsupported $5.3M.

### 4. Recommendation

**Request more data.** The gate stops at R1, a must-address row still In-Progress: the certifier has issued no closure, so proceeding is not open. This is a document gap that a closure letter can fill, not a permanent block. R2 stays Resolved, and its termination right is handled by a pre-signing waiver. The comps support at most $41.4M, $10.6M below the $52.0M ask.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1:** Deal lead to obtain the certifier's closure letter for both non-conformities before signing; if closure is refused, the certified 38% of revenue is revalued and the verdict revisited.
- **R2:** Legal to obtain Larkspur's signed waiver of its change-of-control termination right before signing.
- **R3:** CFO to present a green-coffee forward-buying plan covering the first year after close, before signing.
- **R4:** Operations to obtain the landlord's renewal terms, or a relocation cost estimate, before signing.

## Section 5 for each kind of call

- **Proceed.** The bullets list what must be true at closing. Every risk row is matched to a protection: an indemnity or warranty, a holdback or escrow as large as the quantified exposure, a covenant, or a signed consent or closure letter. First-day integration steps come next, and every manageable row still shows its fix.
- **Request more data.** Each open row is paired with the document that would settle it, the person fetching it and the date, plus what each possible answer would do to the call and the range.
- **Pass.** Each bullet names what must change before the committee will look again. Closing mechanics have no place on this plate.

## Kitchen notes, one fact apiece

- **A right on the menu is not a refusal.** A termination right, a notice right or a review still pending is something to put a condition on, as with Larkspur. Only a stated "we will not continue" or "we will not consent" from a counterparty the business needs can support a Pass.
- **Eighty-six only for the listed reasons.** Ferncastle carries the richest multiple and stays in; Brightcup goes because its growth sits more than 10 points clear of every other comp.
- **Revenue that cannot cross over.** When a contract or grant's own terms stop part of the revenue from moving to the buyer, leave it out of the base, hold the call at Request more data, and lead section 5 with the transfer question.
- **Open in all but name.** Deferred, substantially resolved, In-Progress or a blank cell: the row is still open at the gate, and one clause says why.
- **Nothing to cook with.** A missing metric is written as not provided; the memo values on what it has and asks for the rest in section 5.

The recipe ends here; plate the memo now. The very first characters of the reply are `# IC Memo: ` and the target's name. The most frequent slip is a sentence served in front of them, for instance "Now I will draft the IC memo"; keep the kitchen chatter in the back.
