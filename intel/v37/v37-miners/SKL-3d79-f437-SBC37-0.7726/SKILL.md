# Ledger — post the artifacts, then write what the books say

The Status column, the headline figures, and the comp multiples are
opening postings — claims to be weighed, not balances to inherit.
This skill posts every claim against the record behind it and
writes the memo from the standing that results.

## Posting rules

**Post 1 — The ledger context.** Open with the decision: acquire
<target> on what terms, from these three artifacts. Post the asking
price — or post "no asking price stated" as its own line item,
because an absent ask is a fact about the process, never a number to
invent.

**Post 2 — Status postings.** Every risk row posts twice: its filed
Status and what the record actually shows. Where they disagree —
Resolved over a consent never put in writing, a re-bid still live,
a deferral that is not a closure, a concentration with no contracts
signed — the stale
posting is struck, the row stands open, and the override names the
deciding datum. An analyst note flagging a table-versus-narrative
contradiction posts as confirmation of the override, not as a
nuisance. A Resolved the record supports posts carried — the ledger
verifies, it does not demote. The recurring stale postings: risk
still live behind a Resolved label; paper thin (no consent letter,
auditor opinion, or closure document — a self-certification
standing in for an audit); process confused with outcome (under
appeal, in settlement talks, review deferred).

**Post 3 — The base posting.** The seller's revenue and EBITDA are
opening entries to be restated, not balances to carry. Post the
adjusted base: strip revenue that completed and will not recur;
exclude revenue that does not transfer at close — grants a
for-profit cannot hold, contracts that terminate, customers already
gone; use the net figure where the industry's metric is net and say
why the gross figure is not the base; restate growth on the adjusted
base, not the headline. Where EBITDA is negative on the economics
that transfer, or the comps are pre-profitability, post EV/EBITDA
as not meaningful and carry EV/Revenue. Add-backs settle only when
genuinely non-recurring; evergreen items relabeled as one-time are
rejected — never subtracted twice; an accepted one lifts the base.
A growth projection contingent on unsigned
deals — prospects in study, pilots not yet converted — posts as a
claim: the priced rate is realized growth, the projection is upside
context only.

**Post 4 — Comp postings.** Each comparable posts as included or
excluded with its reason written down. Excluded postings:
growth/margin profile outliers; acquisitions closed at a control or
strategic premium — a banker or broker note flagging it is the
tell; comps whose revenue mix does not map to the target's absent a
stated mix adjustment. Where the comp
set's own note declares a limitation — mix not provided, only one
multiple viable — the limitation posts to the ledger instead of being
priced silently. Choose the surviving multiple, then post the
arithmetic: stated base times stated multiple, low comp to high comp,
produces the range. Post the implied ask multiple in the same line —
the seller's basis is itself an entry to reconcile.

**Post 5 — Settlement.** The recommendation has three standings —
proceed, request more data, pass — and it is the standing of the
books, never a mood. A proceed posted over an open must-address
entry contradicts the ledger itself and fails the memo. The
presumption where open postings stand is request more data — the
ask names each open posting and the named item that would close it.
The count of open postings is not the
test — whether a named record settles each one is. Pass posts only where the books cannot
be made to balance: the core asset does not transfer and no entry
can fix it, a gating posting's settlement belongs to a party already
on record refusing it, or the ask stands more than a third again
above the top of the range — a gap no ask posts against. Where no
must-address posting is open, the books settle clean and the memo
says proceed without manufacturing exceptions to look thorough —
though material open items below must-address still post as named
conditions.
The thesis names exactly one primary risk, and that risk
must appear in the postings table — a risk named in the thesis but
absent from the table is an unposted liability.

**Post 6 — Open postings.** The section exists under every standing:
under proceed it carries the mitigations for carried items; under
request more data it carries the asks; under pass it posts what
would reopen the file. Every condition settles a named posting:
the finding it clears, the specific record required, the party who
owes it, and the deadline or event it precedes. "Further review" is
not a posting. Quantified exposures post in dollars against the
price — a termination scenario, an unbudgeted refurbishment, a
departed-revenue run-rate — not as adjectives.

## Standing vocabulary — row postings (closed set)

- `breach` — an open must-address/critical posting; unsettles the
  whole ledger.
- `unsettled` — a named step must post before close; the step posts
  in the cell — and under proceed the cell carries it, so no row
  reads ignored.
- `carried` — posting verified against the record; stands as filed.
- `struck` — informational or outside the decision; no settlement
  spent on it. No fifth standing exists.

## The postings reconcile

Every input finding posts — none dropped, none merged. Rows sharing
one root cause keep their own lines and cross-reference ("compounds
row N"). A row filed under its true weight keeps its row and names its real
tier in the standing cell.

## The honest ledger

When the books settle clean the reconciliation says so, plainly.
Zero open postings is a complete statement — the conditions section
still posts "nothing gates this," it does not disappear.

## Under-posted input

Where an artifact the memo needs is absent — no asking price, a
missing status column, two narratives that conflict — the gap posts
as its own line item and the memo proceeds on what posted, or asks
the targeted question where nothing can post honestly. A figure the
input does not give gets a bracketed `[to confirm: …]`, never an
invented value.

## Settlements a senior reviewer re-foots

- **Headline by materiality** — the primary risk is the posting that
  can kill or reprice the deal, not the largest number in the file.
- **Post the deciding entry** — the load-bearing figure is often
  unprinted: the implied ask multiple, the run-rate after departed
  revenue, the downside scenario in dollars. Compute it and show it.
- **Direction, then label** — every restated base posts with which
  way it moves the economics.
- **Seller framing is flagged** — a multiple quoted on a flattering
  base gets its basis named and restated in the same line.
- **Conflicts post both ways** — two authorities disagreeing post as
  an open item whose settlement is a condition, not a coin flip the
  memo takes.
- **"Settleable" is a record test, not a difficulty test.** An open
  posting settles wherever a named record would close it: a
  consent, a clearance, an executed renewal, remediation evidence,
  a resolving opinion. Several such postings are several asks, not
  a pass — the count of open items does not unbalance the books. A
  compliance or contract lapse whose remediation is a named record
  posts as an ask plus a protection — indemnity, escrow, holdback,
  or repricing — NOT as a pass, even in combination with other open
  items: regulatory and contract lapses are exactly the class a data
  request exists to settle. Pass is reserved for a posting no record
  can settle — the asset itself does not transfer, the counterparty
  is already on record refusing, the deal's own premise fails — or
  an ask the justified range cannot reach.

## Writing it down

Memo shape — the five required sections, in this order:

1. `**Ledger context**` — the decision, the ask (or its absence),
   then two to four sentences of position ending in the named
   primary risk, verbatim as it posts in the table.
2. `**Postings**` — the table: `# | Finding | Posted as | The record
   shows | Standing`. Every row. Overrides show the deciding datum in
   the record cell. Then `Tally: <n breach / n unsettled / n carried
   / n struck>` re-footing to the row count exactly.
3. `**Valuation ledger**` — the base posting, each comp's posting in
   one clause, the surviving multiple, `base × multiple = range`,
   then the ask posted against it.
4. `**Settlement**` — proceed, request more data, or pass in one
   sentence plus the posting that decides it; never contradicts the
   table.
5. `**Open postings**` — numbered: finding, record, party, timing;
   character follows the standing. Last line: the reconciliation in
   two sentences the committee can repeat.

Budget: the harness cuts an overlong memo at the tail — the
settlement and open postings are the tail. Hold every stated length
bound; where none is stated: context four sentences, rows a line
each, the ledger six lines, the settlement two sentences, each
posting a line. Cut prose before rows. Every entry is
non-interchangeable: a line that could post under another finding
gets the deciding words instead.
