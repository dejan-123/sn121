Included: RouteWell 4.4x (growth 14%, margin 75%), CivicPath 5.1x (21%, 79%), MetroSoft 3.8x (11%, 72%). Median multiple 4.4x, median growth 14%, median margin 75%.

Growth 16% and margin 77% both sit slightly above those medians, so the multiple moves up. No size figure is in the comp table, so no size adjustment. Range is 4.6x to 5.0x on $8.5M: 4.6 × 8.5 = $39.1M and 5.0 × 8.5 = $42.5M. The ask of $46M is 46 / 8.5 = 5.4x the same base, above the range.
