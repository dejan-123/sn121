# Worked IC memos

Every company, figure and finding below is invented. Borrow the way each call is reasoned; every name, figure and finding in a real memo comes from its own input. Each case shows the artifacts in brief, then the memo exactly as the reply should look: it starts on the `## ` line and is nothing but the memo. The reasoning behind it is done privately and shows up only as the memo's own sentences.

## Case one: a curable blocker, a priced-up ask (Hold)

**Artifacts in brief.** Target: Brackenford Cold Storage Ltd, refrigerated warehousing. Risk table, tiers High / Medium / Low:
1. Ammonia refrigeration operating permit at the main site expired; renewal application filed, inspection date not set. High, Resolved.
2. Largest customer, a grocery chain at 36% of revenue, needs to consent to a change of control; consent requested, not received. High, In-Progress.
3. Two forklift-injury claims, insured, in settlement talks. Medium, In-Progress.
4. Roof replacement at the second site, estimated $1.9M, not budgeted. Medium, Unresolved.
5. Fixed-price energy contract ends in 9 months. Low, Unresolved.

Narrative: LTM revenue $36.0M, growth 12%, gross margin 34%. Reported EBITDA $5.3M; seller's adjusted EBITDA $7.4M adds back $0.8M of relocation costs (a completed, documented move) and $1.3M of "growth hires". Cash $1.6M, debt $9.0M. Ask $82M.

Comps (EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, growth, gross margin): Polar Hold Logistics 12x, 1.9x, 10%, 33%. FrostLine Warehousing 14x, 2.2x, 15%, 36%. Coldway Partners 11x, 1.6x, 8%, 31%. ArcticBay Storage, acquired 10 months ago by an infrastructure fund, 18x, 2.9x, 14%, 35%. ChillNet Automation 26x, 4.1x, 41%, 52%.

**The reply, in full, with nothing before or after it:**

## Brackenford Cold Storage: IC memo

**Recommendation: Hold. Do not sign until conditions 1 and 2 are met.** Price: the ask of $82M is not supported; up to $77.4M is, once both conditions clear.
Two High findings are open. The table marks the main-site refrigeration permit Resolved, but a filed application with no inspection date is not a permit; and the grocery chain's consent, covering 36% of revenue, is still outstanding. Both can be cured by documents before signing, so this is a hold, not a decline. On normalised EBITDA of $6.1M the ask is 13.4x, near the top of the trading range.

**Thesis and primary risk**
Thesis: a regional cold-storage platform growing 12% with margins in line with peers, bought at a trading multiple.
Primary risk: row 1. Without the main-site operating permit the core facility cannot run.

**Risk table** (High = must-address, Medium = manageable, Low = informational)

| # | Finding | Tier (given) | Status given → as read | Effect on the decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main-site refrigeration permit expired; renewal filed, no inspection date | High | Resolved → open: an application is not an issued permit | Blocks signing; condition 1 |
| 2 | Grocery chain consent to change of control (36% of revenue) | High | In-Progress → open | Blocks signing; condition 2; downside case below |
| 3 | Two insured forklift-injury claims in settlement talks | Medium | In-Progress → open | Specific indemnity; condition 3 |
| 4 | Roof replacement $1.9M, unbudgeted | Medium | Unresolved → open | Deducted from price |
| 5 | Fixed-price energy contract ends in 9 months | Low | Unresolved | Cost exposure; data request 4 |

**Financial base**
- Reported EBITDA $5.3M. Seller's adjusted $7.4M = 5.3 + 0.8 + 1.3.
- Relocation $0.8M accepted: the move is complete and documented. "Growth hires" $1.3M rejected: these are continuing salaries.
- Normalised EBITDA: 5.3 + 0.8 = **$6.1M** (seller's figure overstates it by $1.3M).
- Net debt: 9.0 − 1.6 = $7.4M, deducted to reach equity value.

**Valuation**
- Included: Polar Hold, FrostLine, Coldway, three trading peers with growth of 8% to 15% and margins of 31% to 36% around the target's 12% and 34%.
- Excluded: ArcticBay, a transaction at an infrastructure fund's price, not a trading multiple. ChillNet, at 41% growth and 52% margin, is an automation business, not a comparable storage operator.
- Position: the target sits between Polar Hold (10%, 33%) and FrostLine (15%, 36%), so 12x to 13x within the 11x to 14x range.
- EV/EBITDA: 11x × 6.1 = $67.1M to 14x × 6.1 = $85.4M. Positioned: 12x × 6.1 = $73.2M to 13x × 6.1 = $79.3M.
- Cross-check, EV/Revenue: 1.6x × 36.0 = $57.6M to 2.2x × 36.0 = $79.2M. The two ranges agree at the middle.
- Roof: 73.2 − 1.9 = $71.3M to 79.3 − 1.9 = **$77.4M** supported.
- Ask: 82.0 ÷ 6.1 = 13.4x normalised EBITDA (11.1x on the seller's $7.4M), and 82.0 ÷ 36.0 = 2.3x revenue, above the 2.2x top of the revenue range.
- Downside, row 2: without the grocery chain, revenue falls by 0.36 × 36.0 = $13.0M to $23.0M. The margin on that account is not supplied, so the EBITDA effect cannot be computed; it is data request 1.

**Conditions and data requests**
1. Row 1: deal counsel to obtain the issued operating permit for the main site before signing. If it is not issued, walk.
2. Row 2: the commercial lead to obtain the grocery chain's signed consent to the change of control before signing. If it is refused, walk, or reprice on the downside base once data request 1 is answered.
3. Row 3: deal counsel to obtain the insurer's coverage confirmation for both claims, with a specific indemnity for any uninsured amount, before close.
4. Row 5: the financial diligence lead to obtain the energy contract and the current market quote, and restate EBITDA at the new price, before signing.
5. Data request 1: margin on the grocery chain account, from the seller, before signing.

**Gaps the IC should know**
- The permit regulator's timetable for the inspection is not in the input.
- Customer margins are not supplied, so the cost of losing the largest account is a revenue figure only.

## Case two: a clean deal with one mislabelled row (Proceed)

**Artifacts in brief.** Target: Lintwood Scheduling Inc., scheduling software for dental practices. Risk table, tiers Must-Address / Manageable / Informational:
1. SOC 2 Type II report lapsed 3 months ago; new audit fieldwork complete, report due next month. Manageable, In-Progress.
2. Pending contractor-misclassification claim by two former support staff. Manageable, Resolved.
3. Top customer, a dental group, 11% of ARR, contract to 2028. Informational, Resolved.
4. A 6% price rise last year; churn rose from 7% to 9%. Informational, Unresolved.

Narrative: LTM revenue $14.0M, 92% recurring, growth 21%, gross margin 78%, NRR 104%. EBITDA $2.5M, no add-backs. Cash $3.0M, no debt. Ask $60M.

Comps (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, growth, gross margin): ChairSide Cloud 4.6x, 26x, 24%, 79%. PracticeLoop 4.0x, 22x, 18%, 76%. SmileDesk Software 3.6x, 19x, 15%, 74%. Molarity Systems 4.3x, 24x, 20%, 77%. ClinicFlow AI 9.5x, NM, 63%, 81%.

**The reply, in full, with nothing before or after it:**

## Lintwood Scheduling: IC memo

**Recommendation: Proceed, at the ask of $60M.** No must-address finding exists. The ask is 4.3x revenue and 24.0x EBITDA, inside both supported ranges for a business growing 21% with 104% NRR. One row is mislabelled: the misclassification claim is marked Resolved but described as pending, so it becomes a closing indemnity, not a reason to wait.

**Thesis and primary risk**
Thesis: a profitable, mostly recurring practice-software business growing above its peer median, at a peer multiple.
Primary risk: row 4. Churn rose from 7% to 9% after the last price rise, which tests whether growth holds without further increases.

**Risk table**

| # | Finding | Tier (given) | Status given → as read | Effect on the decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SOC 2 Type II report lapsed; fieldwork done, report due next month | Manageable | In-Progress → open, near closing | Condition 1, before close |
| 2 | Contractor-misclassification claim by two former staff | Manageable | Resolved → open: the row calls it pending; exposure not quantified | Specific indemnity; condition 2 |
| 3 | Top customer 11% of ARR, contract to 2028 | Informational | Resolved; consistent with the facts | None |
| 4 | Churn up from 7% to 9% after a 6% price rise | Informational | Unresolved | Primary risk; data request 3 |

**Financial base**
Reported EBITDA $2.5M, no add-backs claimed, so no normalisation. Revenue $14.0M, of which 0.92 × 14.0 = $12.9M recurring. No debt; cash $3.0M adds to equity value.

**Valuation**
- Included: ChairSide, PracticeLoop, SmileDesk, Molarity, with growth of 15% to 24% and margins of 74% to 79%.
- Excluded: ClinicFlow, at 63% growth and NM EBITDA, priced on a growth rate three times the target's.
- Position: at 21% growth and 78% margin the target sits between Molarity (20%, 77%) and ChairSide (24%, 79%), above the peer median of 4.15x revenue.
- EV/Revenue, 4.0x to 4.6x: 4.0 × 14.0 = $56.0M to 4.6 × 14.0 = $64.4M.
- EV/EBITDA, 22x to 26x: 22 × 2.5 = $55.0M to 26 × 2.5 = $65.0M.
- Ask: 60.0 ÷ 14.0 = 4.3x revenue; 60.0 ÷ 2.5 = 24.0x EBITDA. Inside both ranges.

**Conditions and data requests**
1. Row 1: the financial diligence lead to obtain the issued SOC 2 Type II report before close. If it shows exceptions, deal counsel to negotiate a remediation covenant.
2. Row 2: deal counsel to obtain both claim filings and counsel's estimate, and cover them with a specific indemnity at close.
3. Row 4: the commercial lead to obtain churn by cohort for the 12 months before and after the price rise, before signing.

## Case three: a blocker no document can cure (Decline)

**Artifacts in brief.** Target: Tallis Freight Brokerage, a truckload broker. Risk table, tiers Red / Amber / Green:
1. Largest shipper, 47% of net revenue, has written that it will terminate its master agreement on any change of control to an owner of a competing asset-based carrier; the acquirer owns one. Red, Unresolved.
2. Carrier payment disputes, $0.3M in aggregate, under negotiation. Amber, In-Progress.
3. Transport management software licensed annually from a company the founder owns. Amber, Unresolved.
4. Broker bond and operating authority in good standing; renewal due in 5 months. Green, Resolved.

Narrative: gross billings $88.0M, carrier costs $74.8M, net revenue $13.2M growing 16%, EBITDA $3.3M. The seller's banker frames value as 0.6x gross billings. No asking price is stated.

Comps (EV/Net revenue, EV/EBITDA, net growth): RouteMatch Brokerage 3.4x, 13x, 14%. LaneWise Logistics 3.9x, 15x, 18%. HaulPoint Inc. 3.0x, 11x, 9%. FreightMind Digital 7.8x, NM, 58%.

**The reply, in full, with nothing before or after it:**

## Tallis Freight Brokerage: IC memo

**Recommendation: Decline.** No asking price was supplied. Row 1 cannot be cured by this buyer: the largest shipper has said in writing that it will terminate on a sale to an owner of a competing carrier, which is what we are. That removes 47% of net revenue at signing. Re-engage only if the shipper signs a waiver naming this acquirer.

**Thesis and primary risk**
Thesis: a broker growing net revenue 16% that would feed loads to our own fleet.
Primary risk: row 1. The thesis depends on the same shipper relationship the sale would end.

**Risk table** (Red = must-address, Amber = manageable, Green = informational)

| # | Finding | Tier (given) | Status given → as read | Effect on the decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shipper at 47% of net revenue will terminate on sale to a carrier owner | Red | Unresolved → open, and not curable by the seller | Decline |
| 2 | Carrier payment disputes, $0.3M, under negotiation | Amber | In-Progress → open | Would be an indemnity |
| 3 | Software licensed annually from the founder's company | Amber | Unresolved → open | Would need a long-term licence or assignment |
| 4 | Broker bond and authority in good standing, renewal in 5 months | Green | Resolved; consistent | None |

**Financial base**
Gross billings of $88.0M include $74.8M paid straight to carriers. Net revenue, 88.0 − 74.8 = $13.2M, is the figure to value. The banker's 0.6x on gross billings (0.6 × 88.0 = $52.8M) applies a multiple to money the broker never keeps; no comp is quoted on that basis.

**Valuation**
- Included: RouteMatch, LaneWise, HaulPoint, all quoted on net revenue. Excluded: FreightMind, at 58% growth with NM EBITDA.
- EV/Net revenue, 3.0x to 3.9x: 3.0 × 13.2 = $39.6M to 3.9 × 13.2 = $51.5M. EV/EBITDA, 11x to 15x: 11 × 3.3 = $36.3M to 15 × 3.3 = $49.5M.
- Downside, row 1: net revenue without the shipper is 13.2 × 0.53 = $7.0M, so 3.0 × 7.0 = $21.0M to 3.9 × 7.0 = $27.3M. That is what we would own on the day of signing.
- The banker's $52.8M sits above even the full-revenue range.

**What would reopen it**
1. Row 1: the shipper's signed waiver of the change-of-control termination for this acquirer, obtained by the commercial lead.
2. Row 3: an assignment of the software, or a licence of at least five years, from the founder's company.
3. A price set on the net-revenue range, not on gross billings.

## Notes on the form

- The first line under the heading is the verdict. A reader who stops there knows whether to sign.
- Every figure in the memo is either in the artifacts or shown as arithmetic from them. When a figure is missing, the memo says so and turns it into a data request.
- Rows keep the input's order and numbering. The status cell always shows the given status and your reading, even when they agree.
- Conditions point back to rows by number, and each has a consequence.
- Length follows the deal. Case two needs less than case one, and that is correct.
- The memo is the whole reply. Each case above starts on its `## ` line; no working, step list or note to the reader comes before it or after it.
