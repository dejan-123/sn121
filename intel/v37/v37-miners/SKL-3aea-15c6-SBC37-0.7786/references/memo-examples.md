### The memo

# Investment Committee Memo: Linenbrook Services (commercial linen and laundry)

**Recommendation: NO-GO at this time (do not proceed to signing).** Two Must-Address findings are open, and the $52M ask is far above the range the comparable laundries support.

**Input check**
- Blocking rows: #1, the permit (In-Progress; an appeal is not a renewal), and #2, the certification (Unresolved).
- Contradicted: #3 is marked Resolved, but the narrative says the renewal is still being negotiated, so it is open.
- Does not reconcile: the seller's "17% margin" against $4.3M ÷ $26.0M = 16.5%.
- Recomputed: net debt $6.9M − $1.3M = $5.6M; ask = 12.1x EBITDA, 2.0x revenue.
- Missing: EBITDA for the hospital line, needed for the downside.

## 1. Deal Thesis (≤ 120 words)

Linenbrook is a regional linen and laundry operator: $26.0M revenue growing 6%, $4.3M EBITDA, serving hotels (64%) and hospitals (36%). The value case is steady, contracted volume that a larger network could route through fewer plants.

**Primary risk: the right to operate is not secured.** The main plant's discharge permit was refused and is under appeal. The certification that hospital work requires has lapsed, which puts $9.4M of revenue (36% × $26.0M) at risk.

## 2. Tiered Risk Table

| # | Finding | Input tier / status | IC treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discharge permit refused; appeal filed | Must-Address / In-Progress | **Blocks proceed** | Without the permit the main plant cannot run; an appeal is not an outcome |
| 2 | Hospital infection-control certification lapsed 5 months ago; no re-audit booked | Must-Address / Unresolved | **Blocks proceed** | $9.4M of hospital revenue depends on it |
| 3 | Largest hotel customer (23% of revenue, about $6.0M) renewal | Manageable / "Resolved" | **Open: contradicted by the narrative** | Terms are still being negotiated; 4 months remain on the contract |
| 4 | Collective agreement expires in 5 months; no talks | Manageable / Unresolved | Condition precedent | Labour cost and continuity through integration |
| 5 | Possible sale of a satellite depot | Informational / In-Progress | Monitor | Small, and no figure is given |

Rows 1 and 2 are deal-breakers until cleared. Row 3 is open and is handled through a condition and structure. Rows 4 and 5 can be managed.

## 3. Valuation Range

**Comp set.**
- Included: CleanSpan (8.5x; 7%, 17%), Hotelwash (7.8x; 5%, 16%) and Medilinen (9.0x; 6%, 18%).
- Excluded:
  - National Textile Care (11.5x): at $900M of revenue it is about 35 times Linenbrook's size, and its multiple reflects national scale;
  - SprintLaundry Tech: NM EBITDA and 31% growth, a different profile.

**Multiple and base.** EV/EBITDA, because EBITDA is positive and the comps report it. The base is the reported $4.3M.

**Bracket.** Linenbrook (6%, 16.5%) sits between Hotelwash (5%, 16%) and CleanSpan (7%, 17%). Medilinen's higher margin puts it just above the bracket, as a check.
- EV/EBITDA: 7.8x × $4.3M = $33.5M; 8.5x × $4.3M = $36.6M
- EV/Revenue cross-check: 1.3x × $26.0M = $33.8M; 1.5x × $26.0M = $39.0M
- **Defended EV range: $33.5M to $36.6M.** Equity value is EV less $5.6M net debt.
- Downside if the hospital line is lost: revenue $26.0M − $9.4M = $16.6M, so 1.3x to 1.5x gives $21.6M to $24.9M. The artifacts give no hospital EBITDA.

**Against the ask.** $52M is 12.1x EBITDA, $15.4M (42%) above the top of the range. It is above even the excluded national player (11.5x × $4.3M = $49.5M).

## 4. Recommendation and Consistency Check (≤ 120 words)

**NO-GO at this time.**
- Two Must-Address findings are open (#1 In-Progress, #2 Unresolved), so "proceed" is blocked whatever the price.
- The primary risk named in the thesis is the first row of the table.
- The IC should revisit only with the permit renewed and the certification restored, at a price inside $33.5M to $36.6M.

## 5. Conditions and Data Requests

1. **Permit (#1):** the water authority's renewal decision. An appeal filing is not enough. Before signing.
2. **Certification (#2):** a passed re-audit and the reissued certificate, before signing. Also the hospital line's EBITDA, to size the downside.
3. **Hotel customer (#3):** the signed renewal, or a holdback released on renewal. The risk table's "Resolved" to be corrected.
4. **Labour (#4):** the negotiation plan and cost range for the new collective agreement, agreed before signing.
5. **Margin (input gap):** reconcile the seller's 17% with the 16.5% the reported figures give.

# Investment Committee Memo: Crestmark Outdoor (regional billboard advertising)

**Recommendation: PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS, at no more than $57M.** No Must-Address finding is open. The ask sits $2.0M above the defended range once the committed capex is priced.

**Input check**
- No blocking row. #1 is Resolved and evidenced by the city's award letter.
- Questioned: #2 says Resolved, but "agreed in principle" is not an executed settlement. The executed settlement becomes a condition precedent.
- Reconciles: $5.0M ÷ $18.5M = 27.0%. Net debt is $9.0M − $2.0M = $7.0M; the ask = 11.8x EBITDA, 3.2x revenue.
- Missing: the revenue share of the concession faces (38% of faces is given, revenue is not).

## 1. Deal Thesis (≤ 120 words)

Crestmark is a regional billboard operator: $18.5M revenue growing 9%, a 27% EBITDA margin, and a renewed 10-year city concession. The value case is long-dated site rights, with upside from converting static faces to digital. Its 27% margin already matches the closest peers, so the price rests on contracted sites rather than a margin catch-up.

**Primary risk: the concession is the core asset.** It covers 38% of faces. It is awarded but not yet executed, and the digital conversion needs $3.0M of capex the seller's forecast leaves out.

## 2. Tiered Risk Table

| # | Finding | Input tier / status | IC treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | City concession (38% of faces) renewed for 10 years | Must-Address / Resolved | Completion deliverable | The award letter evidences it; the agreement is executed at completion |
| 2 | Landowner dispute, 12 sites; settlement "in principle" | Manageable / "Resolved" | **Condition precedent** | An agreement in principle is not a closure |
| 3 | $3.0M digital-conversion capex, not in the forecast | Manageable / Unresolved | Price | Deducted from EV in section 3 |

No deal-breaker is open. Row 1 would become one if the concession agreement were not executed at completion. Row 2 is a signing condition. Row 3 is a price item.

## 3. Valuation Range

**Comp set.**
- Included: Skyline Media (11.0x; 8%, 27%), RoadView (12.0x; 10%, 28%) and Metro Panels (9.5x; 5%, 25%).
- Excluded: BrightGrid Screens (18.0x). It is an all-digital network growing 24%, about 2.7 times Crestmark's growth, and a different business mix.

**Multiple and base.** EV/EBITDA on the reported $5.0M, with the committed capex deducted.

**Bracket.** Crestmark (9%, 27%) sits between Skyline (8%, 27%) and RoadView (10%, 28%). Metro Panels (5%, 25%) is slower and less profitable; it stays in the set but does not bound the multiple.
- EV/EBITDA: 11.0x × $5.0M = $55.0M; 12.0x × $5.0M = $60.0M
- Less capex: $55.0M − $3.0M = $52.0M; $60.0M − $3.0M = $57.0M
- EV/Revenue cross-check (before capex): 3.0x × $18.5M = $55.5M; 3.4x × $18.5M = $62.9M
- **Defended EV range: $52.0M to $57.0M.** Equity value is EV less $7.0M net debt.

**Against the ask.** $59M (11.8x) is $2.0M above the top of the range. The seller's figure leaves out the $3.0M capex.

## 4. Recommendation and Consistency Check (≤ 120 words)

**PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS, at ≤ $57M.**
- No Must-Address finding is open: #1's status is evidenced by the award letter.
- The questioned label (#2) is a condition precedent.
- The primary risk named in the thesis is row 1.
- The price sits inside the section 3 range: $57M is its top, so the $59M ask is not accepted as stated.
- If the concession agreement is not executed at completion, the call becomes no-go.

## 5. Conditions and Data Requests

1. **Concession (#1):** the executed concession agreement on the awarded terms, as a condition of completion.
2. **Landowner sites (#2):** the executed settlement covering all 12 sites, before signing.
3. **Capex (#3):** the $3.0M stays deducted in the price; a conversion schedule is delivered with the SPA.
4. **Concession revenue (input gap):** revenue and EBITDA earned on the concession faces, to size row 1.
5. **Price:** offer no more than $57M EV, adjusted for the $7.0M net debt at completion.
6. **Concession terms (#1):** the renewal's fee schedule and any change in the city's share of revenue, to confirm the 27% margin holds over the new term.

### The memo

# Investment Committee Memo: Northfold Spirits (craft spirits brands)

**Recommendation: NO-GO at this time (do not proceed to signing).** The licence transfer is still under regulatory review, and the $47M ask prices revenue that ends at completion.

**Input check**
- Open: #1, the licence transfer (Must-Address, In-Progress). Counsel's "formality" is an opinion, not an approval.
- Questioned: #2 is marked Resolved, and no artifact shows how the opposition ended. Its decision or withdrawal becomes a condition precedent; on its own it does not block.
- Re-based: #3's $2.2M of private-label revenue ends on a change of control, so the base is $14.0M − $2.2M = $11.8M.
- Recomputed: net debt $4.4M − $2.5M = $1.9M; the ask = 3.4x total revenue, 4.0x the transferable base.
- Missing: own-brand revenue growth on its own.

## 1. Deal Thesis (≤ 120 words)

Northfold owns a portfolio of craft spirits brands: $14.0M revenue growing 25%, a 48% gross margin, and EBITDA of −$0.8M. The value case is $11.8M of own-brand revenue that a larger distributor could scale through its routes to market.

**Primary risk: the business cannot trade under a new owner until its licences transfer.** The application is still under review. The flagship trademark also faced an opposition in the main export market, and the artifacts do not show how it ended.

## 2. Tiered Risk Table

| # | Finding | Input tier / status | IC treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Licence transfer: application filed, regulator review under way | Must-Address / In-Progress | **Blocks proceed** | Without the licences there is no production or wholesale; counsel's view is not the regulator's approval |
| 2 | Flagship trademark opposition in the main export market | Must-Address / Resolved | **Condition precedent: evidence the resolution** | The row still describes the opposition; the decision or withdrawal must be produced |
| 3 | Private-label contract ($2.2M) ends on change of control | Manageable / Unresolved | Price: removed from the base | Revenue the buyer will not own |

Row 1 is a deal-breaker until the regulator approves the transfer. Row 2 must be evidenced before signing. Row 3 belongs in the price.

## 3. Valuation Range

**Comp set.**
- Included: Highland Cask (3.1x; 22%, 50%), Copperline (3.5x; 28%, 52%) and RiverStill (2.7x; 18%, 46%).
- Excluded:
  - GlobalSpirits plc (4.8x): about $2.4B of revenue and 6% growth, a mature global group, not a peer;
  - ViralMixers (6.2x): 70% growth, about 2.8 times Northfold's.

**Multiple and base.** EV/Revenue, because EBITDA is negative (−$0.8M), which makes EV/EBITDA meaningless. The base is own-brand revenue of $11.8M; the $2.2M of private label ends at completion.

**Bracket.** Northfold's 25% growth sits between Highland (22%) and Copperline (28%), but its 48% gross margin is below both. We therefore bracket RiverStill (46% margin) to Highland, 2.7x to 3.1x.
- 2.7x × $11.8M = $31.9M; 3.1x × $11.8M = $36.6M
- **Defended EV range: $31.9M to $36.6M.** Equity value is EV less $1.9M net debt.

**Against the ask.** $47M is 4.0x the transferable base, above every included comp, and $10.4M (28%) above the top of the range. The seller's 3.4x is applied to the full $14.0M, so about $7.5M of the ask (3.4x × $2.2M) rests on revenue that ends at completion.

## 4. Recommendation and Consistency Check (≤ 120 words)

**NO-GO at this time.**
- #1 is Must-Address and In-Progress, so "proceed" is blocked: an expected approval is not an approval.
- #2 is questioned, not open. Its evidence is a condition precedent and would not block on its own.
- The primary risk named in the thesis is the first row of the table.
- The IC should revisit once the regulator approves the transfer, at a price inside $31.9M to $36.6M.

## 5. Conditions and Data Requests

1. **Licences (#1):** the regulator's written approval of the transfer, before signing.
2. **Trademark (#2):** the opposition decision or the opponent's withdrawal, before signing.
3. **Private label (#3):** price on the $11.8M own-brand base; any continuing supply only under a separately priced agreement.
4. **Growth (input gap):** own-brand revenue growth without private label, to confirm the bracket.
