# Compact IC writing — examples of finished decisions

This file illustrates **how to write within an output budget**. All companies, facts, dates and numerical choices below are fictional. Never transfer them into another deal. Follow the scenario's own section-length requirements, if given, rather than these examples. Every memo ends at the final action bullet: **do not print a process audit, appendix or extra conclusion**.

In long packets, preserve all risk and comp rows; make each cell do one job. The final two sections matter as much as the first three. Decide on the verdict and action owners before writing heading 1. The worked examples are concise by design, not templates to copy word-for-word; their chosen ranges are case-specific and never standing multiple haircuts.

## Example A — A claimed resolution conflicts with the financial narrative

**Input sketch.** Buyer considers **Crestfall Cold Storage**. Risk table R: R1 operating authorization, `Must-Address / Resolved`; R2 largest client at 32% volume, contract expires in eight months, `Manageable / Unresolved`; R3 compressor condition survey, `Manageable / In-Progress`; R4 completed environmental review with a written closure letter, `Low / Resolved`. Financial narrative F: $26.0m LTM revenue, $3.2m EBITDA, 12% growth, 39% gross margin, seller ask $44m **EV**; authorization **renewal remains pending**, despite R1 label. Comps C: PolarRoute 1.45x EV/revenue, 10.0x EBITDA, growth 15%, GM 41%; ThermoChain 1.25x, 8.5x, 11%, 38%; ChillSpan 1.35x, 9.0x, 13%, 40%; AeroCold Automation 2.80x, 19.0x, 39%, 59%, materially different robotics model. No peer size data.

### 1. Investment Thesis
Crestfall's $26.0m LTM cold-storage business earns $3.2m EBITDA, but client retention and operational permissions determine its resilience. The **operating authorization** is the primary risk: R1 says Resolved while F says renewal is pending. The buyer cannot rely on uninterrupted permissions yet. The $44m EV ask also exceeds the defendable peer range.

### 2. Risk Assessment
| ID / finding | Original tier / status | Cross-document finding | IC consequence / mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 — Operating authorization | Must-Address / Resolved | **R/F conflict:** renewal pending; no final authorization shown. | Treat open for decision; regulator's signed outcome required. |
| R2 — Largest client, 32% volume | Manageable / Unresolved | Renewal due in eight months; no commitment supplied. | Owner not supplied: assign contract workstream; seek renewal or quantified fallback. |
| R3 — Compressor survey | Manageable / In-Progress | No condition survey or repair budget. | Owner not supplied: assign engineering workstream; obtain scope and capex. |
| R4 — Environmental review | Low / Resolved | Written agency closure stated; no conflicting input. | Monitor; no new action invented. |

### 3. Valuation Range and Comparables
| Comparable | EV/revenue | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| PolarRoute | 1.45x | Include: relevant cold logistics. |
| ThermoChain | 1.25x | Include: similar operating profile. |
| ChillSpan | 1.35x | Include: directly relevant warehouses. |
| AeroCold Automation | 2.80x | Exclude: different robotics economics and outlier growth/margin. |

Included median **1.35x**; growth **13%**, GM **40%**. Crestfall's 12% growth and 39% GM lag included medians slightly, supporting the **lower half of the observed 1.25x–1.45x peer span**, not a repeated per-percent haircut. Peer sizes are missing: no size adjustment. Select **1.25x–1.35x** EV/revenue, anchored at the included low and median. **EV = $26.0m × 1.25 = $32.5m** to **$26.0m × 1.35 = $35.1m**. EBITDA cross-check: median 9.0x × $3.2m = $28.8m. The $44m EV ask exceeds both approaches. No fictional permit-cost adjustment.

### 4. Recommendation
**Request More Data.** R1 is still an open Must-Address issue despite the upstream Resolved label. R2 and R3 also require mitigation evidence. The peer-derived range of $32.5m–$35.1m EV does not support the $44m ask without further economics; pricing cannot cure the missing authorization.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps
- **R1 — Operating authorization:** Owner not identified; assign permit accountability and obtain written final authorization before an IC Proceed decision.
- **R2 — Largest client:** Owner not identified; assign account workstream and obtain signed renewal or quantified fallback before signing.
- **R3 — Compressor survey:** Owner not identified; assign inspection workstream and obtain assessment with capex before pricing is finalized.

## Example B — Proceed with residual mitigations, not the seller's entire price

**Input sketch.** Acquisition of **Elmharbor Dispatch Software**. R: R1 workflow-engine IP rights, `Must-Address / Resolved`, executed assignment confirmed in F; R2 hosting provider renewal, `Manageable / Unresolved`, with executed three-year standby-host option in F; R3 senior relationship-manager concentration, `Manageable / In-Progress`, with funded handover and retention plan; R4 annual security-policy update, `Informational / Resolved`. F: $12m matching LTM recurring subscriptions, 30% growth, 82% GM, **−$1m EBITDA**, seller ask **$48m EV**. C: AtlasFlow 3.2x EV/revenue, 28% growth, 80% GM; BrevityDash 3.6x, 32%, 83%; SignalForge 3.4x, 30%, 82%; GrowthRocket Strategic Deal 6.9x, 67%, 91%, disclosed control premium. No peer scale data.

### 1. Investment Thesis
Elmharbor's $12m recurring subscription base grows 30% at 82% gross margin, but EBITDA remains negative. The principal risk, **workflow-engine IP rights**, is Must-Address yet supported as Resolved by an executed assignment. Hosting renewal and client-relationship concentration retain named mitigations. The $48m EV ask exceeds peer valuation, so proceeding with diligence is not approval at that price.

### 2. Risk Assessment
| ID / finding | Original tier / status | Cross-document finding | IC consequence / mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 — Workflow-engine IP rights | Must-Address / Resolved | Executed rights assignment supplied, no conflict. | Counsel confirm scope at close; gate currently closed. |
| R2 — Hosting renewal | Manageable / Unresolved | Executed standby provider option mitigates service interruption. | Owner not supplied: assign hosting workstream; confirm activation readiness. |
| R3 — Relationship-manager concentration | Manageable / In-Progress | Budgeted retention and handover plan provided. | Owner not supplied: assign retention workstream; complete handovers. |
| R4 — Security-policy update | Informational / Resolved | No contrary evidence. | Ordinary monitoring only. |

### 3. Valuation Range and Comparables
| Comparable | EV/revenue | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| AtlasFlow | 3.2x | Include: recurring software peer. |
| BrevityDash | 3.6x | Include: comparable growth and margin. |
| SignalForge | 3.4x | Include: closely matched software economics. |
| GrowthRocket Strategic Deal | 6.9x | Exclude: strategic premium, much faster growth. |

Median EV/revenue **3.4x**, peer growth **30%**, GM **82%**. Target matches both, giving neutral growth/margin adjustments; peer size undisclosed, so no supported size adjustment. Choose **3.2x–3.6x** around the median. **EV = $12m × 3.2 = $38.4m** to **$12m × 3.6 = $43.2m**, below the $48m ask. Negative EBITDA prohibits positive EV/EBITDA valuation; no net-to-gross mismatch.

### 4. Recommendation
**Proceed**, with documented R2/R3 mitigation verification and valuation discipline at $38.4m–$43.2m EV. R1's upstream resolution is supported by the executed assignment; no Must-Address gate remains open. This authorizes continued deal work, not acceptance of the $48m seller ask.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps
- **R1 — Workflow-engine IP rights:** Owner not identified; assign rights-verification workstream to confirm assignment scope before signing.
- **R2 — Hosting renewal:** Owner not identified; assign hosting workstream to validate standby activation and service continuity before closing.
- **R3 — Relationship-manager concentration:** Owner not identified; assign handover workstream to finish retention and coverage checks before closing.

## Decision contrasts (apply quietly; do not print as extra memo sections)

- **Missing status:** An open Must-Address row with a blank status remains open for the gate. Never silently declare it Resolved. Do not transform uncertainty alone into Pass.
- **Mis-tier without relabeling:** An upstream `Low` risk may conflict with F's revenue-quality evidence. Retain `Low` in the displayed table, flag R/F discrepancy, and normalize the valuation base only to the extent F supports the change.
- **Net/gross comparability:** If an intermediary reports $20m gross flows and $11m net revenue, a 5.0x EV/**net** revenue comp implies $55m, **not** $100m. Gross throughput, GTV and net earned fees are distinct denominators.
- **Bad EBITDA:** An EBITDA multiple marked `NM` can be rejected while the same peer remains a valid EV/revenue comp; explain the metric-level decision.
- **No peer scale:** Never assign a numerical target-size discount unless comparable scale is supplied. State size direction unknown while discussing growth and margin normally.
- **Conditional confidence:** A `Resolved` risk contradicted by F cannot support Proceed. A manageable risk may remain open if a specific workable mitigation has an owner. A seller asking price above the range doesn't by itself convert a factually acceptable deal into Pass.


## Peer-screen and ownership discipline — contrasting decisions

- **Same business model, different economics:** Three recurring-software peers grow near 15–25% with broadly comparable margins; a fourth grows 80%, has a much higher gross margin and trades at a dramatically higher multiple. If those differences make the fourth nonrepresentative for the target, **exclude** it with the growth/margin/scale facts, even though both are called software. State its multiple and exclusion reason in the table. Do **not** exclude merely because its multiple is highest if the peer's operating economics are actually comparable.
- **No fabricated haircut:** If the included comp distribution is 2.0x, 2.3x, 2.7x, and target growth/margins are close to the middle, consider an anchored band such as 2.0x–2.7x or a narrower justified subset. If the target materially lags the peers, explain the direction and select the lower observed region. Don't convert each 1% growth or margin difference into a default −0.05x; there is no universal coefficient.
- **No person or title supplied:** `R2 — contract renewal | owner not identified — assign accountability before approval | signed renewal required` is more faithful than claiming a Chief Commercial Officer exists. An explicitly *proposed* deal workstream is an action, not proof that the source named a current employee.
- **Valuation missing a matching basis:** If all peers quote EV/net fees but the target reports only gross processed value, do not multiply the gross number by a net-fee multiple. State that a defensible EV range cannot yet be produced and name the missing net-fee base.
- **No phantom closure:** A deferral of a formal review does not equal closure, even if the risk table says Resolved. Preserve Resolved in the upstream column, flag the contrary document and block Proceed until resolution evidence.
