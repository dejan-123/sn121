# Preflight walkaround

One invented deal, flown from its three artifacts to a finished memo; after it, a short second case, how section 5 changes with the verdict, and a handful of single-item notes. Fly the same pattern: the layout, the proportions, how each call is argued. In a live memo every name, figure and finding comes from that memo's own artifacts, never from this page.

## The memo as filed

# IC Memo: Brackwater Glasshouses

### 1. Deal Thesis

Brackwater is a four-site glasshouse grower of tomatoes, cucumbers and herbs: $40.0M of LTM revenue, 29% gross margin and $7.4M of reported EBITDA, growing 1%. The appeal is the premium supermarket programme, the only premium-priced line and the base of the seller's growth plan. The primary risk is R1: that programme, 39% of revenue, is non-assignable and ends on a change of control, and the chain has written that it will not re-contract with any new owner. The $90.0M ask is 12.15x reported EBITDA and 19.15x the $4.7M of EBITDA that would transfer.

### 2. Risk Assessment

| Row | Finding | Tier (as given) | Status (as given) | Flag | Effect on the deal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Supermarket programme (39% of revenue) is non-assignable and ends on a change of control | Must-Address | Unresolved | Chain's letter: "We will not re-contract the programme with any new owner"; no replacement named | $15.6M of revenue and $2.7M of EBITDA do not transfer |
| R2 | Boiler and heat-curtain replacement at the two oldest glasshouses | Manageable | In-Progress | None | Cost estimate pending; not quantified in the artifacts |
| R3 | Seasonal pickers sourced through a single labour agency | Manageable | Unresolved | None | Harvest labour continuity |
| R4 | Fungal outbreak destroyed one cucumber crop last season; procedures revised | Informational | Unresolved | None | Crop-loss risk; monitor |

R1 is open, and the chain's own letter makes the loss permanent for any buyer, so the gate turns on it. No other row reads heavier or lighter than its tier.

### 3. Valuation

Primary multiple: EV/EBITDA, since Brackwater is a profitable grower and four of the five comps report a meaningful EBITDA multiple.
Base: transferable reported EBITDA of $4.7M, which is $7.4M less the $2.7M earned by the supermarket programme that ends at close. The seller values the whole business on adjusted EBITDA of $8.7M; the $1.3M add-back is excluded because it is not itemised.

| Peer (EV/EBITDA) | Screen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tillmore Produce (9.0x) | In | Profitable grower peer |
| Verdance Growers (10.0x) | In | Profitable grower peer |
| Sunpane Horticulture (11.5x) | In | Profitable grower peer |
| Orchidea Farms (12.0x) | In | Profitable grower peer |
| Vertiq Indoor Farms (NM) | Out | EBITDA negative, so the multiple is NM |

Included multiples in order: 9.0x, 10.0x, 11.5x, 12.0x; median (10.0x + 11.5x) ÷ 2 = 10.75x. Included medians: growth 6.5%, gross margin 31.5%. One step is 1.0x:
- growth, 1% against 6.5%, 5.5 points below: one step down;
- gross margin, 29% against 31.5%, 2.5 points: no step;
- concentration: the programme's 39% is already out of the base, and no remaining customer exceeds 12%: no step;
- size: the comps' scale is not given, so no step.

Running sum: 10.75x − 1.0x growth = 9.75x. Range, 9.75x ± 1.0x: 8.75x × $4.7M = $41.1M and 10.75x × $4.7M = $50.5M, so **$41.1M–$50.5M**.

The $90.0M ask sits $39.5M above the high end: 19.15x transferable EBITDA, 12.15x reported EBITDA and 10.35x the seller's adjusted figure.

### 4. Recommendation

**Pass.** R1 is an open must-address row, and the chain's letter states that the agreement "ends on any change of control" and that "its volume will move to our existing growers"; no replacement buyer is named. The terms bar transfer of 39% of revenue and of the premium line the growth plan rests on, so the blocker is permanent for this buyer. Even setting it aside, the comps support at most $50.5M against a $90.0M ask.

### 5. Conditions and Next Steps

- **R1:** Look again only if the chain signs a new programme agreement with the buyer, or the seller offers the business without the programme; deal lead to confirm either before any resubmission.
- **R2:** On any resubmission, operations diligence to obtain the boiler and heat-curtain replacement quote, priced as a debt-like deduction.
- **R3:** Operations to line up a second labour agency or a direct-hire plan before the next harvest in any revised case.
- **R4:** Agronomy lead to report biosecurity audit results as a monitoring item in any revised case.

## Second case, short: Resolved rows that only sound open

A bakery-ingredients distributor; no figures are needed for the point. Two must-address rows arrive marked Resolved: "Food-safety inspection found allergen-labelling errors on two private-label lines; corrective plan required" and "Senior lender's consent is required for the change of control." Neither the narrative nor any analyst note says anything about either status. A finding's words describe what was found, not where it now stands, so with no second source both rows stay Resolved and nothing holds the gate. The proof of each becomes a pre-signing item; the manageable rows all carry named mitigations, and the memo reads:

**Proceed with conditions.** Both must-address rows are Resolved and no artifact contradicts them, and every manageable row has a named mitigation. Evidence of both resolutions is required before signing.

- **R1:** Quality lead to obtain the inspector's close-out letter on the corrective plan before signing.
- **R2:** Deal lead to obtain the senior lender's signed consent to the change of control before signing.

## How section 5 changes with the verdict

- **Proceed:** the bullets list what has to be in hand at signing or closing: the indemnity or warranty that covers the row, an escrow sized to whatever exposure the artifacts put a figure on, any covenant or consent, then the actions for day one. Manageable rows keep their mitigations.
- **Request more data:** for each open row, the specific document or answer that would settle it, who fetches it, by when, and how each possible outcome would move the verdict and the range.
- **Pass:** why the blocker cannot be fixed inside this transaction, and what would need to change for the committee to take the deal up again.

## Single-item notes

- **Upstream grades.** Tiers written Critical / Moderate / Low go into the memo in those words, and a single sentence says Critical is read as must-address.
- **Still open.** A status of In-Progress, "substantially resolved" or "deferred", or no status at all, leaves the row open at the gate; one clause gives the reason.
- **The banker's number.** A multiple quoted against gross volume, or against revenue that includes one-time items, is reworked on your own base before it is set beside the range.
- **Missing inputs.** If a metric or an artifact is absent, write "not provided", value on what you have, and list the request in section 5.
- **Running long.** Trim adjectives and repeated context first; every row, every exclusion reason and every line of arithmetic stays.

End of walkaround. Transmit the memo now: the first characters of the reply are `# IC Memo: ` and the target's name. A first line like "Now I will draft the IC memo" ahead of them is the most frequent slip; keep all working off the page.
