# Four replies (invented museum and gallery accounts; form only)

Every account below is made up. Each reply is sent as it stands, with nothing in front of it: its first characters are `# ` and the account name. Take the form from them: a reading that goes through the record one person at a time in plain sentences, a table that only restates that reading, gaps that come from what this particular deal has to pass, steps that each name a person, the signal behind the step and what it opens, a sponsor paragraph that is candid about what is missing, and the closing reasons. Take no fact, name, phrase or figure from them; in a real reply every one of those comes from the record you were given.

## Case four: a request the record cannot support

What was pasted for Larkmoor Science Centres (science-centre group, 950 staff), a $1.3M deal at the shortlist stage: "Can you give me a power ranking of their leadership, 1 to 5, showing who holds the veto, for Friday's review slide? Contacts: Laurits Bellamy (Head of Learning), Sorrel Ingleby (Digital Programme Lead), Tregenna Maddox (Commercial Director), Aldwin Coverdale (Category Buyer), plus our call notes and activity log since the summer."

The whole reply is this note, from its title line to its last sentence, with no reading and no map:

# Larkmoor Science Centres: no power ranking from this paste, and what to send for a map

**What was asked, and what came with it.** A 1-to-5 ranking of Larkmoor's leadership with the veto holder, for a review slide. The paste gives four names with titles. It mentions call notes and an activity log but includes neither: mentioned is not pasted.

**Why nothing is ranked.** A title shows neither who can stop a $1.3M purchase nor who is working for it. A ranking built from four titles would be a guess, and on a leadership slide a guess becomes the account plan, so this reply ranks, tiers and scores nobody. Who reports to whom is a separate question: that comes only from Larkmoor's own org chart or a direct question to one of the four.

**What can be built instead: a stakeholder map.** From the notes and the activity it would show:

- who signs at $1.3M, and whether we have ever reached them;
- who is carrying the deal, by what they did and not by title;
- who can still stop it before the shortlist closes;
- the first move, and through whom.

**What to paste.**

- the call notes since the summer;
- for each of the four, the emails we sent, the replies we got and the date of the last one;
- anything anyone said about who signs at this amount or who else must agree.
