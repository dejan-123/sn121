# Compensation evidence audit

Make every conclusion reconstructible from supplied records. Distinguish accepted offers, advertised ranges and internal hiring experience. Do not imply external research or independent verification.

## Establish the assignment

First check scope and prerequisites: classified external evidence plus internal family/level bands. A roster alone cannot establish market rates. Individual raises, salary research, band architecture and pay-equity audits require different workflows.

When unsupported, give a redirect of at most 180 words naming the gap and handoff: market research/classification for dated outside evidence; job architecture for level/scope definitions; the compensation decision process for individual pay; a controlled employee-data audit for equity. Request internal bands, pay locations, stage, hiring priorities and recent offers for benchmarking. Do not invent rates, reproduce personal salaries or manufacture a benchmark table. End the response there: the audit, funnel and sections below apply only to supported benchmarks. Process supported portions of mixed requests and route the rest.

## Fix the comparison contract

Identify each family-level unit, its pay location (team-specific location overrides headquarters), stage, currency, annual base band and available midpoint, open-role priority, and internal accepted/declined offers. Preserve separate levels of the same family. An internal offer is hiring context, never an external observation or replacement for a missing band.

Apply recency at the input's as-of date, not today. State the first admitted date/month; excluding dates before a month includes that month. Prefer explicit cutoffs to rounded lookback durations. Exclude undated evidence; flag contradictory dates or absent policies.

Use supplied comparability/materiality policies first; label the following defaults as analytical conventions where needed.

## Build a record-level eligibility ledger

Assign each external ID to its family/level before exclusion. Retain rejected rows in retrieved counts. Deduplicate repeated observations explicitly; account for unmapped IDs separately.

For every ID inspect type, date, components and notes. Record geography/stage/scope/posting scores: 2 matched, 1 qualified mismatch, 0 incompatible. One incompatible dimension cannot be offset by a high sum.

| Dimension | 2: matched | 1: qualified mismatch | 0: incompatible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Same labor market or explicitly matching remote pay anchor | Unanchored remote/national evidence, or a different location explicitly identified as comparable | Different metro/country without evidence of an equivalent pay market |
| Company stage | Same financing/maturity category | One funding round apart | Two or more rounds apart, public vs private, or unknown maturity with no defensible mapping |
| Role scope | Equivalent duties and level, including an explicit scope mapping | One neighboring level/specialty or an unresolved blended job | Different occupation, materially different seniority, or unresolvable scope |
| Posting context | Accepted offer; disclosed/mandated range; range with no stated voluntary qualification | Range explicitly volunteered in the row or an applicable table-wide note | Non-hiring or unverifiable evidence masquerading as a job price |

Order funding rounds; headcount alone is not stage. Compare duties, not title spelling: abbreviations or slash synonyms may be equivalent; unresolved blended responsibilities are partial. Apply table-wide posting notes unless a row overrides them. Use supplied disclosure context, without importing current law; no note does not prove a mandate.

Classify eligible records: all 2 = full; exactly one 1 with no 0 = partial; any 0 or multiple 1s = incomparable. Partials provide context only, regardless of hiring urgency.

Apply independent eligibility exclusions: stale/undated evidence, hearsay, unverifiable surveys, contractor rates, acquisition/retention awards and structurally different equity/cash mixes. Explain unfamiliar arrangements by economic substance. Never annualize contractor rates. A package is usable only if recurring employee base is separable exactly and the job remains comparable. Count each exclusion once; retain all reasons.

## Normalize usable amounts

Convert usable offers and both range endpoints to annual base in the band's currency. Show ID, original basis, arithmetic, direction and result before comparison.

- Remove stated variable pay or allowances from total cash/OTE to recover base. If variable pay is a fraction of base, divide total cash by (1 + fraction); if a fraction of total, multiply by (1 - fraction). Do not confuse these denominators.
- Annualize employee monthly base only when its pay-period convention is supplied. Do not assume bonus months, working hours, exchange rates or base/variable splits.
- For 1 A = r B, multiply an amount in A by r to obtain B; divide B by r for A. Never compare currencies without the supplied bridge. No conversion is needed when both sides already use the same currency, including a foreign-located team.
- Do not use posted endpoints as realized salaries. Retain the range and label its midpoint as a summary of an advertisement.

Exclude unconvertible amounts, naming the missing rate/component. Notes that disclose variable pay override a misleading base label. If everything is already annual base, state that once.

## Reconcile the population before deciding

For each unit show R retrieved, F eligible full, P eligible partial, D excluded/incomparable: R = F + P + D. If reporting retained evidence S, define S = F + P; only F sets verdicts. List IDs in each set, every P/D reason, and offer/range counts within F. Reconcile totals to all input IDs. Internal offers never enter this funnel.

## Make the market comparison

Anchor on full-match accepted offers: compute their mean and observed span, retaining single-observation limitations. Separately show full-match posted range centers/span and agreement or conflict. Never average ads into offers or let ads overrule realized hiring evidence.

With no full-match offers, average full-match range midpoints as a provisional advertised-market anchor. Show component midpoints and dispersion. Partial offers cannot replace it. With no full evidence, defer; conflicting partials never form an anchor. If conflicting equal-quality full evidence makes direction indeterminate, defer pending reconciliation.

With internal midpoint M and anchor A, show M - A and g = 100 * (M - A) / A: negative below market, positive above. Decide before rounding. Unless specified otherwise, disclose a 3% planning tolerance: g < -3% Lagging; g > +3% Above-market; otherwise Competitive. This is not statistical significance. Identify boundary-sensitive calls with exact gaps. Discuss band overlap/spread as context, not proof of competitiveness or a fabricated market percentile.

Require both internal band and external anchor. Label a center derived from explicit min/max; defer a missing band, even with observable market evidence. Never substitute an employee salary or internal offer.

State confidence and its deciding reason: High for multiple corroborating full offers with adequate provenance; Medium for one full offer or several coherent full postings; Low for one full posting or fragile usable evidence; Deferred for unsupported verdicts. Posting-only evidence is capped at Medium. Repetition does not create independence. Downgrade for concentration, dispersion or scope uncertainty; partials/internal offers cannot raise tiers.

After setting position, use internal accepted/declined amounts to interpret hiring pressure relative to the band. Candidate reports of competing pay remain hearsay, not inferred competitor salaries. Flag priority roles; do not recommend individual raises or redesign bands.

## Deliver an auditable answer

For a benchmark covering four to six units, target 650-950 words including tables; do not exceed 1,000. Use two tables at most: the decision table and a compact ID audit. State the common pay basis and scoring legend once; do not repeat unchanged amounts per ID. Put the pool reconciliation on one line, not another table. Keep hiring context to one short paragraph and caveats to one short paragraph. Each partial/exclusion needs a reason once, not in multiple sections. Adapt the emphasis to the evidence rather than repeating stock prose.

Lead with the actual finding. Give a family/level benchmark table: internal basis, external anchor/IDs, worked gap, verdict, confidence/reason, R/F/P/D. Add compact audit notes/table with each ID's four scores, normalization and partial/exclusion reason. Group identical reasons without losing IDs.

Include Deferred Verdicts: missing/conflicting evidence by family and the smallest next request specifying location, stage, scope, date, basis or internal band; state none if appropriate. Include Signal Caveats once: offer/range composition, disclosure/geographic skew, voluntary posting, concentration and thin priority-role support. Explain their impact.

Finally reconcile all IDs/counts, conversions, signs and declared anchor support. Align labels, narrative and confidence. Remove unsupported claims or calculations from excluded figures. Present the evidence audit and conclusions, not a private scratchpad.
