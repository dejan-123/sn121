# From the audit to the page

The audit is where a call is made; the page is where it is shown, so the page carries the calls and not the working. A reader who wants the working asks for it.

A family's own accepted offers are evidence about that family's band only. A hire into one job says nothing about what another job's band can attract, and a declined offer is the end of a negotiation, not a price, so neither enters another family's reading. What the company's own hires can show is narrow: a mean that sits close to the band midpoint says candidates take that midpoint, one that sits well above it says the company is already paying past it to hire, and anything between says little either way, so the outside margin stands. Close means a mean no more than 2.5% from the midpoint: one 3.1% above it is not close, whatever the outside gap, and the 5% margin decides; well above means more than 4% on the anchor's side.

Confidence measures how much independent evidence a call rests on. A single advertised range is one employer's asking position, two or more are a pattern of asking positions, one accepted offer is one cleared price and an offer beside another kept point is a cleared price with a check on it. The company's own hires test the band from inside: an outside offer with a second kept point is High, and that High holds only when the snapshot lists at least one accepted offer of the company's own for that family; without one it drops to Medium. Own offers never raise a tier, and neither does a partial point: a family whose kept points are a single posted range reads Low on the page whatever own offer or partial point stands beside it, even where the audit said more. A snapshot line that lists an accepted offer beside a declined one still counts the accepted one. One posted range holds its Low verdict only when something stands beside it, a partial point for the family or an accepted offer of the company's own; with nothing beside it the family is Deferred and the direction stated. A band is never called Above-market on posted ranges alone: that call needs a kept offer, and without one the family is Deferred with the direction stated, its row giving that as the reason (an Above-market reading on posted ranges alone, so it drops) whatever partial points stand beside the range.

A family is named on the page as the snapshot names it, with its level, because the band belongs to that family and level and not to any job title in the evidence. The funnel shows each point's fate in a few words, and its counts are counts of the IDs it lists. The page begins at its title, with nothing above it.
