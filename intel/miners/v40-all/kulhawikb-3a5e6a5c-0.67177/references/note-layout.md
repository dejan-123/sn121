# The note (an invented company; it shows the layout, and none of its facts may be reused)

This is the reply when a request cannot be built into a benchmark: the outside evidence table or the band snapshot is missing, or other work is asked for. The company below does not exist.

## The case

Pasted: a message from the people manager of Sotavento Coast Hotels (six hotels, 410 staff, Faro, Portugal): "Our operations director is sure our chefs are paid below the Algarve going rate. Can you tell us the market rate for each of these roles, and draft a five-grade structure to replace our four grades? We have not gathered any outside salary data, but here is our payroll extract. The owners meet on the 14th."
Payroll extract (name, role, grade, annual base in EUR): Ines Barata, Sous Chef, grade 3, 31,500; Rui Matos, Sous Chef, grade 3, 29,800; Joana Vidal, Sous Chef, grade 3, 30,400; Helena Pires, Chef de Partie, grade 2, 24,600; Tomas Leal, Front Office Supervisor, grade 2, 23,900; Bruno Sa, Front Office Supervisor, grade 2, 24,800; Carla Nunes, Revenue Manager, grade 4, 38,200.

# Sotavento Coast Hotels: no benchmark yet, no outside pay evidence supplied

You asked for three things before the owners meet on the 14th: the market rate for each role in the extract, a check on the operations director's view that the chefs are "paid below the Algarve going rate", and a five-grade structure to replace the current four grades. What arrived is a payroll extract of seven people in four roles: three Sous Chefs (grade 3) at 31,500, 30,400 and 29,800; one Chef de Partie (grade 2) at 24,600; two Front Office Supervisors (grade 2) at 23,900 and 24,800; and one Revenue Manager (grade 4) at 38,200, all annual base in EUR. As the message says, no outside salary data has been gathered.

A competitive-position verdict sets a band against what comparable employers pay, and every market figure must come from evidence supplied with the request. There are no evidence rows here, so nothing survives to compare against: every role would be deferred, and a going rate quoted from general knowledge would put an invented number in front of the owners. The operations director's view can therefore be neither confirmed nor refuted yet. The five-grade structure is separate design work that rests on a benchmark, so it follows one. The extract also names individuals with their pay; a benchmark works from bands per role and grade, so names are not needed.

To build the benchmark, please send:
- an evidence table, one row per data point: source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, role title and level, city, employer size or stage, figure, pay element (base or total cash) and currency;
- the band for each role and grade: minimum, midpoint, maximum and headcount, plus any offers made in the last year, accepted or declined;
- the market the pay is anchored to (Faro, or the Algarve as a whole) and how old a data point may be.

## Now the note

Write the note for your own request now, in exactly this layout and nothing else: the title; one paragraph with each thing asked (in the requester's words, with any deadline as given) and what arrived (each person counted under their role and level, each figure as pasted, no names); one paragraph on why no verdict is possible and on the other work, named as separate; then the list of what to send. About 250 words, no table, no headings below the title, no market figure, no verdict, no bracket placeholders.

The note is itself the answer to the request, and it is pasted as it stands into the requester's document: nothing is said before its title and nothing follows its list.

The reply's first characters are `# `.
