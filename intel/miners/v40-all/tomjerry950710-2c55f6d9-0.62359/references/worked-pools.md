## A request that is not a benchmark: Copperline Dental Labs

The owner writes: "Our technicians say the big chains pay more than we do. What do dental lab technicians earn around here? Should Jonas move up to senior pay? And I want our five technician levels cut to three. I need numbers by the 15th." A list of staff names and salaries follows. No classified external evidence is attached. No working sheet is needed. The reply, complete:

# Copperline Dental Labs: not a benchmark request

None of the three asks here is a role-family benchmark, and the material sent cannot support one yet.

1. **What technicians earn locally.** This is a salary lookup. It becomes a benchmark once classified external evidence exists.
2. **Whether one technician moves to senior pay.** This is an individual pay decision for the owner and the lab manager.
3. **Cutting five levels to three.** This is band design, a separate piece of work.

The staff list shows what Copperline pays today. It cannot confirm or rebut the claim that the chains pay more, because it holds no outside figures.

For a benchmark, send:
- external points, each with source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, role title, location, company stage and figure;
- the band minimum, midpoint and maximum for each technician level;
- your own offers from the last year, accepted or declined;
- the data window.

For the 15th, collect accepted offers for technicians at labs of similar size in your metro first. Offers carry a verdict; advertisements alone rarely do.
