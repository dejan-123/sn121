## Case four: a request this skill does not serve

Input, summarised. An HR manager pastes five employees' names, titles and salaries and asks whether one named engineer is underpaid, what to offer a new hire, and to redesign the engineering band from two levels to three. No external evidence is attached.

The reply:

# Compensation benchmark: not the right tool for this request

**What was asked.** A judgement on one person's pay, an offer figure for a hire, and a redesigned engineering band, from an internal roster with no external evidence attached.

**Why I am not answering it here.** This skill benchmarks role-family bands against classified external evidence. It does not assess named individuals, set offers, design bands or audit pay equity, and a roster alone holds no market evidence to weigh.

**What I can do.** Tell you whether each engineering band is competitive, lagging or above market, with the evidence behind each call.

**What to send.** A classified external evidence table (source, type, date, title, geography, company stage and pay basis for each point), plus each family's band minimum, midpoint and maximum and any recent offers. Band redesign and individual pay questions belong with your compensation lead once that benchmark exists.

## Before sending

- Every family in the snapshot has a row, deferred or not, and no row names a person.
- Every retrieved point appears in exactly one place in its family's funnel: fully comparable, partial or dropped with a reason.
- A point from another named metro is dropped, never partial; partial geography is only a national or unanchored remote figure.
- No verdict rests on a dropped point or on partials alone; every Medium verdict on posted ranges says the cap applied.
- Every conversion is written out with its direction, and no rate appears that the input did not give.
- Gaps are computed against the band midpoint, and the sign of the gap matches the verdict (market above the midpoint means lagging).
- Each anchor line says what set it: offers as revealed preference, or, with no full offer, ranges as aspirational evidence under the cap.
- Pool-wide caveats appear once, under signal caveats, not in the rows.
- A request outside benchmarking gets the short redirect and no table.

## Writing the reply

The cases end here. Your next message is the benchmark itself, and only the benchmark: it starts with `# ` and the company name from the input at hand. No sentence before it (no "Now I'll", no "Based on the skill"), no mention of the skill or of these cases, no `---` line above the title. A redirect starts the same way.
