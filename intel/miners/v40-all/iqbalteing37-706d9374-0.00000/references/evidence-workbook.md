# Build a factual workbook before writing

Contents: workbook fields; Lumenfield's mixed evidence pool; Mariner's table-wide disclosure rule; final reconciliation and reporting handoff.

The workbook is a working message, separate from the client memo. First retain the source facts, then classify. Its purpose is to prevent a salary, date, title or note from migrating to another record during writing.

Use this order for the real request:

- Comparison settings: company, family/level/pay zone, currency/base period, snapshot, cutoff, bands and headcounts.
- Source capture: the statement above the evidence table; dates checked in original row order; every row's ID, original title, kind, source and Notes cell. Preserve exact decisive notes, including "none" where a cell is empty. Keep internal offers and their status on a separate family list.
- Assignment: every external ID belongs to one family, including stale, invalid and unresolved records. Explain any explicit duty/level mapping.
- Record decisions: original fact/notes first; date and basis; comparability findings; disposition and reason; normalized value; anchor or context use.
- Family cards: band midpoint, priorities/own outcomes, full/partial/excluded IDs, support series, arithmetic, verdict, confidence and the explanation the client needs.
- Pool control: sum every family and reconcile all IDs once. Name deferred units and the missing evidence. Preserve one salient misleading contextual/excluded figure and its reason, if any exists.

The fictional examples below demonstrate complete decisions, not salaries to reuse. Their tables deliberately mix families in row order. Family membership comes from each row's work and supplied mapping, not its position.

## Finish the current client's workbook

The real workbook uses the current input, including its own table statement and row notes. Confirm every original ID is assigned and counted once, support and context never mix, and family decisions agree with the pool control. Keep only limitations actually present; absence of a source problem is not a reason to invent one.

With the completed workbook in the working message, call read_reference on `references/client-memos.md`. The reference read is the handoff: the following answer is the company memo alone, beginning with its company-specific title. Do not send this workbook as the client deliverable.
