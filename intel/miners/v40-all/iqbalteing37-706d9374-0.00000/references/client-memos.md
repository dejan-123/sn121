# Write the client memo from the completed workbook

Contents: reporting contract; Lumenfield memo; Mariner memo; final handoff.

The client needs the argument behind the verdict, not a transcript of analysis. The workbook already fixes the facts. Check a suspected contradiction there before writing; never quietly change a disposition, amount or tier in the memo.

Begin with `# <Company>: <specific finding>`. Open on the comparison's actual pay zone, round, annual-base currency and eligible date floor. Name consequential stale or structurally unusable rows by their own titles and IDs where relevant. Do not invent a missing-data warning merely because another example had it.

Use two to four connected explanatory paragraphs before the table. Start with what decides the strongest comparison, then group families sharing evidence forms. Explain accepted offers as observed agreements to pay, or revealed preference; explain posted ranges as aspirational recruiting evidence. The distinction should do analytical work, not appear as a slogan. Show actual conversions once and say what changed. Identify the source observation by its own ID when first discussed. Company offers must be explicitly described as internal; outside hires must never become this company's hires.

The memo contains one benchmark table, in internal-snapshot order: family/level/pay zone; verdict and worked midpoint gap with anchor IDs; complete retrieved/full/partial/excluded funnel with IDs and reasons; confidence with its deciding reason. A full posting beside an accepted anchor belongs in the full count but not the offer mean. Every partial/excluded ID stays accountable. Monetary difference and percentage must agree. A missing anchor has no numeric gap; an observable market with no band has no internal verdict.

Follow with a short Deferred comparisons section, naming the exact gap and the next observation needed. With none deferred, say so and identify the weakest supported call and useful evidence. Then give a single Evidence limits section: only actual source concentration, disclosure, geography, recency or sample limitations; each once. Avoid repeating a policy lecture per family. Priorities and internal outcomes belong where they change the business stakes, without prescribing individual salaries or redesigning bands.

Where an extreme partial/excluded figure could mislead, finish by identifying its actual ID, original currency and why it cannot set this comparison. Do not manufacture an outlier if there is none, and do not compare unlike currencies numerically. Use the supplied tolerance or briefly identify the default planning convention; a boundary-sensitive gap is not statistical significance.

For several families, about 550-850 words is usually sufficient, including the table. Use less for a small request. Keep necessary facts; trim repeated qualifications and sentences about absent problems. No worksheet, reference-reading commentary, generic closing offer, employee list or recommendation on what someone should earn belongs in the final answer.
