# A worked benchmark and a redirect (invented companies; form only)

Both cases are made up. A real reply takes every name, figure and reason from its own input; what carries over is the order of the parts, the way a point is turned away, and that the document starts at its title with no working before it.

## How the evidence was weighed

**Firmware, Lead (P1).** Two points came close and neither fits: G-07 is a real acceptance but in Moncton, and G-08 is a smaller company's advertised range in the right city. One sits below the company midpoint and the other above it, so averaging them would manufacture an answer. This family stays open.

**Power Systems Modelling, Senior (P2).** G-01 carries it, because somebody took that job in this city at this stage for 121,300. The mandated range G-02 centres a little higher, which is where published ranges usually land. G-03 is the figure a candidate will quote, and it prices Toronto at a company three rounds on; G-04 is an agency's word and nothing else.

**Field Reliability, Supervisor.** Nobody's acceptance is on record here, only one employer's voluntary range, G-05, whose centre is almost the company midpoint. G-06 is higher because a later stage pays more. G-13 names no metro, so there is nowhere to put it.

**Regulatory Economics, Senior.** Taken at face value G-09 would look like a match for this band; it reports total pay, and total to base is 131,900 − 13,100 = 118,800, well under midpoint. The consultant's day rate (G-10) and the on-target range (G-12) cannot be turned into a salary, and G-11 is a month too old.

## Deferred verdicts: Firmware

**Firmware, Lead.** No Halifax, Series B point survived, and the two partial points disagree. One accepted Halifax offer at Series B, or two posted ranges from there, inside the window, would settle it.

## Signal caveats: few offers, mostly voluntary ranges

- Accepted offers: G-01, G-07, G-09 = 3 of 13 points. Mandated range: G-02 = 1, a good-faith band that tends to run wide; the other ranges are voluntary and written to attract, so the one verdict resting on a range is more likely too high than too low.
- One source type, one city, one or two points behind each verdict.
- Outside the pool: the company's own modelling acceptance at 120,900 was above its 115,700 midpoint, which fits the lagging call.

## Case two

Input, in brief. Someone at Marlpit Foods pastes named staff with salaries, asks what each should earn, and wants the pay grades merged. No external evidence. The whole message, title first:

# Marlpit Foods: not a benchmark request yet

You asked what each listed employee should earn and for two grades to be merged. This paste holds individual salaries and no classified market evidence, so any figure given here would be invented. It also shows what some people are paid, not the range set for a role, so there is no band to test.

- **What each person should earn** is an individual pay decision, for a compensation partner.
- **Merging grades** is band design, a separate project that can start from a benchmark.

To get the benchmark, send:
- evidence points with source, type, date, location, company stage and figure;
- band minimum, midpoint and maximum by role family and level, without names;
- your location, stage and how old a point may be.

Each family then gets a verdict with its evidence and confidence, or a deferral saying what is missing.
