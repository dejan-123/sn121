# Page check (read after the draft, before the page)

The draft is finished and stays private. Check each line of it against the input once more as the page is written:

- IDs. Every row the page mentions carries the ID this input's own evidence table gives it, copied from the draft's row lines: in the audit lists, in brackets in the reading section, in every funnel cell, beside each reference, and in the deferred entries. An ID is never typed from memory or taken from the example cases, and each row is described by what it is the first time it appears.
- Lists. The audit's first line gives the rows retrieved, the first and last ID, and the three class totals, the same three as the Pool line. `Excluded:` names each excluded row once, with its ID, what it is and the first test it fails; a row in another currency says so, with "no rate supplied", even when its city fails too, and a remote or national row says that no band can be matched to it. A partial row is never in that list, and `Partial:` names every partial row: a partial accepted offer that defers its family is still partial, listed there and never under `Excluded:`. A row kept because its note maps another title to the family's level gets a last line, `Kept on scope:`, with its ID and that note; without such a row the line is absent. The lists state results only: no second thought, correction or recount is typed into the page. `Out of window` and `Undated` carry their own counts and IDs, "none" included; a row is out of window only when its month is earlier than the first inside month, and such a row, named under `Excluded:`, is counted there as well, so that count is never "none" when one exists.
- Window. Copy the draft's window line: the first inside month, with the year the input's own window sentence prints (never the retrieval year by habit). A row is out only when its month number is smaller than that month's; a row dated in the first inside month is inside, fully comparable if nothing else is wrong, and enters its family's reference.
- Rows. One table row per family and level, in the band table's own order: the reading section groups the families by their evidence, the table never does. The funnel cell names every evidence row of that family once, by ID, in its class, rows that are out included with their reason, and no row of another family; its "retrieved" number is the count of IDs in the cell, and each ID stands in one cell only. A deferred family's rows keep their classes: its fully comparable posting is still full, in its cell and in the totals. A row keeps the draft's class, partial only at exactly one stage step and out at two or more, and stands in that same class in the audit and in the table; a row from another city is out whatever its stage.
- Pool. The line under the table adds the funnel cells up, as the draft did: retrieved, fully comparable, partial, excluded. Retrieved equals the number of rows in the evidence table, and the same three class totals stand in the audit's first line.
- Reading. Two to four short paragraphs before the table, taken from the draft's reading sentences: one or two sentences per family, under 240 words in all. Every figure, gap, verdict and tier in them is the one in the table, the verdict clause names the gap the Verdict cell names ("on both readings" only when both gaps passed their lines), what they say about the company's own offers is what the own-offers cell says, and each row they mention carries its ID, in brackets after it or in front of it. A reason a candidate gave for declining is told as that candidate's account, never as a market figure. No paragraph opens with a count of families. A family whose reference is a posted range never stands in the accepted-offers paragraph. No sentence says where a partial row sits against the band, and none adds a count or a date that neither the audit nor the table has. No recommendation and no advice on what to pay.
- Reference and gaps. Copy each family's reference and gaps from the draft: an accepted offer wherever a fully comparable one survives (the median of several), a posted midpoint, shown as (low + high) / 2, only where none does, each named with its ID; each gap to one decimal with its subtraction.
- Verdict. Copy each family's pool line from the draft first, postings and offers each in their own fields, and read the result after its arrow; a pool line that has no result yet gets one now, before anything else is written. A family whose only fully comparable rows are posted ranges is Deferred, whatever its gap, when its own pool line holds a partial accepted offer more than 5.0% from the posted midpoint and no accepted offer of the company's own, or, with no full and no partial accepted offer, an accepted offer that is out of window. A partial offer within 5.0%, or an own accepted offer beside a more distant one, lets the range stand and the family keeps its verdict; the distance is the draft's written sum, (posted midpoint - offer) / posted midpoint, and a pool line without that sum gets it now. The offer tested is always that family's own, never a neighbour's, and a family with postings and no such offer is never deferred by this test. For the others, copy each family's decision line from the draft; the cell gives the test and then the word. A family with accepted offers of its own has a hiring gap in that line whatever its reference rests on, and the gap in the own-offers cell is the one the Verdict cell reads: a hiring gap at -5.0% or beyond is never called inside its line. Lagging when a gap is at its line or beyond: the market gap at -4.5% against an accepted-offer reference or at -5.0% against a posted midpoint, the hiring gap at -5.0% ("market gap beyond -4.5%", "market gap beyond -5.0%", "hiring gap beyond -5.0%", or two of them). Above-market when the market gap is +5.0% or beyond against an accepted-offer reference and the hiring gap, if there is one, is +2.5% or beyond; never against a posted midpoint alone. Otherwise Competitive ("both gaps inside their lines", "market gap alone, inside its line", "market gap beyond +5.0% but hiring gap inside +2.5%", or "over a posted midpoint, no accepted offer behind it").
- Tier. Copy the draft's tier line, which counts the family's fully comparable market rows by kind; the cell gives that count first, then the tier word at once; a note on a partial row or an own offer that is not counted follows in brackets and never changes the word. An offer with two or more full rows in all: High. One offer and no other full row: Medium. No offer and two or more posted ranges: Medium. No offer and one posted range: Low. A partial row and the company's own offers never enter the count, so one posted range beside a partial posting, or beside an own hire, is still Low. Nothing fully comparable, or no band: Deferred, with an entry under Deferred verdicts. A family deferred by the offer test says so in the cell, as the cases do.
- Own offers. Each cell starts with the snapshot's whole line for that family, declined offers and candidates' reasons included, then the hiring gap. A family whose line reports no offers, or that the offer list does not name, reads "no own offers on record; no hiring gap", a deferred family too; a family with only a declined offer keeps its line and ends "no hiring gap". Each line stands in one cell only: the same offer figures never appear in two rows, a declined offer stays with the line that lists it, and a family without a line never takes its neighbour's.
- Caveats. Two to four bullets, each true of this pool: the evidence mix by family, the draft's sentence copied as it stands, with no family added to it (which references rest on revealed preference, accepted offers, and which on posted ranges alone; "no reference rests on posted ranges alone" when there is none), each family with a verdict in exactly one of the two groups and a deferred family in neither, with no count of rows; one source, only when every fully comparable row names the same one; pay elements or currencies, only when the pool mixed them; a gap, only what the draft's `open points:` line holds, said in this input's own words: roles with no metro anchoring that it does not name, with the rows that could not be matched (when the input anchors its remote roles to a metro, or never mentions remote roles, nothing is open and no bullet speaks of remote roles); with `open points: none` the page has no gap bullet; the posting basis, only when the draft's posting-basis line quotes a sentence that stands above the evidence table: that sentence restated as the input words it, and nothing added. When that line reads none, the page has no such bullet and never speaks of a note above the table: notes inside single rows (a mandate, a voluntary posting) do not make one, however many rows carry one, and the page never says that mandated or voluntary ranges run wide, high or low. No bullet restates the basis line, and none only reports that something is absent (no mixed currencies, no gap).
