# Worked benchmarks

Five complete replies (four benchmarks and one redirect), each on an invented company, then three shorter cases and the checks before sending. Each case gives the record in brief, then the whole reply as it should be sent, then a few lines on why it reads that way. The method carries over to any record; the names and figures belong to these cases only.

## Case 4: the request that is not a benchmark

The situation: an operations lead at Lanterne Bakeries pastes a roster of six named bakers with their salaries, asks what bakers "should" earn locally, whether one named baker deserves a raise, whether women on the roster are paid less than men, and asks for the production band to be split into three grades. No external evidence table is attached.

----- reply begins -----

# Compensation benchmark: Lanterne Bakeries (outside benchmark scope)

**What you asked for.** Four things: market pay for bakers in your area, a raise decision for one person, a comparison of pay between women and men on your roster, and a three-grade redesign of the production band.

**Why I am not producing a benchmark table.** A benchmark compares each role family's band with a classified table of external evidence. You have sent a roster of individuals and no external evidence, so there is no comparable pool to anchor a verdict on, and any market figure I gave you would be invented. The raise is an individual pay decision, the women-and-men comparison is a pay-equity audit, and the three grades are band design: each is its own piece of work with its own method.

**What I can benchmark once you send it.** Your production band by role family and level (minimum, midpoint, maximum), plus an external evidence table for those families, classified by geography, company stage, role scope and posting type, with dates. With those I can give a competitive, lagging, above-market or deferred verdict per family, with the funnel and a confidence tier.

**Where the rest belongs.** The raise goes to the manager's pay review, the equity question to a pay-equity review with legal input, and the grade split to job architecture, which can use the benchmark once it exists.

----- reply ends -----

Why this shape: it starts the same way as every reply, says plainly what is missing, and gives the requester the exact inputs that would turn this into a benchmark. It does not repeat the roster, judge any individual, or guess at market pay.

## Cases in brief

- A raw lookup ("what does a Senior Brewer earn in Ghent?") with no table and no band: a redirect in the Case 4 shape, asking for the band snapshot and a classified evidence table. A lookup figure would be individual data with no comparability check behind it.
- A clean pool where every family has an offer and a posting that agree and every gap sits inside 5 per cent: the full reply is still sent, shorter. Each row still shows its midpoint, gap and High tier; the deferred section says "None" with the reason; the caveats name only what is real, such as a single source.
- One person's offer ("can we offer 61,000 to our candidate for the Lead role?"): an individual pay decision, so a redirect. If the family's band and an evidence table are attached, I can benchmark the family, and that verdict can inform the offer; the offer itself stays with the hiring manager.

## Before sending

- The first characters of the reply are `# Compensation benchmark: `.
- Every external point appears once in the audit with all four dimensions, its class and a reason for any partial or drop.
- Each family's funnel adds up: retrieved = full + partial + dropped, and the counts match the audit.
- Every conversion is written out with its direction; every figure excluded for basis is named with why.
- Each verdict comes from its anchor: offers before postings, a partial never anchoring, and the gap written as midpoint minus anchor, in money and per cent of the anchor, with 5 per cent either side as the competitive line.
- Each tier carries its deciding reason; High has a second comparable point behind the offer, a lone offer is Medium, and posted-only verdicts are Low under the posted-range cap.
- Every deferred family has its own entry naming the evidence that would settle it.
- Pool-wide caveats are in the caveats section only, once each.
- Each per cent has been recomputed once from the figures as written, and every figure in the reply is in the input or computed from it.

The reply starts with `# Compensation benchmark: ` on its first line and ends with the last signal caveat, or, for a redirect, with the line on where the rest belongs.
