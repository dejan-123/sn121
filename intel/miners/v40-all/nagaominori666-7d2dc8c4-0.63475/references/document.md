**Pool.** 14 retrieved = 7 full + 3 partial + 4 out.

**Deferred verdicts.** Two families are not called.
- UX Research — Manager: the only points are a Series A and a Series C range on either side of the band. One Columbus Series B accepted offer or posted range dated 2025-03 or later would settle it.
- Customer Support — Team Lead: one voluntarily posted range and nothing else. An accepted offer at a Columbus Series B company, or the company's own next hire, would turn the 7.0% indication into a verdict.

**Signal caveats.** These hold across the pool and are not repeated in the rows.
- Accepted offers (revealed preference) set three of the five verdicts; the other two rest on single posted ranges, which are aspirational. 9 of the 14 points are posted ranges, 4 are accepted offers.
- Ohio has no pay-transparency mandate, so the Columbus ranges marked as such were posted voluntarily and are self-selected.
- 13 of the 14 points come from one feed, so its coverage shapes every row.
- Pay arrives on four bases and in two currencies; only base in each band's own currency is compared.

## Before sending yours

Check the document against the working sheet, silently. Every point ID appears once, in its family's funnel, with its call and a reason in words; figures appear only for the points that set a market reference, for band midpoints and for own offers. No own offer is inside a market reference. Each gap is (reference − midpoint) / midpoint and the word matches the verdict table. Every family the sheet marked DEFERRED is Deferred in the table and has a bullet. Each tier follows its questions: High only with a full offer, a second full point agreeing and own accepted offers on the same side; ranges alone are Low unless two full ranges agree and own hiring points the same way. Each own offer is read against the midpoint with its percent; a gap of 5% or more is Lagging or Above market whatever own offers show, and only a gap of 3% to 5% needs own hires 5% or more above to be called a lean. The first month in window is the month the input names; a point dated even one month earlier is out and is named in the Basis, and only a point dated that same month is on the boundary. The Pool line's three numbers are the lengths of the sheet's three ID lists. The Basis names only the points on the boundary or before the window (points out for any other reason belong in the funnels), and the partial stages it names are exactly one step from the company's own. With no deferred family, the Deferred verdicts paragraph is one sentence naming the thinnest verdict and what would firm it, with no bullets.

Now send the document for the request in front of you. Its first characters are "## " followed by the company's name. It has no line before the title, no horizontal rules, no working, and nothing about this skill, its references or these checks.
