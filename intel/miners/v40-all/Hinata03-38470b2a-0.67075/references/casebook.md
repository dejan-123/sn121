# Casebook: two benchmarks worked row by row

Everything here is invented: the companies, the titles, every city's pay and every figure. None of it is evidence about any real market, so no name, figure or verdict from these pages belongs in a reply. What carries over is the order of judgement and the form of the working.

The working is scratch paper. It is written in full before any report, so that every call in the report is copied from a line already settled. The company's own offers are listed family by family with one figure per offer: a line reading "two accepted at $X, one at $Y" is three offers, written X, X, Y, and the median is taken over all three. Each row gets one line: its kind, its month set against the first month in, then place, round, scope and basis, then the outcome. Under a family's rows comes the family's line: the funnel, the full offers and full ranges listed apart, the anchor, the market gap, the hiring check, the verdict and the tier.
