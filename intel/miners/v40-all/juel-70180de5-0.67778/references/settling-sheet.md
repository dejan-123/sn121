## What decides a line

- Kind, copied from the paste's own type column: an accepted offer or a posted range can count. Hearsay, a survey median, a contract rate, a grant or buy-out, or on-target pay with no split is out, and nothing after it matters.
- Date: decided by the timeline alone. Left of the floor, or undated, is out; the floor month and everything right of it is in, and any month of a later year is right of it.
- Place: the same metro, or remote pinned to it by the paste, is same. Remote with no city is near. Any other named city is out, however alike it feels.
- Stage: count the rounds between the two (Seed, A, B, C, D, E, F). 0 is same, 1 is near, 2 or more is out, and so is public. One round apart is never out on its own.
- Job: the same work and level, or a title the paste maps to it, is same. A neighbouring specialty or one level off is near.
- All same counts. One near, and the rest same, is partial. Two near, or any out, is dropped.
- A partial point joins no average, even when it is the only other figure. A range joins no anchor that has an offer in it.
- Two or more offers counted is High. One offer is Medium. No offer and two or more ranges that agree is Medium, the cap for ranges alone. No offer and one range is Low. Nothing counted is Deferred, and only that is.
- Own offers come from that family's own line. They are never counted, and they raise no tier. A declined offer is one the candidate turned down; the reason given, or what rivals were said to pay, is hearsay and is never set beside a gap.
- Every row of the paste is on one family's list: in = counted + partial + dropped.
