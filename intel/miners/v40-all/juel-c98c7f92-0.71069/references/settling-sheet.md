## What decides a line

- Each point's line opens with its own row's Title and Notes cells, copied as the paste prints them, never the family's name in their place; the job and posting tests read those copies.
- Kind, copied from the paste's own type column: an accepted offer or a posted range can count. Hearsay, a survey median, a contract rate, a grant or buy-out, or on-target pay with no split is out, and nothing after it matters.
- Date: decided by the timeline alone. Left of the floor, or undated, is out; the floor month and everything right of it is in, and any month of a later year is right of it.
- Place: the same metro, or remote pinned to it by the paste, is same. Remote with no city is near. Any other named city is out, never near, however alike it feels or whatever law made it post.
- Stage: count the rounds between the two (Seed, A, B, C, D, E, F). 0 is same, 1 is near, 2 or more is out, never near, and so is public. One round apart is never out on its own.
- Job: the same work and level, or a title the paste maps to it, is same. A neighbouring specialty or one level off is near. A title that leads with another job's name and adds the family's after a slash, in full or as a short form ('Account Executive / Senior Channel Sales', 'Account Executive / Channel'), is near too: the name before the slash is the posting's job, whatever follows it. Word order, a comma, or a short form standing alone as the whole title (Ops, Quant, PM, SRE) changes nothing.
- Posting, for ranges only, taken from the Posting line: a range a disclosure law made public, or one whose row says nothing of why it was posted, is same. A range the paste marks as voluntarily posted, in its own Notes cell or by a line printed above the table that marks every range volunteered save those whose rows say otherwise, is near: it was written to attract, and nothing holds the employer to it. An offer has no posting mark.
- Each line counts its near and its out marks apart. All same counts. One near, no out, is partial. Two near, or one out, is dropped; another city is one out on its own.
- A partial point joins no average, even when it is the only other figure. A range joins no anchor that has an offer in it. With one offer and one range counted, the anchor is that offer as it stands, with no sum at all, and the range's centre is written beside it as agreeing or not.
- Two or more offers counted is High. One offer is Medium. No offer and two or more ranges that agree is Medium, the cap for ranges alone. No offer and one range is Low. Nothing counted is Deferred, and only that is: one counted range keeps its Low verdict even with a partial offer beside it.
- Own offers come from that family's own line. They are never counted, and they raise no tier. A declined offer is one the candidate turned down; the reason given, or what rivals were said to pay, is hearsay. It is named as hearsay where it comes up and is never set beside a gap, never sized, never said to agree with one.
- Every row of the paste is on one family's list, the one the Rows line gave it by its title or the paste's scope note, wherever the row sits in the paste or the timeline; each family block works its own rows from that line and no others. A row struck on the timeline is worked, and dropped, only in its own family: retrieved = survived (the points that count) + partial + dropped.
