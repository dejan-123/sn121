# Edge-case rulings for evidence handling

Use these only after the main rules leave a point unsettled. Figures below are invented for illustration.

## Windows and dates
- Cutoff described as "older than N months, before Month Year": a point dated in that month is still in window; the month before is out.
- A point with no date, or only a year, cannot be placed in the window: drop it as undated.
- Retrieval date is not a point date. Never use the retrieval date to rescue an undated point.

## Geography
- A remote role anchored by the company to its headquarters metro is compared with that metro only.
- A remote role with no anchor is compared with national postings as Partial, and the caveat is raised once in the pool-wide section.
- Pay-transparency mandates explain why a range exists; they do not change whether the metro matches.

## Basis conversions (write direction every time)
- Total minus a stated bonus: 150,000 total including 12,000 bonus gives 138,000 base (down).
- OTE or total compensation with no split: exclude, do not guess a split.
- Range to midpoint: (low + high) / 2, shown as a conversion (neutral).
- Hourly or day rates: contractor engagements are excluded as a non-comparable basis even if an hours-per-year assumption looks obvious.
- Foreign-currency figures: convert only with a rate printed in the input; otherwise exclude and name the missing rate. A figure already in the band's currency needs no conversion even if the geography differs.

## Offers
- Accepted external offers are the strongest evidence. A declined or rescinded offer elsewhere is not market evidence of pay; drop it with that reason.
- The company's own accepted offers sitting above its band midpoint, or its declined offers citing higher competing pay, corroborate a lagging read; own offers inside the band corroborate competitive. They never replace the external anchor.

## Level and title mapping
- Treat a title variant as the same scope when the function is the same and the seniority word matches, or when the evidence note says the level maps across.
- Manager versus senior manager, senior versus staff or principal, individual contributor versus people manager: different level, Fail on scope.
- Families with two levels are two rows; do not pool figures across levels.

## Partial versus fail on stage
- Adjacent stages (one apart) are Partial. Two apart, or any private-versus-public pairing, are Fail.

## Deferral wording
- Name the gap precisely: the missing evidence type, stage, metro, window and count that would settle it, and say whether the gap is external evidence or internal data.

## Tiny pools
- One surviving Full offer gives Low confidence unless a Full posted range agrees, which gives Medium.
- Two Full offers that straddle a verdict threshold in opposite directions: defer and name the conflict.
