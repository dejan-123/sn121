**Pool.** 15 retrieved = 7 full + 3 partial + 5 out.

**Deferred verdicts.** These families are not called.
- UX Research — Manager: the only points are a Series A and a Series C range on either side of the band. A Columbus Series B accepted offer dated 2025-03 or later would settle it.
- Customer Support — Team Lead: one posted range and nothing else. An accepted offer at a Columbus Series B company, or the company's own next hire, would turn the 7.0% indication into a verdict.

**Signal caveats.** These hold across the pool and are not repeated in the rows.
- Security Engineering, Solutions Consulting and Data Platform Staff rest on accepted offers, revealed preference; Data Platform Senior and Payroll rest on one posted range each, an employer's aspirational figure, and only Payroll's range (P-11) was published under a pay-transparency rule.
- Most points come from one feed, the Paylane feed, so its coverage shapes every row.
- Pay arrives on four bases and in two currencies; only base in each band's own currency is compared.

## Before sending yours

Check the document against the working sheet, silently. Each verdict cell's arithmetic uses the reference and midpoint of its own row. Every point ID appears in exactly one funnel, out points included, and the Pool line adds to the pool. Partial points show their figure; no own offer sits inside a market reference. The own-offers cell writes out every offer, so "two at $176,300 and one at $175,300" is three. Each verdict word follows the sheet's Row line: 5% or more decides alone; 3% to 5% is a lean only with an own offer 5% or more above; "under" means the midpoint is below the reference. Every family the sheet marked DEFERRED is Deferred in the table and has a bullet, and what would settle it is an accepted offer, or a range only where a mandate is noted; no sentence names a tier that new evidence would reach. A range-only verdict with no own offers is Low, and High needs an accepted offer behind the reference. The Basis copies the sheet's three window slots and nothing more about dates. The caveats name families with verdicts only, mention a mandate only for a reference range whose note says so, and give no tally of points by type and no claim that notes are absent or voluntary.

Now send the document for the request in front of you. Its first characters are "## " followed by the company's name. It has no line before the title, no horizontal rules, no working, and nothing about this skill, its references or these checks.
