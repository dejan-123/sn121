# From the audit to the page

The audit is where a call is made; the page is where it is shown, so the page carries the calls and not the working. A reader who wants the working asks for it.

A family's own accepted offers are evidence about that family's band only. A hire into one job says nothing about what another job's band can attract, and a declined offer is the end of a negotiation, not a price, so neither enters another family's reading. What a candidate said when declining is hearsay: it is named in the own-offers bullet and confirms no verdict. What the company's own hires can show is narrow: a mean that sits close to the band midpoint says candidates take that midpoint, one that sits well above it says the company is already paying past it to hire, and anything between says little either way, so the outside margin stands. Close means a mean no more than 2.5% from the midpoint: one 3.1% above it is not close, whatever the outside gap, and the 5% margin decides; well above means more than 4% on the anchor's side, and a mean exactly 4.0% above is not more than 4%.

Confidence measures how much independent evidence a call rests on. A single advertised range is one employer's asking position, two or more are a pattern of asking positions, one accepted offer is one cleared price and two are a price confirmed. So the tier is read from the kept offers first: two or more kept offers are High; one kept offer is Medium, with or without a posted range beside it, since a range is not a second offer; with no kept offer, two or more kept ranges are Medium and one is Low. Own offers never raise a tier, and neither does a partial point: a family whose kept points are a single posted range reads Low on the page whatever own offer or partial point stands beside it, even where the audit said more. A snapshot line that lists an accepted offer beside a declined one still counts the accepted one. One posted range holds its Low verdict when anything stands beside it: count the family's partial points and the company's own accepted offers for it, and a total of 1 or more holds the verdict, whatever the verdict is; a total of 0 makes the family Deferred, direction stated, even where the gap sits inside the margin. A band is never called Above-market on posted ranges alone: that call needs a kept offer, and without one the family is Deferred with the direction stated, its row giving that as the reason (an Above-market reading on posted ranges alone, so it drops) whatever partial points stand beside the range.

A family is named on the page as the snapshot names it, with its level, because the band belongs to that family and level and not to any job title in the evidence. The funnel shows each point's fate in a few words, and its counts are counts of the IDs it lists. A kept range keeps the posting basis the input gives it, under a mandate or by choice, whether a row's note or a line above the table says so, and the caveats name which kept ranges are which. Where a rule here changes a call the audit made, change it: that family's cells on the page carry the new call and its reason, what held or what dropped, and the change is made in the cells themselves. The page begins at its title, with nothing above it.
