## Last look before sending

- Every family and level in the snapshot has a table row, deferred or not; no row names a person. A family the snapshot lists as an open role carries "(open P1)" or "(open P2)" after its name, and no other family does.
- Each funnel names every row filed to that family once, by ID, with the reason for each drop and the miss for each partial; a row listed away from its family, the last row of the table included, still sits in its own family's funnel and in no other; the Count line adds up to the number of rows in the input.
- Another named city, a remote or national figure with no metro, one round apart, one level off, a title on the working's Slash titles line and a voluntary posting are each one partial test. One of them makes a row partial; two of them, or any mismatch, drop it.
- Each range's posting word comes from its own row's note, read again for the last rows of the table: "voluntarily posted" is a partial test, a remark that the state has no mandate included; a mandated range stands; a blank note stands unless a line over the table says the ranges were posted by choice.
- A full range beside an offer anchor is still full: it stays in the funnel's full count, is quoted beside the anchor and is kept out of the sum and the tier.
- Every anchor names its outside rows by ID: offers alone where any full offer exists; no own offer, partial or range midpoint inside an offer average.
- Every gap is copied from the working as its two steps, the difference and then the division by the anchor, with the working's own percentage; nothing is worked again in the report. Its sign matches the verdict: a market above the midpoint is a positive gap and means lagging past +3.0%. "Close" stands beside a verdict only when the gap's size is between 2.0% and 4.0%.
- Own hires use the family's own accepted offers, copied from its own line, every offer counted ("two at $X" is two figures); a family with no line reads "None listed". They agree with the verdict or trouble it, and they never change it.
- Every tier counts fully comparable outside rows only: two or more offers, High; one offer, Medium, whatever ranges stand beside it (one offer and one range is Medium, never High); two or more ranges and no offer, Medium under the posted-range cap; one range alone, Low, also when the company's own hires or a partial offer point the same way. A family with partial rows only is deferred, open role or not.
- `## Deferred verdicts` is always there. With no family deferred it says so in one line and names the verdict that rests on least and what would firm it.
- `## Gaps in the internal data` names only what the snapshot in hand leaves out: a family with no band, roles said to be remote and unanchored without being named, families with no own offer on record, a second currency with no rate. About remote roles it says only what the snapshot says; where the snapshot pins them to a tier or says nothing of them, there is no remote gap to name.
- Pool-wide skews are said once, under signal caveats; the closing paragraph repeats figures from the table and adds none; the last line names the highest figure on a dropped row and why it does not price the job.
