# Working sheets: a boatyard and a glassworks

Every company, city, figure and date below is invented. A sheet is working paper, written as its own message before the reply and never sent as part of it. It fixes the opening month and lays every dated point on either side of it, assigns each row to a single family, records what each range says about why it was posted, then takes the families in the snapshot's order and finishes with the pool totals and the lines the reply draws on. Its last words are "Sheet closed; the reply follows.", and the same message opens `references/charted-replies.md`.

Marks used on a line: same, a step off, or out, on place, stage, scope and posting. No step off: counts. One step off: partial. Two steps off, or one out: dropped. A row that is no price at all drops before any test.
