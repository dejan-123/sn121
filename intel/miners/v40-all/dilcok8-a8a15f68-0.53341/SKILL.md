# Connect a pay comparison to the decision it supports

Separate midpoint position, band capacity and evidence limits. A band may reach a reference while its midpoint lags; a successful hire does not establish the market rate.

## Establish the deliverable

Check scope before calculating. Individual salary decisions, new level structures, pay-equity audits and salary searches without classified outside data need different work. For a wholly unsupported request, open `references/handoff.md` and give a useful return note; do not manufacture a benchmark. For mixed requests, complete the supported comparison and route the other parts separately.

Create one comparison unit per family, level and pay zone. Preserve currency, band minimum/midpoint/maximum, headcount and hiring priority. State the window and assessment date; label using retrieval as the assessment date as a choice. Limit headquarters-based comparisons to that pay zone; keep unmapped remote populations separate.

## Assemble an evidence ledger

Account for every external ID. Assign the family before testing fit; wrong-level observations remain in that family's retrieved count. Keep unassignable records separate. Record four scores, usable pay basis, disposition and reason.

Score geography / company stage / role scope / source type, in that order:

| Score | Geography | Stage | Scope | Source type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Same metro or expressly shared pay zone | Same stage | Equivalent duties and level | Identifiable employer range or documented offer |
| 1 | Broad region or unanchored remote market | One adjacent funding stage | Limited duty overlap | Dated secondary source with a stated method |
| 0 | Different market without a shared pay anchor | More distant stage or public/private mismatch | Different work or seniority | Untraceable claim or methodless aggregate |

Use `?` for an unresolved essential fact. Explicit mappings outrank title wording. Accept expressly classified postings/offers as their stated source types unless notes contradict them; missing employer names limit independence rather than invalidate that classification. Disclose material assumptions.

Apply date, event and compensation checks in addition to those four scores:

- Use the observation date, not merely the retrieval date. Exclude missing dates, stale records and dates outside the stated window. If only months *before* a named month are excluded, that month remains eligible. A genuinely ambiguous boundary needs an explicit assumption or a conditional result.
- Merge confirmed copies of one event, retaining the best documented original and breaking equal ties by ID. Count copies as drops linked to that ID. Equal amounts alone do not prove duplication. Shared employers limit independence even when events differ.
- Compare recurring employee base. Contractor invoices, retention awards, acquisition payments and inseparable cash/equity packages cannot supply that measure. A separately identified ordinary base component can qualify. A label such as “base” does not cure contradictory terms. An annualized contractor amount remains contractor pay.

Disposition: **F** means all four scores are 2 and the additional checks pass; **P** means a limited match survives those checks, with no 0 or essential unknown; **D** covers a mismatch, unresolved essential fact or failed additional check. Partials give context only. They supply neither anchor values nor a higher confidence tier.

For each family state retrieved R, full F, partial P and dropped D, with R = F + P + D. If reporting “survived,” define it as F + P and distinguish the anchor subset. Give each drop one primary reason so reasons sum to D; secondary flags can explain the same record. Own hiring outcomes never enter this external ledger.

## Put comparable amounts on one basis

Normalize to the comparison unit's annual employee base and currency before selecting an anchor. Carry the original figure, equation and result in the evidence note. Preserve both ends of a range. Apply only supplied factors and pay definitions:

- Monthly base times stated annual pay periods; hourly employee base times stated annual paid hours. No assumed work-year factor.
- Total cash less a separately stated annual non-base amount. If bonus is fraction r of base, base = total cash / (1 + r); if r is of total cash, base = total cash × (1 - r). An unspecified split, denominator or period remains unresolved.
- With target-currency units per source unit, multiply; with source units per target unit, divide. Currency conversion does not repair a geography mismatch.

Do not present a computable but excluded amount as an eligible anchor. Check units, sign and range ordering. Keep exact values for decisions; round only the display.

## Make the comparison, then explain its reach

Use any explicit assessment policy in the request first. Otherwise use the following declared analytical conventions; they are not universal compensation standards.

**Anchor A.** When F includes offers, use the median of those offer bases. Separate accepted from documented declined offers in the explanation: a declined offer establishes an amount offered, not an amount accepted or paid. Include a documented declined offer as an offer made unless the supplied policy restricts the anchor to accepted offers. When no full offer survives, use the median of the full employer ranges' midpoints, retaining the original ranges as context. A partial offer cannot displace full postings. Neither a disclosure-mandate label nor its absence independently changes comparability.

Show anchor IDs and calculation once; average the two middle values for an even count. Do not mix offers with postings. Posting-only anchors describe advertised pay, not paid-market percentiles. No defensible A means Deferred position. Conflicting partials cannot create a full comparison. Split distinct pay populations; unresolved full-pool mismatches require a narrower or deferred claim.

**Midpoint position.** Use supplied midpoint M; if absent, identify any mean of valid band endpoints as a calculated proxy. For positive A, signed gap = 100 × (M - A) / A. Default tolerance is -5% through +5%, inclusive: below is **Lagging**, within is **Competitive**, above is **Above-market**. Use unrounded amounts at boundaries. A request's asymmetric limits replace this default. A missing internal basis may permit an external evidence summary but requires **Deferred** position.

**Band capacity.** If valid endpoints exist, turn the same tolerance into an amount interval C. For limits l and u expressed as fractions, C = [(1+l)A, (1+u)A]. Intersect C with internal band [Bmin,Bmax]. Report the overlap when useful; if empty, state whether the entire band is below or above C, and the distance to the nearer edge. A shared endpoint counts as overlap. This is capacity under the declared comparison rule, not an instruction to set salaries, a confidence interval or evidence that a hire will accept. It never changes the midpoint label. If endpoints or policy are missing or invalid, omit only this diagnostic. Open `references/decision-boundaries.md` when a boundary, conversion or midpoint/capacity distinction needs checking.

**Confidence.** High requires several recent, independent full offers with secure role/basis mapping and coherent corroboration. Thin but usable offer evidence is normally Medium; unknown independence caps it at Medium. Posting-only evidence is at most Medium, and a single posting is Low. Respect stricter supplied rules. State the deciding reason for each tier. An unsupported conclusion is Deferred. Confidence measures support for the claim actually made, not the importance of the role.

**Hiring interpretation.** Put relevant internal accepts or declines beside that family's finding. Preserve counts and amounts. Pay-citing declines can indicate friction; unexplained declines cannot be assigned a pay cause. An accepted offer above the midpoint can show placement within the current band, not a new external market anchor. Neither own offers nor partial records change the tolerance, anchor or external confidence. Keep priorities tied to the supplied hiring plan, not to invented headcount economics.

## Deliver a compact, traceable answer

Lead with the relevant decision. A family table contains internal band/midpoint, external funnel, anchor/basis, signed gap, position and confidence reason. Attach hiring facts to the affected finding. Explain band capacity where it changes the practical reading, especially a lagging midpoint with usable band room or a band wholly outside the interval. Show every family's capacity only if requested; avoid a second family-wide table for routine overlap. Identify missing data.

Follow with a compact ID audit: four scores, disposition, decisive reason, and any normalization equation. Group IDs only when scores and use match. For unchanged pay figures, refer to the anchor calculation instead of copying all input amounts again. Every ID stays visible; neither a second full ledger nor a repeated sorted series is needed.

Deferred verdicts name the missing observation or mapping and what it would unlock. If none, say so briefly. State relevant selection, dependence, advertised-pay and pay-zone caveats once. Explain exclusions without repeating shared warnings in family rows.

Reconcile IDs and anchor membership. Check gap signs and unrounded boundaries; hiring context stays separate from market evidence. Remove repetition that does not change the decision. Adapt the opening and layout to the request without a fixed word target.
