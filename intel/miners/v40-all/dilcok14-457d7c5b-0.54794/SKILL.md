# Decide one pay segment at a time

Benchmark supplied family bands and classified evidence. Redirect individual pay, raw salary research, band design and pay equity to the appropriate workflow. Give the boundary, next workflow and missing inputs in two to four sentences, at most 120 words, without a table. Keep incomplete families in a valid benchmark.

Roster every requested family, level and pay zone. Separate materially different levels. Preserve band endpoints, midpoint, currency, stage, priorities and assessment date. Use explicit pay anchors. Make HQ proxies conditional. Defer unmapped remote coverage pending role, band and pay-zone mapping. FX supplies no geographic equivalence.

Preserve retrieval assignments before filtering. Assign other records only when supported. Count unassigned IDs with reasons. Explain reuse and count each ID/segment once. Reconcile unique assigned and unassigned IDs separately from overlapping memberships. Reuse adds no independence.

Apply explicit policy at each branch and in verification, subject to structural exclusions, eligible offer priority and other fixed requirements. Name any exception. Admission preserves class. Invent no pay, dates, rates or source facts.

## Walk the six branches for each roster entry

Assess internal readiness, then audit the segment once even if blocked. Use that audit for the remaining branches. Name the first blocker and needed input. Keep the full audit and survivors, then continue the roster.

1. **Internal comparison.** Establish the pay anchor and normalize internal pay as below. Use the supplied midpoint or label the mean of stated endpoints. Name any missing internal pay or anchor fact.
2. **Taxonomy and timing.** Look for a canonical record passing the active taxonomy and window independently of pay. Explicit compatible admission can pass specified partials provisionally. If none passes, name the missing attribute or timing requirement. F = 0 alone does not locate this blocker.
3. **Employee-base eligibility.** Require one passing record with supported annual employee base. Name the missing rate, terms or component, or incompatible structure, if all fail.
4. **Source selection.** Median admitted actual offers first, including documented declined offers with status preserved. Eligible postings remain context. Otherwise median eligible postings using annual fixed amounts or range midpoints. Keep points and endpoints visible. Average the middle pair for even counts. Label posted-only results provisional advertised pay. Defer if neither source supplies an anchor.
5. **Position and defensibility.** For positive anchor A and internal midpoint M, gap = 100 × (M − A) ÷ A. Back-check that gap × A ÷ 100 recovers M − A. Default materiality is competitive from −5% through +5% inclusive, lagging below and above-market above. For active percentage bounds L and U, compare M to A × (1 + L/100) and A × (1 + U/100) with the supplied boundary inclusion. Classify unrounded. Keep a defensible offer result and qualify conflicting context. Recheck actual scope, date, origin or basis contradictions. Defer an indefensible position with its specific conflict. Dispersion alone is insufficient.
6. **Confidence.** State High, Medium, Low or Deferred and the deciding reason. High requires multiple recent independent full offers, secure basis and coherent corroboration. Thin eligible offers normally support Medium. Unknown employer independence caps at Medium. Posting-only caps at Medium and a single posting is Low. Weakness or conflicting context can justify a lower supported tier. Use the compatible limit for admitted partials, defaulting to Low. A company label does not override evidence-based limits. Deferred means an unsupported position.

## Prepare the segment's evidence audit once

Track ID, origin, date, scores, timing, admission, pay checks, disposition and use. Internal hiring suggests pressure without establishing rival pay. Keep it out of external counts and anchors.

Use G/S/R/T for geography, funding stage, role scope and evidence type. Score 2 match, 1 limited, 0 mismatch or ? unresolved. Assess axes independently of pay, even for dropped rows. Honor supplied role-description mappings of scope and level. A common grade alone cannot override conflicting duties.

| Dimension | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Same metro or explicit shared pay zone | Broader market or unanchored remote | Other market without a shared anchor |
| Stage | Same stage | One adjacent stage | Further removed or private/public mismatch |
| Scope | Same responsibilities and level or explicit equivalent | Limited responsibility or level overlap | Materially different work or level |
| Type | Identifiable offer or primary employer posting | Identified, dated secondary evidence with a method | Known hearsay or survey lacking attribution or method |

Full requires four 2s. Any 0 is incomparable. Without a 0, essential ? is unresolved. Unknown evidence type is ?, including for unassigned rows. Limited scores with no mismatch or essential unknown are partial. Apply compatible policy while preserving partial scores when the exception concerns admission. Partials otherwise serve as context only and cannot raise confidence. Treat provider and mandate labels as evidence of selection, with independence requiring its own support.

Keep observation date, as-of date and lookback window distinct. A dated snapshot may establish as-of only. Respect inclusive boundaries. Drop undated events and events outside supplied timing limits. By author default, if a minimum is unspecified and not required, retain dated records with appropriate age caveats. If required freshness is unresolved, drop on that basis and name the date or policy needed. Never infer staleness from a missing cutoff or use today's date.

For a confirmed identical event, keep its documented canonical record, event date and acceptance status. Resolve equivalent ties by stable ID. Drop copies and link their canonical ID. Distinct events from the same employer can survive while sharing one employer origin.

Exclude contractors, one-time grants, retention or acquisition packages and inseparable mixes. Retain a separately stated ordinary employee base when otherwise eligible. Detailed terms control ambiguous labels. Inseparable OTE has no reliable base. Annualizing contractor pay cannot repair structure.

Normalize both sides to comparable annual recurring employee base, currency and applicable workload or gross/net basis. Require supported terms and factors, positive anchors and ordered bounds. Preserve both range endpoints and require documented relationships between range-valued components.

- Multiply monthly pay by supplied annual payments and eligible employee hourly pay by supplied paid yearly hours. Use only supplied workload adjustments.
- Subtract a documented non-base amount from a compatible total. For bonus fraction r of base divide total cash by (1 + r). For r of total cash multiply by (1 − r). Require the denominator and split.
- Multiply by target currency units per source unit or divide by source units per target unit. Use supplied FX only. Carry through an already comparable amount.

Record the original figure, terms, operation and units, and result for genuine conversions. Drop a record with unresolved essential data and state the unknown. Keep unknowns distinct from confirmed mismatches.

For each segment R counts assigned external records before filtering, including copies. F counts eligible full after all checks, P retained eligible partial, and D dropped. F postings kept as context stay F. Explicitly admitted eligible partial anchors stay P. Give each D one primary reason and keep other material flags. Check R = F + P + D and S = F + P, with primary reason counts summing to D. Keep the timing/taxonomy result separate from the final disposition so the branch walk can locate the blocker.

## Join the decision and its audit

State shared basis, window and tolerance once. List every segment with internal band or midpoint, R/F/P/D/S, selected source, numeric anchor and IDs, signed gap, verdict and confidence reason. Show nontrivial aggregation and conversions once. Include informative overlap only. Advertised ranges describe advertised pay and supply no paid-market percentiles.

Give an audit accounting for every ID with its segment, scores, relevant date and basis findings, disposition, reason and anchor/context/excluded use. Group only equivalent vectors, dispositions, reasons and uses and keep each ID traceable. Reconcile unique IDs separately only when needed for shared or unmatched records. Use Deferred once in blocked rows as their verdict and tier. Put their first blocker and next input only in the deferred list. If none, say none. Include one brief limitations paragraph grounded in actual origin, freshness, conflict or coverage.

Check roster coverage, the active source and admission policy, reconciled counts, signed arithmetic, named caps and the complete audit for blocked segments. Keep reverse checks and the branch walk out of the final text unless they clarify an exception or boundary. Avoid repeated conversions, anchor calculations and count tables.
