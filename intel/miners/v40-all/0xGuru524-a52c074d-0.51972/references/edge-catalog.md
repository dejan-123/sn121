# Edge catalog - the hard single calls, each with its strict label

Invented one-line cases. Each is the kind of point that looks like one thing and is read as another. I take the discipline, not the numbers.

## Comparability - what counts as Full, Partial, Incomparable

- A reworded or bundled title that names the same role at the same level ("Senior Platform Engineer, also owns the build system") is **scope Full**, not a mismatch - the duties map to the level.
- A posting in an adjacent cost tier (a tier-2 metro one step from the internal tier-2 anchor, or a nearby smaller metro) is **geo Partial** - it still counts, at capped confidence.
- A remote-national figure against a metro-anchored internal role is **geo Partial**, never Full.
- A coastal tier-1 metro against an inland tier-2 metro is **geo Incomparable** - drop; it inflates the market centre if admitted.
- One funding stage apart is **stage Partial**; two or more apart, or a public/post-IPO company against a private one, is **stage Incomparable** - drop.
- A figure in another currency with a supplied conversion rate is usable; **with no rate it is Incomparable** - drop, never invent a rate.

## Type - the weight ceiling

- An accepted offer is revealed pay: full weight.
- A posted range is an aspiration: admissible, but it caps the family at **Low** confidence.
- Recruiter-reported or "verbally shared" figures are **hearsay** - too weak to carry a verdict, drop.
- A survey median with no date or no methodology is **too weak** - drop; a dated, method-stated survey can corroborate but not lead.

## Exclusions - the figure itself cannot carry base

- Retention grant, sign-on bonus, acquisition or merger package: **no isolable base** - drop.
- An accepted figure given as a package "including an unspecified sign-on": recurring base cannot be separated - **drop**, do not guess the split.
- Equity-heavy or cash-heavy total comp with no stated base: **drop**.
- OTE or total comp with no base split: **drop** - do not invent a base.
- Contractor day-rate or hourly rate: **non-FTE** - drop, do not annualise.
- Out of window (older than the stated window) or undated: **drop**, and count it.

## Conversions - show the arithmetic and the direction

- "216,000 total comp, includes 26,000 bonus" -> **216,000 - 26,000 = 190,000 base (down)**; compare 190k, not 216k.
- "Base 182,000 plus a separate 20,000 bonus" -> base is already stated: **use 182,000**, the bonus is not added.
- "245,000 OTE, no base split" -> **cannot isolate base, dropped**.
- "GBP 110,000 base, no USD rate supplied" -> **dropped, no invented rate**.

## The verdict, at the boundary

- Two or more fully-comparable accepted offers that clear band max -> **Lagging** (High if they agree). The band cannot reach market.
- Surviving range centres inside the band, but a candidate declined band-level pay citing higher competing comp -> **Lagging** - the decline outweighs the within-band postings.
- A single posting whose top pokes past band max, with no accepted offer and no decline -> **Competitive, Low** - a posting is not revealed pay; a within-band posting alone is never lagging.
- Surviving market centre below band min -> **Above-market**.
- A market figure a few points above an internal offer, both inside the band -> **Competitive** - a small in-band gap is not lagging.

## Confidence and the narrow door to a hold

- Two or more fully-comparable accepted offers agree -> **High**.
- One fully-comparable accepted offer -> **Medium**.
- Only posted ranges survive, or the call leans on a lone accepted offer that is a one-dimension partial -> **Low**.
- A single fully-comparable posted range is a **Low** call, not a hold.
- An adjacent-metro or one-stage-off **accepted offer still anchors a call** (Competitive/Low or Lagging/Low), naming the soft dimension - it does not hold.
- **Hold only when nothing anchors a call:** survivors are conflicting posted-range partials, everything lands two or more dimensions off, or no point maps to the family. Name the gap and the one figure that would close it.

## The declined offer

A declined offer is never a survivor and never sits in the funnel's survived list. It is kept aside as market pressure: it can turn a within-band read into **Lagging**, and it belongs in the signal caveats, not in the comparable set.
