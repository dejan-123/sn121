## Why it reads this way

- The screen is the only working in the reply and the only place exclusions appear: one line per point with its class and every failing reason, then a tier test that reads the counts off those lines. The lead, the table and the sections below are read from it and never walk the list again.
- The screen opens on this company's stage, pay anchor, window and count; the lead opens on its most urgent fact with its number, so a different roster opens on its own priority role.
- Route optimization reads lagging at -7.5%, past the 5% band, and because the max still clears the only comparable offer the verdict adds competitive at max. Had a comparable offer sat at or above $183.2k, the band could not hire at market and that fact would lead.
- Sales at +5.9% sits just past 5%, so it reads above-market: the band runs a little rich against one converted offer, a cost to note rather than an alarm. Every gap divides by the market reference, the yardstick the band is read against.
- Partials (E-08 a level up, E-14 a round above) are counted in the funnel and set aside: they never anchor and never move a tier. E-04, two rounds away, is dropped outright.
- Toronto at -4.7% would read competitive on the number alone; its two own hires above the midpoint show recruiters already paying past it, so a gap that close to the line reads lagging. Sales operations sits as close, -4.8% with its own hire above the midpoint, yet stays competitive: its anchor is a posted range, and an advertisement cannot carry a near-line lagging call. With no own offers, the same -4.7% would stay competitive too.
- Field implementation and Toronto both keep comparable points, but only Toronto has an offer, while a lone voluntary range with nothing to test it reads Deferred however clean it looks; posted under a pay-transparency mandate, the same range would carry a Low call under the cap. Had Larkspur made one field implementation offer of its own, that row would read Low, as a lean (competitive, leaning behind) rather than a flat verdict.
- Payroll and field implementation each hold one comparable range and no offer. Payroll's range sits between a Series B partial that reads lower and a Series D partial that reads higher, confirmed from both sides, so it reads Low with the cap in its verdict; field implementation's one partial is a level up on one side, which brackets nothing, so it stays Deferred. Two off-stage partials with no comparable range between them also stay Deferred: there is nothing to bracket. Another metro or a national range is never partial, so it can never form one side of a bracket.
- Own offers test an anchor but are never a second external point: one comparable offer plus own offers and no comparable range would read Medium, not High.
- A point from the cut-off month itself (E-07, 2028-04) is out: the window counts from the retrieval day, and a month-only date cannot show it falls after that day.
- Each conversion is written once, in the screen, with its direction.
