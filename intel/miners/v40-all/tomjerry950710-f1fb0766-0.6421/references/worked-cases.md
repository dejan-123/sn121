## Case 3: a request this skill should not process

Ridgeback Outdoor Gear's HR lead writes: "Maya in design has an outside offer. Should we give her 9% to keep her? Also, please add a fourth level to our design band. Here are our current salaries." No evidence table is attached. No sheet is needed; the whole reply is this note.

# Ridgeback Outdoor Gear: not a benchmark request

This request asks for two things that a role-family benchmark does not produce:

1. **Countering one person's outside offer.** That is an individual pay decision for the manager and the HR business partner.
2. **Adding a band level.** That is band design, owned by the compensation lead. A benchmark can inform it later; it does not draw it.

A salary roster cannot show where the market sits. With no classified outside evidence there is nothing to screen, so any verdict now would be invented.

To get a benchmark of the design family, send:
- the outside points, each with ID, source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, title, location, stage and figure;
- for each family and level, the band minimum, midpoint and maximum (role rows, not people);
- any offers made in the last year, whether taken or turned down;
- how far back the evidence may reach.

With that in hand, every family gets a competitive-position call, the count of evidence kept and dropped, and how sure the call is, or a clear statement of what is still missing. Named salaries are best left out of anything that gets circulated.
