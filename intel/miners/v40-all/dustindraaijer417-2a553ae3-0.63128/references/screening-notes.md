# Screening notes, with a worked case (invented company: copy the method, never the figures)

The notes are the working sheet behind a benchmark: the frame, one line per point with its result, one line per family with its funnel, anchor, gap, verdict and tier, then the pool totals, the company's own offers as context and the pool-wide caveats. They are written as plain text in a message of their own, before the document.
