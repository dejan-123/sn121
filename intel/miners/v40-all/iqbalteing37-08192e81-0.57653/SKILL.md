# Benchmark position and feasible hiring pay

Decide midpoint position first. Then find the band salaries that qualify under the same benchmark, and test dependence on anchor contributors.

## Scope what can be decided

Complete supported benchmarks in mixed work. A roster alone establishes role and level coverage and leaves market and individual pay verdicts unresolved. Use brief prose for a roster handoff. State coverage, blockers and what the deadline permits given the missing data. Route each ask directly. Send raw research to data collection, individual pay to HR Compensation, equity to confidential review and redesign to job leveling. Request the shared inputs once, including segment bands and external IDs, sources, types, dates, roles/levels, markets, stages, amounts/ranges, currencies and pay bases. Omit identifiable salaries and benchmarking procedure from the handoff.

Benchmark each family × level × pay zone separately. Keep a compatible supplied midpoint or derive it from valid endpoints and label it. Honor documented mappings, retrieval attribution and level separation. Keep near-misses in their assigned pool. Verify market fit and band applicability separately. Use explicit pay anchors, condition HQ proxy comparisons on applicability, and defer unmapped remote populations. FX supplies no market match.

## Build one auditable market comparison per segment

Score all four dimensions for each ID, even for separate date, basis or copy drops. Use `?` for unresolved facts.

| Dimension | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Same market or supported common pay anchor | Broader or unanchored remote market | Different market without supported equivalence |
| Stage | Same funding stage | Adjacent stage | Further-away stage or private/public mismatch |
| Role | Equivalent scope and level, including supplied mapping | Limited overlap | Different duties or level |
| Type | Documented actual offer or primary employer posting | Dated attributed secondary data with method | Established hearsay or methodless or unattributed survey |

Missing employer identity leaves a documented type intact. Titles or grades cannot override conflicting duties. Use F for four matches on eligible records, P for eligible limited matches, and D for a mismatch, essential unknown or separate exclusion. P is context unless compatible instructions allow provisional use. State that class and cap. Infer no missing inputs.

Apply the supplied window to observation dates. An as-of date supplies no window. Include the cutoff month when only earlier months are excluded. Drop stale or undated rows and unresolved required timing. Without a required or supplied window retain eligible dated evidence with an age caveat. Drop confirmed copies, retaining the best-documented original event with date and status. Break equivalent ties by stable ID. Distinct events can share an origin.

Compare annual employee base only. Terms control labels. Exclude contractor fees, one-time grants, acquisition or retention pay and inseparable OTE or cash/equity. Keep separately stated or recoverable base. Normalize both sides using supplied components, workload and rates. Multiply monthly pay by annual payments or eligible hourly pay by annual paid hours. Subtract known non-base amounts. Divide total cash by (1+r) for bonus r as a fraction of base, or multiply by (1−r) for r as a fraction of total. Require the denominator. Multiply by target per source currency or divide by source per target. Show operands, direction and result once. Preserve usable native facts and same-currency fallback when FX is missing.

R includes all retrieved rows and copies. Reconcile R = F + P + D and S = F + P. S includes context. Give each D one primary reason and uncounted secondary flags. Distinguish unique inputs, memberships and unassigned IDs. Full postings stay F even as context. Internal hiring outcomes and reported rival pay stay outside external counts and anchors. Infer no competing pay.

Anchor on the median of canonical full actual offers, including documented candidate-declined offers. Otherwise median the eligible posting values, one fixed salary or normalized midpoint per record. Average the central pair when even. Retain status, normalized endpoints and class. Keep offers and postings separate. Posting anchors establish advertised base only. If position is blocked, retain supported external facts and name the missing input.

## Check the midpoint and which band salaries qualify

With comparable basis and positive anchor A, show midpoint M − A and signed percent 100 × (M − A) / A. Default competitive limits are ±5% inclusive, lagging below and above-market above. Apply compatible supplied L% and U% and inclusion rules. Competitive pay spans A × (1+L/100) to A × (1+U/100). Classify before rounding and show the deciding margin near a boundary.

Intersect the policy interval with valid comparable band endpoints. Use the higher lower bound and lower upper bound. Equal bounds admit one salary only if both sides include it. If disjoint, identify the binding ceiling or floor and quantify the distance or strict inequality. Missing or inconsistent endpoints leave whole-band feasibility unresolved without blocking a supported midpoint. For hiring, distinguish an attainable competitive slice from a binding band limit. Scope to source class and geography. An intersection describes benchmark feasibility, without asserting approval or acceptance.

Ordinary High needs multiple recent independent full offers, corroboration and secure basis. Thin offers normally get Medium. Unknown independence and posting-only cap at Medium, one posting at Low. Use Low for weak defensible comparisons and Deferred with a blocker for unsupported positions. Recheck contradicted premises.

## Test dependence on verified contributing employers

Freeze the full result. Group only its canonical same-class contributors by verified employer or documented shared origin. Platforms and separate events prove no independence. Use all anchor records for shares and only verified minimum shares if grouping is incomplete.

Run removals only for supported comparisons with complete verified grouping, at least three contributing origins and at least two left after each omission. Test all when three to six qualify. Above six, select the largest count, lowest group median and highest group median, resolving ties by ascending stable origin ID, or a verified stable name. Deduplicate those choices and fill to six by descending count and ascending stable ID. State how many were tested and identify untested groups.

For each chosen origin, remove its entire group from the original pool. Recompute the same-class median, signed gap, midpoint category and policy pay interval. Keep partial context, other source classes and copies out of the recalculation. Track which removals change the midpoint category and which change whether any band pay qualifies. One kind of change can occur without the other.

When band endpoints are usable, also intersect the approved band with the full-pool policy interval and every tested policy interval. Use the greatest lower bound and least upper bound and retain the strictest inclusion condition at ties. Each omission may allow some band pay while the shared intersection is empty. Identify the origins setting the binding limits if they matter to the decision. Give the common in-band range or say no single salary meets every tested case when hiring flexibility is requested or the change matters. Limit this result to the full pool and tested single-origin omissions.

Keep the full-pool midpoint verdict. If an omission changes that category, reduce ordinary confidence once, High to Medium or Medium to Low. Low is the floor. A band-feasibility change alone leaves the midpoint confidence tier intact. Follow compatible explicit sensitivity rules and apply any final governing caps after the adjustment. Skips and stable tests give no upgrade. With insufficient origin evidence, keep the ordinary benchmark and state shared limitations once. Claim no untested stability.

## Deliver the benchmark

Lead with the consequential hiring result or blocker. In the segment table give internal basis, R/F/P/D/S, anchor and IDs, signed gap, scoped position and confidence reason. Verify any stated distance to a policy bound by adding the shortfall or subtracting the excess to recover the bound. Put geographic conditions beside verdicts. Explain meaningful offer and posting overlap or divergence without merging them. Separate internal hiring facts. If the input marks postings as voluntary or mandate-driven, name that disclosure basis once for the affected market and explain what its advertised sample covers.

Audit every external ID with four scores, disposition and decisive evidence. Group only traceable equivalents. Name exact deferral inputs. Show consequential band feasibility. For a material omission give removed origin and IDs, origins remaining, revised anchor, gap, class, changed pay limit and any confidence effect. Summarize stable tests briefly.
