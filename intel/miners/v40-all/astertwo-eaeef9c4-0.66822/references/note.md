# The note, when the request is not a benchmark (an invented case; take the form, never a fact)

Carrow Glass, its people and its figures are made up and belong in no real reply.

What the head of people pasted:

"Carrow Glass here (Series B, 120 people, Boise ID). Reviews are next month. Is Nadia Frost underpaid? And what do furnace engineers earn these days, and could you cut our four engineering levels to three? We have no market data sorted yet, just this: Nadia Frost, Furnace Engineer, $97,500; Elias Boone, Furnace Engineer, $101,500; Greta Lind, Finishing Technician, $72,500. The directors want it by Wednesday. What can you do for us?"

The message that went back, from its title to its last line:

# Carrow Glass: not a benchmark request

This redirects your request: it asks for four pieces of work, and none of them is a role-family benchmark.

Whether Nadia Frost is underpaid is an individual pay decision, for the manager and people lead who own it, with the band in front of them. What furnace engineers earn is a market salary lookup, for a salary survey or pay-data provider. Cutting four levels to three is a band design, for whoever owns your job architecture, and it is better settled after a benchmark. The analysis the directors want by Wednesday waits for evidence that is not here yet.

A benchmark sets a band against classified outside points, and there are none in this paste. Three salaries are not bands, and they neither confirm nor rebut that Nadia is underpaid; any market figure given now would be a guess.

To run one, send: the outside points with ID, source, type (accepted offers apart from posted ranges), date, role and level, place, company stage, and the figure with its basis (base, total or on-target); a snapshot by role family and level with headcount and band minimum, midpoint and maximum, in place of names; recent offers, accepted and declined; and the data window. Each family then comes back with a verdict, an evidence funnel and a High, Medium or Low confidence, or deferred, with accepted offers (revealed preference) weighed before posted ranges (aspirational). For Wednesday, start with Boise points at Series B, accepted offers first, since those are the ones that will count; if they are not classified by then, tell the directors so.

Keep the three named salaries out of any shared document.

---

The next turn is the real request's note itself and nothing else, in this form: its title, the line that opens "This redirects your request:", each ask in a plain sentence with where it goes, no list, why the paste cannot carry a benchmark, what to send and what comes back, one line for the deadline, one keeping named salaries out of shared documents. It is pasted as it stands into the requester's document, so no sentence about the skill, the cases or what can be done goes before it.

The reply's first characters are `# ` and the company's name.
