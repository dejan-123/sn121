# A band is competitive only against the points that survive comparison

An evidence pool always looks richer than it is. Some points were paid in another city, some at a company rounds away, and some are not salary at all: an accepted offer is what someone was paid (revealed preference), a posted range is what an employer says it might pay, its top an aspiration and its width often a mandate's doing, and a retention grant or a contractor's hour is money of another kind. So every point is scored before the band is looked at, down to the last row of the pasted table, because the bottom rows often hold a whole family; the survivors go on one basis, and only they decide. When none are left, or the ones left point both ways, the verdict is deferred: a verdict resting on a dropped point costs more than an honest gap. A paste with no classified points is not a benchmark request, whatever salaries it lists: it goes back as a redirect that says where each ask belongs. The reply is the document itself, written to the compensation lead; it never mentions this skill or its references.

## Every point takes four tests

Each point is tested against one row of the snapshot, the family and level its title names; two levels of one function are two families.

- **Place.** The company's metro, or the metro the input anchors the role to, passes. Another metro is a miss, whatever mandate produced the figure; so is a remote role with no metro declared, which cannot be placed.
- **Stage.** Count steps on the ladder Seed, A, B, C, D, E, public. None passes; one step, up or down, is a miss; two or more, a public company, or an unknown stage is out.
- **Role.** The same work at the same level passes, under another title too when its words or a scope note map it; another level is out.
- **Type.** An accepted offer or a posted range passes. A recruiter's word, or a survey with no method or date, is hearsay: out, not a miss.

Also out: a date before the window, or none (when points before a month are out, that month itself is in, on the boundary, and so is every later month; the one before it is out, even for an accepted offer); and money that is not base salary: a retention or acquisition grant, a contractor or hourly rate (never annualised: no hours are given), an equity- or cash-heavy package, on-target earnings, total compensation with no split. The one rescue is a split the input states: total less the stated bonus leaves base, written out with its direction. A currency converts only at a rate the input gives; without one, a family paid in it is read in it, and a point in another currency is out.

No miss: the point **survives**. Exactly one miss: **partial**, read for direction, never averaged in and never put above a survivor for being an offer. Two misses, or anything out: **dropped**.

## Two gaps, then the verdict

The outside gap is (midpoint - anchor) / anchor. With one surviving accepted offer the anchor is that offer by itself; with two or more, their mean. A surviving range beside an offer only agrees or disagrees with it and is never averaged with it. Only where no offer survives is the anchor the mean of the surviving ranges' midpoints. Minus is under the market, plus over it.

The own gap is the same sum on the mean of the company's own accepted offers, the revealed preference of its own hires. Those offers have no ID: never in the anchor, the funnel or a count, and no evidence point is ever one of them. A decline signals that the band is not landing and is no point either; a percentage a candidate quotes is hearsay.

- **Own accepted offers exist:** both gaps past 2.5% with the same sign make the verdict, **Lagging** under a minus, **Above-market** under a plus; otherwise **Competitive**, since a band its own hires accept at the midpoint is not out of the market on one outside figure.
- **None accepted, or the family not listed:** the outside gap alone, past 5.5%, because an advertised middle runs high and one offer is one person; otherwise **Competitive**.
- **No survivor:** **Deferred**.

An anchor past the band's maximum or under its minimum is said in the row.

Confidence is read in steps off the surviving points; partials never count.

- Two or more surviving offers: **High**.
- One surviving offer starts at Medium: up to **High** only where a range survives beside it (a partial is no range) and an own accepted offer exists too, down to **Low** where there is neither, else **Medium**; one own accepted offer is enough.
- No surviving offer: **Low** on one range, **Medium** on two or more, under the posted-range cap; own offers never lift it.
- No survivor: **Deferred**.

## What was pasted decides what goes back

A request for one person's pay, for what a role earns in the market, for a band to be drawn or redrawn, or for a pay-equity audit, and any paste that holds a roster but no classified points, gets the redirect note below, titled `# <company>: not a benchmark request`. Everything else, however thin, is a benchmark, titled `# <company>: compensation benchmark`.

## The working, done before the document

Before a benchmark is written it is worked, beside a tool call, where the lead never sees it. First the paste: how many points and their first and last ID; the company's place and round; the window in the input's own words and its first month in; the own offers the input lists, family by family, and the families it does not list. Then family by family, in the snapshot's order: one line for each of that family's points, wherever its row stands in the paste (ID, its month set against the first month in, place, = or which, round, = or how many steps away, role, type, basis, misses, call), and straight under them the family's line: its funnel counted off the lines above it, the surviving offers and ranges, the outside gap, the own gap or none, each gap set against its threshold as a number, the verdict, and the tier by its steps. The document copies its calls from that working and shows none of it.

## The document

1. The title, then one paragraph opening `**Basis.**`: the window in the input's words, with any point on its boundary and any point outside it by ID, and that every other point is inside; each conversion with its operation and direction, or that none was needed; each internal fact whose absence touches a row (a family with no offer line, which roles are remote, an offer window shorter than the evidence window).
2. A table, one row per role family and level of the snapshot, a deferred family's row included, never per person: Role family (level, open role); Band min / mid / max; Verdict; Evidence funnel (`4 retrieved: 2 survived (IDs); 1 partial (ID, the miss); 1 dropped (ID, why)`, four numbers even where one is 0); What the survivors show (the anchor by ID, the gap with its operands, what own offers add, each partial's figure and the way it leans); Confidence and its reason. Under it a line opening `**Pool.**` adds the rows up: 4 + 2 + 3 = 9 retrieved, and the same for survived, partial and dropped.
3. `## Deferred verdicts`: one bullet per deferred family, what blocks it by ID and the evidence that would settle it (how many points, type, place, stage, level, date). Only with no deferred row, no bullets: one or two sentences opening "None:" that read down the funnel column and name each row showing 1 survived, by that ID, as the one that would be deferred without it, and the accepted offer that would firm it.
4. `## Signal caveats`: three bullets on the whole pool, each counted by naming IDs, own offers never among them (accepted offers, revealed preference, against posted ranges, aspirational, and which of those a mandate published, whole hiring spans with weaker middles; how many points come from one source; what stale or off-basis points cost which family). Nothing here is repeated in a row.
5. `**Why these calls hold.**`: three sentences on the verdicts a quick reading would get wrong, each because of the point that decides it, and what was left out and why. No raise, band change or hire is recommended.

## When the request is something else

Under 220 words, no table, no list inside the list, no figure that is not in the paste, no working. The title is the first line; the next opens "This redirects your request:" and says none of it is a role-family benchmark. Then the asks, numbered, each with the work it belongs to and where it goes: a market salary lookup, an individual pay decision, a band design, a full analysis that waits for evidence. Then why this paste cannot carry a benchmark (no classified points; a roster neither confirms nor rebuts that someone is underpaid), and what to send: the outside points with ID, source, type (accepted offers apart from posted ranges), date, role, place, stage, figure and basis; a snapshot by role family and level with headcount and band min, midpoint and max, in place of names; recent offers; the data window; and what comes back then: a verdict, a funnel and a confidence per family, or a deferral, accepted offers weighed before posted ranges. One line meets any deadline (which place and stage to collect first), one keeps the named salaries out of a shared document.

## Before you write

Call read_reference on `references/worked-cases.md` (three invented cases, each input with its working and the whole message sent back; take the form and the reasoning, never a fact). Nothing is written before that call returns, and its last lines say what the next turn is. The document is sent once, from its first character: a line such as "Now I'll produce the benchmark" would land in the lead's document, so never announce it.
