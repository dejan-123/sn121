## Case three: another request

What the head of people pasted:

"Quillon Foods here (Series B, 80 people, Tulsa OK). Reviews are next month. Is Rosa Tennant underpaid? And what do plant engineers earn these days, and could you cut our four engineering levels to three? We have no market data sorted yet, just this: Rosa Tennant, Plant Engineer, $97,500; Idris Vale, Plant Engineer, $101,500; Mara Quist, Process Technician, $72,500. The founders want it by Friday. What can you do for us?"

The message that went back, from its title to its last line:

# Quillon Foods: not a benchmark request

This redirects your request: it asks for four pieces of work, and none of them is a role-family benchmark.

1. Whether Rosa Tennant is underpaid is an individual pay decision; it goes to the manager and people lead who own it, with the band in front of them.
2. What plant engineers earn is a market salary lookup; it goes to a salary survey or pay-data provider.
3. Cutting four levels to three is a band design; it goes to whoever owns your job architecture.
4. The analysis the founders want by Friday waits for evidence that is not here yet.

A benchmark sets a band against classified outside points, and there are none in this paste. Three salaries are not bands, and they neither confirm nor rebut that Rosa is underpaid; any market figure given now would be a guess.

To run one, send: the outside points with ID, source, type (accepted offers apart from posted ranges), date, role and level, place, company stage, and the figure with its basis (base, total or on-target); a snapshot by role family and level with headcount and band minimum, midpoint and maximum, in place of names; recent offers, accepted and declined; and the data window. Each family then comes back with a verdict, an evidence funnel and a High, Medium or Low confidence, or deferred, with accepted offers (revealed preference) weighed before posted ranges (aspirational). For Friday, start with Tulsa points at Series B, accepted offers first, since those are the ones that will count; if they are not classified by then, tell the founders so.

Keep the three named salaries out of any shared document.
