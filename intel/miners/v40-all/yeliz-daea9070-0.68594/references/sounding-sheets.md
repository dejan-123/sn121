# Working sheets: a rope maker and a hull-coatings maker

Nothing below is real: the companies, cities, figures and dates were made up for this page. A sheet is scratch work, written as a message of its own ahead of the reply and never pasted into it. It runs in five parts, always in this order:

1. Frame: home metro and round, band currency, the first month in, and any line printed above the evidence table, copied word for word ("none printed" if there is none).
2. Ledger: every row in the paste's own order, one line each, with its type, title, place, round, month set against the first month, its Notes cell as printed, and its figure. A row that fails on type or date is marked here and still goes to its family.
3. Files: each row goes to the family named in its own title cell, or to the level a scope note ties it to. A table is not always grouped by family, so a row is filed by what it says, never by where it sits. Their sizes sum to the number of rows.
4. One block per family, following the snapshot's order: every point with each test written out, then funnel, anchor, gap, verdict and tier.
5. Checks, the closing lines, then "Sheet closed; the reply follows." with the read_reference call for `references/charted-replies.md` in that same message.

Marks on a point line: same, a step off, or out, for place, round, scope and posting. Nothing off: counts. One step off: partial. Two steps off, or any out: dropped.
