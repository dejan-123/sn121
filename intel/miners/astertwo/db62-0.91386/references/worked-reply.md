Readings that most often go wrong:
- A price the input states anywhere, a multiple of revenue, earnings or book value inside the thesis sentence included, is P1, worked once on its own base; "none stated" is only for an input that gives no price basis at all. A contract total over several years becomes a yearly figure once, the total over the years, before it is set beside a price written on yearly revenue; where the pool never gives the revenue the multiple applies to, the row says the multiple cannot be tested from the pool.
- In an infringement claim the asserted patent is the claimant's, never the target's. It reaches the deal only if its claims read on what the deal buys, so the action maps the asserted claims to that platform; a challenge to the target's own patents is a different record.
- Where only the target calls an asset its own, the register search settles first what it owns (owner of record, status), then whether anyone challenges it. A list the target publishes of what it holds is set against the register as a finding of its own: how many the list names, how many the register returned and in what state (in force, lapsed, under another holder's name), and how many stay unverified, worked from those two counts. The register confirms only what it returned, never the list, and the unverified remainder of what the thesis rests on is the evidence gap the closing paragraph names.
- A denial from the target's spokesperson, named or not, is the target speaking: single-source, not primary. So is the target's own report of praise from an authority it does not name: the row asks for the name and a written confirmation, at deeper-diligence, in the table and in the count line alike. Unverified is for claims no party stands behind. An outlet's own description of the target is the outlet's origin unless it cites the target.
- A claim dismissed with prejudice is answered, and its row sits at monitor however close the asset stands to the thesis: the dispute cannot return. What the target gave up to end it, such as a licence, a covenant not to sue or a royalty on that asset, is a paper the row's action asks for before the next step, never a reason to raise the row. The order and its unknown terms stay one row, and a claim closed for good is never the closest call or the evidence gap the closing paragraph names.
- A report whose parts come from different origins, some on a public record and some from an unnamed source, splits into lettered rows with one label each; no row is labelled mixed.
- A lapse for an unpaid fee is the holder's own omission. An expiry that falls in the year the thesis needs sits at the floor, not past it.
- A study or product registry shows an authority's hold as a change of status in that same entry, so a current, retrieved entry is that record searched, and there is no separate register of holds to name as unsearched. A certificate stands only while its issuer keeps its accreditation, which the accreditor's own list records. A charges register shows that a lender holds security and over what class of asset; the debt and the exact assets sit in the security agreement, and an empty search clears the register on its date, never the loan.
- A register counts as searched only where the pool shows what that register itself returned; a "no signals found" line from a news sweep never opened it. Each register holds one kind of record, and the not-searched line lists only the registers the input itself names, each by what it holds; it never folds a file of lesser notices (letters, inspection findings) into the register of the order or hold a walk-away names. That hole is must-address only when the register is the very record a stated condition turns on; a file of another kind of notice than the one the walk-away names stays deeper-diligence.
- A report on unnamed sources that an authority is already acting on the target, a review, an inquiry or a notice being drafted, is raised when it bears on a stated condition, even though the action is not yet the sanction or breach the condition names: that action is how the sanction starts, so the authority is asked in writing before signing, and the reply says plainly that nothing has been imposed yet. The action must be against the target itself: an action against an unnamed company, or against a product class the target also works in, stays noise however close the class, and a current entry for the target's own product outranks it.
- A condition the deal writes as an absence (unencumbered, free of charges, no sanctions) holds only while nothing of that kind exists. Once an attributed record shows the thing exists at all, the record contradicts the condition now, and the rows, the must-address picture and the gap paragraph say contradicted, not at risk; money raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought. A threshold on the same subject (more than a share by count or by value) is a second test, and its status is open, never contradicted, until a record gives the count: it is worked once in the deal's own units, and that record measures how far the contradiction reaches.
- A lender's, customer's or counterparty's own disclosure of a relationship the target announced is one more origin of that same fact: the rows read together as corroborated and take one tier, the one the confirmed fact earns. A confirmed contract the thesis leans on leaves its condition intact, so the announcement and the customer's record both sit at monitor; a confirmed loan against a condition written as an absence puts that condition at risk, so the press, the release and the lender's record all sit at must-address. The target's denial of a claim, or its hiring of counsel to fight it, confirms nothing of the claim: it reads with the claim's row but stays at monitor, a response and not an origin, and the check line names it as the target's response at monitor, never as part of the finding the claim carries.
- Silence on the figure the thesis turns on (renewals, churn, margin, the count behind a price condition) is not a clean result: the check line says the thesis rests on that figure and the pool cannot reach it, and the closing paragraph names it as a gap, second if another comes first.
- Where departing staff went is part of the signal: people who join a named competitor can take know-how, unpatented methods and customer ties with them, so the row names where they went and its action asks for the invention-assignment, confidentiality and non-compete terms that bind them and those who stay.
- Each row keeps the number the input gave its signal, in the input's order, absences included, and the table holds those signals only: a figure the pool never mentions is named in the evidence-gap paragraph, never given a row of its own.
- Every signal is read once for what confirms, contradicts or repeats it before the first row is written, so an earlier row's label and tier already carry what a later row says about it: a release that a later counterparty record confirms is written as corroborated in its own row, and the table never shows two tiers for rows the check line calls one finding.
- The must-address picture counts findings the way the check line grouped them, so two rows of one finding are one finding. In the count line each row sits under one tier only, and the total equals the rows in the table.
