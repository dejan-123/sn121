## Tallis Bioinstruments: Firmware lags, two bands hold, Field Applications is deferred

**Basis.** Tallis is held to Raleigh and Series A, annual base salary in USD. The input asks for "the last 9 months" to December 2031, so March 2031 is the first month in; it shuts out an accepted Field Applications offer from January 2031 (T-11). One conversion: T-04 came as 101,700 in total cash, less its stated 7,900 bonus, which gives 93,800 base (down to base). Accepted offers lead because they are revealed preference, pay someone took; posted ranges are aspirational, what an employer says it will pay, and North Carolina requires none of them.

Firmware Engineering has the strongest evidence and the closest call. A Raleigh Series A company hired a senior firmware engineer at 135,400 (T-01), and a Raleigh Series A posting (T-02) runs 124,600 to 147,600, around that offer. The market sits 4.48% above the 129,600 midpoint, close to the line; Tallis's own two hires closed at a median of 133,950, already over its midpoint, so the band is lagging, and the open P1 seat is where that will show first. A Series C offer at 141,900 (T-03) is two rounds away and was set aside.

Assay Development and Quality Systems both hold. Assay Development rests on one Raleigh offer (T-04), 93,800 once its stated bonus is removed, which leaves the 96,900 midpoint 3.20% above the market: competitive on a single hire. A Durham posting (T-05) is another city and does not count. Quality Systems has one voluntary Raleigh posting centred on 111,000 (T-06), almost level with its 111,800 midpoint; it can carry a call only because a Series B posting one round up (T-07) sits on the same ground, and with no offer behind it the call is Low.

Field Applications cannot be called. Its only full point is a voluntary posting (T-08) that nothing tests: the Seed-round offer (T-09) is one person's price at a different stage, and the recruiter's "about 100,000" (T-10) is hearsay.
