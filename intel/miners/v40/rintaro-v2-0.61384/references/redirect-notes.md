# Redirect notes

When the request is not a market benchmark, or arrives with no classified outside evidence, the whole reply is a short note. No benchmark table, no verdicts, no salary figures, no individual's name.

## Shape

- Title: `# <Company>: no benchmark can be run on this yet`
- Then three short paragraphs, together under about 170 words:
  1. What was asked and what actually arrived.
  2. Which parts of the ask belong to another kind of work, and who does it: one person's pay goes to the manager and the people partner; adding levels or reshaping a band is job architecture, run by compensation once levels are defined; a pay-equity review is for people analytics with legal; a raw "what does this role earn" question is a market-data lookup.
  3. What to send for a benchmark: the bands by role family and level (min, midpoint, max, headcount), the company's own recent offers, and a classified outside evidence table with source, kind (offer or posted range), date, role title, location, company stage and figures, plus the window to apply.

## Two invented examples

**A roster and three asks.** Corbel Instruments, 40 people, sends a list of names and salaries and asks whether two named engineers are underpaid, for a new three-level engineering band, and for "market numbers" by Friday.

# Corbel Instruments: no benchmark can be run on this yet

You sent a list of people and their salaries and asked three things: whether two engineers are paid fairly, for a new engineering band, and for market numbers by Friday. No outside evidence came with it, so there is nothing to compare the bands against.

Whether a particular person is paid fairly is a conversation for their manager and your people partner. A third engineering level is a job-architecture decision your compensation lead makes once the level definitions exist; it cannot be read off a market table.

For the market question, send your bands by role family and level with headcount, your recent offers, and a classified outside evidence table (source, offer or posted range, date, title, location, company stage, figures) with the window you want applied. The benchmark can then be run family by family against companies like yours.

**A benchmark with one extra ask.** If the same request had come with a proper evidence table and also asked for a gender pay-gap review, run the benchmark and add one line at the end: "The pay-equity review is a separate exercise for people analytics and legal, run on individual records."

Write the note now. It begins with `# ` and the company name; nothing comes before it.
