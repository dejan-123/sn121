# Decide from the middle of the eligible market evidence

First check the requested decision and supplied inputs. Benchmark classified outside evidence against internal family/level bands. Individual salaries are neither bands nor a market sample. Without prerequisites, give a useful redirect, normally within 180 words, then stop: explain the unsupported claim, identify supplied family/level coverage without personal names/pay, and request the missing handoff. This needs dated, attributable external offers/ranges with geography, stage, scope and components; internal bands/pay zones; priorities and recent offers. Use known company context. Make a deadline depend on obtaining evidence, not invented rates.

Route salary research to data gathering/classification; individual pay to compensation review; band design to job architecture with duties/levels; equity to a controlled audit. Existing salaries cannot design a ladder. For mixed requests benchmark supported segments and route the rest. The following sections apply only to supported benchmarks.

## Set the comparison and retain the eligible series

Separate family, level and pay zone. Preserve band endpoints/midpoint, currency, stage, assessment date and priorities. Supplied policy takes precedence. HQ is only a proxy for roles paid in that zone, not an employee distribution. Benchmark supported HQ roles; defer an unmapped remote population pending family/band/pay-zone mapping. Currency and labor-market equivalence are separate questions.

Assign every retrieved ID before exclusion; wrong-level rows remain scope failures in their retrieved segment. Preserve distinct levels and account separately for unassigned rows. Score every ID, including disqualified records, in geography/stage/scope/evidence-type order: 2 full, 1 limited, 0 mismatch, ? unresolved.

| Dimension | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Same metro or explicit shared pay zone | Broad market or remote without a specific anchor | Different metro or country without a shared anchor |
| Stage | Same funding stage | One adjacent stage | Further removed or private/public mismatch |
| Scope | Equivalent responsibilities and level, including explicit mappings | Limited overlap | Materially different work or level |
| Evidence type | Identifiable offer or primary employer range | Identified, dated secondary evidence with a stated method | Hearsay, anonymous or methodless survey |

Four 2s gives full comparability. Any 0 defeats eligibility. Records with 1s but no 0/essential unknown remain partial context after date/basis checks; adjacent stage is not a full match. For each ?, name the missing fact. If no definitive exclusion already applies, essential-unknown records enter D with primary reason 'unresolved'; retain ? scores and the resolution request. They never disappear from R = F + P + D.

Partials cannot set anchors or raise confidence. Explain material disagreement with full evidence without averaging it in or treating stage as a known pay adjustment. An observed difference is not a causal explanation. Internal offers remain outside external counts/anchors; they indicate hiring pressure, not exact rival pay.

Separate source quality from publication motive. A primary employer range can be full whether voluntary or mandated, but remains advertising. Supplied disclosure notes inform selection caveats, not realized pay or population coverage. A feed label alone does not establish original-document provenance. Accept explicit source/type classifications as supplied; missing employer names alone limit independence, not type eligibility. Use ? for an actually unresolved essential characteristic.

Apply the observation window at the supplied as-of/retrieval snapshot. Include a cutoff month when only earlier months are excluded. Drop stale/undated records. Deduplicate confirmed events using the best documented original and stable ID for equivalent ties; count copies as drops linked to that original. Preserve shared provenance for distinct events.

Separately require ordinary recurring employee base. Exclude contractors, one-time awards, acquisition/retention packages and inseparable mixes; retain a separately identified eligible base component. Pay terms override misleading labels. Unsplit OTE supplies no base; annualizing contractor pay cannot make it comparable.

Normalize to annual employee base in the band's currency. Preserve both range endpoints. Show each required original amount, supplied factor/component, direction, equation and result; state a common unchanged basis once.

- Multiply monthly base by the stated annual pay periods. For eligible hourly employees use stated annual paid hours.
- Subtract a known annual non-base cash component from a compatible total. With bonus fraction r of base, divide total cash by (1 + r). With r of total cash, multiply total cash by (1 − r). An unspecified denominator, split or period cannot supply a guessed rate.
- Multiply by target currency units per source unit, or divide by source currency units per target unit. Use the supplied quote only.

Partition IDs as F eligible full, P retained partial context, D dropped, including unresolved. R = F + P + D; retained S = F + P. Give each D one primary reason plus relevant flags; primary drop counts must sum to D. Mark anchor records separately from contextual F/P records.

## Determine position from the median rank

Anchor on eligible full offers, including documented declined offers with status stated. Keep full postings as separate context. Only if no full offer survives, use full posted-range midpoints and label the anchor an advertised-pay indicator, preserving ranges. Partial/excluded offers cannot activate priority. Do not blend offers and ads or infer paid-market percentiles from advertised bounds.

Sort chosen amounts with all their IDs. The median is the central amount for odd counts, or (L + U) ÷ 2 for the lower/upper central pair of even counts. Use supplied midpoint M; label a center derived from band endpoints if necessary. A missing band permits a market observation but no position. Defer missing anchors, unmapped populations or substantive unresolved conflict, naming the gap. Opposing partials need a full match, not an average.

Unless supplied policy differs, disclose the author convention: Competitive where 0.95A <= M <= 1.05A, inclusive, for positive anchor A. Otherwise M < 0.95A is Lagging; M > 1.05A is Above-market. Equivalent anchor bounds are M/1.05 and M/0.95. Check unrounded central amounts/pairs; near boundaries compare 100M with 95A/105A. This convention is not statistical significance.

Render the signed gap as 100 × (M − A) ÷ A using adequate precision near either boundary. Recheck the sign against the signed difference. The displayed percentage follows the decision made from unrounded amounts. If substantive conflict prevents a defensible conclusion, name the conflict and defer.

High requires multiple recent independent full offers, secure basis and coherent support. Thin offers are usually Medium; unknown employer independence caps at Medium. A shared feed proves neither duplication nor independence: count distinct records, deduplicate confirmed events and state the limit. Posting-only is at most Medium, or Low for one posting. Qualified Low means a supported but fragile direction; Deferred means unsupported. Give the deciding reason and honor stricter supplied policy. Partials, copies and internal offers never raise confidence.

## Optional evidence-intake analysis

Read `references/evidence-intake.md` only when asked what additional evidence could change a supported position. Otherwise omit hypothetical acquisitions. Current verdicts/counts/confidence use observed records alone.

## Present a concise, reconstructible decision

Lead with this input's decisive finding. For four to six segments, aim for 650-850 words including tables; group audit rows before omitting required evidence. State matching and offer-priority defaults as author policies, alongside the tolerance. Use a family/level/pay-zone table: band/midpoint, anchor class/IDs, median or central pair, monetary and percentage gap, verdict, confidence/reason and R/F/P/D/S. Label advertised anchors; state the tolerance once and qualify sparse/boundary cases. Competitive midpoint does not prove adequate hiring coverage or a percentile.

Add a compact grouped audit: every ID's four scores, F/P/D, anchor/context use and decisive P/D reason. Show conversions once and one pool reconciliation. Do not repeat table cells in prose or calculate anchors from dropped amounts.

Always include Deferred Verdicts: state None if supported; otherwise name each unsupported segment, its exact gap/conflict and smallest usable next request. Preserve an observable market anchor when only the band is missing. Separate unmapped remote populations from supported HQ segments without invented family assignments or headcount.

Put shared provenance/independence limits once in the pool note; row confidence reasons should cite their own evidence depth/class. Give relevant internal outcomes/priorities in a short hiring-context paragraph. In one Signal Caveats section explain supported disclosure/geographic, recency and dependence limits and their consequences. Candidate claims do not establish rival salaries. Keep hypothetical intake separate.

Finally check IDs/drop counts, dates, component/FX equations, central ranks, signs/boundaries and narrative agreement. Anchors must use their declared full series; each deferral needs an actionable gap. Do not imply outside verification.
