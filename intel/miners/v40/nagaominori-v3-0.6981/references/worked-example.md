## What to do now

Now the real request. Write its working sheet as plain text, in full, in the form above. First the pool line with every ID; then the placement line, ID by ID in ID order, each row copied whole from the table (type, date, role title, geo, stage, figures) with the one family its title belongs to (placed by title, never by where the row happens to sit); then the company line with stage distance counted in letters from the company's own letter; then the first month in window, copied from the month the input names, and the date step, which tests only points dated in that month's year or earlier, or undated, and names the ones before the first month (a date in any later year is inside and is not tested); then the own-offers line, the input's offer lines in the input's order, each copied with the family it names and every offer written out ("two at X and one at Y" is X; X; Y), and a family the list never names has none. After that, family by family in the band table's order (not the offer list's), each under a header that gives its midpoint and repeats its own offers from the own-offers line (or "own offers: none"), one line per point with its own date and "inside" unless the date step named that very ID, then its geo and its stage written out as the placement line gives them for that ID, each set against the company's (same, another metro, or how many letters away), then its type and its figure, again as the placement line gives them (an own offer's figure never stands on a point line), and for a posted range its Notes cell copied as note: "..." or note: blank (out points too; a partial point's figure or midpoint is set against the band midpoint in numbers); then the Counts, Deferral test (when one posted range is the only full point it must be backed: a mandate in its own note, an own accepted offer, a decline about pay or a partial point, asked in that order, with the 5% check when the only backing is a partial accepted offer), Reference with its "Rests on" tag (accepted offer or posted range) and the signed gap, Row (which row of the verdict table, settled before own offers; for ranges in the 3-to-5 row, whether a partial accepted offer sits 5% or more above), Own reading (the header's offers, each one's percent from the midpoint, and nothing more; with none in the header it says none), the Outweigh check line (only when the row is 5% or more and one outside offer is the family's only full point), Verdict and Tier lines. Last the totals block as above: the three ID lists, the window slots, the two rests-on lists, the posting notes, the source line and the snapshot gaps. End the sheet with the line "Totals checked; the document is next." and, in that same message, call read_reference for `references/document.md`. The document is written only after that call returns.
