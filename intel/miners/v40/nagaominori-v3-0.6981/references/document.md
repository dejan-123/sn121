## Before sending yours

Check the document against the working sheet, silently. Each row has its seven cells, in the band table's order, and no two cells run together: the own-offers cell ends before the arithmetic begins. The own-offers cell holds what the family's sheet header lists and nothing else: "none on record" when the header says none, and never a figure that belongs to a P- or E- point. The verdict cell opens with its bracketed arithmetic, even when own closes outweigh the reference, on the reference and midpoint of its own row, and "under" means the midpoint is below the reference. Every point ID appears in exactly one funnel, out points included, with its call and a reason in words; a partial point shows its figure; the Pool line's three numbers are the lengths of the sheet's three ID lists. The own-offers cell writes out every offer, so "two at $176,300 and one at $175,300" is three. Each verdict cell's word and reason are the ones on that family's own Verdict line in the sheet, never the example's: 5% or more decides alone, plain Lagging from an offer and "Lagging, marginal" from ranges, except one outside offer against two or more own closes all within 3% (Competitive); 3% to 5% is "Lagging, slight" only with an own offer 5% or more above, or, from ranges, a partial accepted offer 5% or more above. Every family the sheet marked DEFERRED is Deferred in the table, with its indication when it has one, and has a bullet; a family whose range the sheet found backed keeps its verdict. What would settle a deferral is an accepted offer, or a range only where a mandate is noted; no sentence names a tier that new evidence would reach. With no family deferred, that paragraph is one sentence on the thinnest verdict, with no bullets. A range-based tier is tested once more as the row is written, whatever the sheet's Tier line says: Medium only when two full ranges agree and, as well, own offers or a pay-citing decline point the same way; with either one missing it is Low; High needs an accepted offer behind the reference, a second full point and own offers on the same side. The Basis copies the sheet's three window slots and says nothing more about dates. The first caveat is the sheet's two rests-on lists, family for family, with no deferred family in either; a posting note is given only for a range behind a verdict, ID by ID, in the input's words or as "no note" where the cell is blank; a posting rule the input states for the whole pool is quoted once; nothing says "all", "none" or "every" about notes, and no points are tallied by type. A rival's quoted premium stays a percent.
