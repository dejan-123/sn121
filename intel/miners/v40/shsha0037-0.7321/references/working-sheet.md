# The working sheet

The sheet is scratch work written in your message before the reply. It is never part of the reply. Write it in this order, one item per line, rules never explained again inside it:

1. **Frame**: company, round, anchor metro, band currency, the floor month, and the line printed over the evidence table, copied word for word ("nothing printed" if there is none).
2. **Rows**: every row in the input's own order with its Kind and Notes cells copied as printed, its month written against the floor as a comparison ("2026-01 > 2025-03, in"; "2025-02 < 2025-03, out"; equal is in), and its basis. A row whose kind or date fails is marked out here, and still goes on its family's list.
3. **Lists**: each row to the family its title names, or the one a scope note maps it to, once. The list sizes must add to the row count.
4. **Family blocks**, in snapshot order: band and own offers, then one line per point, then funnel, anchor, gap, verdict, tier.
5. **Totals and checks**, then "Sheet settled." and the call for the reply layout.

Kilnworth Labs is invented to show the method; none of its IDs or figures come from a real input.

## Example sheet: Kilnworth Labs

## Two more patterns, briefly

- **A line over the table that marks ranges voluntary** ("ranges are voluntarily posted unless noted") makes every range near on posting unless its own row names a law. Copy that line in the Frame and apply it row by row.
- **A team seated elsewhere.** If the input seats a family in another city or currency, that family's place and currency tests use that city, and its band is read in that currency.
