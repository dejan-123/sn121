# Return a request to the right workflow

A useful redirect leaves the requester knowing what can happen now, what information is missing, and which decision becomes possible afterward. It does not turn employee salaries into outside market observations.

Deliver a brief return note: one opening that identifies the blocked conclusion, then a compact set of next steps. Cover each request once and end when the handoff is clear. Do not reproduce or calculate an internal roster inventory unless that deliverable was requested. The return note is not a tutorial on the benchmarking method.

Start with the user's actual question and constraint. If they ask whether a role is underpaid, explain that a roster cannot establish market competitiveness. If they ask for a deliverable by a date, distinguish work that can be completed from evidence still needed; do not promise external research has already happened.

Use a small number of practical steps, with each input tied to a result:

- For market-data gathering and classification, specify role duties and level, employee pay-zone coverage, company stage, annual recurring base definition, assessment date/window, and dated, attributable employer ranges or offer observations. The input should identify offer status and any conversion factors.
- For band benchmarking, request band minimum, midpoint and maximum for each comparison unit, plus headcount, hiring priorities and recent internal offers with outcomes. Internal offers add hiring context; they do not replace the external pool. With these inputs, the narrator can provide the family comparison, an evidence funnel, confidence and explicit deferrals.
- For a change in the level structure, route to job architecture: responsibilities, scope boundaries, progression criteria and current level definitions come before pricing new levels. Do not invent a third level or pay band in the redirect.
- For individual decisions, route to compensation review; for pay-equity questions, route to an appropriately scoped equity analysis. A market benchmark by itself does not determine either outcome.

Do not request every item when some are already supplied. Separate the question that is blocked from a supported neighboring task. If one family has adequate evidence, complete its comparison while returning the unsupported families for collection or mapping.

For an urgent board request, identify an interim packet the team can prepare, such as the evidence request and unresolved decisions. A full market conclusion remains conditional on comparable evidence arriving. State that dependency once, without drafting the entire interim packet.
