## Notes on the moves

1. Dates are decided by year first. R9 falls in the cutoff month and stays; R10 is one month earlier and goes.
2. One Miss drops a point (R3, R13), even an accepted offer. One Near alone makes a partial (R5, R6, R12).
3. R1's total was reduced to base with the subtraction shown. Without the 2,700 in the note, R1 would have been dropped for an unknown split.
4. The tier count lists accepted offers only. R2 corroborates dispatch but is not counted, so dispatch is Medium, and fleet analytics with two adverts is still Low.
5. With several full posted ranges and no offer, the anchor is the average of all of them.
6. Own offers appear as a clause of corroboration. They are absent from the funnel, the anchor and the tier.
7. There are two grounds for deferral and both appear: no full point (customs), and an advert anchor contradicted by a partial accepted offer (yard).
8. A deferral names the exact point that would end it.
9. The counts line under the classification is copied into the funnel cells, and the totals add up to the number of IDs supplied.
10. A point in another currency with no exchange rate supplied is dropped for that reason; a rate is never brought in from outside.
