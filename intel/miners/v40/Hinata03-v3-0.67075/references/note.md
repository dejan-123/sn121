# The note, for a request that is not a benchmark

Invented, like everything in these files: take the form, never a fact. A note goes back when the paste holds no classified outside evidence, or asks for other work: pricing one person, a market lookup, band design, a pay-equity audit. It keeps the benchmark's title, so the requester files it in the same place, and it has no table, no figures and no working.

## A request with nothing to benchmark

What the input held. A head of production at Ashgrove Bakeries pastes the salaries of nine bakery staff, asks whether the two shift leads named in the list are underpaid, asks what oven-line supervisors earn in Boise, and wants a new band for a night-bake tier. No outside evidence is attached.

The note as sent:

# Role-family benchmark: Ashgrove Bakeries

This request is not a band benchmark, so this note points each part to where it belongs. Whether the two named shift leads are underpaid is a decision about two people, for their manager and HR. What oven-line supervisors earn in Boise is a market lookup, which needs a sourced survey. A night-bake band is band design, set by the compensation committee once a benchmark shows where the current bands stand.

Nothing pasted can carry a benchmark: it is a roster of the company's own salaries with no classified outside evidence, and a roster neither confirms nor rebuts that anyone is underpaid.

To benchmark the bakery families, send each outside point with an ID, source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, title and level, metro, company stage and annual base; each family's band minimum, midpoint and maximum; the data window; and recent offers, accepted and declined. Each family then gets a verdict, its evidence funnel and a confidence tier, or a deferral where the evidence is thin.

What makes it work: the first sentence tells the person who asked that this is not a band benchmark; each ask is named with where it belongs; one short paragraph says why the paste cannot carry a benchmark; the last paragraph lists exactly what to send and what comes back. About 150 words, never over 200, plain paragraphs, no headings or bullets under the title. Where part of a request is a benchmark with its evidence present, that part is done instead, through `references/casebook.md`.
