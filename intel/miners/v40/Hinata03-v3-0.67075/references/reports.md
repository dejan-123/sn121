## Last look before sending

- Every family and level in the snapshot has a table row, deferred or not; no row names a person.
- Each funnel names every row filed to that family once, by ID, with the reason for each drop and the miss for each partial; the Count line adds up to the number of rows in the input.
- Another named city is a mismatch; only a remote or national figure with no metro is partial on place; one round apart is partial, never dropped.
- Every anchor names its outside rows by ID: offers alone where any full offer exists; no own offer, partial or range midpoint inside an offer median.
- Every gap is measured over the band midpoint, and its sign matches the verdict: a market above the midpoint means lagging.
- The hiring check uses the family's own accepted offers, copied from its own line, every offer counted ("two at $X" is two figures); a family with no line reads "None listed", and a check above +5.0% makes the band lagging.
- A full range beside an offer anchor is still full: it stays in the funnel's full count and in the tier, and is only kept out of the median. Partial means one miss on place, round or scope, nothing else.
- Every tier counts fully comparable outside rows only; one range alone is Low, and two ranges with no offer are Medium under the posted-range cap.
- Pool-wide skews are said once, under signal caveats.
