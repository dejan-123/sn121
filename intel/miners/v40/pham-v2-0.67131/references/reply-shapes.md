## Harbourline Marine Systems: role-family pay benchmark

**Basis.** Series C, every family priced in the Halifax market, annual base salary in CAD. The input asks for "the past 18 months" up to March 2032, so the first allowed month is September 2030: H-10 (August 2030) misses by one month and every other point is inside. One conversion: H-04's total cash 117,900 − the stated 9,400 annual bonus = 108,500 (down to base). No own offers are recorded for Dock Operations or Fleet Safety Assurance. The gap is how far the market anchor sits above or below the band midpoint as a share of that midpoint: +5.00% or more is lagging, −5.00% or less above-market, and from 4.00% to 4.99% the company's own accepted offers decide. Accepted offers lead because they are revealed preference, a price a candidate took; posted ranges are aspirational, and a lone voluntary one cannot carry a call unless something tests it.

## What this request needs instead

A role-family benchmark tests existing bands against an outside pool that has already been gathered and labelled. These asks sit elsewhere:
- **A counter-offer for a resigning engineer**: one person's pay is an individual decision; a benchmark only shows where the band sits.
- **A new six-grade structure**: designing grades is band architecture, built from job evaluation, not a market check.
- **What rival yards pay deckhands**: no pool came with the request, and quoting figures from memory would give numbers nobody can source.

Send the band per role family and level, and outside points each with source, type, date, place, funding stage and figure; the benchmark then returns a call, its evidence count and a confidence tier per family.
