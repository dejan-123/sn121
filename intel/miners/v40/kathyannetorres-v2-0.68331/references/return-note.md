# The return note, written out once

Corvid Tooling is invented; take the shape and the reasoning and none of the facts.

## What arrived

"Our machining team at Corvid Tooling (Series A, Tulsa OK) is below", followed by five names, each with a title (CNC Programmer Senior, CNC Programmer Mid, Quality Technician Senior) and a salary. "The plant manager is sure our programmers are paid too little. What is the going rate? We would also like one more level in the programmer band. The founders want a market view by Friday."

No band and no classified outside signal is in that paste, so nothing can be weighed.

## The note that went back

## Not a benchmark request: Corvid Tooling

Nothing in this paste can be benchmarked yet: it is five people and what they earn, with no classified outside signals, and it asks for four different things.

1. **The going rate for CNC programmers and quality technicians.** That is a market-rate lookup, not a benchmark. Recruiting or a pay-data provider gathers it; quoting pay from memory would hand you a number nobody can trace.
2. **Whether your programmers are paid too little.** Five salaries neither confirm nor rebut the plant manager's view. A benchmark reads bands by role family and level and never a named person, so what an individual should earn stays with their manager and your people lead.
3. **One more level in the programmer band.** That is band design, and it belongs to whoever owns your pay structure. A finished benchmark feeds that work; it does not do it.
4. **A market view by Friday.** It waits on evidence nobody has collected yet. Begin with Tulsa signals at Series A for CNC Programmer, accepted offers before job ads, because offers carry the weight; if they are not in hand by Friday, tell the founders the review is waiting on data.

**Send this.** Bands (minimum, midpoint, maximum) for CNC Programmer Senior, CNC Programmer Mid and Quality Technician Senior, instead of the named salaries. Outside signals, one per row: source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, role and level, place, company stage, and the figure with its pay basis. Your own recent offers, accepted and declined, and the window to apply.

**What you get back.** For each of those three families a position (lagging, competitive or above market), the evidence funnel behind it and a confidence tier, or a deferral where too little counts, with accepted offers weighed ahead of posted ranges. Until the evidence exists all three are deferred.

## Before yours goes out

Each separate ask has its own numbered line with the kind of work and its owner. No salary from the paste is repeated, no figure of any kind appears, no data vendor and no person is named. The families, levels, place and stage are the request's own.

Now send the note for the request in front of you. Its very first characters are "## Not a benchmark request: ". Nothing stands before the title, no rule and no dashes, and nothing mentions this skill, its references or these checks.
