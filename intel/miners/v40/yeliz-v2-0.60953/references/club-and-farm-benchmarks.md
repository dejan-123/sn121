# Three worked benchmarks: invented gym chains, an indoor farm and a cycling studio

Every company, club, city, figure and date in this file is invented. The replies are here for their form and their size: how a finished benchmark is set out, how much each table cell holds, and how a note reads when the ask is no benchmark at all. Each reply starts, on its first characters, with its `## ` title, and nothing sits above it. What carries over to a live reply:
- a title naming the company and what was benchmarked, then a short paragraph with the home metro, funding round, window, the day the pool was gathered, the pay basis and the pool count split into full, partial and dropped;
- straight after that paragraph, a Point-by-Point Grading list, one line per point (its family, offer or range with the figure, the dimension that decided it, where it landed); every grading call is made there;
- a table row for each family at each level, its funnel running retrieved → full → partial → dropped by ID, with a one-word reason for each drop;
- range midpoints worked in view, full offers leading the anchor, partials only backing or weakening it, and every conversion written as arithmetic with its direction;
- the gap in money and in percent of the anchor, the verdict, and a confidence cell that names the reason deciding it, with ranges alone never above Medium;
- under Deferred Verdicts, an entry for each uncharted family saying what knocked out each point, which partials disagreed, or which internal figure never came, and the sounding that would chart it; Signal Caveats said once for the whole pool;
- for a request outside the chart, a brief note that gives a judgment, what the paste itself shows, and an exact list of what to send.

No company, city, job title, figure or date carries over. Each of those comes from the request in hand, never from this file.

### Deferred Verdicts

- Regional Group Fitness Director: two points fell outright (a listed chain's package, a survey with neither date nor method), and the two left are partials that disagree: an offer one round up at $88,900, under the midpoint, and a mapped near title at $108,000, far over it. Neither may anchor, and between them they cancel out. One accepted offer or posted range for a Regional Group Fitness Director in Tacoma at a Series C company, dated August 2025 or later, would chart it.

### Signal Caveats

None of the kept ranges was posted under a mandate, so each is an employer's own ask; that is why the offers lead. Senior Strength Coach is a P1 requisition standing on ranges alone, and the $59,500 offer that was declined, below the midpoint, lost to a counter-offer: the band's lower half does not move people, though that sets no price.

Club General Manager is the call to act on: both full points and both of Ironvale's own hires sit $3,750 to $6,750 above the $84,500 midpoint, so the band trails what hiring already costs. Membership Billing holds inside its band on one offer, and Member Services on two points that agree.

## Case three: an offer approval with no market evidence

What the head of studios at Spindrift Cycle Studios sent. Spindrift is a Series A indoor-cycling chain with 70 staff in Flagstaff AZ. "Can you approve this offer? We want to hire a Studio Operations Manager at $74,000 base plus a $4,800 relocation payment, and our studio director thinks the band is too low, so tell us whether to raise the maximum to $79,000. Our bands are below; we haven't gathered any market data yet." Bands given (minimum / midpoint / maximum): Studio Operations Manager $61,000 / $66,000 / $72,000; Lead Instructor $44,000 / $48,000 / $52,500; Member Experience Manager $52,000 / $57,000 / $62,500. No evidence table came with it.

The reply:

## Spindrift Cycle Studios: an offer approval and a band change, not a benchmark

You asked me to approve a $74,000 base offer with a $4,800 relocation payment for one Studio Operations Manager candidate, and to say whether the band maximum should rise to $79,000. This benchmark cannot answer either question. It positions role-family bands against classified outside evidence, and none came with the request; it also speaks for families, never for one person's offer, and a new maximum is band design, a decision for whoever owns compensation.

What your bands do show: the proposed $74,000 base sits $2,000 above the Studio Operations Manager maximum of $72,000 and $8,000 above its $66,000 midpoint. Whether that is the market's price or a premium is exactly what evidence would decide. The relocation payment is a one-off and stays out of any base comparison.

To benchmark the Studio Operations Manager, Lead Instructor and Member Experience Manager bands, send:
- the evidence, a line for each point: its source, offer or posted range, when, the role and its level, the metro, the employer's round, and the figure with its pay basis;
- the home metro and any roles with no metro tie, plus the months the evidence may span and the date it was pulled;
- Spindrift's own offers in that window, accepted or declined, with any reason given.

With those, each band gets a verdict, a gap and a confidence tier: footing an approval or a band change can then stand on.
