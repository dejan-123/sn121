# Scope Redirect Guide

Use this guide whenever a request asks for an individual pay decision, a raw salary lookup, band architecture or re-leveling, or a pay-equity audit, or supplies only an internal employee roster with no classified external salary records.

Write a crisp, plain-prose redirect note of roughly 160 to 230 words across three or four short paragraphs. Include no benchmark table, no bullet lists, no invented market figures, and no repeated employee names or individual salary amounts. Cover five essentials smoothly:

1. **Separate each distinct ask and route it to its right owner:** raw salary lookups go to a market-survey vendor or compensation data provider; individual pay/raise decisions go to managers and HR; band or level redesigns go to job architecture (which must define finished levels before benchmarking); pay-equity audits go to a dedicated equity review with legal counsel.
2. **Anchor directly to the requester's specific context:** name the requester's specific role families (at role-family grain only, zero personal names or salaries), home metro, and company financing stage when explaining what external market evidence is missing.
3. **Explain why an internal roster cannot anchor a benchmark:** note the count of attached employee roster rows as individual-grain records ineligible for band benchmarking -- held against itself, a roster reflects who stayed at current pay (a survivors' list) rather than external hiring cost; quoting market numbers without audited outside data would mean fabricating figures, so deferral is the only defensible posture until evidence arrives.
4. **Name the exact required inputs, evidential weight, eligibility rules, and output tiers:** ask for external pay records classified by source, signal kind (distinguishing revealed-preference accepted offers from aspirational employer-posted ranges), date (so stale or undated records outside the data window can be excluded), role/level, metro, financing stage, and annual base basis (flagging equity-heavy mixes, stage equity-structure differences such as early options vs later RSUs, retention awards, or contractor rates as non-taxonomy exclusions), alongside internal role-family salary bands (floor, midpoint, ceiling, headcount), recent hiring offers, and the data window. Explain that the resulting analysis produces per-family competitive, lagging, above-market, or deferred verdicts with complete funnels and High, Medium, Low, or Deferred confidence tiers (weighting offers ahead of ranges, capping range-only rows, and noting that narrow local/stage pools can remain thin or deferred).
5. **Meet any stated deadline and protect privacy:** name which local, same-stage records to gather first (accepted offers ahead of postings) -- or advise telling stakeholders that market evidence is not yet classified -- and remind the requester to remove named employee salaries before sharing files into a benchmarking workflow.

## Sample Redirect 1 -- Multi-Ask Roster with Band Redesign and Deadline

Input summary: HR lead at Alderbrook Ceramics (Series A, 62 staff, Cleveland OH) pastes five named employees with salaries, asks what local market salaries pay, whether ceramics engineers are underpaid, to split a two-level engineering band into three levels, and for a full board analysis by Friday with no external data classified yet. Roles on roster: Ceramics Engineer, Kiln Automation Engineer, Materials Analyst.

# Alderbrook Ceramics: external evidence needed before benchmarking

This request folds four separate pieces of work together, and none can be benchmarked from what is attached. Looking up general local pay belongs to a market-survey vendor or compensation data provider; checking whether specific engineers are underpaid is an individual pay review for managers and HR (and any demographic fairness question belongs in a pay-equity audit with legal counsel); splitting two engineering levels into three is job-architecture work that should come first so finished levels exist to test; and the board analysis waits for classified outside evidence.

The five attached individual roster records are personal-grain pay lines ineligible for a band benchmark and cannot confirm or refute underpayment. Held against itself, an employee list shows only current incumbents -- a survivors' list rather than external hiring cost -- and quoting market salaries without an audited external pool would mean inventing figures, so the only sound posture today is Deferred.

To run a role-family benchmark for Ceramics Engineer, Kiln Automation Engineer, and Materials Analyst against Cleveland Series A peers, send classified outside records listing source, record type (separating revealed-preference accepted offers from aspirational posted ranges), date (so stale or undated records outside the data window are excluded), title and level, metro, stage, and annual base pay (excluding non-FTE contractor rates, retention grants, equity-heavy mixes, and unadjusted stage equity-structure differences such as early-stage options vs later-stage RSUs), plus internal band ranges (minimum, midpoint, maximum, headcount), recent company offers, and the eligible date window. That delivers per-family competitive, lagging, above-market, or deferred calls with full funnels and High, Medium, Low, or Deferred confidence tiers -- prioritizing offers over postings, capping range-only verdicts, and recognizing that a thin Cleveland Series A pool may keep sparse families deferred.

For Friday's board deadline, gather Cleveland Series A accepted offers first for those three engineering and analytics families, or inform the board that market evidence is not yet classified. Please strip individual names and salaries before sharing files into a benchmarking workflow.

## Sample Redirect 2 -- Single-Employee Lookup

Input summary: Engineering manager at Northstar Marine Acoustics (Halifax NS, Series B) asks what senior sonar technicians earn locally to decide a counter-offer for one employee.

# Northstar Marine Acoustics: outside benchmarking scope

Asking what senior sonar technicians earn to price one employee's counter-offer combines a raw salary lookup (best handled by a market-survey vendor) with an individual pay decision owned by the manager and HR under internal policy. Role-family benchmarking evaluates pay bands against audited external evidence rather than pricing a single person, so no market verdict can be issued here.

To test your Senior Sonar Technician band against Halifax Series B peers, provide the band's low, middle, and high figures with headcount, recent hiring offers, your date window, and a classified table of outside offers and job postings (showing date to filter stale rows, location, financing round, and annual base salary, keeping out contractor or equity-heavy packages). That returns a band-level verdict, complete evidence funnel, and High, Medium, Low, or Deferred confidence tier -- placing revealed-preference offers ahead of advertised postings -- to guide recruiting without setting one person's counter-offer.

Both sample notes begin directly at `# ` followed by the company name. Your next message is the standalone redirect note for the requester and nothing else: its very first characters are `# ` and the company name, with no greeting, no preamble, no horizontal rule, and no closing question.
