# The return note, shown once in full

Halden Dental Labs is made up; borrow the shape and the reasoning, none of the facts.

## What arrived

"Here is our lab team at Halden Dental Labs (Series A, Boise ID)", then five names, each with a title (Dental Ceramist Senior, Dental Ceramist Mid, CAD Designer Senior) and a salary. "Our lab director is convinced the ceramists are underpaid. What does the market pay? We also want to add a level to the ceramist band. The owners need a market view by Thursday."

The paste holds no band and no classified outside signal, so there is nothing to weigh.

## The note that was sent

## Not a benchmark request: Halden Dental Labs

Nothing here can be benchmarked yet: it is five people and their pay, with no classified outside signals, and it asks for four separate things.

1. **What the market pays dental ceramists and CAD designers.** That is a market-rate lookup, not a benchmark. Recruiting or a pay-data provider collects it; a number quoted from memory could not be traced by anyone.
2. **Whether the ceramists are underpaid.** Five salaries neither prove nor disprove the lab director's view. A benchmark reads bands by family and level and never a named person, so what any one individual should earn stays with their manager and your people lead.
3. **An added level in the ceramist band.** That is band design, owned by whoever runs your pay structure. A finished benchmark informs that work; it does not do it.
4. **A market view by Thursday.** It depends on evidence nobody has gathered yet. Start with Boise signals at Series A for Dental Ceramist, accepted offers ahead of job ads, since offers carry the weight; if they are not in by Thursday, tell the owners the review is waiting on data.

**What to send.** Bands (minimum, midpoint, maximum) for Dental Ceramist Senior, Dental Ceramist Mid and CAD Designer Senior, in place of the named salaries, which stay out of the benchmark. Outside signals, one per row: source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, role and level, place, company stage, and the figure with its pay basis. Your own recent offers, accepted and declined, and the window that applies.

**What comes back.** For each of those three family and level cells (two families, three cells) a position (lagging, competitive or above market), the evidence funnel behind it and a confidence tier, or a deferral where too little counts, with accepted offers weighed ahead of posted ranges. Until the evidence exists, all three cells stay deferred.

## Before yours goes out

Every separate ask gets its own numbered line with the kind of work and who owns it. No salary from the paste is repeated, no figure of any kind appears, and no data vendor or person is named. The families, levels, place and stage are the request's own, counted from the roster rows: each family once, each family and level cell once.

Now send the note for the request in front of you. Its very first characters are "## Not a benchmark request: ". Nothing stands before the title, there are no rules or dashes, and nothing mentions this skill, its references or these checks.
