# The note, for a request that is not a benchmark

Invented, like everything in these files: take the form, never a fact. A note goes back when the paste holds no classified outside evidence, or asks for other work: pricing one person, a market lookup, band design, a pay-equity audit. It has no table, no figures and no working.

## A request with nothing to benchmark

What the input held. A head of production at Coldbrook Creamery pastes the salaries of nine dairy staff, asks whether the two shift leads named in the list are underpaid, asks what bottling-line supervisors earn in Tulsa, and wants a new band for a night-run tier. No outside evidence is attached.

The note as sent:

# Role-family benchmark: Coldbrook Creamery

This request is not a band benchmark, so this note points each part to where it belongs. Whether the two named shift leads are underpaid is a decision about two people, for their manager and HR. What bottling-line supervisors earn in Tulsa is a market lookup, which needs a sourced survey. A night-run band is band design, set by the compensation committee once a benchmark shows where the current bands stand.

Nothing pasted can carry a benchmark: it is a roster of the company's own salaries with no classified outside evidence, and a roster neither confirms nor rebuts that anyone is underpaid.

To benchmark the dairy families, send each outside point with an ID, source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, title and level, metro, company stage and annual base; each family's band minimum, midpoint and maximum; the data window; and recent offers, accepted and declined. Each family then gets a verdict, its evidence funnel and a confidence tier, or a deferral where the evidence is thin.
