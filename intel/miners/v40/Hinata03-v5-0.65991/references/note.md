The note as sent:

# Role-family benchmark: Coldbrook Creamery

Nothing in this request can be benchmarked yet, so this note sends each of its four parts to where it belongs. Whether your two shift leads are underpaid is a decision about individuals, and it stays with their manager and HR, who can weigh each person's record. What bottling-line supervisors earn in Tulsa is a market lookup: it needs sourced evidence, and a figure quoted from memory would be a guess you could not defend. The night-run band is band design, which your compensation committee can take up once a benchmark shows where the current bands stand. And the market study your owners expect by Friday waits on the same missing evidence; the date decides what to gather first, not what counts.

What you pasted is a roster of nine named salaries with no classified outside evidence, and a roster on its own neither confirms nor rebuts that anyone is underpaid.

For a benchmark of the dairy families, send each outside point with an ID, source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, title and level, metro, company stage and annual base, along with each family's band minimum, midpoint and maximum, the data window, and your recent offers, accepted and declined. Each family then gets a verdict with its evidence funnel and a confidence tier, or a deferral where the evidence is thin; until that evidence arrives, every family is deferred. Please send bands in place of the named salaries: a benchmark reads families and levels, never a person.
