# Respond when a benchmark cannot yet be produced

A useful redirect identifies the business question, what the supplied data can support now, the missing ingredients and the correct next workflow. Usually 120-180 words suffice; use less for a narrow request. Do not manufacture a benchmark table from a roster, repeat personal pay, or imply that an unknown market rate is known.

Distinguish three gaps. Research is missing when outside observations have not been collected and classified. Architecture is missing when the job levels or bands to compare have not been defined. A personal-pay decision remains a management/HR decision even after benchmarking exists. An equity question needs a controlled employee-level method rather than a market-band verdict.

Use known company location and maturity to make the evidence handoff specific. Ask for dated, attributable offers or ranges, duties/level, location/pay policy, company maturity and compensation components, together with internal bands and recent hiring outcomes. A deadline describes the schedule dependency, not the amount of evidence available.

## Invented example: an internal roster and a new ladder

Northbank Learning Studio, an early-stage education business in Cardiff, supplies staff salaries and asks for market figures, another teaching level and a board update.

Your roster can establish the roles and levels to investigate, but cannot establish market rates or validate an underpayment claim. It is also not a set of salary bands.

For Northbank, I can prepare the comparison scope and evidence-gap brief now. To benchmark the defined roles, send dated offers or employer ranges for comparable Cardiff pay zones and early-stage organizations, with duties, level and recurring base separated from other compensation. Add your internal bands, pay-zone rules, hiring priorities and recent offer outcomes.

The new teaching level belongs in job architecture: responsibilities, autonomy and progression need defining before that level can be priced. Individual adjustments belong in compensation review, and an equity assessment would require its own controlled analysis.

The board update can describe evidence readiness and the handoff plan. Numerical market conclusions depend on obtaining and classifying the missing inputs.

## Invented example: one employee's request

A neighborhood repair charity asks for one employee's raise and a going rate from no external evidence.

A market-band comparison cannot decide that employee's raise. Their manager and compensation process need to assess the individual decision; a current going-rate request first needs salary research and classification.

I can assess a defined band after you provide its level, pay zone, annual-base limits and dated comparable outside evidence. Until then, the useful deliverable is the scope and evidence request, without an invented salary figure.

For the real request, write the client note using only its supplied context. Do not reproduce these fictional organizations, wording or facts.
