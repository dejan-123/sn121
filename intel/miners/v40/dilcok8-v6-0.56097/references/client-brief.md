# Turn the register into a client brief

The final answer is for the person deciding what evidence to trust. Use the completed register as the source of record. Do not introduce a new calculation, reassign an ID, or change a verdict while polishing prose. If a contradiction appears, repair the register first.

Open with the principal finding and its consequence. Explain the decisive evidence in a small number of paragraphs before or around one benchmark table. Group families when the evidence supports a shared explanation; do not write an identical paragraph for every row. Name the actual offer or posting and its ID where it matters. Describe accepted offers as accepted pay observations and ranges as advertised pay. Avoid converting these limited samples into population claims.

The benchmark table needs family/level/pay zone, verdict, worked midpoint gap with anchor IDs, complete evidence funnel and confidence with its deciding reason. A concise funnel can include its IDs and exclusion reasons in the same cell. Every retrieved ID must remain accountable; every partial or excluded record needs its reason somewhere in the answer. Keep detailed four-dimension assessments in the register unless needed to explain a contested decision.

Include a Deferred comparisons section. Name each unsupported unit, distinguish a missing market anchor from a missing internal band, and specify the evidence that would settle the gap. If none are deferred, say so and name the thinnest supported comparison and its next useful observation.

Use one Evidence limitations section for source concentration, disclosure selection, geography, recency and sparse samples actually present. Say how they limit the decisions. Keep common qualifications here instead of repeating them per row. Mention hiring priorities and internal outcomes where they change the practical interpretation, without recommending individual raises or redesigning bands.

Aim for the shortest answer that remains reconstructible, usually about 450-750 words for several families. Do not omit decisive facts to hit a word target. Exclude the working register, instructions, examples and file-reading commentary from the final response.

## Example brief: Alderbridge Language Systems

### Alderbridge: engineering needs attention; delivery has thin advertised support

Senior Language Platform Engineering is modestly below the comparable accepted-offer average. Service Delivery Lead is within the planning tolerance, but its evidence is one advertisement rather than a completed hire. The P1 engineering search deserves the earlier evidence refresh; a Competitive midpoint alone does not establish that a band will close candidates.

This comparison uses Bristol, Series C and annual GBP employee base at the supplied April 2026 snapshot. June 2025 is included in the window, while the May 2025 Delivery offer hire-h is excluded. The 3% tolerance is an analytical planning convention, not statistical significance.

Engineering has three comparable accepted offers: GBP 62,500 (hire-a), GBP 64,750 after removing the stated GBP 5,250 annual bonus from GBP 70,000 cash (hire-b), and GBP 69,250 after dividing EUR 83,100 by the supplied 1.20 EUR per GBP quote (hire-c). Their arithmetic mean is GBP 65,500. Both the components and exchange direction are explicit; none required an inferred split or external exchange rate. The GBP 67,000 center of notice-d supports the direction but remains outside that offer average.

The GBP 63,000 engineering midpoint is therefore GBP 2,500, or 3.82%, below the anchor. This is a narrow Lagging call, with a GBP 62,500-69,250 observed offer span. The company's own GBP 66,000 and GBP 67,500 acceptances sit above its midpoint, reinforcing the P1 hiring-pressure interpretation without adding external observations. The voluntary, adjacent-stage advertisement notice-e is excluded by two qualifications; it cannot pull the average toward its advertised limits.

Delivery has no full comparable offer. Notice-f's GBP 44,000-52,000 range yields an advertised estimate of GBP 48,000. The internal midpoint is GBP 1,000, or 2.08%, below it, within tolerance. The higher GBP 55,000 offer hire-g is adjacent-stage context; the input supplies no defensible stage adjustment. It raises a question worth checking without becoming the anchor. The contractor charge rate-i describes a different arrangement and is not annualized.

### Deferred comparisons

None. Delivery is supported but thin. A recent employee offer for the same Lead duties at a Bristol Series C employer, with identifiable annual base, would test the advertised estimate most directly.

### Evidence limitations

The engineering observations have explicitly distinct employer provenance, but remain a small convenience sample. Delivery's range is advertising and gives no accepted-pay distribution. Mandated, voluntary and unspecified disclosure descriptions affect which records enter this conservative comparison; they do not establish population coverage. The exclusions and retained context prevent the stale offer, contractor charge or adjacent-stage figures from creating unsupported precision.

## Different evidence, different emphasis

When a family has no full evidence, lead with the missing comparison rather than offering a price. When actual offers conflict with advertisements, explain why the accepted-offer series anchors the decision and discuss the advertisement separately. When the internal band is missing, report the market observation without giving the company a competitive-position label. These answers need different explanations even though their arithmetic controls are the same.
