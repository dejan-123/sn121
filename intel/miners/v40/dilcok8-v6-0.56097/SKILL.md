# Prepare the evidence before writing the compensation brief

Treat the assignment as a comparison between defined jobs and markets. The deliverable must let an HR partner explain which observations support a conclusion and which do not. An employee roster, a recruitment advertisement and an accepted offer answer different questions.

## Route the request

Check the requested decision and the supplied inputs first. Market benchmarking needs classified outside evidence and internal bands at family/level grain. A roster does not establish either market rates or a compensation ladder. Individual raises belong in compensation review; salary research needs collection and classification; band architecture needs duties and level definitions; pay equity needs its own controlled analysis.

If the request lacks benchmarking prerequisites, first read `references/scope-intake.md` with read_reference. Give a short client note identifying what the supplied material supports, the missing evidence and the correct handoff for each unsupported ask. Use supplied company context and acknowledge deadlines without promising unsupported figures. Do not print a market table or reproduce employee names and salaries. For mixed requests, complete supported comparisons and route the rest separately.

## Use two working stages for a supported benchmark

1. Read `references/record-lab.md` with read_reference. Build an evidence register for this input: comparison settings, one factual assessment per external ID, family decision cards, then reconciled totals. This register is an intermediate work product, not the client brief.
2. Once the register reconciles, place its compact facts and calculations in the working message accompanying a read_reference call for `references/client-brief.md`. That guide governs the final answer. Transfer the established decisions into the brief without recalculating them from memory.

Use the examples to learn the operations. All company names, records and amounts in the references are invented teaching data, not facts for another assignment. Obtain every fact for the real brief from its own input.

## Establish each comparison

Separate family, level and pay zone. Record the internal minimum, midpoint and maximum, annual-base currency, company maturity, dated snapshot, hiring priorities and internal offer outcomes. Team-specific pay policy overrides headquarters. A headquarters address can support an explicitly limited local comparison; it does not locate an unmapped remote workforce. Defer that population until family, band and pay-zone mapping is supplied.

Use the stated cutoff and as-of date. A rule excluding months before a named month includes that month. Do not move a supplied cutoff by recomputing an approximate lookback from today's date. Undated or stale observations remain in retrieved counts and are excluded with their reason.

Read each record's own title, type, date and notes before assessing it. Apply a table-wide qualification unless its row expressly overrides it. Preserve supplied classifications without claiming independent verification. Resolve clear abbreviations and explicit scope mappings; do not invent duties or pay-zone equivalence.

## Assess eligibility consistently

Use a supplied comparability policy first. Otherwise use the following conservative analytical convention, and disclose that convention briefly in the method note. Assess four dimensions as matched, qualified or incompatible:

- Geography: the same pay market or an explicitly shared remote anchor matches. Unanchored remote evidence or another named metro in the same employment market is qualified context, never a local anchor. An unsupported cross-country comparison is incompatible.
- Company maturity: the same financing stage matches. One adjacent round qualifies; two or more rounds apart, or public versus private, is incompatible. Do not manufacture a maturity mapping from headcount alone.
- Role scope: equivalent duties and level match. A neighboring level or specialty qualifies. A title spanning different responsibilities qualifies unless the input resolves its scope. Reordered words and clear abbreviations alone change nothing. Materially different work is incompatible.
- Evidence form: an identifiable employee offer matches. A primary posted range with mandated or unspecified disclosure matches as advertising. An explicitly voluntary range is qualified under this conservative convention; publication motive is not proof of a realized hiring price. Hearsay or an untraceable survey is incompatible.

All matched dimensions make a full comparable. Exactly one qualified dimension and no incompatibility makes partial context. Multiple qualifications or any incompatibility exclude the record. A missing essential fact is unresolved: count the record among exclusions with the missing fact stated, rather than letting it disappear. Missing employer identities alone do not erase an explicitly classified offer, but limit source assurance.

Independently exclude structurally different pay: contractor charges, one-time retention or acquisition awards, and inseparable equity/variable-pay packages. A separately stated recurring employee-base component can remain usable. An OTE label with no split does not identify base. Give unfamiliar arrangements the same economic test rather than relying on a memorized list.

## Put usable observations on one basis

Use annual recurring employee base in the band's currency. Show each required equation, original basis, supplied component or factor, direction and result. Apply it to both range endpoints.

- Subtract a stated non-base component from total cash. A bonus fraction of base requires total cash divided by one plus that fraction; a fraction of total cash requires multiplication by one minus it.
- Annualize monthly employee base using the stated pay periods, or hourly employee base using stated paid hours. Contractor annualization never converts a service charge into employee base.
- Multiply by target-currency units per source unit, or divide by source units per target unit. Use only a supplied exchange quote. A foreign team compared with evidence in its own currency needs no exchange operation.

Do not infer missing splits, hours, rates or dates. Notes describing the components override a misleading headline label. Reject an unconvertible amount with the precise missing information.

## Reconcile and decide

Assign every external ID to its relevant family before exclusion; keep distinct requested levels separate. Deduplicate confirmed repeat events, retaining their IDs as exclusions linked to the canonical record. A common feed does not alone prove duplication. For every family verify:

retrieved = full comparables + partial context + excluded.

Give each excluded record one primary reason, with secondary flags if needed. Internal offers enter neither these counts nor external anchors.

Use full accepted offers as the primary series. Compute their arithmetic mean and observed span. Keep full postings beside that series as a separate check; never average advertisements into accepted offers. If only documented made/declined offers are available, identify them as offered pay, not accepted pay, and qualify confidence. If no full offers survive, average the full posted-range midpoints and identify an advertised-market estimate. Partial offers cannot activate offer priority. No full evidence means no market anchor.

Let M be the internal midpoint and A the chosen positive anchor. Show M - A and 100(M - A)/A; negative means below the anchor. Unless supplied policy differs, use a disclosed 3% planning tolerance: below -3% is Lagging, above +3% is Above-market, otherwise Competitive, inclusive at the boundaries. Determine the label before rounding. Near a boundary show enough precision to expose sensitivity; the tolerance is not statistical significance. Band overlap is context, not proof of a competitive midpoint or a market percentile.

Do not substitute internal accepted offers or employee salaries for a missing band. A center derived from stated band endpoints must be labeled. Report an observable external estimate separately when the internal comparison is missing. Defer an unsupported comparison or a substantive unresolved conflict and identify what would settle it.

High confidence needs multiple corroborating full accepted-offer observations with no known dependence or material basis conflict. One full offer is ordinarily Medium. Posting-only evidence is capped at Medium for multiple coherent observations, or Low for one. A documented dependence or serious dispersion can lower these tiers. Do not invent employer independence; state common-source limitations once. Partial records and internal outcomes never raise confidence. Low is a supported but thin estimate; Deferred means no supported position.

Interpret contrary partial evidence without promoting it. Do not assume a financing round explains a salary difference. Use internal offers to explain hiring pressure and priorities after the external verdict is established. A candidate's account of competing pay remains a claim, not a measured rival salary.

Before the reporting read, verify IDs, exclusions, cutoff dates, conversion direction, support-series membership, arithmetic, tiers and the family/pool identities. The brief must agree with these decision cards.
