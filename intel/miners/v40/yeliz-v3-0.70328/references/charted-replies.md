# Finished replies: the boatyard and the glassworks, and a note for an ask with no chart

Every company, city, figure and date here is invented. The first two replies are what the reader received once the sheets in `references/sounding-sheets.md` were closed; the third is the note for a request that needs no sheet. They show form and size only. What carries over to a live reply:
- a `## ` title naming the company and its verdicts in words, then an opening paragraph: the home metro and round, the opening month and what it struck, the rows setting no price, and each conversion with its sum and direction;
- two to four argued paragraphs, firmest call first, each opening on what decides it, with accepted offers named revealed preference and posted ranges aspirational the first time each is weighed, volunteered ranges called partial, a partial's lean given in a clause, and hearsay named as such;
- one table whose gap cell shows its sum with the anchor's IDs and whose funnel counts all four piles by ID, then a `Pool:` line;
- Deferred Verdicts, always present, and Signal Caveats said once for the pool;
- a last line answering the loudest figure that was dropped;
- for a request outside the chart, a short note giving a judgment, what the paste shows, and what to send.

No company, city, job title, figure or date carries over. Each comes from the request in hand, never from this file.

## Reply three: an offer sign-off with no market evidence

What the operations director at Riddlewick Escape Rooms sent. Riddlewick is a Series A escape-room operator with 60 staff in Chattanooga TN. "Can you sign off this offer? We'd like to hire a Site Manager at $63,750 base plus a $2,900 relocation payment, and our COO thinks the band is too tight, so should we lift its maximum to $68,500? Bands are below; we have no market data yet." Bands given (minimum / midpoint / maximum): Site Manager $52,800 / $57,400 / $62,600; Lead Game Master $38,700 / $42,100 / $45,900; Puzzle Designer $55,200 / $60,300 / $65,700. No evidence table came with it, so no sheet was kept.

The note:

## Riddlewick Escape Rooms: an offer sign-off and a band change, not a benchmark

You asked for sign-off on a $63,750 base offer, with a $2,900 relocation payment, for one Site Manager candidate, and for a view on lifting the band maximum to $68,500. A benchmark can settle neither. It places role-family bands against classified outside evidence, and none arrived with the request; it speaks for families, not for one person's offer; and a new maximum is band design, which sits with whoever owns compensation.

What the bands themselves show: the proposed $63,750 base is $1,150 over the Site Manager maximum of $62,600 and $6,350 over its $57,400 midpoint. Whether that reflects the market's price or a premium is the question evidence would answer. The relocation payment is one-off money and stays out of any base comparison.

To benchmark the Site Manager, Lead Game Master and Puzzle Designer bands, send:
- the evidence, one line per point: its source, offer or posted range, the month, the role and its level, the metro, the employer's funding round, the figure with its pay basis, and whether a range was posted under a law or by choice;
- the home metro and any roles with no metro tie, the months the evidence may span, and the date it was pulled;
- Riddlewick's own offers in that window, accepted or declined, with any reason given.

With those, each family gets a verdict, a gap and a confidence tier. Where the maximum should sit stays with the band owner.
