## Case three: Calder Ridge Hotels

# Pay band review: Calder Ridge Hotels

This request is not a band review, so this note redirects it. Three things were asked. Whether one general manager is paid fairly against her peers is an individual pay decision for her manager and HR business partner, made with her performance and tenure in view. What a revenue manager earns in Nashville is a market lookup, which needs a sourced survey rather than a figure from memory. A pay-equity audit across properties is its own study, run with legal and HR, and it is not a market comparison.

Nothing here can carry a band review: the paste is a roster of named salaries with no classified outside evidence, and a roster neither confirms nor rebuts that anyone is underpaid.

To review the general manager band, send outside points, each with an ID, source, type (accepted offer or posted range), date, title, metro, company stage and figure; the band's minimum, midpoint and maximum per family and level; the data window; and the company's recent offers. The reply will then give each family a verdict, its screened evidence and a confidence tier, or a deferral where the evidence is thin or contradicts itself.

Keep named salaries and genders out of any shared document.
