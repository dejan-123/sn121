# Build supported compensation claims

Check scope first. Benchmark bands against classified external evidence. For individual pay, raw salary research, band design or pay equity, give the boundary, next workflow and missing inputs in two to four sentences, at most 120 words, without a table. Keep incomplete segments in a valid benchmark.

## Define the claims before drawing conclusions

A segment is family, level and pay zone. Preserve band, midpoint, stage, currency, assessment date and priorities. Separate materially different levels. Scope claims by segment, population, pay measure, currency, facts or IDs and condition or blocker. First inspect the complete relevant pool.

| Claim | Required evidence | What can survive a missing link |
|---|---|---|
| Geographic coverage | Comparable external labor market and applicability of the internal band to that pay population | A metro anchor and comparison conditional on the band applying there |
| Annual-base facts | Employee base and period, component, currency and workload terms | Supported amounts in their own units |
| External anchor | Eligible canonical observations on a common annual-base basis within one market and currency | Native-currency or external-only anchor without FX or internal midpoint |
| Internal pay position | Known or conditional population, compatible internal comparator and defensible anchor | Narrow anchor-relative result when a broad market claim is unresolved |
| Confidence | Supported position, source type, recency, independence, coverage, agreement and secure basis | Confidence for the supported position. Otherwise Deferred. |

Apply compatible explicit policies for classification, admission, source choice, estimator, bounds and confidence. Name exceptions. Keep structural exclusions and eligible-offer priority. Invent no amounts, rates, dates, markets or source facts. Scope deferral to the failed dependency.

## Qualify the full evidence pool

Honor retrieval attribution or use the most specific supported segment. Keep retrieved wrong-level rows as scope drops and correctly assigned other-level rows in their own pool. Count unassigned IDs with reasons. Use role-description scope and level mappings. A common grade cannot override conflicting duties. Internal hiring signals pressure outside external counts and anchors, without disclosing rival pay.

Even for drops, score G/S/R/T (geography, stage, scope, type) as 2 match, 1 limited, 0 mismatch or ? unresolved.

| Axis | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Same metro or explicit shared pay anchor | Broader market or unanchored remote | Other market without documented equivalence |
| Stage | Same funding stage | Adjacent stage | Further away or private/public mismatch |
| Scope | Equivalent duties and level or explicit mapping | Limited overlap | Materially different work or level |
| Type | Identifiable offer or primary employer fixed or range posting | Identified, dated secondary evidence with method | Known hearsay or survey lacking attribution or method |

Four 2s gives full. Any 0 is incomparable. Essential unknowns are ?, blocking eligibility. Otherwise eligible limited matches are partial context by default. Explicit compatible P admission preserves class and count and needs a cap. Platform alone establishes no independence.

Separate event date, as-of and window. A snapshot date supplies no cutoff. Use supplied cutoffs, including the boundary month when earlier ones alone are excluded. Drop undated, out-of-window or unresolved required timing, naming the cause. With no required or supplied lookback, retain eligible dated records with any age caveat. Keep the best-documented original of each confirmed duplicate event, with its date and status. Break ties by stable ID and link dropped copies. Distinct events from one employer share an origin.

Exclude contractor, one-time, retention, acquisition and inseparable OTE or mixed pay. Documented ordinary employee base can survive. Detailed terms control labels. Limit claims to the compared measure.

Normalize both sides to compatible annual employee base using supplied period, currency, components and workload. Keep ordered endpoints, positive factors and supported relationships. Show the original amount, factor, direction, equation and result once.

- Multiply monthly pay by stated annual payments, and eligible hourly pay by stated annual paid hours. Use only supported workload adjustments.
- Subtract known non-base amounts from compatible totals. For bonus fraction r of base, divide total cash by (1+r). For r of total cash, multiply by (1−r). Require the stated denominator and component relationship.
- Multiply by target currency per source unit or divide by source currency per target unit. Use supplied FX only. Currency conversion establishes no geographic equivalence.

Declare pool segment, annual basis and currency. R counts retrieved observations before filtering, including copies. F is eligible full for that pool, P retained partial, D dropped, S = F + P. Check R = F + P + D. Give one primary reason per D, keep other material flags and reconcile reasons to D. Cover unassigned IDs. Count each ID/segment once and distinguish memberships from unique inputs when reusing IDs.

## Construct the supported anchors and positions

**Anchor.** Use the median of all eligible full offers plus any explicitly admitted P. Retain documented candidate-declined offers with status. D and context-only P cannot activate priority. Eligible postings remain F or P context. Without admitted offers, median eligible annual fixed points or posted range midpoints. Preserve point or endpoints. Average the middle pair for even counts. Label posting results provisional advertised pay.

**Missing FX or pay facts.** Evaluate same-currency candidates independently. An unconvertible offer drops from the common-currency comparison and cannot block an eligible local-currency posting. Retain valid native-currency facts. If a narrower external anchor is useful, verify the same market, basis, timing and eligible IDs separately within its native currency. State that scope and currency. Explain how the records are treated in the comparison funnel. Reusing facts adds no retrieved IDs or independent origins. Never blend currency numbers without supported conversion.

**Internal comparator and coverage.** Use a compatible supplied midpoint or label the mean of valid endpoints. If an official midpoint is required and missing, defer that position and preserve descriptive facts. A metro anchor has its own scope. Require internal band-to-zone mapping for an unconditional position. Put HQ proxy or aggregated-band conditions beside the verdict. Preserve metro anchors and defer unmapped coverage. Infer no remote family or employee assignments.

**Signed position.** For compatible annual units and positive anchor A, use signed_difference = internal annual midpoint − external annual anchor. Use signed_gap_percent = 100 × signed_difference ÷ external annual anchor. Check that internal below anchor gives a negative gap, and that gap × A ÷ 100 recovers the signed difference. The default author labels are competitive from −5% through +5% inclusive, lagging below and above-market above. With active bounds L% and U%, compare against A × (1+L/100) and A × (1+U/100) with supplied inclusion. Classify before rounding.

**Source disagreement.** A contrary lower-priority posting alone leaves a defensible offer-relative classification intact. Show that classification and any unresolved broader market claim together in its row. Qualify the offer-relative confidence or representativeness and attach deferral only to the blocked claim. Recheck scope, date, pay basis or origin when context reveals an actual contradiction. If selected evidence cannot support a defensible direction, explain the missing or conflicting dependency. Dispersion alone is insufficient. Conflicting partial-only evidence defers under the default.

**Confidence.** Give High, Medium, Low or Deferred with a specific reason scoped to its position. High requires multiple recent independent full offers, secure basis and coherent corroboration. Thin offers normally get Medium. Unknown independence and posting-only cap at Medium. Weak but defensible evidence or one posting gets Low. Use the compatible cap for admitted P, default Low. External-only facts carry no position confidence. Advertised bounds imply no paid-market percentile.

## Present the scoped benchmark

In one row per segment give internal basis, scoped funnel, anchor IDs and calculation, gap, position and confidence with conditions. Keep supported narrower claims. Audit every ID with scores, disposition, reason/use and material basis or date facts. Group equivalent rows with IDs visible. List blocked claims and required inputs, or say none. Give one pool caveat. Show genuine conversions and useful overlap once.

Backtrace claims to scope, facts and IDs. Check compatible units, positive anchor, signed arithmetic, active bounds, counts and admission from F or explicitly allowed P.
