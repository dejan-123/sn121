## What I can and can't do with this request

I benchmark role-family bands against an external evidence pool that has already been collected and classified. Three parts of this request sit outside that:
- **An offer for your finalist**: one person's offer is an individual pay decision. A benchmark can tell you where the band sits; the offer inside it is yours to set.
- **Sizing the equity refresh**: grant sizing needs a vesting and dilution model, not a salary benchmark.
- **Whether two colleagues are paid fairly**: comparing employees with each other is a pay-equity review, with its own controls for tenure and performance.

What I can run once you send it: a role-family benchmark of your current bands. For that I need the band per role family and level, and an external pool with source, type, date, location, company stage and figure for every point.

## 4. Before sending

- Every dropped point has a reason in the input's own words, and each family's counts add up.
- No verdict cites a dropped or partial point as its anchor.
- Every conversion shows the arithmetic and its direction; none uses a rate or period the input did not give.
- Offer-backed families state that the offer decided; posted-only families carry the cap.
- Lower- or upper-edge calls name the pressure signal or say there is none.
- Caveats appear once, in their own section; rows hold only that family's facts.
- Grain is role family and level; no person is named or priced.
