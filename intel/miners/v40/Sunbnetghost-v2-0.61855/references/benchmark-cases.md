## Case 4: a payroll extract and three asks, with no outside evidence

The input:

Ferrowind Turbine Services is a B-round company with 85 staff in Duluth MN. Message from its finance director:
"The board meets on the 14th and wants to know our wind technicians aren't underpaid. Please also build a band for a new Senior Blade Inspector level, and tell me what to offer Kai Brandvold, whom we're promoting into it. Payroll extract below; we have no outside data yet."

The reply:

# Ferrowind Turbine Services: not a benchmark yet

Nothing here can be benchmarked yet. What arrived is a five-person payroll extract covering two technician levels and one inspector level, with no classified outside pay evidence.

Your note contains three asks, and each belongs somewhere different:

1. Whether wind technicians are underpaid is the market question. I can answer it here once evidence arrives, as a verdict for each technician level against Duluth employers at your stage.
2. A band for a new senior inspector level is band design. It follows from how the work differs between levels, which no market table settles, so it belongs with whoever owns your job architecture.
3. What the promoted inspector should be offered is an individual pay decision. It belongs to that person's manager and finance, working from records this benchmark does not hold.

The extract cannot stand in for market evidence. It shows what you pay the people who stayed, so it can neither clear nor confirm the board's worry. Names also belong outside a benchmark document.

Please send:
- outside points for each technician and inspector level, each with an ID, source, kind (accepted offers kept apart from listed ranges), month, title and level, city, funding round and annual base;
- the low, middle and high of your band for each level;
- your own accepted and declined offers from the past year;
- the dates the evidence must fall within.

Before the board meets on the 14th, collect accepted offers from Duluth B-round employers first, since they carry the most weight. Each level will then come back with a verdict, its funnel and a confidence tier, or with a deferral that names what is still missing.

## Notes on writing the reply

Open with the verdicts the reader asked for. Then give each family a paragraph that shows its evidence, using the IDs, months, places and figures of the input in front of you.

Let the length follow the difficulty. A family settled by two clean offers needs little. Some steps need writing out: a converted figure, a stated-bonus rescue, a probable re-post, and a gap that sits near the 5% line.

Set each tier by counting independent survivors. A single surviving point is Low however strong it looks, and a range never lifts a family above Medium. Count each funnel from your own paragraphs and add up the Pool line before sending.

The open-requisitions section speaks only in figures the input gave. A redirect stays brief, builds nothing and quotes no market figure. The message starts at its title.
