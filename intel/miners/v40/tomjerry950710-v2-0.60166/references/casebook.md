## C. Tidewater Veterinary Group (a redirect, sent in full)

The practice manager writes: "Our clinic leads think the female associate vets are paid less than the men. Can you confirm? Should Dr. Rhodes get a raise to match a rival clinic's offer? And what do associate vets earn nationally? The board meets Monday." A staff salary list is pasted below. There is no classified outside evidence. No ledger is needed; this is the whole reply.

# Tidewater Veterinary Group: what this request needs first

Three requests are bundled here, and none of them is a role-family benchmark:

1. **Pay differences between women and men.** This is a pay-equity audit for the people lead, usually with legal counsel. It needs its own method and data.
2. **Whether one vet should be raised to match a rival offer.** This is an individual pay decision for the clinic lead and HR.
3. **What associate vets earn nationally.** This needs classified market evidence, and none came with the request.

A list of current salaries shows what the practice pays. It cannot show where the market sits, and on its own it cannot settle the equity question either.

To benchmark the associate-vet band, send:
- outside rows, each with ID, source, kind (accepted offer or posted range), month, title, place, funding stage and figure;
- band minimum, midpoint and maximum for each family and level;
- your own offers from the past year;
- how far back the evidence may go.

Before Monday, the most useful thing to gather is accepted offers for associate vets at comparable practices in your own region.
