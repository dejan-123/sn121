# Benchmark the full pool, then test source dependence

Check scope first. For individual pay, raw salary research, band design or pay equity, give the boundary, workflow and missing inputs in two to four sentences, at most 120 words, with no table. Keep empty or incomplete segments in a valid benchmark.

## Establish the ordinary benchmark

Keep segment grain at family, level and pay zone throughout. Keep supplied pay, stage, dates and priorities. Follow retrieval attribution or the most specific supported match. Keep wrong-level near-misses as scope drops in their retrieval pool and assigned sibling levels in their own pool. Use role descriptions and supplied mappings. Common grade alone cannot override conflicting duties.

Separate band applicability from labor-market fit. Put HQ proxy or mixed-band conditions beside the verdict. Preserve metro anchors and defer unmapped populations without inferring their family or level. FX supplies no labor-market match.

Score each record G/S/R/T independently of timing and pay basis. Use 2 match, 1 limited, 0 mismatch, ? unresolved.

| Axis | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geography | Same metro or explicit shared pay anchor | Broader market or unanchored remote | Other market without documented equivalence |
| Stage | Same funding stage | Adjacent stage | Further away or private/public mismatch |
| Role | Equivalent duties and level or documented mapping | Limited overlap | Different duties or level |
| Type | Identifiable actual offer or primary employer fixed or range posting | Attributed dated secondary evidence with method | Known hearsay or survey without attribution or method |

Four 2s gives full. Any 0 excludes the record. Essential unknowns block eligibility. Otherwise eligible limited matches are P context by default. Internal recruiting outcomes can indicate hiring pressure outside external counts and anchors. Do not infer rival compensation.

Separate observation date, assessment date and eligibility window. Include a boundary month when earlier ones alone are excluded. Exclude undated, out-of-window or unresolved required timing and explain the blocker. A snapshot alone supplies no lookback. In the absence of a required or supplied window, keep eligible dated observations with an age caveat. For confirmed copies, retain the best-documented original event date and status, break equivalent ties by stable ID, and link each dropped copy. Distinct events can share an origin.

Exclude contractor pay, one-time grants, acquisition or retention packages and inseparable OTE or cash/equity. Detailed terms control labels. Keep eligible separately stated employee base. Limit claims to the measure compared.

Normalize internal and external pay to compatible annual employee base using supplied period, components, workload and FX only. Keep both normalized range endpoints. Show material inputs, direction, operation and result once.

- Multiply monthly pay by stated annual payments and eligible hourly pay by stated annual paid hours. Require supported workload terms for adjustments.
- Subtract documented non-base amounts from compatible totals. If bonus r is a fraction of base, divide total cash by (1+r). If r is a fraction of total cash, multiply by (1−r). Require the actual denominator.
- Multiply by target currency per source unit or divide by source currency per target unit. Missing FX blocks that currency comparison. Keep separately supported native facts and evaluate eligible same-currency fallback.

R counts retrieved observations before filtering, including copies. F is eligible full in the declared segment and comparison basis, P eligible partial, D dropped, S = F + P. Check R = F + P + D. Give each D one primary reason and reconcile reason counts. Show secondary flags without recounting. Distinguish unique inputs, segment memberships and unassigned IDs.

**Anchor.** Use the record-level median of all eligible full actual offers, retaining documented candidate-declined offers and status. Full postings stay F context. Without eligible full offers, use the median of eligible canonical posting values, each a normalized fixed amount or range midpoint. Preserve shape and label advertised pay. Average the middle pair for even counts. Use F by default. Disclose compatible explicit provisional exceptions, evidence class and cap. Keep full-offer priority and source classes separate. Advertised ranges establish no paid-market percentile.

**Position.** Keep a compatible supplied midpoint or label the arithmetic mean of valid endpoints. If a required internal basis or anchor is unavailable, preserve supported external facts and audit. For comparable units and positive anchor A, difference = internal annual midpoint − A. Gap percent = 100 × difference ÷ A. Check the subtraction order and recover the difference with gap × A ÷ 100. The author default is competitive from −5% to +5% inclusive, lagging below and above-market above. Apply compatible supplied bounds and inclusion to the base and every removal. For L% and U% use A × (1+L/100) and A × (1+U/100). Classify before rounding and show precision near an edge.

**Ordinary confidence.** Give High, Medium, Low or Deferred with the deciding reason. High requires multiple recent independent full offers, corroboration and secure basis. Thin offers normally get Medium. Unknown independence and posting-only cap at Medium, one posting at Low. Weak defensible comparisons get Low. Apply ordinary-tier policy and caps first. Recheck scope, date, pay basis or provenance if an essential premise is contradicted. Defer unsupported comparisons and say why. Keep a supported offer-relative position when contrary postings limit broader claims. Scope confidence and deferral to each affected claim.

## Review the origins behind every anchor

Freeze the anchor, its class and canonical contributing IDs, base gap and verdict, original inventory and funnel. Report provenance even without a supported internal comparison. Within that exact segment and class, group contributing IDs disjointly by verified origin, merging documented shared employers or provenance. Identify unresolved origins separately. A vendor platform, contextual evidence or record count supplies no independence.

Report contributing count and known groups with ID links. Use all canonical contributors as the denominator. With complete grouping give exact shares. Otherwise give confirmed minimum shares, unattributed count and established concentration without assuming a distinct-origin total.

**Gate.** Require a supported base comparison, complete grouping and at least three known-independent contributing origins. After each removal at least two such origins must remain. If blocked, state the actual reason. A skip implies no stability claim, automatic downgrade or deferral. Keep ordinary evidence limits, including concentration.

**Select before computing outcomes.** With three to six proven origins, select every group. Above six, seed with the group contributing the most canonical values, the lowest within-group median and the highest within-group median. Resolve each tie by ascending stable origin ID and deduplicate the seeds. Fill to six distinct groups by descending contributor count and ascending stable origin ID, skipping selected groups. Use a stable supplied name if no identifier exists. This author heuristic permits at most six removals per segment.

**Recompute.** For each selected group remove all its contributors together from the full set. Recompute the record-level median, signed gap and active bounds using the remaining eligible records in that class. Reclassify the comparison. No contextual postings, newly promoted partials or copies. Restart at the full set each time and retain the original benchmark.

**Qualify.** If any tested omission changes classification, apply one step down once to ordinary confidence, High to Medium or Medium to Low, with Low as floor. Keep the full-pool verdict and describe source dependence. Explicit compatible sensitivity rules can override that default. Apply governing final-tier constraints after sensitivity and disclose any departure. Stable or skipped results confer no upgrade. A flip alone does not invalidate an otherwise supported comparison.

## Report benchmark and sensitivity scope

Give one row per segment with internal basis, R/F/P/D/S, numeric anchor, IDs and calculation, gap, position and confidence. Audit all IDs with scores, dispositions, conversions, reasons and use. Show meaningful overlap and shared caveats once. List blocked claims and required inputs, or say none.

Per anchor, distinguish provenance from the numeric review outcome. Report tested origins, k of N when established, and mention untested origins explicitly when k < N. For unresolved provenance report known groups, unattributed observations and skip status. For every flip give affected IDs, remaining independent-origin count, counterfactual anchor, signed gap and classification. Show ordinary-to-final confidence once. For stable results give the tested scope. The result covers tested single-origin removals under active bounds. It establishes neither joint-removal robustness nor statistical uncertainty.
