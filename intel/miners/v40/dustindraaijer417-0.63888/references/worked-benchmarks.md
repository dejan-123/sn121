## Before you send

Most benchmarks turn on a handful of calls, and each has a wrong twin that reads almost the same. An offer and a posted midpoint in one family never share a median: the offer anchors alone and the midpoint only checks it. A partial point, an offer included, never anchors and never confirms. Another metro is a mismatch even for a company that hires unanchored remote roles; only a range with no metro at all can be partial. One of our own accepted offers within 5% of an offer anchor makes it High, while a posted anchor stays Medium whatever agrees. A point dropped on the screen never reappears among a family's survivors. And the reply starts on its `#` title, the scope note included: whatever made it a scope note is said inside it, never above it.
