# Working sheets: a chocolate maker and a hearing-instrument maker

Every company, city, figure and date below is invented. A sheet is working paper, written as its own message before the reply and never sent as part of it. Before any family is touched it sets every row's month against the opening month and copies every row's Notes exactly as printed, because most of what decides a point is written there: a law, a volunteered posting, a missing city, a round apart, a bonus inside a total. Then it assigns each row to a single family, takes the families in the snapshot's order, and finishes with the pool totals and the lines the reply draws on. Its last words are "Sheet closed; the reply follows.", and the same message opens `references/charted-replies.md`.

Marks used on a line: same, a step off, or out, on place, stage, scope and posting. No step off: counts. One step off: partial. Two steps off, or one out: dropped. A row that is no price at all drops before any test. Every point line starts from its ID and then its note in brackets, so the note is in view before any mark is set.
