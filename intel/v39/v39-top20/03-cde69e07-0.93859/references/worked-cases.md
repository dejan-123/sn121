# Five worked cases (invented; take the form and the reasoning, never the facts)

Every account, person, figure and date below is made up, and none of them belongs in a real reply. Copy the form: the reading that goes contact by contact and says why each tier holds rather than the one the title suggests, the figures worked from their counts, a table holding only the named contacts, gaps drawn from the deal's size and stage, a plan that opens with the unblocking move, the sponsor call, and a closing that gives the reasons. Copy no fact, name or figure.
