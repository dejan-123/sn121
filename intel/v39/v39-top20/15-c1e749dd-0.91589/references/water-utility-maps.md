# Water-utility maps, invented

Every organisation, person, amount and count below is invented and appears nowhere else. The two maps show page layout, proportions and the kind of restraint a record calls for; none of their particulars belongs in a reply.

## Map one: a nine-site expansion while the champion changes jobs

The record, in short: Ostreham Moor Water Board already runs our alarm-telemetry software at one treatment works ($61K a year). The proposal extends it to all nine treatment and pumping sites, adding $214K a year; stage: proposal under review. Casimir wrote that he approves operating spend up to $300K a year. Counts are given per contact. Notes refer to "the other eight site managers" without naming any. The commissioning review with Henrike is already booked for the 14th.

## Map two: a renewal with a small add-on, where restraint is the answer

The record, in short: Brantwick Fen District Water renews its laboratory sample-tracking module ($17K a year) and adds a second bench ($6K), $23K in total; stage: quote requested. Marguerite Olawale, Laboratory Services Lead, wrote that she approves lab spend up to $30K a year; 9 sent / 8 replied, last reply 2 days ago; she has twice asked how a rival prices per bench. Ptolemy Quennell-Sallis, Depot Supervisor, uses the module daily and asked for the add-on bench; 3 sent / 3 replied. A support ticket from "P. Quennell" also asks for a depot login. The requester wants each person's influence as a percentage. No procurement, IT or legal condition appears.

The parts of the page that carry the restraint:

```
## Coverage gaps against the approval path
Coverage fits. Marguerite's written limit covers the $23K total, and no gate is named. No
finance, IT, legal or executive contact is needed on this record. Trigger: a total above
$30K, or a procurement condition, would change this section.

## Engagement sequence
1. Send Marguerite both quotes: the renewal and the add-on bench. We owe them.
2. Answer her per-bench question about the rival in the same message, plainly.
3. Ask Ptolemy whether the depot login ticket from "P. Quennell" is his before creating anything.

## Executive sponsor call
No separate sponsor is needed; Marguerite approves this amount herself.
```

Marguerite is Authority graded A. Ptolemy is an Influencer graded B: he asked for the add-on and answers every message, but nothing shows him carrying it further, and he holds no authority over it. Two contacts, one signer inside her own limit: no single-thread warning. The percentage request gets one sentence saying that influence cannot be measured as a percentage from this record, and the table stands as the supportable part.

## What carries over

Disclosure and restraint. Each grade quotes its counts, each tier names its behaviour, and a reader can check every call against the record. A gate closed in writing is stated once and leaves the plan; a spoken or conditional OK stays open until it is written down at a contact already booked. A role without a name stays a gap. A sponsor is named when authority over the amount, engagement and a path all appear, and the page says plainly when they do not.
