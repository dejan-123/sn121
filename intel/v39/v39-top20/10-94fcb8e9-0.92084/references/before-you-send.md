# Before you send

Run the map you are about to write past each line below, without commenting on it, then write. Each line is a call that maps like this often get wrong.

1. Names, titles, dates and counts all come from the record; counts and recency are quoted in its words, no gap between two dates is computed, and a role mentioned without a name keeps no name.
2. The economic buyer is the person a stated limit, a described sign-off or a requested business case points to. A finance team that has to accept how the cost is booked is reviewing; a remark that senior management wants a last look points to nobody in particular. If no one qualifies, write "buyer not established" and ask the champion.
3. A champion whose offer to take the deal higher has not yet produced a meeting keeps the label with "reach unproven", and one step asks for that meeting: who and when.
4. A senior figure who backed the project and passed it to a colleague is an executive sponsor working through that colleague, and their silence is normal. Someone senior who was warm once, passed nothing on and now goes unanswered is a sponsor gone quiet, with both signals in the evidence cell.
5. A person who sat silent and leaves follow-ups unanswered is quiet with position unknown, not a blocker, and is approached through someone who works with them.
6. A test passed in writing stays passed; a CRM stage still saying pending is named as out of date, and the tester gets no further requests.
7. A small purchase inside its champion's own limit has no gaps: one sentence says so, and the plan adds nobody.
8. Single-threading is stated in the opening and repaired in step 1; if the single contact is also the buyer, one sentence says why the risk does not apply.
9. Whatever we owe goes back first, or goes in the step 1 message to the same person.
10. A real conflict shows both signals, which counts for more, a provisional call, and the step that will settle it.
11. The sponsor section judges both sides: who backs the deal from above on their side, and someone from our leadership only when the record gives them a counterpart and a way in.

Then write the reply: nothing before it, and its first characters are `# `.
