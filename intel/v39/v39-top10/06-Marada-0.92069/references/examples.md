# Four worked cases (invented; form only)

Every company, person, date and number below is invented. The setting is one vendor selling collections-management software to museum trusts and galleries. Copy the shape of the reasoning, never a fact: in a real reply every name, count and date comes from the input in front of us.

Three notes the cases lean on:

- **Delegation.** A senior who met us once and passed the work to a colleague has delegated, not left. The rationale reads "title suggests decision maker, behaviour shows the evaluation was handed to <name>", and their strength comes from their own counts, not the delegate's.
- **Named, never contacted.** A person the input names with no contact at all is still a table row, strength "None, never started". A person mentioned only by role is never a row; they go under `Heard of, never met:` and Coverage gaps.
- **The reading carries the reasons.** The person-by-person reading holds the two sentences of evidence; the table cell then needs only one short clause pointing to the deciding signal.
