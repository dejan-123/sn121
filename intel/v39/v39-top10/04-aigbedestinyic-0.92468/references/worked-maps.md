# Worked maps (invented accounts; form only)

Four made-up accounts. Copy the form: a short reading, a rationale for every row that names the deciding signal, a conflict stated with the act that settles it, gaps measured against what the purchase needs, a sequence that opens on the blocker, a sponsor paragraph that recommends. Copy no name, figure or phrase; in a real reply each one comes from the material supplied. A reply starts with its first heading and nothing ahead of it.

## One: a large deal, four people

Supplied for Corran Aggregates (quarrying, 1,400 staff), $640K a year, fleet maintenance planning, shortlist of two, award expected in March:

- CRM: Ines Dorrance, Operations Director, 9 emails sent / 8 answered, last touch 6 days. Malcolm Reyes, Group Treasurer, 1 / 0, 118 days. Tariq Belmont, Fleet Planner, 8 / 7, 3 days. Greta Olund, Head of Legal, 3 / 0, 44 days. Stage field: "commercials agreed".
- Notes: 4 Sept, Ines opened the first meeting with her own downtime log and passed our cost model to three plant managers that week; she signs to $250K, "past that it is Malcolm". 19 Sept, Tariq sent a long list of integration questions and chased us for answers. 29 Sept, Greta was invited to the terms call and sent apologies an hour before; our supplier-terms form went to her afterwards. 21 Oct, Ines: "I'm pushing it." 6 Nov, Ines: "Finance hasn't seen numbers yet, leave it with me." She added that the plant managers' forum "will want a say"; no names.

The reply:

# Corran Aggregates, $640K shortlist: a willing backer without the signature

## What the record shows

Four people are on record for a $640K award due in March against one other shortlisted supplier, and only two of them have ever answered us.

Counts: Ines 8 of 9, Tariq 7 of 8, Greta 0 of 3, Malcolm 0 of 1 with 118 days since any touch. $640K against Ines's $250K limit is $390K over.

Referenced, not reachable: the plant managers' forum (Ines, 6 November: "will want a say"), no member named.

**Single thread.** Ines Dorrance is the only path to the signer, Malcolm Reyes.

| Person | Influence tier | Strength | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malcolm Reyes, Group Treasurer | T1 decides | 0 | "Past that it is Malcolm" makes him the signer at this value; never replied. Conflict: the stage field says commercials agreed, yet on 6 November Finance had not seen numbers. The dated note weighs more; ask Ines to confirm and correct the field. |
| Ines Dorrance, Operations Director | T2 drives, champion | 3, fading | Two acts on 4 Sept (her downtime log, our cost model passed to three plant managers). The title overstates her: she cannot sign past $250K. Her last two notes hold intentions and no step, so the score follows them, not August. |
| Tariq Belmont, Fleet Planner | T2 gates, technical fit | 4, steady | The most junior and the most engaged: a long list of questions, and he chases us for the answers. |
| Greta Olund, Head of Legal | T2, provisional | 1 | Conflict: the title points to a terms gate; the conduct is apologies for the one call she was asked to and 0 of 3. Conduct weighs more for the tie. Whether she reviews supplier terms herself is not on record; ask Ines who does. |

**Coverage gaps.** A $640K award above the champion's limit needs the Group Treasurer's signature, a technical yes and agreed terms, and Ines says the forum has a voice. Against that: the signer has never spoken to us; the forum has no named member (ask Ines who chairs it and when it next meets); the terms form has no confirmed reader.

**Engagement sequence.** The block is that the one person who can sign has seen nothing, and only Ines reaches him.

1. Ines, on "leave it with me": ask for one dated act, a numbers session with Malcolm that she hosts. Her limit makes it unavoidable. Nothing goes to Malcolm directly before it.
2. Ines: who in Legal handles supplier terms day to day; resend the form, unreturned for 44 days, to that person. Independent of step 1.
3. Ines: the forum's chair and date, and whether Tariq's answers should be tabled there. Depends on step 1 being booked.
4. Tariq: close the open integration questions so the technical yes exists in writing before the forum.

**Executive sponsor.** None on their side yet: Ines behaves like one and lacks the authority, and the person who has it has not engaged. Keep our own executive back until step 1 is booked, then pair them with Malcolm in that session; an approach before it would be cold.

## Two: a small purchase

# Fennick Veterinary Group, $19K at trial: one buyer, one checker

## What the record shows

Counts: $19K sits $6K inside Odile's $25K authority. Referenced, not reachable: nobody.

| Person | Influence tier | Strength | Rationale |

**Engagement sequence.** Nothing blocks it but the trial itself.

1. Build Thursday's trial on Odile's own rota, from her photographs.
2. At the end of it, ask her what paperwork a purchase inside her limit needs, and by when.

**Executive sponsor.** Not needed on either side: the signer is already in the room, and an executive of ours would add weight this purchase does not call for.
