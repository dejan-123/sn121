## The pattern across the four

- The reply opens on the reading, sentences only: one paragraph per named contact, then the parties with no record, then the deal's value, stage, ceiling and open conditions.
- The table holds named people and nobody else; a role in someone's sentence or an unnamed attendee is a gap.
- Each tier names the conduct that earned it; each warmth score quotes replies against sends, the last touch and a direction, and counts that flatter are called out.
- What we owe goes first, the quiet approver is reached through whoever holds access, and when one contact controls every route to an approver who is not that contact, the reply says single-threading.
- The small deal keeps to a line of coverage, two moves and "At this size, no separate executive sponsor is needed."
- The sponsor paragraph names someone only on dated conduct; otherwise it says nobody qualifies and what would change it.
