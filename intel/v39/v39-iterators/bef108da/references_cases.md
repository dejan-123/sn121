# Worked cases: a lab-data platform vendor selling to research universities

The seller in every case is Quillstone, which sells a lab-data platform (instrument data capture, electronic lab notebook, retention controls for grant-funded data). All names are invented. Each case shows the pasted input, then the complete reply.

## Coverage gaps

**Needed for this deal:** at $455K past a pilot, a budget holder, a champion with reach to that holder, a user validator, security sign-off, and the purchasing step the account requires above $250K.

- **Budget holder unintroduced.** The Associate VP for Research Administration controls the money at this size (Tomas, 2026-04-08) and has no CRM record or contact with us. This is the deal's decisive gap.
- **Single-threaded on the decision (structural risk).** The evaluation has three live two-way threads (Tomas, Ines, Rhiannon), but every line to the budget holder and the committee runs through Tomas alone. This is not one more missing seat: if Tomas changes role or loses patience, the deal has no path to its money.
- **Sole-source justification open.** Required above $250K; Gideon has not engaged. Security is not a gap: cleared 2026-03-27.
- The Vice Provost is not a missing approver on the evidence; she handed the deal to Tomas in writing.

## Engagement plan

1. **Tomas, to reach the Associate VP for Research Administration (gap)**: repair the single thread first. Answer his 2026-04-16 request with the cost-per-lab breakdown before April 24, and in that same reply ask him to introduce us to the AVP through a joint briefing he hosts ahead of May 6, with Ines presenting pilot results. Signal: the reply is owed, the breakdown is what the budget holder asked for, and Tomas already has his ear (2026-04-08). No dependency. No direct outreach to the AVP.
2. **Gideon, through Tomas**: ask Tomas for the sole-source justification template and who drafts it, then send Gideon the vendor facts it needs with Tomas copied. Signal: 0 of 1 replies cold; the requirement came from Tomas. After step 1.
3. **Ines**: ask whether a second core facility manager would back the pilot figures to the committee, a second line beyond Tomas. Signal: her unprompted weekly reports. After step 1.
4. **Adaeze Whitcombe**: no new ask. Send a one-paragraph pilot summary through Tomas after the committee decision, only if he advises it. Signal: her delegation in writing; four unanswered sends make a fifth counterproductive.

## Executive sponsor recommendation

Inside the account, Tomas Ekwueme-Lund is the working sponsor: he has carried the deal since January, briefs the budget holder, and the Vice Provost named him point in writing, though his title does not sign at $455K. The budget seat, the Associate VP for Research Administration, cannot be judged as a sponsor until met. Our own executive should join the step-1 joint briefing at Tomas's invitation, peer to the AVP, and not approach the Vice Provost separately, since her one reply asked us to work through Tomas.
