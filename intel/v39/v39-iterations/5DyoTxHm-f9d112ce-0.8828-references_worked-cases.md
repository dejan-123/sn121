# Five worked cases (invented; take the form and the reasoning, never the facts)

Every account, person, figure and date here is made up. A reply goes straight out with nothing before it, no sentence about what comes next: its first characters are `## Reading ` and the account's name, or, for the note in case four, `# ` and the account's name. Take the form: the reading that goes contact by contact and says why each tier holds rather than the one the title suggests, the figures worked from their counts, the table with only the named contacts, the gaps drawn from the deal's size and stage, the plan with the unblocking move first, the sponsor call, and the closing that gives the reasons. Take no fact, name or figure; in a real reply every one comes from the paste in front of you.
