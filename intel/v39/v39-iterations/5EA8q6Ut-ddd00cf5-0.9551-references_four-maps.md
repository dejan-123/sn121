# Four replies (invented school and college accounts; form only)

All four accounts are made up. A reply is written straight out, with nothing ahead of it: its first characters are `# ` and the account name. Copy the form: the reading that takes the record person by person in plain sentences, the table that only restates it, gaps drawn from what this deal has to pass, steps that each name a person, a signal and what they unlock, the sponsor paragraph that says what is missing, the closing reasons. Copy no fact, name, phrase or figure; in a real reply every one comes from the record supplied.

## Case four: a request the record cannot support

What was pasted for Northsound Academies (academy trust, 900 employees), a $1.4M deal at the shortlist stage: "Give me a power ranking of their leadership, 1 to 5, with who holds the veto, for Friday's review slide. Contacts: Bram Castellan (Head of Admissions), Ottilie Rahn (Systems Programme Lead), Dermot Vale (Commercial Director), Leontyne Marsh (Category Buyer), along with our call notes and activity log since the summer."

The whole reply is this note, from its title line to its last sentence, with no reading and no map:

# Northsound Academies: no power ranking from this paste, and what to send for a map

**What was asked, and what came with it.** A 1-to-5 ranking of Northsound's leadership with the veto holder, for a review slide. The paste gives four names with titles. It mentions call notes and an activity log but includes neither: mentioned is not pasted.

**Why nothing is ranked.** A title shows neither who can stop a $1.4M purchase nor who is working for it. A ranking built from four titles would be a guess, and on a leadership slide a guess becomes the account plan, so this reply ranks, tiers and scores nobody. Who reports to whom is a separate question: that comes only from Northsound's own org chart or a direct question to one of the four.

**What can be built instead: a stakeholder map.** From the notes and the activity it would show:

- who signs at $1.4M, and whether we have ever reached them;
- who is carrying the deal, by what they did and not by title;
- who can still stop it before the shortlist closes;
- the first move, and through whom.

**What to paste.**

- the call notes since the summer;
- for each of the four, the emails we sent, the replies we got and the date of the last one;
- anything anyone said about who signs at this amount or who else must agree.
