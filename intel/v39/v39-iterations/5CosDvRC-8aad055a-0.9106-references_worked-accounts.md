### The reply

# Bramwell College of Art and Design - no RACI can be built from this record yet

You asked for a RACI of Bramwell's steering group for tomorrow's forecast call. What arrived is three names with titles; the discovery notes are mentioned but were not pasted, so I have not read them.

A RACI says who is accountable and who signs, and titles cannot tell us that. "Registrar" or "Head of Digital Services" says nothing about who will approve a $1.1M purchase or who is pushing for it, and a guessed grid on a forecast call becomes the plan everyone works to. So this note assigns no roles, tiers or scores.

To build it, paste the discovery notes and, for Ezra, Lotte and Anouk, the emails we sent, the replies and the date of the last one, plus anything anyone said about who approves at this amount or which reviews come before an order. From that I can show who is actually carrying the deal, how strong each line is, what is missing and the first move - a better answer to the forecast question than a grid of titles.
