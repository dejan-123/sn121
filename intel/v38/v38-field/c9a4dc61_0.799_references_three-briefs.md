# Three briefs (invented workplaces; form only)

All three cases are made up. A brief is written straight out, with nothing ahead of it: its first characters are `# ` and the report's name. Copy the form: the title built from the week's signals, the header that combines and computes, the one line that keeps, overturns, drops or routes every draft item before any item is written, the sentence budget, the parking lot that holds only what failed the venue reading. Copy no fact, name, phrase or figure; in a real brief each one comes from the inputs supplied.

## Case one: full inputs

What the manager supplied for Ilse Brandvold, Pharmacy and Inventory Lead at Marrowfield Veterinary Group:

- Draft agenda: "Kudos - she's been great lately" / "Status: controlled-drug cabinet audit" / "Status: anaesthetics supplier switch" / "Quick feedback: weekly stock-count sheet came in late" / "Coaching: her weekend handover notes need work" / "Development: she asked what a practice manager role would take".
- Action log: file the cabinet audit pack with regional compliance (Ilse, DONE Tuesday); move anaesthetic order lines to the new supplier (Ilse, IN PROGRESS, 8 of 20 moved); order the replacement vial scanner (Manager, DONE); send Ilse the practice-manager role profile (Manager, OVERDUE).
- Figures: 3 order lines moved this fortnight against a trailing average of 12; 9 lines on hold until the landlord's contractor installs the cold-storage unit.
- Messages: Tuesday, Dr. Anneke Voss (regional compliance officer) in #clinical-ops: "Ilse's cabinet audit pack reconciled to the vial - first one this year with nothing to chase." Monday, Ilse in #facilities: "Cold-storage install has slipped again; contractor gives no date. Nine lines can't move until it's in." The stock-count sheet was posted after the Friday noon cutoff on the 6th and again on the 13th. Saturday, the weekend locum Dr. Piet Marais in #clinical-ops: "Handover note left out the two open ampoule boxes - found them at the evening count." No other message mentions a handover note. Thursday, Ilse to the manager: "Count sheets: both late ones were weeks when the Saturday delivery got moved to Friday morning. I should have said so."
- Ilse's own topic: "What would I need to show for a practice manager role?"

The brief:

# Ilse Brandvold, Pharmacy and Inventory Lead: supplier switch stalled on the cold-storage install

**Context.** The cabinet audit is closed and praised, but the supplier switch slowed to 3 order lines this fortnight against a trailing average of 12 (down 75%), with 9 lines waiting on a cold-storage unit the landlord's contractor has not dated. In the action log the audit pack and the manager's scanner order are closed, and the switch is open at 8 of 20 lines (40%).

**Manager owes.** The practice-manager role profile for Ilse, overdue: bring it or name the day.

**From the draft.** "Kudos - she's been great lately" -> kept as Recognition, but on Dr. Voss's Tuesday message, not on "great lately"; "Status: controlled-drug cabinet audit" -> dropped: the log shows it done on Tuesday, and it is now the evidence for item 1; "Status: anaesthetics supplier switch" -> not a status item: 9 lines wait on the install, so it is an Unblock the manager owns; "Quick feedback: stock-count sheet" -> not a one-off: the 6th and the 13th make two instances, so Coaching, asked as questions; "Coaching: weekend handover notes" -> not coaching: Saturday's message is the only dated instance, so a single Feedback observation; "Development" -> kept as Career, Ilse's own question, so it gets protected time.

## Agenda for Ilse: the audit result first, then the stalled switch, the two observations, and her own question

**1. Recognition: cabinet audit pack**
Dr. Anneke Voss wrote in #clinical-ops that the pack "reconciled to the vial - first one this year with nothing to chase". Worth saying to Ilse in those words, with what it spared: a follow-up round with compliance.

**2. Unblock: anaesthetics supplier switch**
Ilse told #facilities on Monday that the contractor gives no date. Manager's move: call the landlord's facilities contact this week and bring back a dated install, or approve interim storage so the 9 lines can move.

**3. Feedback: weekend handover note**
Dr. Piet Marais wrote on Saturday that the note left out the two open ampoule boxes, which he found at the evening count. One instance is one observation, not a skill to coach: state it, restate the standard that the note lists every open box, and leave it there.

**4. Coaching: stock-count sheet timing**
- "You linked both late sheets to the Saturday delivery moving to Friday morning: what happens to the count when that delivery lands?"
- "You wrote on Thursday that you should have said so: who needed to hear it, and by which day?"

**5. Career: practice manager path**
- "Compliance had nothing to chase in the audit pack: which parts of the practice-manager role does that already show, and which does it leave untouched?"
- "The supplier switch has you dealing with facilities and the landlord: is that the kind of work you want more of?"

## Parking lot for Ilse
Empty this week: nothing in these inputs is pay, a level decision, a personal matter or someone else's process to fix.

## Case two: a draft with almost no signals

What the manager supplied for Tomas Wekesa, Life-Support Systems Technician at Harbourlight Aquarium:

- A note: "Short on time, no figures pulled. Draft: 1. Filtration retrofit - where are we? 2. Coaching: he seems checked out lately. 3. Feedback: Sunday's water-quality log was missed. 4. He asked about regrading his shift allowance - should cover that. 5. Development chat."
- One message, Monday, Tomas to the manager: "Sunday's log is missing because I was on the quarantine-tank pump failure all shift. Back-filled the readings from the probe data this morning. Won't happen again without a note to you."
- A second message, Tuesday, the curator Dr. Maren Halloran to the manager: "Sunday's gap showed up in the weekly water report. I'd like us to review how logs get covered during incidents."
- No action log, no figures, no other messages.

The brief:

# Tomas Wekesa, Life-Support Systems Technician: one missed log, already back-filled, and a retrofit with no figures

**Context.** Two messages are all that was supplied: on Monday Tomas explained that Sunday's water-quality log was missed while he worked a pump failure on the quarantine tank and that he had back-filled the readings from probe data, and on Tuesday Dr. Maren Halloran asked for a review of how logs get covered during incidents. No action log, no figures and no other messages came with the draft, so no completion rate or trend can be stated and the retrofit item has nothing behind it yet. Before the meeting, pull the retrofit task list for item 2 and the log record for the past month for item 1. There is no opener, because nothing supplied credits a specific piece of his work; the shift-allowance question and the curator's review both leave this meeting.

**Manager owes.** Nothing recorded: no action log was supplied.

**From the draft.** "Filtration retrofit - where are we?" -> kept as Status, though no signal was supplied; "Coaching: he seems checked out lately" -> dropped: no dated instance, and the one message shows him covering a failure and repairing the record unasked (an observed instance would bring it back as Feedback); "Feedback: Sunday's water-quality log" -> kept as Feedback and not raised to coaching: one instance, already owned; "regrading his shift allowance" -> parking lot, pay; "Development chat" -> kept as Career, though Tomas supplied no topic. From the messages: Dr. Halloran's request for a review -> parking lot, her process to run.

## Agenda for Tomas: the one evidenced item, then two open questions

**1. Feedback: Sunday's missed water-quality log**
The log was missed during the pump failure, and Tomas back-filled it on Monday and offered a note to the manager next time. Accept that, and restate the one standard: a missed log is flagged within the same shift, even mid-incident. It is a first occurrence, so it stays a single observation; the review Dr. Halloran asked for is routed below and is not argued here.

**2. Status: filtration retrofit**
- "Where does the filtration retrofit stand against its task list, and is any part of it waiting on someone else?"

If part of it is waiting, the item becomes the manager's to unblock.

**3. Career: development chat**
- "Which part of the filtration retrofit would you want more of in your next role here?"

## Parking lot for Tomas
- Shift-allowance regrading (he raised it) -> a dedicated pay conversation with HR's input; book it this week and give Tomas the date. Nothing on pay is drafted here.
- Review of how logs get covered during incidents (Dr. Halloran asked for it) -> a separate session with Dr. Halloran, who owns the procedure; book it and bring Tomas's account of Sunday.

## Case three: only the notes of the last meeting

What the manager supplied for Odalys Ferreira, Volunteer Programme Coordinator at Greywater Botanic Garden: the notes of their meeting on 4 March and the line "prep next week's 1-on-1 from this". The notes record: spring volunteer sign-ups at 45 against 36 in autumn; Odalys asked for a decision on weekend shift leads so she can finish the training rota (manager to decide by 10 March); manager to ask facilities for a second set of tool-store keys by 7 March; Odalys to draft the induction handout by 11 March; and that Odalys mentioned a bereavement and may need some days away this month.

The brief:

# Odalys Ferreira, Volunteer Programme Coordinator: three commitments from 4 March to confirm, two of them the manager's

**Context.** The only input is the notes of the 4 March meeting, so every status below is unconfirmed and no completion rate is stated. Those notes record spring sign-ups at 45 against 36 in autumn (up 25%); that was discussed then, so it is context and not an opener. Three commitments fall due before the next meeting: the manager's decision on weekend shift leads (10 March), the manager's request to facilities for a second set of tool-store keys (7 March), and Odalys's induction handout draft (11 March). No draft agenda, messages or figures for this week were supplied, so the agenda is those commitments and nothing more.

**Manager owes.** The shift-lead decision and the tool-store keys, both from 4 March: items 1 and 2.

**From the notes.** Odalys's request for a decision on shift leads -> answered by the manager's commitment, so Status and no item of its own; weekend shift-lead decision -> Status, the manager's, due 10 March; second set of tool-store keys -> Status, the manager's, due 7 March; induction handout -> Status, Odalys's, due 11 March; spring sign-ups -> header only, already discussed; the personal matter -> parking lot.

## Agenda for Odalys: the manager's two commitments first, then Odalys's own

**1. Status: weekend shift leads** (the manager's, due 10 March; confirm before the meeting)
Odalys asked for this decision so she can finish the training rota. Bring the decision, or the day she will have it.

**2. Status: second set of tool-store keys** (the manager's, due 7 March; confirm before the meeting)
Bring what facilities answered. If they have not answered, this becomes an Unblock and the chase is the manager's.

**3. Status: induction handout draft** (Odalys's, due 11 March; confirm before the meeting)
- "The induction handout was due on 11 March: where does the draft stand, and what in it depends on the weekend shift-lead decision?"

## Parking lot for Odalys
- A personal matter raised on 4 March (a bereavement; days away this month) -> a private conversation at a time Odalys chooses, with HR for the leave options; offer the time, and keep the detail out of this brief.

## What would change a class

- Had the cold-storage install carried a date, with no lines waiting, the supplier switch would be live work nobody is blocked on: a header clause, not an agenda item.
- Had the draft carried no kudos line, "Status: controlled-drug cabinet audit" would itself become item 1: Recognition, because the log shows it done and Dr. Voss's message credits it.
- Had a second locum reported a thin handover note on another date, item 3 would be Coaching, two questions, and still one item.
- Had a promotion case for Ilse already been in motion, only her own next step toward it would stay on the agenda; the decision would sit in the parking lot against its venue (calibration or a skip-level slot), with no talking points. Her question about what the role takes puts nothing there.
- Had Dr. Halloran asked for nothing, the missed log would be the same Feedback item and the parking lot would hold only the pay question.
- Had the role profile been sent, every manager row would be closed and the line would read "Manager owes. Nothing open."
- Had the draft carried "career check-in" with no question from Ilse, it would still be an item: one question setting the audit result beside where she wants to go next.
