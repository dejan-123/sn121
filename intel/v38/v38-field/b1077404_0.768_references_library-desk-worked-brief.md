## The brief as sent

# 1-on-1 brief: Tamsin Aldecott, E-Resources Librarian

*Built from your draft, the service-desk queue, the renewal tracker, the migration checklist, last week's actions and chat from Monday to Thursday.*

## Where things stand

Tamsin's queue is moving faster, not slipping: 21 e-resource tickets closed against a weekly average of 15 (+40%), first-reply resolution at 68% against 61% (+7 points), and none past the desk target. The slow stream is renewals, 1 item closed against a monthly average of 4 (-75%), and the cause is not her pace. Of last week's actions only two are open: the load test (IN PROGRESS, nothing in its way, so no meeting time) and your usage-cost figures (OVERDUE). The migration checklist is at 8 of 11 steps (73%). The live risk sits outside her control: the e-journal bundle is parked at IT security, the licence lapses at month end, and no input gives a review date.

**You owe:** the usage-cost figures, which item 2 depends on.

## Agenda

**1. Recognition: authority-file mapping** *(draft: status check; the checklist shows it done Tuesday)*
Hollis Brandt wrote in #catalogue-migration that the mapping came through clean, with not one record sent back. Name that, and that it kept the migration moving on its checklist.

**2. Unblock: e-journal bundle renewal** *(draft: status; the tracker shows it waiting on IT security's data-processing review)*
Tamsin has nudged IT security twice, so the next move is yours: ask the IT security lead for a review date that lands before month end. Send your overdue usage-cost figures the same day, so the case is complete when they clear it. Ask her: which clause in the vendor's terms is most likely to come back from that review?

**3. Career: agree the date** *(her Thursday message asks for proper time on the systems librarian track)*
Which week suits a full conversation on the track, and should you bring the job family descriptions she has been reading?

## Off the draft

- **Coaching on responsiveness**: dropped. The queue contradicts it (+40% closed, none past target), and on Wednesday she cleared the outage backlog and pinned an FAQ so the desk stops paging her.
- **Status on the load test**: moved to the header. In progress with no blocker, so a progress question would only take time from item 2.

## Parking lot

- **Systems librarian track** → a dedicated career conversation on the date agreed in item 3, with the job family descriptions and her migration and e-resources work as the evidence.
- **Change of working pattern next term** → a private conversation, then the HR flexible-working process if she wants to pursue it. No detail belongs in this brief.

## Why the calls went this way

The renewal was labelled status, but Tamsin's side of it is finished and the work is stuck at another team, so the move belongs to whoever can reach the IT security lead, with the manager's own overdue figures beside it. Work in progress with nothing in its way, whether the draft asks about it or it only shows in the log, is one clause of context. The responsiveness concern was an impression that three signals overturn, so it leaves the agenda with its reason in one line. The career request is serious, which is why it gets a room of its own instead of the last minutes of a weekly; the agenda only fixes the date. The working-pattern remark is personal, so the brief names the topic and the venue and nothing else.

## What would change the calls

**If the coaching concern had evidence behind it.** Late replies on two named patron threads in separate weeks would be a pattern, so a Coaching item would stay, asked as questions about the moment the backlog became visible and what would make raising it early the easy move. One late reply with nothing else around it would carry no coaching item at all, only a single question on whether that patron has had an answer.

**If the input were thin.** With only last week's notes, every action would read "status unknown, confirm", and a figure praised at that meeting would sit in the header rather than open the agenda. With no signals at all, the brief would name what to pull before the meeting and mark each question as waiting on a signal, and a pay question would appear only in the parking lot, routed to the compensation process.
