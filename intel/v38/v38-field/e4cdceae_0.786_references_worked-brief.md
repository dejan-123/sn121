# A worked brief (invented consultancy; form only)

The case below is made up to show the tests on paper. Copy the form - the order, the tags, how a relabel sits beside its item, how a figure carries its inputs. Copy no fact, name, figure or project: in a real brief every one comes from the input in front of you. The brief is written straight out: do not announce it or say what you are about to do, and put no notes ahead of it.

## The input, in brief

Report: Teodor Halloran, Structural Engineer, Buildings group at Marrow & Penn, a civil and structural engineering consultancy.

Manager's draft (typed note): "Teodor this week: start with a shoutout, he did something good on the basement job I think. Status on the retaining-wall calc package. Status on the podium slab RFIs, they're dragging. I want to coach him on attention to detail - the contractor says he's slow on site queries. He wants to talk about the bridges team. He also mentioned a family situation and maybe leave in the new year, let's sort that."

Action items from the last 1-on-1:
- Teodor: retaining-wall calc package to check - DONE, checked and issued Tuesday
- Teodor: propping-sequence sketch to site - DONE
- Teodor: log the abutment cracking in the defects register - DONE
- Teodor: footbridge site-visit report - IN PROGRESS, draft with the checker
- Teodor: close podium RFI-14, RFI-15, RFI-17 - OPEN
- Manager: share the fee split for the next scheme - DONE
- Manager: send the architect the signed fee variation for the penetration redesign - OVERDUE

Drawing-review tracker: 11 sheets through internal check this fortnight, 9 the fortnight before. Retaining-wall package issued with no checker comments carried forward.

RFI log: RFI-14, RFI-15 and RFI-17 all read "awaiting architect's revised penetration layout"; Teodor chased the architect's project lead on Monday and Wednesday.

Site-visit notes, Thursday: Teodor made an unplanned visit to the north abutment, found hairline cracking, logged it the same day and briefed the contractor's site agent.

Chat excerpts:

Teodor's own topic: "I'd like to talk about moving across to the bridges team at some point."

## The brief as sent

# Teodor Halloran (Structural Engineer, Buildings): 1-on-1 brief

**Context.** Teodor closed out every piece of the basement and abutment work that was his to close; the podium slab is the one stream stalled, and it waits on the architect, not on him.
- Source: draft note, action log, drawing-review tracker, RFI log, Thursday's site-visit notes, chat excerpts.
- Figures: sheets through internal check, 11 vs 9 the fortnight before (+22%).
- Open actions: the footbridge site-visit report (in progress with the checker, no blocker), RFI-14/15/17 (blocked, Teodor), my fee-variation letter to the architect (overdue, mine); the rest of the log is done.
- Gap: no pour date for bay C is given, so the RFIs' urgency rests on the contractor's message alone.
- Draft: 6 items - 2 relabelled, 1 dropped, 1 routed.

**Agenda**

1. **Recognition - retaining-wall calc package** (draft: status check → Recognition, because the tracker shows it checked and issued Tuesday)
   - Open with Hester Quayle's note in #structures: "the cleanest calc set I've checked this year", issued with nothing carried forward.
   - This is the specific result behind the draft's unnamed shoutout.

2. **Unblock - podium RFI-14, RFI-15, RFI-17** (draft: status, plus coaching on "slow on site queries" → Unblock, because all three await the architect's revised penetration layout after Teodor's chases on Monday and Wednesday)
   - I escalate to the architect's project lead and copy our project director before the contractor's bay C pour, sending my overdue fee-variation letter with it so the layout has nothing left to wait on.
   - Ask Teodor: beyond the project lead, who at the architect has he chased, so my escalation lands with the right person?

3. **Career - a move to the bridges team** (raised by Teodor)
   - Which part of this fortnight, the RFI coordination or the abutment inspection, felt closest to the bridges work he wants?
   - What would a bridges role need to offer before he asks for it, and does that need a dedicated career conversation?

**Dropped**
- "I want to coach him on attention to detail": contradicted by the checker's note on the retaining-wall package and by the same-day defects entry after his unplanned abutment visit; the site agent's "slow on the slab queries" is the wait in item 2, not his pace.

**Parking lot**
- A personal matter → a private conversation; HR if a formal leave arrangement follows.

## What would change the call

**A pattern keeps the coaching item.** Suppose the tracker had returned two of Teodor's packages, a fortnight apart, for the same missing load combination, and he had written "I should have run the combination check before sending". Two instances he has already named make a pattern: a Coaching item after item 2, asked as questions on those two returned packages and aimed at the mechanism (what he checks before a package leaves his desk), never at "attention to detail" in general.

**Inputs that are thin or old.** The brief keeps its shape; a few lines change.
- Draft only, no signals: the Source line says so; no opener, no relabel, no figures. The Gap line names the missing tracker, log and messages and says to pull them before the meeting. Each draft item stays in the draft's own words, tagged "(no signal yet)", with one question; a sensitive item still goes only to the parking lot.
- Last 1-on-1 notes only: the Source line says so, and nothing has come in since. The Open actions line lists every action in the notes as "confirm", with its owner and due date, never as done, sent, on track or under way. A result the notes record goes on the Figures line, not into an opener. A personal matter they mention appears only as "A personal matter → private conversation".

**Nothing named means no opener.** Suppose the package were still with the checker and nobody had commented on it. The draft's "he did something good" does not become a Recognition item; the brief opens at the unblock, and the context says no signal this week names a result.

**A soft label on a real incident moves up.** Suppose the draft had said "quick feedback: Teodor sent a sketch to site before check", and the contractor had built from it and had to break it out. That is not quick: it becomes a proper item on the fix he owns and the check step that would have caught it, asked as questions because it is his first, while the project director's process review goes to the parking lot. If instead a checking backlog marked "low" left three other teams' packages waiting on him as the only checker, it is a Decision for me: back his own proposal to spread the checking.

Before sending, read the brief once against the input: a project, document, person or figure the input does not contain came from this example and is deleted. Do not announce the brief or say what you are about to do: the very first characters sent are `# ` and the report's name.
