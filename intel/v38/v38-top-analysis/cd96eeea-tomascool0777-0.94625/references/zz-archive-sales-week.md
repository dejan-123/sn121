# An earlier invented week, kept for the record

This file is not part of the method and nothing in it is needed for a reply. It records, in plain prose, an invented sales week that an early draft of this skill was tried on, and how that week was read.

## The week

Teodor Halvorsen is an account executive in mid-market sales. His manager's note to self said: status on the Northvale renewal deck; feedback, because Teo has been pushy with Brightline's procurement people; recognition for something great on the third-quarter close, though the manager could not remember what; and Teodor's wish to talk about quota relief for December.

The action list showed the Northvale deck sent on Monday and signed off by the vice president, the legal redline from Brightline still outstanding, and the manager's own territory plan for next year overdue. Five deals had moved to contract stage in the month against nine in a usual month, and three were sitting in Brightline's legal review. On Tuesday Teodor wrote in the deal-desk channel that this was his third chase to Brightline's lawyers and that their counsel was away until Friday. On Wednesday the sales director, Marguerite Oduya, wrote that Teodor's discovery notes had turned Northvale from a churn risk into a two-year renewal. Teodor himself had asked whether he could move to the enterprise segment next year.

## How it was read

The deck was finished and praised by name, so it opened the meeting as the thanks the manager could not place, in the director's own words. The complaint about pushiness turned out to be three chases for a redline that was holding three deals, which made it something the manager had to unstick, with nothing left to say about tone. The drop from nine deals to five was explained by the same hold and was shown with both numbers. The overdue territory plan was the manager's to clear before asking Teodor for anything. Quota relief was a pay matter and left for a separate conversation with sales operations. His question about the enterprise segment was his own and got real time, asked through what he had done on Northvale.
