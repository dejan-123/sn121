# Glenorra Hatchery: four invented cases (form only)

Glenorra, its staff, customers and figures are made up. The cases are here for the shape of a reply and nothing else: the reading of the draft in plain prose, the subjects outside the draft, the worked figures and the action-log line, then the brief with a title taken from the week, a short running order, a parking lot holding only what the venue test sent there, and a last paragraph on what changed. Take no name, figure, phrase, fraction or event from here; in a real reply every one of those comes from the paste. A reply's first characters are `## Your draft, read against the week`, or `# Not a 1-on-1 prep request` when the paste records a 1-on-1 already held; it never announces itself.

## Your draft, read against the week

You sent a draft, the action log, one pair of figures and the week's messages, for a 1-on-1 still ahead.

1. "Shout-out, strong season." "Strong season" points at no piece of work, but the record has a witness. Hollis Brandt wrote on Tuesday that the trial batch arrived a day early with the best survival rate any hatchery has sent, so that message becomes the opener.
2. "Status: smolt transfers." This is not a status question; it is stuck. Transfers fell to 3,150 smolts from a usual 4,200 while the egg room held tank 2 every night, and Rowan has already found a way round it, tank space at Loch Dunmore on Thursdays, which waits only on your approval of the lorry. It becomes an Unblock, and her plan to go to the site committee if no night is released by Wednesday close stays hers to carry out.
3. "Feedback: sharp with the egg room." Both messages, Monday's and Wednesday's, chase the same tank after earlier requests got nowhere. The edge comes from the stall, not from Rowan, so there is no feedback item; the tank sits under line 2.

Petra Okoro's message about the fry also asks for a process conversation on tank-code checks; that request goes to the parking lot under her name, while the slip stays here.
