# ISO/IEC 27001:2022: Annex A identifiers and how its recurring rows are called

The 2022 Annex A has 93 controls in four themes: A.5 organisational (A.5.1 to A.5.37), A.6 people (A.6.1 to A.6.8), A.7 physical (A.7.1 to A.7.14), A.8 technological (A.8.1 to A.8.34). The 2013 edition's A.9 to A.18 is withdrawn, and an auditor reads it on a 2022 audit as a competence error. Write a control as its number and short name, for example "A.8.2 privileged access rights".

## Further identifiers

Asset inventory A.5.9; access control, identity and authentication information A.5.15, A.5.16, A.5.17; supplier agreements and ICT supply chain A.5.20, A.5.21; monitoring of supplier services A.5.22; incident assessment, learning and evidence A.5.25, A.5.27, A.5.28; screening A.6.1; remote working A.6.7; endpoints A.8.1; secure authentication A.8.5; configuration A.8.9; backup A.8.13; redundancy A.8.14; logging A.8.15; monitoring A.8.16; secure development life cycle A.8.25; change management A.8.32. Management-system clauses: 6.1.2 risk assessment, 6.1.3 risk treatment and the statement of applicability (SoA), 7.2 competence, 7.5 documented information, 9.2 internal audit, 9.3 management review.

## Engagement rules

Stage 1 reads the documented system; Stage 2 samples whether the controls run. A major nonconformity raised at Stage 2 blocks certification until it is corrected and verified, and a first certification has no later surveillance visit that could absorb a gap, so nothing is deferred and every P1 closes before fieldwork. A minor nonconformity needs an accepted corrective-action plan. The memo quotes the time to fieldwork as the input states it.

Calling "no physical security policy" a gap for an organisation with no premises invents a requirement; the reverse error is as costly as the first. Reliance on a host's redundancy is the highest-risk process gap, because the host does not make the firm's own recovery decisions.

## Calibration

P1 means a Stage 2 auditor would likely raise a major: anything Missing, privileged access never reviewed, alerts with no triage record, a failed leaver revocation, no continuity plan. P2 means a working control short of a current document or an exercise. P3 means excluded controls justified in the SoA and controls whose evidence stands.

## Lines the memo must carry

- The verdict in plain words, "not ready for Stage 2" while any P1 is open, with the statement that a Stage 2 major nonconformity blocks certification and cannot be deferred.
- The gap distribution counted and summed: Implemented n + Partial n + Missing n = rows, and the same for P1, P2 and P3.
- The SoA in the evidence pack, each exclusion justified there.
