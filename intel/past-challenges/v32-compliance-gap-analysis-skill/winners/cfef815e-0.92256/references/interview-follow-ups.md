# Follow-up questions, continued

## Supplier oversight
- How did this supplier get onto the approved list? *A due-diligence record dated before the first data flow.*
- What does their assurance report leave for you to do? *The list of controls the report expects the customer to run, each mapped to one of yours.*
- When the supplier changed its subprocessors, how did you find out? *A notice received and a dated decision to accept or object.*
- Who would you call if this supplier failed tomorrow? *An exit or substitution note, even a short one.*

## People and awareness
- Show me the screening record for someone hired this year. *A completed check dated before system access was granted.*
- What did staff sign when they joined? *An acceptable-use or confidentiality acknowledgement per person, with a date.*
- How do you know the training landed? *Completion records with scores or attestations, and a chase list for those overdue.*
- What happens when someone breaks a security rule? *A disciplinary step written down, and one case handled under it, anonymised.*

## Change and release
- Who can push to production without a second pair of eyes? *A branch-protection or deployment setting export, plus the exception list.*
- How is an emergency change approved after the fact? *A retrospective approval within a stated number of days, traced on one example.*
- What separates the people who write code from those who release it? *A role matrix, or a compensating review where the team is too small to separate them.*
