# Spot checks, last round

The final questions, the ones asked of leadership as well as of control owners.

## Privacy rights and records
- Walk me through one access request from receipt to reply. *A log entry with the identity check, the search performed and the date of the answer.*
- How do you know data is deleted when its period ends? *A schedule per data class and a deletion job's history or a dated manual purge record.*
- Where is the record of what you do with personal data, and when did someone last check it against reality? *A version-dated register reviewed after the most recent system change.*
- What does a visitor see before anything is stored on their device? *A screen recording or scan showing that no non-essential storage occurs before the choice.*
- Which transfers leave the country, and on what basis? *A transfer register naming each destination, the safeguard relied on and the assessment behind it.*

## Governance and assurance
- When did leadership last review the security programme, and what did they decide? *Minutes with attendees, the inputs reviewed and the decisions taken.*
- Who checks the controls independently of the people running them? *An internal audit plan, its report and the tracker of its findings.*
- Where are the risks you have chosen to accept? *A register entry per risk with the owner, the reason and a review date.*
- Which controls do you say do not apply, and why? *A scoping statement that names each excluded control and the fact that makes it irrelevant.*

## Reading the answers
An owner who reaches for a screenshot is offering a moment; an assessor wants a record that runs across the period. An owner who promises a document "next week" is describing a gap that is open today. The row's closing evidence is the record that would have answered the question the owner could not.
