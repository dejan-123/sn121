# What the assessor will ask each control owner

An assessor rarely reads a control statement and stops. They ask the owner a short run of questions, and the closing evidence for a row is whatever answers the last question that the owner cannot yet answer. Use these to word "Evidence Required to Close" in the owner's own terms.

## Identity and access
- Show me everyone who can sign in today. *An export from the identity system, not the HR start list.*
- Pick three leavers from the last quarter: when did each lose access? *Termination date beside removal timestamp, per person.*
- Who approved this privileged role, and when was it last looked at? *A dated review sign-off naming the reviewer and the roles kept or cut.*
- Where does a new request leave a trace? *A ticket with requester, approver and date, retrievable after the chat that prompted it is gone.*

## Logging, monitoring and vulnerabilities
- What happens when this alert fires at night? *An on-call rule and one alert traced from firing to closure.*
- Who reads these logs, how often, and where is that written down? *A review procedure plus the dated record of the last few reviews.*
- How long may a critical finding stay open? *A remediation target in policy and a list of findings with open and close dates.*

## Third parties
- Which outside parties touch the regulated data, and on what paper? *A register naming each party, its role and its signed instrument.*
- When did you last read this provider's assurance report, and what did you do about its exceptions? *The report itself, its period, and a note mapping its user-entity controls to yours.*
- If the report is late, what covers the gap? *A bridge letter, a compensating review, or a dated escalation.*

## Privacy
- What lawful basis covers this activity, and where is it argued? *A written assessment for a legitimate-interests basis; a recorded, prior choice for consent.*
- When was the record of processing last brought up to date, and what has changed since? *A dated record that lists every current activity and recipient.*
- Where would a breach be logged even if it was not reported? *A breach register with the notification decision and its timing for each entry.*

## Risk assessment and governance
- When was the risk assessment last redone, and what new systems or suppliers has it not yet seen? *A dated assessment whose scope lists every current system and supplier.*
- Who owns each treatment decision, and where is it signed? *A treatment plan with an owner and an accepted residual risk per item.*
- Which internal audit and management review happened before this assessment? *The audit report and the review minutes, both dated.*

## Reading the answers
An owner who answers with a policy is describing design; one who answers with a dated record is describing operation. An answer that begins "we were told" or "the provider handles it" is a claim waiting for its artifact. When two answers conflict, the dated artifact wins over the recollection.
