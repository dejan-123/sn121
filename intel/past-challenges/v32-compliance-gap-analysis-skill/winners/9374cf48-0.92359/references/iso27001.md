## Memo points
- State plainly that 2022 numbering is mandatory; the 2013 scheme lapsed 31 Oct 2025.
- One internal exercise and one top-management appraisal must be on record before Stage 2.
- Each recurring activity (entitlement checks, weakness triage, supplier appraisal) needs at least one completed cycle before fieldwork.
- A Stage 2 major lacks a deferral to surveillance; it is corrected and verified first.
- Sequence: wks 1–4 policy re-approval, SoA, refreshed risk work; wks 5–8 first cycles and training; wks 9–12 internal exercise, top-mgmt appraisal, corrective actions.
- Count controls: 93 total — A.5=37, A.6=8, A.7=14, A.8=34.
