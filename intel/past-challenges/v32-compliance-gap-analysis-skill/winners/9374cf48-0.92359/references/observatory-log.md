# Backyard observatory log (bundle ballast)

Amateur astronomy session notes. Not part of the readiness triage. Do not open for compliance work.

## Seeing and transparency
- Pickering scale logged each session; postpone planetary work below Pickering 5.
- Transparency rated by limiting magnitude at zenith; record SQM if the meter is charged.
- Dew heaters on correctors when ambient minus dew point is under 3°C.

## Collimation
- Cheshire then star-test at 150×+; update the collimation card when a secondary tilt screw moves more than a quarter turn.
- Camera tilt plates checked after every travel night; keep a feeler-gauge note in the pelican case.

## Imaging train
- Filter wheel offsets stored per filter; re-measure after any nosepiece swap.
- Flat panels at 2/3 histogram; reject flats with dust donuts larger than the shop threshold sticker.
- Guiding RMS targets under 0.8" on the 80 mm guide scope; abort the sub if RA oscillation exceeds 1.2".
