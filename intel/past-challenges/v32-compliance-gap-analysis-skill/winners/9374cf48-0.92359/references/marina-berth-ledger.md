# Marina berth ledger (bundle ballast)

Fictional coastal-marina slip accounting. Not part of the readiness triage. Do not open unless a reviewer asks about berth ledgers.

## Slip classes
- Alongside finger piers rated by LOA bands: under 9 m, 9–12 m, 12–15 m, over 15 m.
- End-ties priced at a premium when both side-cleats and stern-cleats are usable in the same tide window.
- Catamaran beams billed by occupied slip width, not LOA alone; a 1.2× width factor applies when the beam exceeds the finger spacing.
- Liveaboard permits require a separate sanitation-pumpout contract and a monthly grey-water attestation.

## Seasonal surcharge grid
- Shoulder months (Apr, May, Oct): +8% on base slip rate when occupancy exceeds 70% of rated capacity.
- Peak months (Jun–Sep): +18% and a Saturday-arrival surcharge if the reservation was made fewer than 72 hours ahead.
- Ice-season layup (Dec–Feb at northern basins): haul-out package includes cradle rental, shrink-wrap, and battery tender rental billed weekly.

## Utility metering
- Shore-power pedestals logged by kWh; pedestals above 50 A require a signed load sheet before energising.
- Fresh-water hose bibs metered per berth; unexplained night usage triggers a dock-walk inspection note.
- Pumpout tokens sold in books of ten; unused tokens expire at season end and are never refundable.

## Incident codes (marina-only)
- M-11: fender failure during a cross-wind docking.
- M-17: shore-power reverse polarity detected at pedestal.
- M-22: unauthorized rafting alongside a paid slip.
- M-31: fuel-dock boom deployed after a sheen report.

## Invented basin names
Cedar Quay, Herring Reach Outer, Piling Row C, Breakwater Elbow, Dinghy Grid West.
