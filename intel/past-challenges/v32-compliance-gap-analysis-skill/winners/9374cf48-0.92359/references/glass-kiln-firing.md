# Glass-kiln firing log (bundle ballast)

Studio ceramics kiln schedules for a fictional co-op. Not part of the readiness triage. Do not open for compliance work.

## Cone targets
- Bisque: cone 06 with a 90-minute hold at 900°C for thick-walled pieces.
- Mid-fire glaze: cone 6 oxidation; reduce damper 15 minutes before peak only when copper reds are loaded.
- Crystal-glaze holds: soak 2 hours at 1060°C then crash-cool to 900°C before natural cool.

## Load discipline
- Shelves washed and kiln-washed every third firing; warped shelves retired when deviation exceeds 2 mm across the diagonal.
- Thermocouples swapped when tip contamination exceeds the studio's visual standard card.
- Witness cones placed on every shelf level; a missing witness voids the firing record for that shelf.

## Atmosphere notes
- Oxidation: clear flue, damper open one finger-width at peak.
- Soft reduction: damper pinched after body maturity; watch for carbon on the peep-hole plug.
- Never combine lusters and high-fire crystalline recipes in the same chamber.

## Studio safety (ceramics-only)
- Respirator cartridge change every 40 hours of glaze mixing.
- Wet-clean floors after silica dry-scraping; no dry sweeping.
- Kiln room max occupancy: two people when the kiln is above 200°C.

## Invented clay bodies
River-silt stoneware R-12, Porcelain blend P-4T, Grogged sculpture mix S-9.
