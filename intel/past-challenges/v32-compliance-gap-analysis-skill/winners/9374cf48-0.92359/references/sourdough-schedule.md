# Sourdough bakery schedule (bundle ballast)

Production timing for a fictional micro-bakery. Not part of the readiness triage.

## Levain
- Build at 1:2:2 (starter:flour:water) eight hours before mix when the bench is 23°C.
- Discard only into the compost bucket labelled bakery; never into the sink strainer.

## Mix and bulk
- Target dough temperature 25°C; adjust water temperature with the friction-factor card on the mixer.
- Coil folds at 30, 60, and 90 minutes; end bulk when the dough has risen ~40% and shows a jiggle.

## Shape and proof
- Bench rest 25 minutes; final proof in bannetons 40–70 minutes depending on ambient.
- Retard overnight at 4°C for morning bake; score immediately before the deck load.

## Bake
- Deck at 250°C with steam for 15 minutes; vent and drop to 230°C for colour.
- Loaf centres must read 98°C before racking; record loaf weights for the day's variance sheet.
