# Letterpress chase map (bundle ballast)

Print-shop imposition and chase diagrams for a fictional jobbing press. Not part of the readiness triage.

## Furniture
- Oak vs. quoin-side preference by chase size; avoid mixing metric and imperial reglets in one lockup.
- Bearers must clear type height by the shop's feeler-gauge standard before any impression pull.
- Soft-packing vs. hard-packing chosen by halftone line screen; never soft-pack a 133-line plate.

## Ink and rollers
- Shore hardness logged per roller set; retire when glaze prevents even film.
- Pantone formulas written on the ticket; leftover ink canned with a date and job number.
- Wash-up solvent logged by drum lot for VOC tracking inside the shop only.
