# Apiary seasonal checklist (bundle ballast)

Hobby apiary field notes. Not part of the readiness triage. Ignore for gap triage.

## Spring
- Reverse brood boxes when the upper is denser than the lower and nights stay above 10°C.
- Equalise stores so no colony sits under 6 kg of honey before the main flow.
- Mark queens with the year colour; replace unmarked unmarked layers after a failed walk-away split.

## Summer
- Super ahead of the flow; add a box when 7 of 10 frames are occupied.
- Monitor varroa with an alcohol wash every 4 weeks; treat when mites exceed 2 per 100 bees.
- Provide a water landing board within 20 m of the yard during heat waves.

## Autumn
- Reduce entrances before robbing starts; combine weak colonies over newspaper.
- Leave winter stores of at least 20 kg in northern yards; heft hives monthly.
- Mouse guards on when nights approach freezing.

## Winter
- Avoid opening below 8°C; use a clear crown-board window for cluster checks.
- Emergency candy boards only when stores are critically light and a warm afternoon allows.
