## Dimensions

**contextual_judgment** — Does the response demonstrate genuine situational reasoning — using coverage math, segment context, and snapshot deltas together — rather than flagging anomalies by rote pattern-matching? A strong response knows which slippages matter given the current coverage picture and which are benign; it does not treat every close-date movement as equally alarming.

**output_quality** — Are the coverage waterfall, anomaly table, and narrative structurally sound and arithmetically defensible? Prioritization by dollar-exposure impact (not count or recency) should be evident. The anomaly table should concentrate on the exposure that actually threatens the period number.

**adaptability** — Does the response handle missing or ambiguous inputs correctly? No prior snapshot means no slippage claims. No stated quota means asking rather than deriving one from pipeline size. Mixed currencies prompt a clarification request rather than silent assumptions.

**domain_knowledge** — Does the response reflect accurate understanding of forecast mechanics — commit vs. best-case vs. pipeline coverage ratios, stage regression versus close-date movement, commit-category override conflicts with deal signals, and the difference between structural pipeline health and one-time anomalies?

**communication** — Is the VP-ready narrative a single coherent paragraph that separates structural trends from isolated anomalies, names the single biggest risk with deal-level evidence, and avoids burying the lead with minor issues?
