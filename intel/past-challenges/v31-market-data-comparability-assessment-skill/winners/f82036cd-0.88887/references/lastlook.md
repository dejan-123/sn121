# Last look before the assessment

If the previous message was not the private worksheet, send the worksheet now as its own message, end it by calling `read_reference` on `references/lastlook.md` again, then continue.

Run these checks against the worksheet only. Fix failures silently. The assessment restates none of the worksheet marks legend.

1. **Anatomy** — every figure was scored against the KPI's exact unit, period, definition and segment wording, including qualifiers.
2. **Notes** — every footnote and metadata line was read for a confirmed gap or non-comparability warning. Quantified bands kept the dimension as clash; any shown range used both ends.
3. **Prose** — dimensions a prose source states are match. Unstated dimensions are silent, with the assumed convention named.
4. **Gap kind** — every clash is foundation or coverage. Every foundation clash is REJECT. Every coverage clash is LOW (never MEDIUM; never REJECT unless another rule triggers). A stated computation difference was not demoted to a caveat.
5. **Counts** — recount silents. Three or more → REJECT. Exactly two → LOW. MEDIUM needs no clash and at most one silent, plus only a representativeness caveat. HIGH needs four matches and no caveat. Prior-year cost/price data is silent, not lean. Metric-form-obvious units may be lean.
6. **Soft silence** — before REJECT on silent-count alone, recheck each silent. If the basis is stated anywhere, it is not silent. A row with only two genuine silents stays LOW texture.
7. **Source warnings** — "not comparable to X" language from the source itself → REJECT.
8. **Restatements** — every implied range passes a toy check (larger source denominator → multiply, do not divide). Homemade point estimates are not citations.
9. **No clash at MEDIUM** — any clash on any dimension → LOW or REJECT.
10. **Denominator / composition** — confirmed eligibility-rule or weighting differences that are only directionally estimable stay LOW. Same eligibility rule averaged over a year vs a month-end is period grain, not coverage.
11. **Ambiguous event definition** — if it can move the rate, do not park the row at MEDIUM.
12. **Size-band / panel / mix** — with matched definition and same product, these are MEDIUM caveats, not LOW and not HIGH. Mild FY/CY overlap is lean, not a clash.
13. **Direction** — every clash and material silent has a stated bias direction. Prefer a labeled convention over "ambiguous" when the industry pattern is clear.
14. **Claim test** — intended headline tested against the best surviving figure; reversals stated in the pre-table note and handoff.
15. **Rationales** — each Why fails a swap test; names that figure's evidence.
16. **Quarantine** — each REJECT has a reason and a named re-retrieval ask, or "not rescuable" plus a better source class.
17. **Handoff** — headline status, MEDIUM qualifier sentences, LOW directional lines, reversal if any.
18. **Provenance** — every number from the packet or shown arithmetic; conventions labeled.
19. **Lift test** — no tier raised by prestige, closeness, or intended use.
20. **Length** — assessment roughly 5,000–8,500 characters; grid cells about a dozen words.
