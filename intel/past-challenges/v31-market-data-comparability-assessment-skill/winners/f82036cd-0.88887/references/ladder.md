# Equivalence ladder and specimens

Open once, transfer the logic, close. Specimens use invented operators in industries the live packets do not use.

## Ladder (apply in order)

1. Foundation clash on unit, definition/basis, or period *concept* → **REJECT**.
2. Foundation segment failure (explicit exclusion, or incompatible throughput regimes in one rate) → **REJECT**.
3. ≥3 silent marks, or wrong reporter class → **REJECT**.
4. Coverage clash only (same formula, different scope/weighting; composition stream; count vs value) → **LOW**.
5. Exactly two silents, no clash → **LOW** (texture). Do not REJECT soft silence.
6. No clash, ≤1 silent, plus size-band / panel / mild self-selection / sub-line mix → **MEDIUM**.
7. Four matches, no caveat → **HIGH** (rare).

Downward pressure is mandatory for foundation clashes. Over-rejecting coverage or soft-silence rows is the opposite error.

## Edge cards

| Pattern | Gap kind | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Footnote confirms a different construction (gross/listed/rack/stacked vs the KPI's basis) | foundation | REJECT |
| Quantified adjustment band in that footnote | still foundation | REJECT (show range; do not upgrade) |
| Whole-company statutory ratio, formula matches, specialty line included, no breakout | coverage | LOW |
| Peer is same metric, size band one tier up, definition confirmed | caveat only | MEDIUM |
| Opt-in registry / voluntary panel with matched unit and period | panel caveat | MEDIUM |
| Year-average of the same eligibility rule vs a month-end snapshot | period lean | does not alone force LOW |
| Denominator eligibility differs (includes idle/suspended units); direction estimable | coverage | LOW |
| Event definition ambiguous enough to move the rate; other dims match | silent on definition | LOW (not MEDIUM) |
| Paywalled summary: period basis and fine definition unstated; no categorical clash | two silents | LOW |
| Per-stop cost beside per-route cost; no conversion in packet | foundation | REJECT |
| Extra disclosed numerator line inside matched formula | coverage | LOW |
| Vague "total customer spend" against a fee-only KPI | indeterminate | REJECT |
| Prose article states matching basis, period, cohort in plain words | — | those dims are match |
| Supplier "partner site" average, undefined population | wrong reporter | REJECT |
| Intended headline is the REJECT row | — | say so; pick best survivor; flag reversal |

## Invented worked rows

### Cedar & Kiln — cash collections per wholesale account

Internal: $412 collected per active wholesale account, calendar 2024, after distributor discounts.

| Figure | Marks | Gap | Tier | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trade sheet $511 "invoice per account" with note "distributor discounts typically 17–23%" | U match · P match · D clash · S match | foundation | REJECT | Note confirms gross invoices, not collections. Ask for the sheet's collections line. |
| Regional co-op median $398 collected / active account, CY2024, 12–40 door cohort | all match; size band one notch wider | caveat | MEDIUM | Definition and period match; qualify the size band. |
| Conglomerate 10-K $440 "revenue per customer" across retail + wholesale | D match shape · S coverage | coverage | LOW | Same money-per-account shape but blended channels. Directional only. |

Narrative: intended "we are below market" cannot use the $511 row. Against the MEDIUM co-op print, Cedar sits slightly above — say the flip.

### Northfen Servicing — 60+ item-count delinquency

Internal: 3.6% of loans 60+ past due by **loan count**, non-conforming book, YE2024.

| Figure | Gap | Tier | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade print 4.0% 60+ on **UPB-weighted** non-conforming | coverage (weighting) | LOW | Same arrears concept; value-weighting pulls toward large tickets. State direction from book mix. |

If the UPB print was the intended headline and the count-basis MEDIUM shows the company *above* peers, the handoff must reverse the story.

## Pitfalls checklist (silent)

- Bridging footnote treated as rescue.
- Prose match downgraded because it was prose.
- Coverage denominator clash promoted to MEDIUM because numbers looked close.
- Soft two-silence summary rejected.
- Size-band or voluntary-panel matched row dropped to LOW.
- Averaging grain of the same eligibility rule treated as coverage.
- Vague total-spend language left usable against a fee-only KPI.
- Narrative flip left unsaid.
- Interchangeable rationales.
- Invented conversion factor where the packet planted a gap.
