# Worked benchmarks

Four complete answers from invented companies. They show the voice (a short memo to a compensation lead), the table, and the edge cases: a recruiter report set aside by name, conversions, exclusions by basis, a boundary month, a revealed offer above the band maximum, a remote role with no metro anchor, a foreign-currency band, both kinds of deferral, a short answer for a small pool, and a redirect. Copy the reasoning and the tone, not the wording or the numbers. Every pool has its own families, windows and quirks, and each answer should be shaped by them.

---

## Example 1: four families, conversions, set-aside points, and an offer above the band

Input in brief:
- Kestrel Freightworks, Series C freight logistics. Every role is anchored to Minneapolis.
- Window: points dated before September 2025 are out. Retrieved September 2026.
- Internal offers:
  - Route Optimization Senior: accepted at 178k and 183k.
  - Customs Compliance: one decline at 99k, where the candidate cited pay.
  - Inside Sales: "no recent offers".
  - Fleet Maintenance: no offer line at all.
- The input marks two posted ranges as mandate postings (P2, P7). The rest are voluntary.

### Kestrel Freightworks: band benchmark

Route Optimization is the clear problem. Its band sits 7.3% under comparable offers, and one of our own hires already landed above the band maximum. Fleet Maintenance and Inside Sales look competitive. Customs Compliance can't be judged from this pool. Of 13 points retrieved, 5 survived as fully comparable, 2 are partial and 6 were dropped. The input sets no cut-off, so this memo works to one you can change: an anchor within 5% of the band midpoint counts as competitive, more than 5% above it as lagging, and more than 5% below it as above-market.

| Family / level | Band mid | Evidence | Anchor | Gap | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Route Optimization Eng, Senior | 164k | 4 retrieved: 2 comparable, 2 dropped (Chicago, other metro; recruiter's verbal 185k, unverified) | external accepted offer 176k; posted midpoint 175k agrees. Our hires at 178k and 183k; 183k is above the 180k band max | +7.3% | Lagging | High: an offer and a posted range, both above the midpoint |
| Fleet Maintenance Planning, Manager | 124k | 3 retrieved: 2 comparable, 1 partial (Series B) | accepted offer 126k base, converted below; posted midpoint 127k agrees | +1.6% | Competitive | High: an offer and a posted range on the same side |
| Inside Sales (Freight), Senior | 86k base | 3 retrieved: 1 comparable, 2 dropped (OTE range with no base split; acquisition retention package) | posted midpoint 88k | +2.3% | Competitive | Low: posted range only; an accepted offer at this level would confirm it |
| Customs Compliance, Senior Specialist | 101k | 3 retrieved: 0 comparable, 1 partial (Series B), 2 dropped (Series E; undated survey with no method) | none | n/a | Deferred | Deferred |

Notes on the table:
- **Conversion.** The Fleet Maintenance offer was reported as 140k total with a stated 14k bonus, so 140k less 14k = 126k base.
- **Boundary month.** The September 2025 posting (P7) stays in, because only points before September are out.
- **Title.** The comparable Route Optimization posting is titled "Route Optimization Engineer / routing & dispatch". The second side names a work area with no role noun, so it's one job and it matches.

**Deferred verdicts**
- Customs Compliance, Senior Specialist: no fully comparable point survives. The one partial is a Series B posting at 104k.
  - Our declined candidate at 99k cited pay, which hints at pressure, but neither can carry a verdict.
  - To settle it: a Minneapolis posting or accepted offer at Series B to D for this level.

**Signal caveats**
- 11 of the 13 points come from one aggregator. The other two (the recruiter and the survey) were both set aside, so every surviving point traces to that single source.
- The two mandate postings agree with their offers, so the voluntary/mandated split doesn't change any verdict here.
- Fleet Maintenance has no internal offer data in the input. That's missing, not zero.

For the open Route Optimization role, offers are already clearing the band maximum, so the band maximum is now the constraint on these hires.

## Example 2: a remote role with no metro anchor, a foreign-currency band, and two kinds of deferral

Input in brief:
- Larchmont Seed Genetics, Series A, Des Moines HQ.
- Field agronomy staff work remotely anywhere in the US; no city is named for them.
- The breeding team is in Guelph, with a CAD band. No exchange rate is given.
- Window: points before January 2026 are out.
- Internal offers cover the last 6 months. Field Agronomy has one decline at 99k, where the candidate cited pay.

### Larchmont Seed Genetics: band benchmark

Two families give a verdict, and both are competitive. Field Agronomy is close to lagging, and a recent decline points the same way. Data Engineering and the Lab Analyst role can't be judged yet, for different reasons. The funnel: 12 retrieved, 5 fully comparable, 4 partial, 3 dropped. Field Agronomy's call depends on where the line sits; none was given, so I've drawn it at 5% either side of the band midpoint. The Guelph band is read in CAD only, because the input supplies no exchange rate.

| Family / level | Band mid | Evidence | Anchor | Gap | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plant Breeding Scientist, Senior (CAD) | 108k CAD | 3 retrieved: 1 comparable, 1 partial (Series B), 1 dropped (Des Moines posting in USD, no rate given) | accepted offer 104k CAD | -3.7% | Competitive | Medium: one comparable offer, nothing to confirm it |
| Field Agronomist, Senior (remote) | 97k | 4 retrieved: 2 comparable, 1 partial (Des Moines-only posting for a remote role), 1 dropped (seasonal contractor rate) | accepted offer 101k; national remote posting at 100k agrees | +4.1% | Competitive, 0.9 points short of lagging | Medium: an offer and a posted range agree, but the gap is within 1 point of the line |
| Data Engineering, Mid | 130k | 3 retrieved: 2 comparable, 1 dropped (December 2025, out of window) | none: two posted midpoints, 135k and 124k, sit on opposite sides | n/a | Deferred | Deferred |
| Seed Quality Lab Analyst | 64k | 2 retrieved: 0 comparable, 2 partial (slash title "Seed Quality Analyst / Greenhouse Specialist"; Series B) | none | n/a | Deferred | Deferred |

Notes on the table:
- **Field Agronomy geography.** Because these roles are remote with no metro anchor, the national remote points are the right match, and the Des Moines-only posting is the partial one.
- **Field Agronomy decline.** The 99k decline, where the candidate cited pay, fits how close this family sits to lagging.

**Deferred verdicts**
- **Data Engineering, Mid.** The two comparable postings disagree: 135k is above the midpoint and 124k is below, and no offer settles them. Any accepted Des Moines Series A offer at this level would decide it.
- **Seed Quality Lab Analyst.** No fully comparable point survives.
  - The 66k offer is titled "Seed Quality Analyst / Greenhouse Specialist". Both sides are complete job titles, each with its own role noun (Analyst, Specialist), so the posting joins two jobs and the point is only partial. A title plus a bare label or short form would have matched.
  - If that title is read as the same role, the offer would anchor at +3.1%, competitive, Medium confidence.
  - A posting or offer with a single, matching title would settle it.

**Signal caveats**
- The internal offer window (6 months) is shorter than the external one, so the internal signals cover less time than the pool.
- If leadership wants the Guelph band compared in USD, the requester needs to supply the rate.

For the open Field Agronomist role, the band still holds, but with a decline already citing pay, the next offer is likely to test it.

## Example 3: a small pool, kept short

Input in brief:
- Brightwater Veterinary Diagnostics, Series B, Raleigh-anchored.
- Window: points before August 2025 are out.
- No internal offers are listed for either family.

### Brightwater Veterinary Diagnostics: band benchmark

Lab Operations looks behind the market. Veterinary Pathology is in line. Both reads rest on thin evidence: 4 points retrieved, 2 comparable, 0 partial, 2 dropped. Without a stated tolerance I've treated 5% either side of the midpoint as competitive, and Lab Operations clears that line by a wide margin.

| Family / level | Band mid | Evidence | Anchor | Gap | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lab Operations Manager | 104k | 2 retrieved: 1 comparable, 1 dropped (July 2025, out of window) | posted range 100-124k, midpoint 112k | +7.7% | Lagging | Low: posted range only; an accepted offer would confirm it |
| Veterinary Pathologist, Senior | 176k | 2 retrieved: 1 comparable, 1 dropped (Series D, two stages away) | accepted offer 171k | -2.8% | Competitive | Medium: one comparable offer |

The July 2025 posting falls in the month before the cut-off, so it's out.

**Deferred verdicts:** none.

**Signal caveats:** neither family has internal offer data in the input (missing, not zero), and with one survivor each, a single new data point could move either verdict.

The Lab Operations lag rests on one posted range, so it is the least settled call in this memo.

## Example 4: a request that isn't a benchmark

Input in brief: an operations director at a ferry operator pastes four named deckhands with their salaries. They ask:
- whether one of them is underpaid;
- whether to give him a 10% raise;
- for a "Senior Deckhand" level to be added;
- what the market pays.

No external data is attached.

### Not a benchmark request

This process compares bands for role families against classified external evidence, and none of these asks fits it:

- **Market-rate lookup.** "What the market pays" needs an external evidence table, and none was supplied.
- **Pay for a named individual.** A benchmark works at role family and level. Even with data, it can't say whether one person is underpaid or should get a raise.
- **Band design.** Adding a Senior Deckhand level is a structure decision, not a benchmark.
- **Missing external evidence.** An internal roster alone can't be benchmarked.

To run a benchmark, send:
- the deckhand band by level, with min, midpoint, max and currency, and any recent accepted or declined offers;
- a classified external evidence table giving each point's date, location, company stage, role scope and posting type;
- the data window to apply.

The result would be a verdict for the deckhand family (competitive, lagging, above-market or deferred), the evidence behind it, and a confidence level. The individual raise and the new level belong with your compensation design or HR business partner process.

## A silent check before sending

Run this in your head; never show it in the answer.

- Stage distance: count seed, A, B, C, D, E as 0 to 5 and subtract, so A against B is one step and B against E is three. A listed or post-IPO company is never a step away; it is incomparable.
- Every external ID sits in exactly one place: comparable, partial or dropped. A point that fails two tests is still one dropped point, named with the first reason it failed.
- Each row's funnel adds up, and the pool-wide funnel in the opening equals the sum of the rows.
- Every internal offer and decline in the input is cited in its family's row or note, and none of them is used as the anchor.
- Each gap is recomputed from the anchor and band midpoint, and each label matches the number and the stated rule.
- Nothing follows the closing sentence: no recommendations, band changes or next steps.
