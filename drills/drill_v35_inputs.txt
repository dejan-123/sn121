---
**RIDGELINE FREIGHT — BRIDGE LOAN REQUEST BRIEF (prepared by CFO)**

Ridgeline Freight operates 94 diesel trucks serving regional freight lanes in the Mountain West. Founded in 2019, the company has grown revenue from $8.4M (2022) to $22.6M (2025), a 39% CAGR over three years.

Electrification thesis: Federal EV freight incentives (Inflation Reduction Act, Section 48C) provide a 30% tax credit on qualifying commercial vehicle purchases. For Ridgeline's planned 40-truck electric fleet at $340K per truck ($13.6M total), the credit is worth approximately $4.1M, reducing net fleet cost to ~$9.5M. Charging infrastructure adds $1.8M. Total net investment: ~$11.3M. The company is requesting $14M to include a 6-month operating reserve.

Financial position: Ridgeline is currently EBITDA-negative at −$1.2M on a trailing 12-month basis. Revenue is growing (22% YoY in 2025), but fuel cost inflation and driver wage increases compressed margins. The CFO projects EBITDA breakeven by Q3 2027 on current diesel operations.

Electrification economics: Electric operating cost per mile is estimated at $0.31 vs. diesel at $0.47. At 2025 annual mileage of 8.4M miles across the fleet, the annual operating savings would be approximately $1.34M [(0.47−0.31) × 8.4M] on the electrified portion of the fleet. The electrified 40 trucks represent 43% of the fleet (40/94), so actual annual savings on the electrified portion = $0.58M [0.43 × $1.34M].

Note 1: The Section 48C tax credit is subject to Congressional reauthorization. The current authorization expires December 31, 2026. Ridgeline's planned purchase timeline is Q2 2027. If the credit is not reauthorized, the net fleet cost rises from ~$9.5M to ~$13.6M, adding $4.1M to the net investment and extending payback significantly.

Note 2: The CFO's cost-per-mile estimate of $0.31 for electric is based on current electricity rates in the Mountain West. Regional grid constraints have led to time-of-use pricing volatility; the fleet operations team notes that actual charging costs in peak periods can reach $0.44/mile, narrowing the savings to $0.03/mile.

Note 3: Ridgeline's Mountain West freight lanes include significant elevation gain on key routes. The company has not completed range testing for the specific electric vehicle models under consideration on these routes; the $0.31/mile figure is from manufacturer spec sheets, not field data.
