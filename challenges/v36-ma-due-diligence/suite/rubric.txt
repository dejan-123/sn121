DIMENSIONS — score each separately at 0.0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0

**contextual_judgment**: Does the response make the right call for this scenario's specific signals, stated conditions, and planted facts? Top marks require correctly distinguishing what warrants pre-close action from what can wait, given what the user actually said.

**output_quality**: Is the classification table coherent, internally consistent, and grounded exclusively in the scenario's provided signals? Good evidence-tier labeling is specific to each finding, not generic.

**adaptability**: Does the response handle adversarial or ambiguous inputs — thin evidence, contradicting sources, missing topics the user implied should be cleared — without collapsing into uniform hedging?

**domain_knowledge**: Does the response demonstrate genuine understanding of deal-stage consequences, evidence sufficiency, and the difference between absence of signal and confirmed absence?

**communication**: Are findings and required actions expressed with the precision a decision-maker needs, without padding or vague placeholders?
