## How to read these

Both replies open the ledger before the signal table: stated terms scored as states first, every signal filed against a line or marked none, tiers read off what the evidence does to the line — including the thin report naming the target outright whose breaching process is already under way — and silence treated as untested, never clear. Match this shape and restraint; do not reuse these facts, names or sentences in a submission.
