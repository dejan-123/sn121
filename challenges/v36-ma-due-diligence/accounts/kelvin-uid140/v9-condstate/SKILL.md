# Stake-first signal triage

Produce the standard diligence reply — one tiered table, a count line, a short read — but reach every verdict the same way: score what the deal stakes *before* judging any signal, then let each signal's tier fall out of what it does to a staked term. No go/no-go call, no numeric score.

## Phase A — Map the stakes (internal pass, never printed as its own table)

Read the deal context and write down, in the input's own words and figures:

- **T0** — the thesis dependency: the fact the value rests on (a fleet, a patent family, a licence, a contract base). Not a stated condition — never promoted into one — but tracked the same way.
- **W1, W2...** — each walk-away threshold, quoted with number and unit.
- **P1, P2...** — each price condition, stated and implied: a multiple of a figure assumes the figure real; value in one asset assumes it exists and is owned.
- **Stage deadline** — pre-LOI, post-LOI, pre-close. Urgency tests read against it.
- **Unsearched registers** — every record the input says was NOT searched. A scope-named register no numbered signal reports gets no table row — the gap paragraph carries it.

Each staked term opens in one of four states: **holding / at risk / untested / untouched**. All begin untouched until evidence moves them. No stated terms → T0 alone, anchor says "none stated."

## Phase B — Evidence-mark every signal

Three marks per signal, no verdicts yet:

**Attribution** — the target's exact entity, or a party the pool ties to it (named lender, certifier, counterparty, suit opponent, stated subsidiary). An anonymous post naming the target still attributes; anonymity lowers the label, not the attribution. A near-match — letter off, other suffix, differently-named affiliate, even "(sic)" — stays unresolved until a register entry marries both names; it can never carry the top tier. A signal naming nobody in the deal is topical retrieval, not a fact about the target — noise, no matter how grave. Two subjects or provenances → lettered rows, a/b, marked independently.

**Origins + label** — count independent access, not URLs. N outlets quoting one release = 1 origin; a citing outlet adds nothing; the target's own channels together = 1 origin that never corroborates itself. Independent origins had their own access — docket, regulator's notice, registrar, counterparty filing, earnings call. The count sets the label: **corroborated** (2+ independent, one outside the target), **single-source** (primary-and-binding only for a docket, regulator's notice, statutory filing, charging document or counterparty filing), **unverified** ("sources familiar," anonymous posts), **absence** (never-searched register row).

**Which staked term it touches** — W1, P1, T0, or none. A signal that touches nothing staked can never rise past monitor.

**Silence kinds.** A searched-and-empty register clears that register as of its date only. A never-searched public register — dockets, regulators, enforcement databases, registrars, certification registries, patent and financing-statement records — proves nothing and leaves its term untested. "No signals found" on internal facts (client status, churn, payroll) is no register: the row asks the parties for the papers.

## Phase C — Move the states

- **holding** — corroborated or primary-and-binding evidence shows the term met as of a date; searched-empty supports only its date.
- **at risk** — attributed evidence shows the breaching thing exists or its process underway. Terms written as an absence — "unencumbered," "no liens," "no sanctions," "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists: financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought; a dismissal without prejudice leaves a challenge able to return. The target's own denial does not lift at-risk — it rides the same origin.
- **untested** — the deciding record was never searched, or evidence contradicts in kind but does not measure. Party-held papers are never a register gap.
- **untouched** — nothing bears on it.

A register that partially confirms — T0 matched but items lapsed or under other names — writes the lapse as a loss taken, the other name as an identity question, and asks the item-by-item schedule. Compute each threshold once on the base it is written on — share by unit count, margin over its own book, term totals divided by years — figure beside the term. Where the target's asset list and the register disagree, the register governs.

## Phase D — Tier each row by what it did to the states

- **must-address** — the row(s) whose evidence puts a staked term at risk or leaves a deciding term untested where that term gates the next step at this stage — weighed for magnitude, probability (shown occurring, not merely possible), reversibility. Includes: a thin report naming the target *exactly* whose reported process is already underway on it and which would by itself breach a stated term if true (label stays honest, a denial does not disarm it, a near-match never qualifies); the never-searched register that decides a stated term (split lettered when one gap serves two); a cluster of thin signals together establishing one fact against one term — linked rows take the same tier and mark "read with row N".
- **deeper-diligence** — touches a staked term but the evidence is thin, date-bound or party-held, settled by one verification: the target's unconfirmed word on what value rests on; a searched-clean register speaking only for its date (re-verify before signing); a certificate whose issuer another row undermines; a claim ended on staked ground (obtain the order and terms); a departure or hire in the very function a term or T0 runs through — even anonymous; every unresolved near-match (action: confirm the legal entity).
- **monitor** — attributed but touches nothing staked, or its term is already confirmed holding by an independent record: light action. A hire in a function nothing depends on sits here.
- **noise** — nobody in the deal: none, with a one-line reason.

**Never must-address:** a near-match name whatever the accusation; papers only the parties hold; a nobody-row — deeper-diligence never holds it either; a hire or departure no staked term turns on. No stated terms → only a live adverse claim on T0 on an independent record reaches the top. A grave topic missing a term stays deeper-diligence — a sector review has sanctioned no one, a complaint is not an ordered recall, a warning sits below enforcement.

## Phase E — Cross-read, then write the standard reply

Rows judge rows once: a certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate; a register can contradict the deck; a live register entry outranks a class scare; a complaint and a later order are one dispute at two moments — merge rows that change each other, lettered under the lower number.

Audit before writing: every must-address names its staked term and the evidence moving it; every dramatic deeper-diligence names the test it failed; every numbered signal has a row and a tier before any paragraph; every action assignable. A mixed pool — registers, denials, rumours — is when discipline runs slowest: never tier by the loudest topic.

Open with `## ` immediately — no preamble:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):" quoted; "Not searched:" naming every unsearched register; "Conditions:" each staked term's state in a word — holding, at risk, untested, untouched. "None stated" where silent. The tier column takes only the four tiers — a state is not a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered. Columns: signal | attribution | origins + label | bears on | tier | required action. Every action assignable — role, verb, exact record or party, step it clears — unique per row. Noise rows: "none — <reason>".
4. **Cross-signal check line** — which rows moved and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; each tier with row numbers and count; the four counts summed to the row total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — (a) the must-address picture, ending on the questions needing answers before the next step; (b) the elevation rationale — the closest row, the test it failed, why any dramatic finding sat lower; (c) the most important evidence gap and the record that would close it.
