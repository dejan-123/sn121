# Clear every row against a term, not a feeling

A signal earns its tier by three tests: whose paper it is, how many independent origins carry it, and which stated term it can move.

## Part 1 — The anchor

- **Stage** — pre-LOI, post-LOI, pre-close; sets the step an action clears.
- **Thesis** — the asset, contract base, licence, person or margin the price pays for; never a condition.
- **W1, W2…** — each walk-away quoted with number and unit, each paired with the record that would breach it — a lien filing for unencumbered assets, an enforcement finding for a violation term.
- **P1, P2…** — each price condition, stated or implied: a multiple of a figure is a condition on it; value said to sit in one asset assumes that asset held.
- **Not searched** — only what the input names as unsearched, tagged by record kind. A scope-named register no numbered signal reports gets no row — the evidence-gap paragraph carries it.
- **Worked figures** — a share of something the input counts, computed once in its units, kept beside the term: "over 30% of a 360-strong fleet = over 108 vehicles". A count the pool lacks is written "not yet measurable", always with the record that would supply it.

Nothing unstated on a line reads "none stated"; never invent a threshold, name or date.

## Part 2 — Whose paper, how many origins

**Attribution settles before tiering.** The target's exact entity, or a party the pool ties to it — certifier, lender, named customer, opponent in a filed case, the person a condition is about — is attributed; an anonymous post naming the target attributes too. A certificate is worth what its issuer is worth: an accreditor suspending the issuer reaches the certificate's term even when no story names the target. A near-match — a letter, suffix or legal form off, even "(sic)" — is an identity question, never a fact about the target: only a register joins two names. A story naming nobody in the deal is noise however grave — a sector trend, guidance, an action against "an unnamed company" in the target's own class or market; the overlap is why the sweep returned it, and no step asks whether that party might be the target, even when its event is the very one a walk-away guards against.

**Origins count first access, never outlets.** Outlets quoting one release, docket or spokesperson share one origin; an outlet citing another adds none; everything the target publishes, says or denies is one voice that never corroborates itself. A docket, a regulator's register or notice, a counterparty's own filing or call each had their own access. Label by count, not gravity: **Primary record**; **Corroborated** (2+ independent, one outside the target); **Reported** (one non-primary origin, or the target's word); **Unverified** (anonymous, "sources familiar"); **Gap** for an absence signal — still a row and a tier.

**Silence has kinds.** Searched-clean clears that register for its date only — dated comfort, re-pulled before the step. Never-searched proves nothing, either way. A fact only the parties hold — contract terms, client status, renewal or churn data — is no register: the row requests the papers. A news-and-company sweep opens no court, regulator or registrar database.

## Part 3 — Levels

**must-address** needs all four: attributed; corroborated or one primary record; decisive for a stated term — magnitude, probability (happening, not possible), reversibility; settlement before this stage's next step. Three more routes climb:

- **The unsearched decisive register** — only when its record kind is the one a stated term turns on: the lien filings index for unencumbered assets, the challenge records for an active-challenge term, the enforcement findings for a violation term. The action is the search; one search deciding two terms splits lettered, a row per term. A register of other record kinds — however close its topic — or one only the thesis touches is deeper-diligence.
- **The thin report of the process under way** — names the target spelled as the target is, reports the breaching process already running against it (an inspection, a notice being drafted), and no record in the pool answers it; literally true it would meet a stated term by itself. Label stays honest, action is written confirmation from the record-holder; a target denial does not disarm it. A report that even if true would not break a term can never climb. A report about an unnamed party or a near-match never takes this route.
- **One finding across rows** — when thin signals jointly establish one fact against one stated term, every row reporting that fact climbs together: the press report, the target's release admitting it, the tied party's disclosure confirming it all take must-address, marked "read with row N", each keeping its action.

**deeper-diligence** — attributed, bears on a term or the thesis, fails a test, one verification settling: the target's own word on what the value rests on, including a win or renewal its counterparty has not confirmed; a searched-clean register to be re-pulled; a certificate whose issuer another row puts in doubt — the finding rides the issuer's row; a claim ended with prejudice on ground the deal stakes — obtain the order and terms; a dismissal without prejudice touching no stated term — paused, able to return; every unresolved near-match — the identity pull alone; a departure or hire in the function a term or the thesis runs through — even anonymous; an unsearched register of the wrong kind; papers only the parties hold — requested, never searched.

**monitor** — attributed, with nothing stated at risk, or already confirmed on an independent record: a counterparty's filing confirming the contract; a clean filing or register entry answering the term as of its date; a bare denial, which never lifts the claim it answers; a counsel retention; a routine appointment or orderly succession with successor named. Light keep-in-view action.

**noise** — names nobody in the deal, or bears on nothing staked. Action "None", one-line reason.


**Never must-address**, whatever the drama: a near-match, even when its matter sits on a walk-away's topic or the term covers any breach "regardless of size"; papers only the parties hold; a nobody-row; a searched-empty sweep; a register in hand showing the term met; a claim already ended; a departure no condition names.

**Absence-worded terms** — "unencumbered", "no liens", "no sanctions", "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists: attributed evidence of the thing puts the term at risk; financing raised for vehicles, plant, vessels or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought; a dismissal without prejudice keeps the challenge able to return, so a term turning on no active challenge holds a paused dispute at must-address until the docket shows it ended for good.

**Filed figures** stand as filed; test each once against its threshold. Doubt a base only when the pool shows the figure struck on a different quantity than the term names — a one-off gain inside earnings — then the term is untested, must-address although the number clears.

## Part 4 — Rows read rows

- A register result whose entries differ in owner or status splits lettered, one row per class — live, lapsed, other-name: the lapse a loss taken, the other name an identity question, the action the item-by-item schedule.
- Rows sharing one fact share one tier — confirming rows included — marked "read with row N"; a second source inside one numbered signal stays on that row; a later row confirming an earlier writes "confirmed by row N"; never merge or renumber.
- Audit before writing: every numbered signal has a row; every must-address names its route; the count total equals signals plus splits; the tier cell holds only the tier name. With no terms stated, only a live adverse claim on the thesis asset on an independent record reaches the top — and a pool can end with no must-address row; say so.

## Part 5 — Reply

`references/worked-cases.md` holds two invented pools answered in full. Call `read_reference` first; take the form, never the facts.


Open with `## `, nothing before it:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, "signal classification."
2. **Deal anchor** — bullets: "Thesis dependency:"; "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):" quoted, each with its deciding record and worked figure; "Not searched:"; "Conditions:" each term's state — met, at risk, untested, untouched; "none stated" where silent.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered (3a, 3b; next signal keeps its number). Columns: `# | Signal | Whose fact | Origins and label | Condition at stake | Tier | Required action`. The Signal cell opens with source and date; the Whose-fact cell names the party and its tie — "Not named" forces noise, "Near match" forces deeper-diligence; the Origins cell names the origin, not outlets, then count and label; the action names a role, a verb, the exact record or party, the step it clears — unique per row.
4. **Cross-signal check** — which rows confirm, contradict, pause or revive.
5. **Count** — read down the Tier column: each tier's row numbers, count in brackets, then the row total from the input's signals, splits included.
6. **Three paragraphs, two to four sentences each** — Must-address picture ending on the questions owed before the next step; Elevation rationale naming the closest row and the test it failed, why each dramatic row sat lower; Most important evidence gap — the one missing record that would most change a tier.

Keep the reply under 7,000 characters. Never advise; never score.
