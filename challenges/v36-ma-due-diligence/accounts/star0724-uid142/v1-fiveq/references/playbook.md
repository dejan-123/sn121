# Question bank and one pool taken start to finish

The questions a retrieved pool keeps asking and the verdict each one earns, followed by a fabricated deal run through all five moves. Every company below is invented; only the reasoning is real. Reuse none of these facts, names or sentences.

---

## The questions, by kind

**On identity**

- A name one letter, one suffix or one word off — "(sic)" or not? Identity open: deeper-diligence whose entire action confirms the legal entity through the register; every other judgment waits on it; the top tier is out of reach.
- The register's own entries disagree? Split lettered, a row per status class: live under the exact name monitors; lapsed is a loss taken plus an item-by-item schedule request; another name is an identity question.
- The target's asset list versus the register? Count both directions — what the list claims and the register lacks is deeper-diligence with the schedule request; the register governs.

**On silence**

- An unsearched record that would settle a stated term? Must-address — the action IS the pull. One record settling two terms splits lettered, a row per term. A record named in the scope that no numbered signal reports takes no row; the evidence-gap paragraph carries it.
- Searched, came back empty — cleared? Never below deeper-diligence: the pull cleared that record as of its date and wants repeating before the deadline; it cannot reach the top. A register entry that answers a stated term — clean standing, nothing held — reads identically; only confirmations on ground no term stakes take monitor.
- Nothing on figures only the parties hold — churn, renewals, payroll, contract terms? Deeper-diligence requesting the paper from the parties. Silence on internal facts is a document request, not a finding — never a register event.

**On proof**

- Second and third outlets — corroborated? Only where each had independent access. Outlets quoting one release or spokesperson count once; a masthead citing another masthead adds nothing; a remark nested in a story rides that story's origin.
- The target's own word on the staked fact — enough? No — one origin that can never corroborate itself. Deeper-diligence until a counterparty or register confirms it.
- A lettered row per outlet or phrasing? Never — letters belong to a signal's own divergent parts: entries differing in status or footing (live versus lapsed, sworn versus hearsay). A pair of outlets, or one fact in two phrasings, remain one row.
- Thin items that together prove one fact against one term? A cluster named against that term — they climb together. And wherever several rows carry the same fact — press, the target's admission, a counterparty's record — all take the tier that fact earns, each action pointing back: "read with row N".
- Does the stated number cross? Work it once, on the base it is written on — unit counts in units, margins over the book they earn on, multi-year totals per year. Contradicted in kind but unmeasured: "untested"; the action supplies the count.

**On the fact as reported**

- Taken literally, does this thin report break a stated term? Yes + exact name + nothing in the pool answers it: must-address however thin — the fact as reported must already be the breach the term states (the violation, not the review toward it; the exit, not the talks), honest origin label, action naming who verifies today, a denial never disarms it. No: it cannot climb — at most the most urgent deeper-diligence.
- A review, complaint, inquiry or "no enforcement announced" — a breach itself? Never — it fails the counterfactual. Test the fact as printed, not the event it might precede: talks of a role are not a departure; an inquiry is not a finding. Deeper-diligence, urgency living in the action.
- Is the financing a lien? Raised for vehicles, plant or equipment, it is ordinarily secured on what it bought. A term assuming the asset unencumbered stands at risk; the action pulls the security schedule.
- Did the dispute end? "Without prejudice", or talks still open, leaves it able to return: must-address where a stated term depends on it, and only until the docket shows it ended for good; deeper-diligence elsewhere. "With prejudice" on ground the deal stakes: deeper-diligence — obtain the order and its terms. On ground nothing stakes: monitor.
- A departure, hire or claim — does it move a term? On the function a term or the thesis runs on: deeper-diligence, even anonymous. In a function nothing depends on: monitor. A term naming an individual plus a confirmed exit on a binding record: must-address — reports of talks, intent or steps toward it stay deeper-diligence.
- The parties a term names — what state are they in? Map each: confirmed on an independent record, asserted by the target alone, or missing from the pool — the missing ones carry the document request.
- The denial or counsel-retention row? Monitor — nothing new to check, and it never moves the claim it answers; the claim keeps its own tier.
- Confirmed good news? Monitor — a light keep-in-view; the record already did the work.

**On what cannot climb**

- A sector scare, an unnamed company, a class overlap — nobody in the deal? Noise, however grave. The target's exposure is tested through its own signals and unsearched records; the action is none, and no step wonders whether the unnamed party is secretly the target. Fluff that names the target but carries no evidence — praise, morale talk, generic approval — is noise too: attributed, yet bearing on nothing.
- Nothing stated — what still reaches the top? Only a live adverse claim against the thesis asset itself, standing on an independent record — a filed action naming the target on the asset the value rests on.
- An anonymous post naming the target on the staked topic? Attributed — anonymity weakens the label, never the link. An unverified single source never reaches the top unless the tripwire fires; it is the pool's most urgent deeper-diligence, action naming who confirms it today.

## Margin notes

- A headline restating a filing adds a masthead, never an origin.
- A figure contradicted in kind but never measured stays "untested" — mood does not resolve it; the action supplies the count.
- The loudest row in the pool is usually the bait; the quietest absence is usually the question.
- Where two readings survive, the row keeps the more conservative tier and the action carries the ambiguity — never split the difference across rows.
- "Satisfied to date" on a register pulled before the deadline still wants one re-pull; dates decide what "clean" means.

## A second sheet only — a quiet pool

Pre-LOI, no walk-away and no price term stated; the value rests on a miller's water abstraction rights. The sheet drafted: Q1 — the docketed claim against the miller on its rights: a live adverse claim against what the value rests on, borne by an independent record? (The only route to the top tier with nothing stated.) Q2 — the register search returned entries in three statuses: one row per class? Q3 — the sale memorandum lists six rights and the register returns five: which is the missing one? Q4 — the neighbour's diversion dispute: does it touch the target's own title? Q5 — the newsletter's "founder in talks with a rival estate": do talks meet "departure"? (No — the words report talks, and a term naming a departure is not met by talks. The row: unverified single origin | W1 | Deeper-diligence | deal counsel puts the report to the founder and board today — the pool's most urgent unverified row, held below the top because the fact as printed is a discussion, not an exit.) The table then answered the sheet; nothing else earned a raised tier.

## How to use this

Draft the sheet, place the evidence, rule on the questions, write the standard reply. Attribution settled before tiers; origins counted, never links; silences kept in their kinds; stated terms quoted as given and never invented; the top tier rare and always justified by name; urgency carried in the action rather than borrowed into the tier. Match the shape and the restraint; reuse none of these facts, names or sentences.
