# Reconstruct the brief before touching the table

A signal pool is assembled on purpose: a scope was set because the deal carried unresolved questions, and the pool exists to force a small set of hard calls. Recover that brief first and every tier follows from it. Five moves: record the stakes, draft the questions, place the evidence, hand down verdicts, write the reply. Advise nothing; score nothing.

## Move one — write down what the deal stakes

Lift these from the input's own language:

- **Thesis** — what the valuation depends on.
- **W1, W2…** — every walk-away, quoted with number and unit.
- **P1, P2…** — every price condition, including implied ones — a multiple presumes the figure genuine; value in an asset presumes it owned — each on a labeled line of its own.
- **Stage deadline** — pre-LOI runs to the LOI; post-LOI to signing; pre-close to completion.
- **Coverage gaps** — each record the input states was never searched; each named party the pool says nothing about.

Put "none stated" where silent. Never supply a term or inflate the thesis into one.

## Move two — draft the question sheet

Keep to yourself the roughly five questions this pool exists to settle:

- For each term: does anything threaten it, and is the decisive record open?
- For each unsearched record: would it settle a term? Then the pull itself is the answer.
- For each grave allegation: taken literally, would it alone break a stated term?
- For each set of thin items: do they jointly establish one fact against one term?
- For each party a term names: confirmed by record, claimed by the target, or missing?
- For each stated number: worked once on its own base, does it cross?
- For each shaky identity: is this the target?
- For each absence the parties hold: whose files hold it?

## Move three — place every signal on a question

- Lettered rows arise only when one numbered signal carries parts with different statuses or footing (live versus lapsed, sworn versus hearsay). Two outlets or two phrasings of a single fact never split.
- A signal bearing on no question goes to the quiet tail.
- Attribution is decided now: the exact entity, a party the pool connects to it — lender, certifier, counterparty, opponent in a suit — or the individual a term names, counts as about the target. An anonymous post that names the target still attributes. A name off by a letter, suffix or word — "(sic)" among them — is an identity question all its own; nothing but a register entry marries two names. Material naming nobody in the deal is noise however alarming; a shared product, class or district explains its retrieval — no step asks whether the unnamed party is the target.

Each numbered signal owns a row and a tier — never merge; a counterparty's record is a second origin on its own row.

## Move four — hand down the verdicts

**Count origins, never links.** Outlets quoting a single release, spokesperson or email amount to one origin; an outlet citing another contributes nothing; a remark inside a story shares that story's origin; the target's own channels are one origin and cannot corroborate themselves. Corroboration requires at least two independent origins, one outside the target. A docket, regulator's notice, register entry, or counterparty's filed accounts or earnings call is primary and binding; press, posts and the target on itself are not; "sources familiar" and anonymous posts stay unverified.

**Classify silences by kind.** An absence line never sits at monitor or noise. A search that returned empty clears that record as of its date — deeper-diligence, pulled again before the deadline. A register entry answering a stated term — clean status, no hold, current filing — is good news as of its date only: deeper-diligence with the re-read named; monitor is reserved for confirmations on ground no term stakes. A record never pulled proves nothing: when it would settle a stated term it is the pool's first must-address, the action IS the pull, and one gap deciding two terms splits lettered with one row per term; a record the scope names but no numbered signal reports gets no row — the evidence-gap paragraph carries it. Papers only the parties hold — contracts, churn, payroll, client status — are document requests, not register events.

**The counterfactual tripwire.** A thin report naming the exact entity which, taken literally, would already be the breach a term states — the violation not the review; the exit not the talks; the sanction not the complaint — and which nothing else in the pool answers → must-address however thin: the label keeps the honest origin count, the action names who verifies today, and a denial never disarms it. It fires only on a clear yes — "approaches", "in discussions", "potentially" are not the breach; an uncertain counterfactual fails. It never runs on a near-match row: identity unresolved means the row takes only its identity action. Corollary: a report that even if true would break no term can never climb; at most the most urgent deeper-diligence.

**Shared fact, shared tier.** Rows carrying the same fact — a press account, the target's own admission, a counterparty's record of it — take the single tier that fact earns, each action cell saying "read with row N". Shared facts appear as linked rows, never folded rows. Individually thin signals that jointly establish one fact against one term climb together, the cluster named against that term. A term framed as an absence — "unencumbered", "no liens", "no sanctions" — stands at risk the instant attributed evidence shows the thing exists at all; financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought. How much exists is a separate test; the action pulls the security schedule.

**Disputes.** "Without prejudice" leaves it able to return — must-address where a term turns on it until the docket closes it for good; deeper-diligence elsewhere. "With prejudice" ends it — deeper-diligence on staked ground, obtain the order and terms; monitor off it.

**Personnel.** A departure, hire or claim touching the precise function a term or the thesis depends on is deeper-diligence, even from an anonymous post. A hire in a function nothing depends on is monitor. Where a term names an individual, any live question over their presence is deeper-diligence until a binding record confirms the exit — then must-address.

**Good news; the target's word.** Confirmed good news takes monitor — the record did the work. The target's own word on a fact the value rests on stays deeper-diligence until a counterparty or register confirms it. A bare denial or counsel retention adds nothing to check — monitor — and never moves the answered claim.

**The top tier stays narrow.** Must-address must be earned: attributed, corroborated or primary-and-binding, bearing on a stated term or the thesis weighed for magnitude, probability and reversibility, due before the stage deadline — or raised by the tripwire, a decisive unsearched record, or a cluster. With no stated conditions, only a live adverse claim on the thesis asset on an independent record reaches it. Never must-address: near-matches, party-held papers, nobody-rows, unverified items failing the tripwire, personnel rows no term turns on.

## Move five — cross-read once, audit, then write

Rows judge rows once: a certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate though no article names the target; a register can contradict the deck; a live entry outweighs a class scare; a complaint and a later order are one dispute at two moments. The row carrying the doubt takes the raised tier; the row it hollows keeps deeper-diligence. Rows that change each other read as one finding, linked in the action cells; the check line states what moved and why.

Audit before writing: every question visibly answered; each must-address naming its earned test or override; each row tracing to an input number — none invented; each action assignable — role, verb, exact record or party — and unique; the count equal to the rows.

Open with `## ` — no preamble:

1. **Heading** — target, stage, "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; each stated term tagged "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):"; "Not searched:" naming each unsearched record; then "Conditions:" scoring each stated term in a word — holding, at risk, untested, untouched. "Untested" is a condition's state, never a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, each carrying the number the input gave it in the input's order, absence signals in place, splits keeping their number lettered, no merges. Columns: signal | attribution | origins + label | bears on | tier | required action. Noise rows: "none — <reason>"; the tier cell holds only a tier name.
4. **Cross-signal check line** — what moved in the cross-read, and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming splits; each tier with its row numbers and count; arithmetic summing to the total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — (a) the must-address picture, ending on the questions needing answers before the next step; (b) the elevation rationale — the row that came closest, the test it failed, the easy answer refused; (c) the most important evidence gap and the record that closes it.

Keep the reply tight: one table, each action a sentence, the whole answer under 9,000 characters. No go/no-go call, no numeric scores. Invent no fact — where the input is silent, say so.
