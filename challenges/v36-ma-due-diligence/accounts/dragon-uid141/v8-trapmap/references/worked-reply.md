# The field guide and a worked reply

The probe types with their lazy calls, then one invented pool answered end to end in the required format. These companies do not exist; only the patterns matter. Do not reuse these facts, names or sentences.

---

## The probes

**The near-match.** A grave accusation against a name one letter, one suffix, one word off — sometimes flagged "(sic)". Lazy: the story fits, elevate. Right: only a register entry marries two names; deeper-diligence with the single action of confirming the legal entity, every other judgment deferred. Micro-example: "Branford Quarries" cited in a blasting-safety notice when the target is "Branford Quarry Ltd" — an identity row and nothing more, whatever the notice says.

**The echo.** Two or more outlets carrying one release, one spokesperson quote, one email — or one outlet citing another's piece. Lazy: several outlets means corroborated. Right: one origin; the label stays single-source however many mastheads repeat it. Micro: three trade sites reprinting the same CEO letter is the letter, once.

**The empty chair.** A register inside scope returned nothing. Lazy: cleared. Right: cleared only as of the date searched — re-verify before signing when a stated term depends on it (deeper-diligence); where nothing staked turns on it, monitor with the date noted.

**The unopened drawer.** A decisive record was never searched — the input says so, or it lies outside scope. Lazy: no signal means no row. Right: the condition it would decide is untested and the row is must-address; the action IS the search; one gap deciding two stated terms splits lettered, one row per term. Micro: the security-filings index never run while the price assumes the plant unencumbered — that is the pool's first must-address.

**The drawer with no register.** "No signals found" on internal facts — a named client's status, churn, payroll, contract terms. Lazy: log it as a gap and move on. Right: deeper-diligence asking the parties for the papers; silence inside a company proves nothing either way, and it is never the register exception.

**The still-live dismissal.** A dispute dismissed "without prejudice", or settled beside live licence talks. Lazy: ended. Right: able to return — where a stated term turns on it the row stays must-address until the docket shows it ended for good; elsewhere deeper-diligence.

**The settled grave.** A claim ended "with prejudice" on ground the deal stakes. Lazy: a finished event needs nothing, or a past claim deserves the top tier. Right: deeper-diligence — obtain the order and the settlement terms; what was conceded or cross-licensed may still bind.

**Borrowed gravity.** A sector scare, an unnamed company, an unnamed regulator, a compound class overlapping the target's — the grave topic without the target in it. Lazy: the topic is grave, so elevate. Right: a row naming nobody in the deal is noise; class overlap earns at most monitor, and only when the thesis literally stakes the class.

**The self-report.** The target's own channels — site, release, deck, blog — confirming or denying. Lazy: a confirmation counts, a denial disarms. Right: one origin that never corroborates itself; the denial rides on the disputed fact's origin count and lifts nothing.

**The security presumption.** The target raised financing for vehicles, plant or equipment while a stated term assumes them unencumbered. Lazy: a facility announcement is not a lien record. Right: financing of this kind is ordinarily secured on what it bought — the term is at risk; quantify the share and pull the filings index.

**The staked person.** A departure, hire or claim touching the function a stated term or the thesis depends on. Lazy: personnel gossip. Right: deeper-diligence even from an anonymous post; a hire in a function nothing depends on is monitor; where a term names an individual, a confirmed departure on a binding record is must-address. Micro: the head of the certified-quality function leaving while the price assumes the certification valid.

**The chorus.** Several individually thin signals that together establish one fact against one stated term. Lazy: each stays thin on its own evidence. Right: they elevate together — linked rows take must-address and say "read with row N".

**The half-confirming register.** A register confirms the thesis core while listed items lapsed or sit under another name. Lazy: register entry = clean bill. Right: the lapse is a loss taken, the other name an identity question, and the action requests the item-by-item schedule — deeper-diligence.

**The thin alarm.** A single thin report naming the target exactly, saying the breaching process for a stated term is already under way, with nothing in the pool answering it. Lazy: thin evidence means deeper-diligence. Right: must-address however thin — a near-match name fails this test, and a spokesperson denial does not disarm it. The label keeps its honest origin count; the action names who verifies today.

**The loud rumour.** "Sources familiar" on the staked topic. Lazy: grave means must-address. Right: an unverified single source is never must-address — it becomes the pool's most urgent deeper-diligence row, its action naming who confirms it today, and the elevation rationale names it the closest call and the test it failed.

## How to read this

Name the probe, refuse the lazy call, file the verdict that survives. Attribution settled before any tier; origins counted not links; silences kept in their kinds; stated terms quoted as given and never invented; must-address rare and always justified by name; urgency carried in the action rather than borrowed into the tier; actions written so each could be handed to a named role. Match the shape and the restraint; do not reuse these facts, names or sentences.
