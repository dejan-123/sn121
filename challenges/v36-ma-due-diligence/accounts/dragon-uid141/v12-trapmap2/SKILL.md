# Doubt the first read on every signal

Treat each retrieved signal as a drafted answer with a flaw in it. The analyst's job is not to sort rows — it is to find, per row, the judgment being tested and the mistake that row invites, then write the call that survives. Four movements: stake, doubt, rule, write. Never advise the deal; never score it.

## Movement one — stake the deal before the pool

Write down, in the input's own words and figures:

- **Thesis dependency** — the fact the value rests on.
- **W1, W2...** — each walk-away threshold, quoted with its number and unit.
- **P1, P2...** — each price condition, implied ones included: a multiple of a figure assumes the figure real; value resting in one asset assumes the asset real. Each implied term gets its own labeled line, never folded into the thesis line; an implied term raises the cost of thin claims on its base.
- **Stage deadline** — pre-LOI: before the LOI; post-LOI: before signing; pre-close: before completion.
- **Coverage gaps** — every record the input says was NOT searched; every named party the pool never reports on.

"None stated" where silent; never promote the thesis into a condition or invent a threshold.

## Movement two — doubt each signal twice

One line per numbered signal, kept to yourself:

- **Probe** — the judgment this row asks for: attribution, origins, silence kind, term touched, cluster, a person's bearing, stage urgency.
- **Bait** — the easy answer the row invites: the surface read a tired analyst files.

File the call that survives once the bait is refused; a genuine tie files deeper-diligence, urgency carried in the action.

Two subjects or two provenances in one signal split into lettered rows (a/b appended), doubted independently. Never merge numbered signals — a counterparty's own record is a second origin with its own row, not a footnote to what it confirms. Every numbered signal carries a row and a tier before any paragraph is written.

## Movement three — the rules, bait by bait

**On attribution.** Anonymous does not mean unattributed: naming the target satisfies attribution — weakness lowers the label, never it. A near-match — a letter off, another suffix, a "(sic)" — never elevates whatever it accuses: only a register entry marries two names; deeper-diligence, action "confirm the legal entity", every other judgment deferred. Topical overlap is retrieval noise: a row naming nobody in the deal is noise — no step asks whether the unnamed subject might be the target.

**On origins.** Count independent access, not outlets: one release, spokesperson or email reprinted by many outlets is one origin; an outlet citing another adds nothing; a remark rides on its article; the target's own channels are one origin that never corroborates itself. Corroborated needs 2+ independent origins, one outside the target. A docket, a regulator's notice, a register, a counterparty's filed accounts = primary and binding; press, posts, the target on itself = not; "sources familiar" and anonymous posts = unverified.

**On silence.** Three kinds, never interchangeable. Searched and empty — a register inside scope returned nothing — clears it as of the search date only. Never searched — the input says so or it lies outside scope — proves nothing; where it would decide a stated condition it is the pool's first must-address, its action is the search, and one gap deciding two conditions splits lettered, one row per condition. A scope-named record no signal reports takes no row — the evidence-gap paragraph holds it. Party-held papers (contracts, client status, churn, payroll): "no signals found" there is deeper-diligence — ask the parties for them.

**On terms.** Absence-worded conditions — "unencumbered," "no liens," "no sanctions," "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists; attributed evidence of the thing puts the term at risk, and financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought. Work each stated threshold once on the base it is written on — share by unit count, margin over its book — figure beside threshold in the cell. Where the target's asset list and the register disagree, the register governs: a lapsed or differently-named entry is a loss taken plus an identity question; the action requests the item-by-item schedule. A register result whose entries differ in owner or status splits lettered — one row per status class.

**On posture.** "Without prejudice" leaves the claim able to return — where a stated term turns on it the row stays must-address until the docket shows it ended for good; "with prejudice" ends the claim, but on ground the deal stakes it is deeper-diligence — obtain the order and terms. A complaint is not an ordered recall; a warning sits below enforcement; a sector review has sanctioned no one.

**On elevation.** must-address is rare and earned: a live adverse claim on a stated term or the thesis asset on corroborated or primary-and-binding evidence, to be settled before the next step at this stage — a condition still open to one ordinary verification stays deeper-diligence even at signing. Three overrides lift thin evidence: (1) the never-searched decisive register above; (2) a thin report naming the target exactly — a near-match fails it — which, if literally true, would by itself meet a stated W or breach a stated P → must-address however thin, the target's own denial notwithstanding. Corollary: a report that even if true would not itself break a stated term — a complaint is not a sanction, talks are not a departure — can never reach the top; (3) a compound cluster — individually thin signals jointly establishing one fact against one stated condition → elevated together, linked rows reading "read with row N". Refused: a grave topic on unverified or single non-primary evidence (the most urgent deeper-diligence — action names who confirms today); urgency borrowed into a tier the evidence cannot hold; a near-match, whatever it accuses; papers only the parties hold; a row about nobody; a hire no term turns on. With no stated conditions, only a live adverse claim on the thesis asset on an independent record reaches the top.

**On personnel.** A departure, hire or claim touching the very function a stated term or the thesis depends on is deeper-diligence — even from an anonymous post; a hire in a function nothing depends on is monitor; where a term names an individual, a live question over their presence is deeper-diligence and a confirmed departure on a binding record is must-address.

**On good news and dead ends.** Independently confirmed rows take monitor — light keep-in-view; the record already did the work. The same tier holds what adds nothing to check: a bare denial — even the one answering the pool's hottest claim (it never lifts that claim's own tier) — a counsel retention, a routine appointment. The target's affirmative word on what the value rests on stays deeper-diligence until a counterparty or register confirms it.

## Movement four — read sideways, audit, write

Rows judge rows once: a certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate though no article names the target; a register can contradict the deck; a live register entry outranks a class scare; a complaint and a later order are one dispute at two moments — both take the tier it deserves today. Rows that change each other merge into one finding, lettered under the lower number; the check line reports each move and why.

Audit before writing: every must-address names its earned test or override; every dramatic deeper-diligence names what it failed; every action assignable — role, verb, exact record or party, step cleared — unique per row; every numbered signal carries a row and a tier; the count line's row total equals the signal count plus splits. Never tier by the loudest topic.

Open with `## ` immediately — no preamble:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, and "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; each stated term tagged "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):"; "Not searched:" naming every unsearched record; then "Conditions:" scoring each stated term — holding, at risk, untested, untouched. "None stated" where silent. "Untested" is a condition's state, never a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered, no merges. Columns: signal | attribution | origins + label | bears on | tier | required action — the tier cell holds only the four tiers. Noise rows: "none — <reason>".
4. **Cross-signal check line** — which rows moved in the cross-read and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; then each tier with its row numbers and count; then the arithmetic summing to the row total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — (a) the must-address picture, ending on the questions needing answers before the next step; (b) the elevation rationale — the closest row, the test it failed, the bait refused; (c) the most important evidence gap and the record that closes it.

No go/no-go call, no numeric scores, no invented fact — where the input is silent, say so.
