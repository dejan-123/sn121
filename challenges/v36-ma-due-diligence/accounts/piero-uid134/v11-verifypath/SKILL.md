# Resolution-path diligence triage

Every signal on a target resolves through exactly one record — a register entry, a filing, an order, a schedule only the parties hold. Name that record for each row, then let the record's status fix the tier: answered in the pool, unanswered but public, party-held, or unanswerable. Deliver the standard reply — tiered table, count line, short read. Never advise on the deal; never attach a score.

## Stage 1 — Read the stakes out of the context

From the deal context, in the input's own figures:

- **Thesis** — the fact the value rests on (a fleet, a patent family, a licence, a contract base). Signals bear on it; it is not a stated condition.
- **W1, W2...** — each walk-away threshold, quoted with number and unit.
- **P1, P2...** — each price condition, stated and implied: a multiple of a figure assumes the figure real; value in one asset assumes it exists and is owned.
- **Stage** — pre-LOI, post-LOI, pre-close; the deadline test reads against it.
- **Unsearched records** — every register the input says was NOT searched. A scope-named register no numbered signal reports gets no row — the gap paragraph carries it.

Nothing stated → "none stated" in the anchor; never invent a threshold or promote the thesis into a condition.

## Stage 2 — For each signal, name whose fact and how it was born

**Whose fact** — the target's exact entity, or a party the pool ties to it (named lender, certifier, counterparty, suit opponent, stated subsidiary). An anonymous post naming the target still attributes; anonymity weakens the label, not the link. A near-match — letter off, other suffix, differently-named affiliate, even "(sic)" — stays unresolved until a register entry marries both names; its settling record is an identity pull, and it can never carry the top tier. Nobody in the deal → topical retrieval, not a fact about the target: noise however grave. Two subjects or provenances → lettered rows, a/b, worked independently.

**How it was born** — count independent access, not links: outlets quoting one release are one origin; a citing outlet adds none; the target's own channels together are one origin that never corroborates itself; independent origins had their own access — docket, regulator's notice, registrar, counterparty filing, earnings call. The count sets the label — **corroborated** (2+ independent, one outside the target), **single-source** (primary-and-binding only for a docket, regulator's notice, statutory filing, charging document, counterparty filing), **unverified** ("sources familiar," anonymous posts), **absence** (a never-searched register row).

## Stage 3 — Name the settling record, then tier by its status

Each row carries in its action cell the one record that would resolve the question — "pull the charges-register extract," "obtain the Kestrel-style facility's security schedule," "confirm the legal entity via the company register." The record's status sets the tier:

- **must-address** — the signal bears on a stated W/P or the thesis AND its settling record is (a) shown in the pool contradicting a stated term on attributed evidence, (b) an unsearched public register whose pull decides a stated term, or (c) demanded by a stated term's text — weighed for magnitude, probability (shown occurring, not merely possible), and reversibility at this stage. By override also: a thin report naming the target *exactly*, whose reported process is already underway on it, that would by itself breach a stated term if true — the record pull becomes the action; honest label kept; a denial does not disarm it; a near-match never qualifies. And a cluster of thin signals that share one settling record and together establish one fact against one term — linked rows share the tier and mark "read with row N". One unsearched gap deciding two stated terms → split lettered, one must-address each.
- **deeper-diligence** — the settling record exists but sits with the parties or outside scope: the target's unconfirmed word on a fact value rests on; a searched-clean register speaking only for its date (re-verify before signing); a certificate whose issuer another row undermines; a claim ended on staked ground (obtain the order and terms); a departure or hire in the function a term or the thesis runs through — even anonymous; every unresolved near-match — the company register settles identity; internal facts "not found" (client status, churn, payroll) — papers only the parties hold.
- **monitor** — attributed, but either its record already settles in the pool in the target's favour (a corroborated renewal, a live register entry) or nothing staked turns on it: light action, a hire in a function nothing depends on included.
- **noise** — nobody in the deal: none, with a one-line reason.

**Terms written as an absence** — "unencumbered," "no liens," "no sanctions," "no active challenge" — are settled only while nothing exists: attributed evidence of the thing puts the term at risk before any register pull; financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought; a dismissal without prejudice leaves a challenge able to return; the target's own denial cannot settle what an outside record must.

**Never must-address:** a near-match name whatever the accusation; papers only the parties hold; a nobody-row — deeper-diligence never holds it either; a hire or departure no staked term turns on. No stated terms → only a live adverse claim on the thesis from an independent record reaches the top. A grave topic missing a term stays deeper-diligence — a sector review has sanctioned no one, a complaint is not an ordered recall, a warning sits below enforcement.

**The math** — compute each threshold once on the base it is written on (share by unit count, margin over its own book, term totals divided by years); the target's asset list set against the register, and where they disagree the register governs; a partially-confirming register (thesis matched, items lapsed or under other names) writes the lapse as a loss taken plus an identity question plus the item-by-item schedule.

## Stage 4 — Cross-read, audit, write

Rows judge rows once: a certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate; a register can contradict the deck; a live register entry outranks a class scare; a complaint and a later order are one dispute at two moments — merge rows that change each other, lettered under the lower number. Audit: every must-address names its settling record; every dramatic deeper-diligence names the test it failed; every numbered signal has a row and tier before any paragraph; every action assignable. A mixed pool is when discipline runs slowest — never tier by the loudest topic.

Open with `## ` — no preamble:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):" quoted; "Not searched:" naming every unsearched register; "Conditions:" each term's standing in a word — holding, at risk, untested, untouched. "None stated" where silent; a standing is not a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered. Columns: signal | attribution | origins + label | bears on | tier | required action — the action naming the settling record, role, verb, and step cleared; unique per row. Noise rows: "none — <reason>".
4. **Cross-signal check line** — which rows moved and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; each tier with row numbers and count; the four counts summed to the row total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — (a) the must-address picture ending on the questions needing answers before the next step; (b) the closest row to the top and the test it failed; (c) the most important evidence gap and the record that closes it.
