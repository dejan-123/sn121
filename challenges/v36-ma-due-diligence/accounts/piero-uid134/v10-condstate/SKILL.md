# Exposure-mapped diligence triage

Deliver the standard diligence reply — tiered table, count line, short read — but derive every verdict the same way: treat each stated deal term as an exposure line that evidence moves between states, and classify signals by the move they cause. Never advise on the deal; never attach a score.

## A. Draw the exposure map first (working pass, not printed as a table)

Before judging a signal, extract from the deal context, in the input's own words and figures:

- **T0** — the thesis dependency: the fact the price rests on (a fleet, a patent family, a licence, a contract base). Tracked like a term but never promoted into one.
- **W1, W2...** — every walk-away threshold, quoted with its number and unit.
- **P1, P2...** — every price condition, stated and implied: a multiple of a figure assumes that figure real; value in one asset assumes that asset exists and is owned.
- **Stage deadline** — pre-LOI, post-LOI, pre-close; urgency reads against it.
- **Unsearched registers** — every record the input says was NOT searched. A scope-named register with no numbered signal gets no row — the gap paragraph carries it.

Every exposure line sits in one of four states — **holding / at risk / untested / untouched** — and starts untouched. No stated terms → T0 alone; the anchor says "none stated."

## B. Mark every signal — evidence, not verdicts

**Whose fact.** The target's exact entity, or a party the pool ties to it (named lender, certifier, counterparty, suit opponent, stated subsidiary): attributed. An anonymous post naming the target still attributes — anonymity weakens the label, not the link. A near-match (letter off, other suffix, differently-named affiliate, even "(sic)") stays unresolved until a register entry marries both names, and never carries the top tier. Nobody in the deal → topical retrieval, not a fact about the target — noise however grave the subject. Two subjects or provenances → lettered rows, a/b, marked independently.

**Origins and label.** Count independent access, not links: outlets quoting one release are one origin; a citing outlet adds none; the target's own channels together are one origin that never corroborates itself; independent origins had their own access — docket, regulator's notice, registrar, counterparty filing, earnings call. The count sets the label — **corroborated** (2+ independent, one outside the target), **single-source** (primary-and-binding only for a docket, regulator's notice, statutory filing, charging document, counterparty filing), **unverified** ("sources familiar," anonymous posts), **absence** (a never-searched register row).

**The line it touches** — W1, P1, T0, or none. Touching nothing staked caps a row at monitor.

**Silence kinds.** A searched-and-empty register clears that register as of its date alone. A never-searched public register — dockets, regulators, enforcement databases, registrars, certification registries, patent and financing-statement records — proves nothing and leaves its line untested. "No signals found" on internal facts (client status, churn, payroll) is no register: the row asks the parties for the papers.

## C. Let evidence move the lines

- **holding** — corroborated or primary-and-binding evidence shows the term met as of a date; searched-empty speaks only for its date.
- **at risk** — attributed evidence shows the breaching thing exists or its process underway. Terms written as an absence — "unencumbered," "no liens," "no sanctions," "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists: financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought; a dismissal without prejudice leaves a challenge able to return. A denial from the target alone does not lift the state — it rides the same origin.
- **untested** — the deciding record was never searched, or evidence contradicts in kind but does not measure; party-held papers are never a register gap.
- **untouched** — nothing bears on it.

A partially-confirming register — T0 matched but items lapsed or under other names — records the lapse as a loss taken, the other name as an identity question, and asks the item-by-item schedule. Compute each threshold once on the base it is written on — share by unit count, margin over its own book, term totals divided by years. Where the target's asset list and the register disagree, the register governs.

## D. Tiers fall out of the states

- **must-address** — rows whose evidence puts a staked term at risk, or leaves a deciding term untested where that term gates the next step at this stage — weighed for magnitude, probability (shown occurring, not merely possible), reversibility. Included by override: a thin report naming the target *exactly* whose reported process is underway on it and which would by itself breach a stated term if true — honest label kept, denial does not disarm, near-match never qualifies; the never-searched register deciding a stated term — split lettered when one gap serves two; a cluster of thin signals jointly establishing one fact against one term — linked rows share the tier and mark "read with row N".
- **deeper-diligence** — touches a staked term on thin, date-bound or party-held evidence, settled by one verification: the target's unconfirmed word on what value rests on; a searched-clean register speaking only for its date (re-verify before signing); a certificate whose issuer another row undermines; a claim ended on staked ground (obtain order and terms); a departure or hire in the function a term or T0 runs through — even anonymous; every unresolved near-match — confirm the legal entity.
- **monitor** — attributed, touches nothing staked, or its line is already confirmed holding: light action — including a hire in a function nothing depends on.
- **noise** — nobody in the deal: none, with a one-line reason.

**Never must-address:** a near-match name whatever the accusation; papers only the parties hold; a nobody-row — deeper-diligence never holds it either; a hire or departure no staked term turns on. No stated terms → only a live adverse claim on T0 on an independent record reaches the top. A grave topic missing a term stays deeper-diligence — a sector review has sanctioned no one, a complaint is not an ordered recall, a warning sits below enforcement.

## E. Cross-read, audit, write the standard reply

One pass where rows judge rows: a certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate; a register can contradict the deck; a live register entry outranks a class scare; a complaint and a later order are one dispute at two moments — merge rows that change each other, lettered under the lower number.

Audit: every must-address names its line and the evidence that moved it; every dramatic deeper-diligence names the test it failed; every numbered signal has a row and tier before any paragraph; every action assignable. A mixed pool — registers, denials, rumours — is when discipline runs slowest: never tier by the loudest topic.

Open with `## ` immediately — no preamble:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):" quoted; "Not searched:" naming every unsearched register; "Conditions:" each term's state in a word — holding, at risk, untested, untouched. "None stated" where silent. The tier column takes only the four tiers — a state is not a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered. Columns: signal | attribution | origins + label | bears on | tier | required action. Every action assignable — role, verb, exact record or party, step it clears — unique per row. Noise rows: "none — <reason>".
4. **Cross-signal check line** — which rows moved and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; each tier with row numbers and count; the four counts summed to the row total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — (a) the must-address picture ending on the questions needing answers before the next step; (b) the elevation rationale — the closest row, the test it failed, why any dramatic finding sat lower; (c) the most important evidence gap and the record that would close it.

`references/worked-reply.md` holds two invented pools answered in full — one elevated, one mixed. Call `read_reference` before writing; write the reply in that shape.
