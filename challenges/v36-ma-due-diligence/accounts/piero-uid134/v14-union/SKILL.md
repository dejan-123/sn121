# Terms on trial — an ordered procedure

Every signal is testimony for or against a stated term. Work the five steps in order; do not skip.

Vocabulary: a **signal** is a lead, not a fact — who reports it, where seen, whether the record exists. A **term** is a stake — a walk-away, a price condition, or the thesis itself. A **register** is a record-settled source, never a press summary. Tiers grade evidence, not severity.

## Step 1 — Read the indictment

Extract, from the input's own words only:

1. **Stage** — the step findings must clear.
2. **Thesis** — what the price pays for: the asset, contract base, licence or person; never a condition.
3. **W1, W2…** — quote each walk-away with number and unit, **each matched to the record that would breach it** — a lien register for an unencumbered-assets term, an enforcement list for a violation term.
4. **P1, P2…** — each price condition, stated or implied — a multiple presumes its figure real; value in one asset presumes it owned — matched like walk-aways.
5. **Skipped registers** — every register the sweep missed, tagged by record kind. A scope-named register no numbered signal reports gets no row — the evidence-gap paragraph carries it.
6. **Worked thresholds** — a share of something the input counts, worked into its units.

Lines left blank read "none stated"; invent nothing.

## Step 2 — Four questions per signal

**Q1 Whose fact?** The target's exact entity, or a party the pool ties to it — certifier, lender, named customer, filed-case opponent, the person a term is about. Anonymous posts attribute when they name the target. A certificate is worth what its issuer is worth. A near-match — a letter off, another suffix or legal form, even "(sic)" — is undecided identity; only a register joins two names.

**Q2 How many origins?** Count first access, never outlets: outlets quoting one release or spokesperson share one origin; an outlet citing another adds none; everything the target publishes or says is one voice that never corroborates itself. Independent origins each had their own access — a docket, a notice or register, a counterparty's own filing. Labels: **corroborated** (2+ independent, one outside the target); **single-source** (say if primary and binding — docket, notice, filing, counterparty); **unverified** (anonymous, "sources familiar"); **absence** (a never-searched register).

**Q3 What kind of silence?** A searched-clean register clears its date only: answering a stated term it is dated comfort, re-pulled before the step; supporting the thesis it monitors as found. Never-searched proves nothing. Party-held facts are no register — ask for the papers.

**Q4 Which term does it touch?** W1, P1, the thesis or none — a row touching nothing staked never climbs past monitor; the record that settles the question writes the action cell.

## Step 3 — Tier in order; first match wins

| # | The row shows | Tier | The action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | nobody in the deal, or bears on nothing staked | noise | none — however grave the event, even the very one a walk-away guards against; the overlap is why the sweep returned it |
| 2 | an unsearched register of the very record kind a stated term turns on | must-address | the search; one search deciding two stated terms splits lettered, a row per term |
| 3 | the target spelled as the target is (a letter, suffix or form off fails outright) + the process producing the breach already under way — an inspection, a notice in draft, a regulator's own review of its conduct — and nothing in the pool answers it | must-address | written confirmation from the record-holder; a denial does not disarm it |
| 4 | thin rows together establishing one fact against a stated term — press, the target's own release, the tied party's disclosure | must-address each | "read with row N" each, each keeping its action; no record supplying the implied count → write "not yet measurable" and name the register |
| 5 | all four: attributed + corroborated or a single primary-and-binding record + decisive for a stated term or the thesis (magnitude, probability occurring not possible, reversibility) + settling before the next step | must-address | the decisive step |
| 6 | bears on a term or the thesis but fails a test, one verification settling | deeper-diligence | that verification |
| 7 | attributed; an independent record already confirms it, or nothing staked turns on it | monitor | light keep-in-view |

Row-class notes:

- Wrong-kind registers — observation logs beside a hold term, warning letters beside an enforcement term — and topics only the thesis touches stay deeper-diligence.
- Private chatter — talks, approaches, in-discussions — is never the breach process; a report that even if true would break no term can never climb; a near-match or an unnamed party never takes the tripwire.
- Deeper-diligence inventory: the target's own word on what the value rests on, including a win or renewal its counterparty has not confirmed; a searched-clean register answering a stated term (re-read before the step); a certificate whose issuer another row puts in doubt — the finding rides the issuer's row; a claim ended for good on ground the deal stakes — obtain the order and terms; a dismissal without prejudice touching no stated term — paused, able to return; every unresolved near-match; a departure or hire in the function a term or the thesis runs through where succession is unnamed or unconfirmed — even anonymous; talks about a person a term names; an unsearched register of the wrong kind; papers only the parties hold.
- Monitor inventory: a bare denial, which never lifts the claim it answers; a counsel retention; a trade-body membership, which is not the regulator's view; an orderly succession with successor named.

### Step 3b — Overrides

**Never must-address**, whatever the drama: a near-match even when its matter is "referred for review" or the term covers any breach "regardless of size"; papers only the parties hold; a nobody-row; a searched-empty sweep; a register in hand showing the term met; a hire no term turns on; a claim already ended; a report of private talks. No terms stated → only a live adverse claim on the thesis asset on an independent record reaches the top.

**Absence-worded terms** — "unencumbered", "no liens", "no sanctions", "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists: attributed evidence of the thing puts the term at risk; financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought; a dismissal without prejudice leaves a challenge able to return — where a term turns on no active challenge, that paused dispute stays must-address until the docket shows it ended for good. A term can be contradicted in kind while its measure stays unknown: write "not yet measurable" and name the supplying record.

**Filed figures** stand as filed; test each against its threshold once. Doubt a base only when the pool shows the figure struck on a different quantity than the term names — a one-off gain inside earnings — then the term is untested: must-address although the number clears.

## Step 4 — Rows read rows

- A certifier stripped of accreditation hollows every certificate it issued; the finding rides the accreditor's row; the certificate's row reads "read with row N".
- Set the target's own asset list against the register — the register governs disagreements. Entries differing in owner or status split lettered, one row per class — live, lapsed, other-name: the lapse a loss taken, the other name an identity question.
- Rows carrying one established fact share one tier, marked "read with row N". A second source inside one numbered signal stays on that signal's row; two numbered signals on one matter stay two rows. A later row confirming an earlier writes "confirmed by row N" — never a merge, never a renumber.
- Audit before writing: every numbered signal has a row and tier before any paragraph; every must-address names its earned test or override; the count's row total equals signals plus splits; the tier cell holds only the four tier words. A pool can end with no must-address row — say so and name the closest row.

## Step 5 — Shape the reply

Open with `## ` — nothing before it:

1. **Heading** — the target, the stage, "deal-risk classification".
2. **Anchor** — labelled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):" quoted with its deciding record and any worked figure; "Not searched:"; "Conditions:" each term's state — met, at risk, untested, untouched.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absences in place, splits lettered. Columns: number, signal, attribution, origins and label, bears on, tier, required action — role, verb, record or party, step; unique per row. Signal cells open on source and date; origins cells name the origin, never outlets. Noise rows: "none — <reason>"
4. **Cross-signal check** — which rows moved which, and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; read down the tier column: each tier's row numbers and count, then the arithmetic summing to the total.
6. **Three paragraphs, two to four sentences** — must-address picture ending on what needs answering before the next step; elevation rationale — the closest row and the test it failed, why each dramatic row sat lower; most important evidence gap and the record that closes it. Under 7,000 characters.
