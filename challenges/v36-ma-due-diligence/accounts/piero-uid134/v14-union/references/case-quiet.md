# Case two — a silent pool

A pool can hold no must-address row at all; say so plainly and name the row that came closest. This invented deal shows the read. Nothing here exists; carry over the reasoning and the shape, never the names, facts or sentences.

## The deal and the sweep

Input (abbreviated): acquiring Salborne Water Testing plc, pre-close. Thesis: accredited municipal water-testing capacity; the contract base rides on the laboratory-accreditation certificate. No walk-away stated. P1: a multiple of FY2025 filed EBITDA, reported at £6.8M. P2: the accreditation certificate current at closing. Scope: news and company sweep; the litigation database and the national accreditation register searched; the inspectorate's observations archive NOT searched.

Signals:
1. Filed accounts FY2025: EBITDA £6.8M, struck on continuing operations, no one-off items.
2. National accreditation register: the accreditation of Salborne's certifying body stands suspended in the water-testing scope pending reassessment.
3. Salborne's accreditation certificate on file: issued by that certifier, valid to March 2028.
4. Trademark register: TM-118 "SALBORNE" live under the exact name; TM-204 "SALWATER" renewal pending under the exact name; TM-377 "SALBORNE TESTING" registered to "Salborne Testing Holdings plc".
5. Borough Chronicle: a Midlands testing laboratory faces a noise-abatement complaint from residents. No company named.
6. Litigation database search: no active proceedings returned against the exact name.
7. No signal addresses renewal status of the three largest municipal contracts.
8. Laboratory Trade Journal: Salborne's head of quality has resigned; successor not yet named.
9. No entries from the inspectorate's observations archive — outside scope.
10. Court docket: a distributor's claim against Salborne was dismissed with prejudice in March last year.

## Misreads to avoid

- Two mastheads on one report are still one origin; a denial contributes none, and instructed counsel contributes less.
- Rows reporting one established fact share one tier: when the fact elevates, the press report, the target's own release and the tied party's disclosure climb together — "read with row N" — and the count the fact implies rides the register, not the headlines.
- One search deciding two stated terms takes two lettered rows; the same pull can carry an in-kind condition and its counted threshold.
- A clean pull has a date on it; "not searched" is never a verdict — it becomes a must-address row only when a stated term turns on that record, and a register of other kinds stays at deeper-diligence however close its topic.
- A story naming nobody in the deal names nobody; a name one letter or suffix off is an identity question, not a finding.
- Money raised against vessels, vehicles, plant or equipment is normally secured on them; when the count is missing, write "not yet measurable" and name the index that counts it.
- A certificate leans entirely on its issuer's standing: an accreditor withdrawn carries the must-address finding on the issuer's row, an accreditor suspended holds the certificate at deeper-diligence — and the certificate's own row always reads "read with row N".
- "With prejudice" finishes a claim; "without prejudice" merely pauses it, and a term demanding no active challenge keeps a paused claim at the top.
