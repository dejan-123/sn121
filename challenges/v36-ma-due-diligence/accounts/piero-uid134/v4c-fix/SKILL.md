# Reading signals on a deal target

Turn a public-records sweep into one tiered table, a count line, and a short read on each condition's standing. Five phases — map the deal, sort signals, tier rows, read rows against rows, write.

## 1. Map what the deal stakes before judging any signal

A signal means nothing until you know what it could break. First pass pulls:

- The **thesis asset** — the fact the price rests on (a fleet, a patent family, a licence, a contract base). Not a condition — it is what conditions protect.
- **Walk-away terms (W1, W2...)** — quoted exactly, with number and unit.
- **Price conditions (P1, P2...)** — stated, plus implied: a multiple of a figure assumes the figure real; value in one asset assumes it exists and is owned. Write each as the event breaching it.
- **Deal stage** — pre-LOI, post-LOI, pre-close. The deadline test reads against it.
- **Untested territory** — every register the input says was NOT searched. A scope-named register with no numbered signal gets no row — the evidence-gap paragraph carries it.

No stated walk-away or price term → "none stated"; never invent a threshold or promote the thesis into a condition.

## 2. Sort each signal by whose fact it is, then by origin count

**Whose fact is this?** Attribution follows the named subject, not source quality:

- **The deal** — names the target's exact entity, or a party the pool ties to it (its lender, certifier, counterparty, suit opponent, stated subsidiary). An anonymous post naming the target still attributes; anonymity lowers the label, not attribution.
- **Nobody in the deal** — however grave the topic: topical overlap is why the sweep returned it; the unnamed subject is never assumed to be the target. Tier: noise; action: none, one-line reason.
- **A near-match** — name differs by a letter, a suffix, a differently-named affiliate, even "(sic)". Never elevatable, whatever the accusation: deeper-diligence, action "confirm the legal entity via <register>"; only a register entry marrying both names resolves it.

A signal carrying two subjects or provenances splits lettered — same number, a/b, judged independently.

**Then count origins by independent access, not URLs.** N outlets quoting one release, spokesperson, or email = 1 origin; a citing outlet adds nothing; the target's website, releases, deck and blog together = 1 origin, never independent of the target. Independent origins had their own access — a docket, a regulator's notice, a registrar, a counterparty's filed accounts or earnings call. The count sets the label — **corroborated** (2+ independent origins, one outside the target), **single-source** (1 origin — primary-and-binding only for a docket, regulator's notice, statutory filing, charging document or counterparty filing), **unverified** (nothing traceable — anonymous posts, "sources familiar"), **absence** (a never-searched register row).

**Silence has three kinds — not interchangeable.** A register inside scope that returned nothing clears it as of the date searched, nothing beyond; where the input does not say either way, treat an absence line this way. A never-searched public register — dockets, regulators, enforcement databases, registrars, certification registries, patent and financing-statement records — proves nothing; a gap that would decide a stated condition takes the elevation below. "No signals found" on internal facts (client status, churn, payroll) is no register: deeper-diligence asking the parties for the papers, never the never-searched exception.

## 3. Tier the row

**must-address** is rare and earned — all four tests: (1) attributed to the deal under §2; (2) corroborated, or a single primary-and-binding record; (3) bears on a stated W/P or the thesis — magnitude, probability (shown occurring, not merely possible), reversibility; (4) must settle before the next step at this stage — a condition still open to one ordinary verification stays deeper-diligence even at signing.

A grave topic failing a test stays deeper-diligence. Read events for what they are: a sector review has sanctioned no one; a complaint is not an ordered recall; a warning sits below enforcement. A dismissal "without prejudice" or live licence talks leaves a dispute able to return — where a stated condition turns on it, must-address until the docket shows it ended for good; elsewhere deeper-diligence. "With prejudice" ends it — obtain the order and terms first.

**Conditions written as an absence** — "unencumbered," "no liens," "no sanctions," "no active challenge" — hold only while nothing exists: attributed evidence of the thing puts the condition at risk; financing raised for vehicles, plant or equipment is ordinarily secured on what it bought. A register that partially confirms — thesis core matched but items lapsed or under other names — writes the lapse as a loss taken, an identity question, and an itemized schedule.

**Three elevations override thin evidence** — nothing else does:

- A **thin report naming the target's exact name** that would by itself meet a stated W or breach a stated P if true, the reported process *underway on the target* (inspection, review, notice-drafting — not merely a scary topic) → must-address however thin. Label keeps its honest origin count; a spokesperson denial does not disarm it; a near-match never qualifies. A named report not itself decisive stays deeper-diligence, urgency in the action.
- A **never-searched public register** that would decide a stated condition → the condition is untested; the absence row is must-address, its action the search. One gap deciding two conditions → split lettered, one each.
- A **compound cluster** — several individually thin signals together establishing one fact against one stated condition → elevate as a cluster, named in the bears-on cell.

Never elevate: party-held papers (missing deck, unreceived contract copy); nobody-rows never reach deeper-diligence either; hires and departures in functions nothing staked depends on.

**Personnel:** a departure or hire in the deal-critical function — the one the thesis or a stated condition runs through — → deeper-diligence even anonymous, verified via register or counterparty; a hire elsewhere → monitor. **No stated conditions?** Only a live adverse claim on the thesis asset from an independent record reaches the top; the rest ranks by bearing.

**Lower tiers.** deeper-diligence — bears on the thesis or a condition but misses a test, settled by one verification: the target's unconfirmed word on a fact the value rests on; a searched-clean register speaking only for its date (re-verify before signing); a certificate whose issuer another row undermines; a claim ended on ground the deal stakes. monitor — touches nothing staked or confirmed by an independent record: light action. noise — none, with reason.

**The numbers.** Compute each threshold once on the base it is written on — fleet share by unit count, margin over the book it earns on, multi-year totals divided by their term — figure shown against the threshold. Set the target's own asset list against the register: units listed vs live under its exact name; where they disagree, the register governs. A condition contradicted in kind but unmeasured is untested — the row says what would supply the count.

## 4. Read rows against rows before locking

One pass where rows judge rows: a certifier losing accreditation hollows its certificate; a register can contradict the deck; the target's live register entry outranks a class scare; a complaint and a later order are one dispute at two moments. Mark correlated rows "read with row N" in the same tier; merge rows that change each other into one finding lettered under the lower number. The check line reports each move.

## 5. Audit, then write

Audit before writing: every must-address passes all four tests or names its elevation; every dramatic deeper-diligence row names the test it failed; every numbered signal has a row and a tier before any paragraph is written; every action assignable. A mixed pool — registers, denials, rumours — is when sorting runs slowest: never tier by the loudest topic. Open with `## ` — no preamble:

1. **Heading** — target name, deal stage, "deal-risk classification."
2. **Anchor** — labeled lines: "Thesis dependency:"; each condition "W1 (walk-away):" / "P1 (price):"; "Not searched:" naming every unsearched register; "Conditions:" scoring each in a word — holding, at risk, untested, untouched. "None stated" where silent. The tier column takes only the four tiers; "untested" is a condition's state, not a tier.
3. **Table** — one row per numbered signal, input order, absence signals in place, splits lettered. Columns: signal | attribution | origins + label | bears on | tier | required action. Every action assignable — role, verb, exact record or party, step it clears — unique per row. Noise rows: "none — <reason>".
4. **Cross-signal check line** — which rows moved and why.
5. **Count line** — `<rows> rows from <signals> signals`, naming any split; each tier with row numbers and count; the four counts summed to the row total.
6. **Three short paragraphs** — (a) the must-address picture, ending on the questions needing answers before the next step; (b) the elevation rationale — the closest row, the test it failed, why any dramatic finding sat lower; (c) the most important evidence gap and the record that would close it.

Never offer a go/no-go call or numeric risk scores; never invent a fact — where the input is silent, say so.
