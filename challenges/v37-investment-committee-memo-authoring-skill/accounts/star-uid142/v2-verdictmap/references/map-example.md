# Worked example — the verdict map

A full run on a fictional deal. The shape transfers: every finding
maps a trigger state beside its filed label, the verdict reads the
board, and the memo prints what armed and what cleared. Entities
and figures invented; the mapping pattern is the deliverable.

## The input (abridged for illustration)

Risk table filed:

| # | Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regional hospital network master guarding agreement = 36% of revenue; counterparty holds termination right on 60 days' notice following change of control | Must-Address | Unresolved |
| 2 | Firearms endorsements for armed-post officers filed current; the roster shows 2 of 9 expired last quarter | Manageable | Resolved |
| 3 | Workers' comp experience modifier runs 1.31 vs 1.00 industry | Manageable | Unresolved |
| 4 | Dispatch console software reaches end-of-life in 18 months | Informational | Unresolved |
| 5 | Founder-CEO stepping to advisory over 12 months | Manageable | In-Progress |

Narrative: LTM revenue $24.7M including a completed $1.2M venue
contract; adjusted base $23.5M; EBITDA $3.1M; organic growth 8%.
Ask $25M. Comps: GuardCore 6.9x, SentinelGroup 5.4x, Watchline 6.1x
EV/EBITDA, EliteForce 9.6x (banker note: "acquired in a strategic
consolidation at a premium").

## The memo

**request more data** — the hospital-network termination right is
armed; ask $25M as stated.

## 1. Governing terms

Acquire Hale Protective Services for $25M — a route-density guarding
business on a $23.5M adjusted base (the completed $1.2M venue
contract stripped) carrying $3.1M EBITDA at 8% organic growth. The
deciding trigger is whether 36% of revenue survives the close: the
hospital network can terminate on 60 days' notice once control
changes, and the waiver it would sign has not been signed. Two of
nine armed-post endorsements also map expired against a Resolved
filing.

## 2. Verdict map

| # | Finding | Filed | Trigger | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hospital network 36% of revenue; 60-day CoC termination right | Unresolved | A signed waiver or novation of the termination right | **triggered** — the deciding signature sits with the network; until it signs, the revenue the ask prices is terminable at close |
| 2 | Firearms endorsements filed current; roster shows 2/9 expired | Resolved | Renewed endorsement certificates for the two officers | **pending** — filed Resolved but the roster contradicts it; a Manageable row flags pending with the reason, never re-tiered |
| 3 | Experience modifier 1.31 vs 1.00 | Unresolved | Safety program review and insurer confirmation of the modifier trajectory | **pending** — a quantified exposure; carries a named disposition, not a block |
| 4 | Dispatch software end-of-life, 18 months | Unresolved | Migration plan and budgeted replacement | **pending** — dated runway; a plan and budget carry it |
| 5 | Founder to advisory over 12 months | In-Progress | Signed advisory agreement with retention terms | **pending** — dated transition; retention terms carry it |

**Map count:** 1 triggered / 4 pending / 0 clear / 0 void

## 3. Price supported

Base trigger: $23.5M stands after the completed venue contract maps
out; EBITDA $3.1M holds, so EV/EBITDA arms here. Growth maps at the
realized 8%, not the 14% projected off two unawarded municipal
tenders.

Comp triggers: EliteForce is excluded — the banker note itself
reads 9.6x as a strategic consolidation premium; a buyer's price is
not a trading multiple. Included: SentinelGroup 5.4x, Watchline
6.1x, GuardCore 6.9x — a 5.4x–6.9x band, 6.1x median. Hale's
modifier problem and endorsement lapses argue mid-band.

Arithmetic: 5.4x × $3.1M = $16.7M; 6.9x × $3.1M = $21.4M. The map
supports $16.7M–$21.4M.

Ask reading: $25M implies 8.1x the posted EBITDA — above the band.
The gap maps as a price condition, not a pass.

## 4. Verdict

Request more data. One gating trigger is armed — the hospital
network's unwaived termination right — and it clears with a named
record the network can sign inside the deal's timeline: the waiver
or novation. Proceed cannot map while a gating trigger is armed;
pass does not map because a signature nobody has requested is
requestable, not outside-held — the network has refused nothing.
The pending rows (endorsements, modifier, software, succession)
each carry a named disposition below. The $25M ask at 8.1x maps
repriced as the negotiation the request carries.

## 5. Triggers for closing

1. Hospital network's written waiver of the change-of-control
   termination right, or novation of the agreement, before
   signing — closing deliverable.
2. Renewed firearms endorsements for the two lapsed officers
   before close — closing deliverable; an expired roster entry is
   not a resolved one.
3. Written safety-program plan with insurer-confirmed modifier
   trajectory — specific indemnity posture priced at binding.
4. Dispatch migration plan with budget before close — price
   reduction for the quantified, unfunded need.
5. Founder advisory agreement at close covering the 12-month
   transition — retention instrument.
6. [to confirm: guard-related liability claims, trailing 36 months
   — the artifacts are silent.]

The read — a real guarding book priced ~$4M above its band on one
unsigned waiver and a handful of manageable items. The map asks
for the signature and pays for the $23.5M base.

## When nothing arms — the clear shape

Same file with the waiver signed and the endorsements renewed:

**proceed** — no gating trigger armed; the ask maps inside the band
at 6.9x posted EBITDA.

## 5. Triggers for closing

None gating. Standing items: the modifier's insurer review on its
annual cycle, the dispatch migration filed for the first post-close
quarter.

The board maps identically either way — a clear map changes the
verdict, never the mapping.

## When the trigger cannot fire — the void shape

If the hospital network's termination right were exercised already —
a written notice on record terminating on change of control — the
trigger is not armed-and-clearable, it has already ruled: a
counterparty on record refusing. The base maps net of the 36%
cohort, the range recomputes on what survives, and where the ask
still prices the dead revenue the verdict is pass: name the notice,
show the arithmetic, name the withdrawal that would rearm the map.
