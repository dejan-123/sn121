### 1. Deal thesis (up to 140 words)
Kestrel sells milk-run routing software to regional distributors:
$11.4M LTM revenue after removing a completed $1.8M customs
project, 84% recurring, 63% gross margin, EBITDA $1.1M. The thesis
prices the routing base; row 2 — the anchor distributor's unsigned
assignment consent covering 29% of revenue — is the primary risk.

### 3. Valuation (up to 300 words + comp table)
Base $11.4M = $13.2M reported less the completed project. Comps
price EV/Revenue.

| Comparable | EV/Rev | Growth | In/Out | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RouteWise Inc. | 2.9x | 19% | In | Trading peer |
| DepotLoop Co. | 2.4x | 12% | In | Trading peer |

| MileOps | 3.3x | 27% | In | Trading peer |

Included median 2.9x (odd count — the middle of 2.4, 2.9, 3.3).
The pair: median comp RouteWise and its neighbour on the downside
side the margin gap points, DepotLoop: 2.4x × $11.4M = $27.4M;
2.9x × $11.4M = $33.1M. Range: $27.4–$33.1M. The $27M ask is 2.4x
the base — under the range.

### 4. Recommendation (up to 130 words)
**Request more data.** Row 2 is the one open blocking row and it
answers to a document — the executed assignment consent. Rows 3
and 4 are priced or monitored inside conditions; they do not hold
the call. The ask prices under the range — price is not the
question. Verified: primary risk = row 2, first blocking row;
figures identical across sections.

### 5. Conditions
1. Row 2: executed assignment consent before signing — absent it,
   walk or re-price excluding that revenue.
2. Row 3: $120K violation reserve escrowed at close.
3. Row 4: lease renewal option exercised at close.
