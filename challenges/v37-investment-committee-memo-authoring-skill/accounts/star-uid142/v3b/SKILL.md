# The memo that survives being quoted back

A committee member reads this memo again in a year — at the good
outcome and the bad one. Every line must still hold: a row's filed
words beside what the record actually showed, the base beside what
the seller claimed, every comp's fate beside its reason. Three
authors wrote the artifacts at different times; where they
disagree the memo says so openly and never repairs one with
another. A fact the artifacts lack is `[to confirm: …]`, never
invented.

## Lines about the rows — read, never re-filed

Each row keeps its filed tier and status verbatim. Blocking rows
are labels meaning clear-before-deal (Must-Address, Critical,
Deal-Breaker, Blocker, High); a row filed lower never joins them —
it takes a condition or a price note, flagged where the label
understates.

| Filed as | The file shows | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Resolved | another artifact shows it live — re-bid running, review deferred, no closing letter | open, flag quoting source |
| Resolved | only its own wording still describes the exposure | stands, questioned — proving document becomes a condition |
| Unresolved / In-Progress / blank | — | open |
| verified, uncontradicted | — | stands |

## Lines about money — what actually transfers

Base before multiple: strip one-time work, revenue that cannot
transfer (grants, contracts ending on control change), customers
already lost — the file's own post-loss run-rate. Revenue merely
at risk — a re-bid, a near-expiry — stays in, named downside.
Plainly one-time add-backs only, each tested alone; the corrected
figure beside the seller's. Negative EBITDA on the transferable
base → EV/EBITDA is not meaningful; EV/Revenue carries it.

## Lines about the comps — all accounted for

Every comparable named in or out, one reason each: a control or
strategic premium beside trading peers; NM on the metric in use;
an outlier whose gap to its nearest neighbour exceeds the spread
of the rest — else retained, its gap becoming the adjustment.
The comp table's last row is the included median. The range: two
products — with an even included count, the comps flanking the
median; odd, the median comp and the neighbour on the side the
adjustments point — each multiple × base = value, $M to one
decimal, quantified debt-like items off each end. The range is
the valuation's last line. The ask is one implied multiple on the
priced base, placed under/within/above with the $M gap.

## Lines about the call — provable, not argued

- `proceed` — zero open blocking rows; every open lower row
  carries its named mitigation. Where no blocking-tier row exists
  at all, write plainly *no finding here is a deal-breaker* — the
  absence is a finding the committee must be told.
- `request more data` — ≥1 open blocking row a document answers:
  consent, closure letter, executed renewal, clearance, audited
  figure. Name the record, its issuer. Open lower rows never
  downgrade a proceed — their asks live inside its conditions.
- `pass` — an open blocking row fails all three: unresolved;
  removes what the base prices at a size no range absorbs; no
  document or act before signing cures it. A right lapsing on a
  date no party moves — a patent term, a statutory licence — is
  incurable where the thesis prices it. A refusal counts only
  unconditional and on record — 'indicated', 'may not', 'if the
  buyer is not…' are positions, not refusals; a replaceable role
  (sponsor, processor, licensor of record) stays fillable; a
  cure's length is not its incurability; a row turning on an
  unstated buyer fact is askable by definition. A term or rule
  barring the transfer itself is the only bar no consent papers.
  An ask above the range is a price condition, never a pass.

## The memo — written for the re-read

`# Investment Committee Memo — <target>` then a first line holding
the call word, the ask (or its absence) and the priced base — the
line that gets quoted. Five sections, bounds in the headings:

1. `## 1. Deal thesis (up to 140 words)` — the asset, the
   economics on the restated base, the primary risk by row.
2. `## 2. Risk findings (a row per finding)` — # | finding | tier
   filed | status filed | read | deciding fact ≤30 words, input
   order. Beneath: `Open blocking rows: <n>` or `none — no
   deal-breaker in the file`, ≤2 flag lines.
3. `## 3. Valuation (up to 300 words + comp table)` — base and
   why, the comp table, adjustments in words, the ask, the two
   products, the range as the last line.
4. `## 4. Recommendation (up to 130 words)` — the call word
   first, the deciding rows in their own words, the ceiling where
   the ask runs over, and `Verified:` — risk = first blocking
   row, price inside range, figures identical everywhere.
5. `## 5. Conditions (a bullet per open or questioned row, up to
   40 words)` — record or instrument, issuer, timing mark from
   the file's own dates, consequence: walk, re-price, escrow.

Roughly 6,000–8,000 characters; cut prose before rows, products,
the call or conditions. Call read_reference once for
`references/defense-memo.md` — one invented deal answered in
full, then its §4/§5 under the other votes.
