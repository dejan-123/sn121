# Verdict Map — the memo is a map of which triggers fired

Every element of a diligence pack has one verdict the record awards
it. A filed Status is a claim awaiting its trigger check; a seller's
base is a claim awaiting its restatement check. This skill finds
each element's deciding trigger, records honestly whether it fired,
and writes the memo as the resulting map.

## The mapping pass

**Map 1 — Governing terms.** Write the frame: acquire <target> on
what terms, from these artifacts. Record the asking price — or record
"no asking price stated," which is itself a mapped fact, never a
number to invent.

**Map 2 — The status triggers.** Each risk row's deciding trigger is
the record that would actually close it. For a row filed Resolved,
the trigger is documentary: a signed consent, an auditor's opinion,
a closure letter, an executed renewal — not a deferral, an
expectation, talks in progress, or a plan accepted for scheduling.
Where the trigger's document exists, the
row maps clear as filed. Where it does not — a re-bid still live, a
certification lapsed and unrenewed, a consent never issued, an
outcome confused with a process under way — the row maps to its true
verdict: the trigger has not fired, the item stays armed, and the
override names the missing document. An appended analyst note
flagging a contradiction is a trigger check already run — carry its
result. An under-mapped row — a standards breach filed routine, a
churn signal filed informational — is re-mapped to the trigger its
content implies.

**Map 3 — The base trigger.** The valuation base maps to one
question: what revenue does the acquirer actually own the day after
close? The seller's figure fires false wherever it counts revenue
that completed and will not recur, revenue that does not transfer —
grants a for-profit cannot hold, terminable contracts, departed
customers — or a gross figure where the industry's metric is net.
The base that maps is the restated one, and growth maps on the same
restated basis. EBITDA maps as a viable trigger only where it is
positive on the economics that transfer and the comps themselves
are profitable; elsewhere it maps void and EV/Revenue carries the
range. Add-backs map only where genuinely non-recurring — a rejected one
is never subtracted twice. Growth contingent on unsigned deals —
prospects in study, unconverted pilots — maps as a claim: realized
growth is the priced rate.

**Map 4 — The comp triggers.** Each comparable has an inclusion
trigger. Fired, it belongs in the range. Failed triggers — a
growth/margin profile so far off the target's that inclusion would
bend the multiple, an acquisition consummated at a control or
strategic premium (a banker note flagging the premium is the
trigger's own confession), a revenue mix that cannot map absent a
stated adjustment — strike the comp with the reason recorded. Where
the comp set's own note declares a limitation — mix undisclosed,
only one viable multiple — the limitation maps into the memo rather
than being priced around. Then the range maps as arithmetic: the
stated base times the stated multiple, low comp to high comp. The
ask maps against the range as its own implied multiple — recorded,
not argued.

**Map 5 — The headline verdict.** The verdict has three states —
proceed, request more data, pass — and it is whatever the map shows,
not a preference. A must-address or critical trigger still armed
voids proceed — always, on any file; a proceed mapped over an armed
trigger contradicts the map and fails the memo. Then check whose
hand is on the trigger. Where each armed item has a named record
that would fire it shut — a consent, a clearance, a signed
renewal, a resolving opinion — the verdict is request more data:
the memo asks for the records and each armed row names the one that
settles it.
Outside-held is a narrow map: a trigger counts only on a sovereign
determination — a court ruling, agency verdict, accreditation
decision — a holder on record refusing, or a finding no record can
settle (an expired patent cannot be re-papered). A signature nobody
has requested yet — a licensor's consent, a customer's renewal — is
askable, not outside-held. Two or more armed must-address triggers
outside-held maps pass: a map that needs strangers' verdicts is a
voided one. The same verdict holds where the armed trigger is
structural — the core asset does not transfer, the revenue base does
not survive close — or the ask cannot reach the mapped range. Where no must-address trigger is armed, proceed
is a complete map — the empty region is a finding, stated plainly,
never filled with manufactured alarms; items below
must-address map as hygiene in the read. The thesis names one
primary risk — the armed trigger
that decides the deal — and it appears in the map; a thesis
risk absent from the map contradicts the memo.

**Map 6 — Dispositions.** Each condition is a trigger that can be
fired shut: the finding it clears, the exact record that fires it,
the party who must produce it, and the deadline or event it must
precede. A trigger no party can fire — a consent belonging to a
third party already declining — is not a condition; it is the
harder verdict. Quantified exposures map in dollars against the
price.

## Verdict vocabulary — row standings

- `triggered` — the deciding trigger is armed and unfired; it gates
  the headline.
- `pending` — a named record would settle it; the disposition is
  written — under proceed the cell carries it, so no armed row is
  left unowned.
- `clear` — trigger checked, record confirms the filed status;
  carried.
- `void` — informational or outside the decision; no disposition
  spent. No fifth standing exists.

## The map covers every row

No input finding is dropped or merged. Rows armed by the same root
trigger keep separate entries and cross-reference ("armed by the
same trigger as row N"). A verdict that could be pasted under
another finding is map noise — replace it with the deciding words
from this input.

## Empty regions are data

A map with zero armed triggers is complete, and the conditions
section maps "none gating" rather than vanishing.

## Ungovernable input

Missing ask, missing status column, two authorities in direct
conflict — the gap maps as its own entry and the memo proceeds on
what maps, or makes the missing record the condition. Two cited
authorities in disagreement map a pending resolving opinion — the
map never picks the convenient reading. A specific the input does
not give maps as a bracketed `[to confirm: …]`, a named gap, never
a guess.

## Triggers a senior reviewer checks first

- **The deal-killing trigger** — the primary risk is the armed item
  that reprices or vetoes, not the largest number in the file.
- **The unprinted verdict** — the implied ask multiple, the run-rate
  after departed revenue, the downside in dollars: compute the
  deciding figure and show the work.
- **The direction of the map** — every restated base maps which way
  it moves the economics.
- **Whose terms these are** — a multiple quoted on a flattering
  base gets its basis named and restated in the same line.
- **Triggers that cannot fire** — a condition no party can deliver
  is not a condition; it is the harder call in costume.

## How the map fails

- Filed statuses mapped without checking their triggers — the most
  common misread; upstream maps run optimistic and this pass exists
  because they do.
- A comp averaged in with no inclusion trigger checked.
- Two currencies of truth on one map — gross against net comps,
  full LTM against a recurring range.
- Dispositions that delegate rather than fire.
- A headline the triggers cannot carry — clear over an armed
  must-address, or pass where a signature ends the matter.

## Drawing the map

Memo shape — the five required sections, in this order:

1. `**Governing terms**` — the decision and the ask (or its absence),
   then two to four sentences of case closing on the named primary
   risk, verbatim as it maps in the table.
2. `**Verdict map**` — the table: `# | Finding | Deciding trigger |
   Fired? | Verdict`. Every row; each override names the missing
   document in the verdict cell. Then `Map count: <n triggered /
   n pending / n clear / n void>` reconciling to the table.
3. `**The price the map supports**` — the restated base, each comp's
   inclusion verdict in a clause, the multiple, `base × multiple =
   range`, the ask mapped against it.
4. `**The verdict**` — proceed, request more data, or pass in one
   sentence plus the armed trigger that decides it; never contradicts
   the map.
5. `**Triggers for closing**` — numbered: finding, record, party,
   timing; the character follows the verdict. Last line: two
   sentences the committee can repeat.

Budget: the harness cuts an overlong memo at the tail — the verdict
and the triggers are the tail, so nothing may run long enough to
starve them. Hold every stated length bound the scenario gives;
where none is stated, the case is four sentences, rows a line each,
the price six lines, the verdict two sentences, each trigger a line.
Cut narration before cutting rows.

in that shape.
