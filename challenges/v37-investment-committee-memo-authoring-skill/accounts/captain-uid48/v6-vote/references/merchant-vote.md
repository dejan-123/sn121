# Committee vote: Wildfern Floral Supply

Wildfern Floral Supply distributes flowers to shops and event coordinators. The fictional packet below is for the worked memo only. It is unrelated to any other transaction.

The register contains four findings, none filed as a clear-before-deal risk:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Refrigerated van fleet has deferred routine maintenance | Manageable | In-Progress |
| One event venue disputes an unpaid delivery invoice | Manageable | Unresolved |
| Customer reorder rates have softened | Informational | Unresolved |
| Insurance policy was renewed last quarter | Informational | Resolved |

The narrative gives $11.0M of last-twelve-month revenue, including a $1.0M one-time festival contract. Ordinary sales are $10.0M. The buyer expects future orders but none is in the reported base. Gross margin is 26%, growth on ordinary sales is 8%, and no debt or cash number is supplied. The ask is $19.0M enterprise value.

The trading comparables are Laurel Distribution at 1.5x EV/revenue (7% growth, 25% margin), Copperleaf Trade at 1.8x (11%, 28%) and Hazel Goods at 2.2x (16%, 33%). Roseworks at 3.6x is a completed sale of control to a strategic owner, not a trading quote.

**Recommendation: proceed** -- no filed blocking risk is open; $15.0M-$18.0M enterprise value on ordinary sales against a $19.0M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis

Wildfern supplies repeat retail orders through an existing cold-chain network. Ordinary $10.0M sales support a modest trading multiple, not the festival-driven $11.0M headline. The primary risk is the refrigerated van fleet's deferred maintenance: service interruptions could affect customer reorder rates, so obtain a priced repair plan and protect the purchase price. It is filed Manageable and does not change the voting tier.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Standing on evidence and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Van maintenance deferred | Manageable | In-Progress | Repair work still underway; obtain inspection and quotes, with a documented price adjustment for any verified shortfall. |
| Disputed delivery invoice | Manageable | Unresolved | Obtain the venue's position and matching records; reserve the documented amount if collectible value is impaired. |
| Softer reorder rates | Informational | Unresolved | Retain existing sales in the base; monitor actual renewals before assigning any projected improvement. |
| Insurance policy renewed | Informational | Resolved | No other artifact disputes renewal; get the certificate as an ordinary closing deliverable. |

Zero gating rows are open: the source register contains no finding filed at the blocking tier. Open Manageable rows remain conditions, not a reason to defer today's vote.

## 3. Valuation

Reported revenue is $11.0M; less a stated one-off $1.0M festival order gives a $10.0M ordinary-sales base. Laurel (1.5x), Copperleaf (1.8x) and Hazel (2.2x) are retained trading distributors. Roseworks (3.6x) is excluded as a completed strategic control acquisition. Wildfern's 8% growth and 26% margin place it near Laurel and below Copperleaf, supporting 1.5x-1.8x:

- 1.5x x $10.0M = **$15.0M enterprise value**
- 1.8x x $10.0M = **$18.0M enterprise value**

The enterprise range is $15.0M-$18.0M. The $19.0M ask is 1.9x ordinary revenue and exceeds its $18.0M ceiling by $1.0M. No equity bridge can be calculated without debt and cash.

## 4. Recommendation

Proceed at no more than $18.0M enterprise value. There is no filed blocking-tier risk. The open van, invoice and reorder matters need instruments and follow-up, not a newly invented Must-Address tier. The $19.0M ask is a negotiation to reduce by $1.0M, not grounds to pass. A material inspection finding may change the price; it does not excuse signing before repair cost is known and protected.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Operations receives the vehicle inspection and vendor repair quotes before signing; incorporate documented unfunded costs into the price or a seller-funded repair commitment.
2. Finance obtains the disputed invoice correspondence before closing; reserve only the measured uncollectible amount.
3. Commercial lead reviews customer reorder records before closing and reports any confirmed loss; uncertain future orders never enter the current base.
4. Counsel obtains the renewal certificate at close and checks coverage continuity.
5. Deal lead counters the $19.0M ask within the supported $15.0M-$18.0M enterprise range before signing.

## Different facts, different votes

An unrelated packet may have one genuinely open highest-tier finding: a required independent assay has been ordered but not completed. Today's answer is **request more data**; request the signed assay from the qualified provider before signing, with a favorable result reopening proceed and a failed result bringing the deal back. The question is not answered by appointing an adviser.

A different top-tier right may end by its own unchangeable terms while the offered business still includes the income that right alone protects. Once the packet establishes that no renewal or transferable successor exists, today's answer is **pass**. Other open requests do not turn a fixed negative fact into an information gap; a memorandum describing the right cannot preserve its income.

Conversely, a counterparty's statement that it *would* withdraw if a future buyer lacks a required qualification is conditional, not an unconditional refusal. Ask whether the actual buyer qualifies and whether another qualified party can take the role. Do not invent the buyer's status or assume there is no replacement.
