# Worked brief: Redbank Supply

This invented wholesale distribution case illustrates the finished memo. The figures and parties exist only in this example.

## A complete vote with no filed top tier

For the unrelated Ashgrove Costume Hire pack described at the end of this file, the brief is:

**Recommendation: proceed** -- no finding was filed as a deal-breaker; $8.3M-$9.9M enterprise value on ordinary rentals, with no asking price given.

## 1. Deal thesis
Ashgrove rents theatrical wardrobes to repeat clients. Ordinary rentals, not the single festival engagement, support the acquisition. The primary risk is unmatched customer deposits: if liabilities have not been reconciled, working capital at close may disappoint. The row is Manageable, so obtain the ledger and protect the price rather than hold the committee's vote.

## 2. Tiered risk table
| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Evidence and treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deposit matching | Manageable | Unresolved | Open operating item; obtain reconciled deposit ledger and adjust working capital for verified balances. |
| Storage change under lessor review | Manageable | In-Progress | Lessor has not decided; counsel to secure its written answer under the closing plan. |
| Damage complaint | Manageable | Resolved | No later contradiction supplied; confirm settlement paper without reopening the filed status. |
| Utilization decline | Informational | Unresolved | Carry into pricing assumptions and the operating plan. |

There are zero open gating rows: none was filed at the gating tier. The lessor's pending answer does not change that count.

## 3. Valuation
Rental revenue of $6.0M includes a $0.5M one-off festival job, leaving $5.5M ordinary rentals. Two quoted trading peers at 1.5x and 1.8x frame a conservative range; both are retained. At 1.5 x $5.5M = $8.25M, rounded to $8.3M; at 1.8 x $5.5M = $9.90M, rounded to $9.9M enterprise value. No asking price was supplied, so no ask multiple can be calculated.

## 4. Recommendation
Proceed. The register has no filed gating finding. The Manageable lessor and deposit questions require records and price protection, not a new tier or a request-more-data verdict. Do not approve an asking price until one is actually supplied.

## 5. Conditions and next actions
1. Finance obtains the reconciled deposit ledger before signing; adjust the working-capital bridge for a verified shortfall.
2. Counsel obtains the lessor's written answer before closing; a restriction on planned storage calls for a revised operating plan.
3. Operations documents the utilization plan after close; do not price unearned recovery.

The supplied risk table reads:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Independent inventory count for the winter buying cycle has not been signed off | Must-Address | In-Progress |
| Two depot leases require the buyer to notify the landlords after closing | Manageable | Unresolved |
| Delivery vehicles are due for routine replacement | Informational | Resolved |

The narrative reports $24.0M last-twelve-month sales. It includes a single $2.0M liquidation of old packaging inventory that is not expected to repeat. Ordinary sales therefore stand at $22.0M. Earnings of $2.6M include no adjustments; growth in ordinary sales is 9%. A separate operations note says the inventory count is still being reconciled and its final valuation may change the earnings figure. The seller asks $80.0M enterprise value. Cash and debt have not been provided.

The peer list quotes Brookline Supply at 2.6x sales (8% growth, 24% gross margin), Meridian Route at 3.0x (12%, 27%) and Talon Wholesale at 3.3x (16%, 30%). It also lists Copper House at 5.1x, a completed sale of the whole business to a strategic purchaser rather than a trading quote. Redbank reports 10% growth in the headline narrative, 9% in ordinary sales and a 25% gross margin.

**Recommendation: request more data** -- the inventory count remains open; a $57.2M-$66.0M enterprise range on ordinary sales sits below the $80.0M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis

Redbank Supply serves repeat purchasers through an established distribution route. Ordinary sales of $22.0M and 9% growth support an acquisition case if the warehouse records support the margin. The primary risk is the unsigned independent inventory count: until the reconciled count and earnings bridge arrive, the committee cannot rely on the recorded stock value. The depot notices are an operating matter, not a substitute for that count.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Evidence and treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent inventory count | Must-Address | In-Progress | The operations note confirms reconciliation is unfinished. Open: final signed count and earnings bridge required before signing. |
| Depot lease notices | Manageable | Unresolved | Open but not a voting gate. Obtain landlord receipt of notice at close; counsel checks the notice terms. |
| Routine vehicle replacement | Informational | Resolved | No independent evidence contradicts the filed status; ask operations for its maintenance schedule during confirmatory diligence. |

One Must-Address finding remains open. The other two rows do not change its standing.

## 3. Valuation

Reported sales of $24.0M less the one-time $2.0M inventory liquidation give $22.0M of ordinary sales. The unfinished count prevents the $2.6M earnings number from setting a firm earnings-based price. Trading revenue peers are:

| Comparable | EV/Sales | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Brookline Supply | 2.6x | Included: similar growth and margin. |
| Meridian Route | 3.0x | Included: somewhat faster growth. |
| Talon Wholesale | 3.3x | Included: meaningfully faster growth and higher margin. |
| Copper House | 5.1x | Excluded from the trading band: completed strategic acquisition includes a control premium. |

Redbank's 9% ordinary growth and 25% margin place it near Brookline and below Meridian. A 2.6x-3.0x range is supportable: 2.6 x $22.0M = $57.2M; 3.0 x $22.0M = $66.0M enterprise value. The $80.0M ask implies 3.64x ordinary sales and exceeds the $66.0M ceiling by $14.0M. Cash and debt are unstated, so this is not an equity-value range.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. The unfinished independent inventory count is a Must-Address finding, so this vote does not authorize signing. Obtain its signed reconciliation and quantify any earnings or stock adjustment before returning to committee. A clean reconciliation may support proceeding, subject to a price no higher than $66.0M enterprise value on the current ordinary-sales base; an adverse reconciliation requires a new valuation. The depot notices can be completed without treating them as a second deal blocker.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. The seller's finance lead supplies the signed count and inventory-to-earnings bridge before signing. A material variance sends valuation back to committee; absence of the count prevents signing.
2. Buyer's counsel obtains landlord notice receipts for both depot leases at close. If a lease requires more than notice, bring that term back for review.
3. The operations lead confirms the vehicle replacement schedule in diligence; fund ordinary replacements in the operating plan rather than inventing a holdback.
4. The deal lead makes any further offer contingent on the evidence and on an enterprise price within the $57.2M-$66.0M range.

## A contrasting clean record

If the count had instead been filed Resolved and no other artifact disputed completion, that top-tier row would stand closed. The memo would still request the signed count as a diligence deliverable, but it would say **proceed** on the evidence now in hand; the outstanding depot notices would appear under conditions, not turn a clean file into an information-request vote. The valuation would use the same sales bridge and the price ceiling would still apply.

In a separate file with *only* Manageable and Informational rows, the open gating count is also zero. A repair backlog filed Manageable can need vendor quotes before close, but it cannot become a new Must-Address finding merely because the quotes are missing. The present vote is proceed with a condition addressing the backlog. The memo says explicitly that no filed blocking finding remains.

## Second decision: the all-lower-tier register

Ashgrove Costume Hire rents theatre wardrobes. Its entire filed risk table reads: returns not yet matched to deposits (`Manageable | Unresolved`); a lessor still reviewing a proposed storage change (`Manageable | In-Progress`); an old damage complaint with settlement reported (`Manageable | Resolved`); and reduced utilization (`Informational | Unresolved`). No other artifact contradicts the complaint's filed resolution. The legal team would like a written lessor response, and finance wants better deposit records. Neither wish refiles a row. Its $6.0M LTM rentals include a $0.5M one-time festival job: price $5.5M of ordinary rentals. Peers quoted at 1.5x and 1.8x give $8.25M-$9.90M EV; no asking price appears.

The finished decision opens: **Recommendation: proceed** -- no finding in the source table was filed as a deal breaker; $8.3M-$9.9M enterprise range, no ask supplied. The risk table reproduces all four filed tiers/statuses, notes the two unfinished Manageable matters, and says "No gating findings are open; this table contains no gating-tier row." Section 4 says proceed *now*, not after the lessor writes back. Section 5 gives counsel the lessor letter as a condition, finance the deposit reconciliation, and operations the utilization plan. An unsigned lower-tier item is work to do, not permission to relabel the vote.

## Boundary cases

If the packet instead establishes that the exclusive trading territory underpinning the seller's revenue ends on a fixed date and no successor can be secured, the answer is pass even when another finding still awaits information. The dated end is a decided fact; a letter about it cannot extend the right.

If a required network partner says it would withdraw only when an acquirer lacks a qualification, and the packet does not establish that this buyer lacks it or that no replacement exists, the row stays open and the answer is request more data. A conditional warning is not a final refusal.
