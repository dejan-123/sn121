# Deliverability audit: Fernhill Packing Co.

Fernhill Packing packs specialty foods for regional grocers. The fictional packet feeds the worked memo only; it is unrelated to any real transaction.

The register:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Fernhill's co-packing line operates under a safety certificate that was renewed this year; the renewal certificate is filed | Must-Address | Resolved |
| Cold-store lease assignment requires landlord consent; consent letter is unsigned and the landlord has not yet responded | Must-Address | Unresolved |
| One delivery vehicle needs transmission repair | Manageable | In-Progress |

The narrative: $9.4M LTM revenue including $1.4M from a discontinued seasonal contract; continuing revenue $8.0M. Growth 10%, gross margin 31%. No debt or cash supplied. Ask $13.0M enterprise value.

Comparables: Packwell Ltd. 1.4x EV/revenue (8% growth, 29% margin), Cratewise 1.7x (13%, 34%), SealFresh 1.1x (5%, 26%), and MetroPack at 2.8x - a completed buyout by its largest customer.

**Recommendation: request more data** -- the cold-store lease assignment lacks the landlord's signed consent; $8.8M-$11.2M enterprise value on continuing revenue against a $13.0M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis

Fernhill converts $8.0M of continuing co-packing revenue through a leased cold-store. The deliverability question is the lease: packing cannot continue at the site without the landlord's assignment consent, which is unsigned but not refused. The renewed certificate stands; the vehicle repair is routine.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Deliverability mark and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Safety certificate renewed | Must-Address | Resolved | Uncontested; obtain the filed certificate as a confirmatory deliverable at close. |
| Cold-store lease assignment | Must-Address | Unresolved | Instrument missing, obtainable: a signed landlord consent ends the question; a refusal ends the site's use and the deal. |
| Vehicle transmission repair | Manageable | In-Progress | Routine condition: repair quote and seller credit before close. |

1 open gating row: the lease consent.

## 3. Valuation

Reported $9.4M less $1.4M discontinued seasonal revenue gives an $8.0M deliverable base. Packwell (1.4x), Cratewise (1.7x) and SealFresh (1.1x) are retained; MetroPack (2.8x) is excluded as a completed control buyout. Fernhill's 10% growth and 31% margin sit at Packwell's level, supporting 1.1x-1.4x:

- 1.1x x $8.0M = $8.8M enterprise value
- 1.4x x $8.0M = $11.2M enterprise value

Range $8.8M-$11.2M. The $13.0M ask is 1.6x the base and $1.8M over the ceiling. No equity bridge is possible without debt and cash.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. The one open gate is a named instrument a non-refusing counterparty can sign; proceed is unavailable while the site itself lacks assignment consent, and pass is premature because the landlord has not said no. The deal returns priced no higher than $11.2M.

## 5. Conditions / next steps

1. Counsel obtains the landlord's signed assignment consent before signing; a written refusal ends the deal.
2. Operations files the renewed safety certificate as a closing deliverable.
3. Deal lead counters the ask within $8.8M-$11.2M before signing.
4. Maintenance gets the transmission repair quote and a seller credit before close.

## Same record, decided against

Suppose instead the register showed the cold-store landlord holding an *unqualified right to terminate at will*, covering the site's entire packing revenue, and a second top-tier row showing a competitor's earlier registration of the "Fernhill" mark with no ruling or consent securing it. Neither is an instrument the packet can produce: the income is revocable at the landlord's option and the priced brand is already claimed. Two decided-against gates make a compound break - **pass**, and the memo says the price discussion never opens.

Contrast a conditional statement - the landlord "would not consent *if* the buyer lacked food-handling accreditation." That is a question about this buyer, not a refusal: request the accreditation or a qualified substitute operator before treating the gate as decided.
