# Vote on the evidence that exists today

The memo answers one question: may the committee sign now, and at what price. Everything in the packet is evidence for or against that answer - nothing else enters the writing. Apply the six checks below, then produce the finished document. `references/willow-price-v7.md` shows one complete memo on invented facts plus two shortened endings; study the shape, never the numbers.

## 1. Which filed findings can block today

Gating means filed at a tier the source treats as blocking signature - Must-Address, Critical or equivalent as the register defines it. Tier labels mean what the source says, not what sounds severe; a packet whose top filed tier is Manageable has no gate at all - pending or In-Progress lower-tier items are conditions, never gates - and the call is proceed with conditions. Under-tiering may be remarked beside a row but is never silently re-ranked into the count.

## 2. Open means open - paper pending is paper missing

A blocking row is open when: filed Unresolved, In-Progress or blank; filed Resolved but the packet itself says the act is still undone; or filed Resolved while the resolving signature, consent or instrument is not evidenced anywhere in the packet. **An unsigned consent is an open consent.** There is no intermediate verdict for a signature still being gathered - an open gate that a defined document can close yields request more data, naming that document, its issuer and the outcomes that close or kill. Persistent exposure inside a closed row - a standing cancellation right, an earlier rival filing, a consent the license has always required - is residual risk priced or conditioned below, not a reopened gate unless the packet contradicts the closure itself.

Count and print: `N open gating rows`. N=0 = proceed. N>0 curable = request more data. N>0 incurable = pass.

## 3. The verdict ladder

- **proceed** when the tally is zero: every residual exposure is assigned a condition, protection or monitoring line and none of them headlines the vote.
- **request more data** while open gates still carry a named, obtainable cure - executed deed, signed consent, regulatory outcome, issued licence. The committee votes on today's file; "assume it arrives" is not a vote.
- **pass** when the record itself forecloses a material gate for this buyer - an unconditional documented refusal, an express bar on the priced asset, or terminal exclusivity with no successor evidenced.

Condition words control: `if`, `unless`, `provided`, `would`. An adverse statement that fires only when a qualification is missing poses a question - ask whether this buyer meets it or a qualified substitute exists; never presume failure. An ask above the defended range is repricing talk, not a gate.

## 4. Price the fragility, not the headline

Corrected base = reported less stated one-time items, departed revenue, gross pass-through volume and recurring "one-off" add-backs. Revenue sitting under a cancellation right or inside a dominant single customer stays in the base but is priced: state the share of the book it represents, and reflect it in multiple placement or an explicit protection - never silently struck out, never silently kept. Where earnings are negative or unsupported, value on revenue and say why.

Comps: each named peer is in or out by rule - metric-compatible trading rows only, control deals and NM rows out, reasons printed. Place the target on growth, margin, retention and scale. Then the range endpoints are shown as products, low first then high, each written `multiple x corrected base = enterprise value` so the arithmetic reproduces.

That pair is the range everywhere it appears. **Debt and cash in the packet make the equity bridge mandatory:** EV less net debt, arithmetic shown - the fairness of any ask is judged on equity. Restate the ask on the corrected base and report the gap; no supplied ask, none invented. Nothing is fabricated - no retention figures, deadlines, holdbacks or thresholds the packet does not give.

## 5. Every condition names its paper

Condition = finding + named instrument + issuer/owner + timing against signing + consequence on failure (walk-away, price reset or packet-sized protection). A regulatory outcome still pending, a consent still unsigned, IP not yet assigned, a licence not yet granted - each is converted into its specific deliverable: "obtain X", never "continue diligence".

## 6. Output is a decision document

Open with the verdict line: bold `Recommendation:` followed by exactly one of proceed, request more data or pass, then the deciding finding and the defended EV range - the equity range too when debt and cash are supplied. Nothing appears above that line. Five numbered sections follow, and only five:

1. `## 1. Deal thesis` covers the asset bought, the durable base and the lead risk that reappears in the table (under 150 words).
2. `## 2. Tiered risk table` lists every filed row in order - finding, tier, status, standing on the evidence, consequence - with the gate count printed beneath.
3. `## 3. Valuation` carries the base bridge, comp decisions, placement, both products, the equity bridge when supplied and the ask comparison (under 300 words plus table).
4. `## 4. Recommendation` restates the verdict from line one, states what is allowed now and what would change it, and keeps signing permission separate from price permission (under 140 words).
5. `## 5. Conditions and next actions` numbers each item: finding, owner, instrument, timing, consequence.

Finished professional memo, under 900 words; no placeholders, no invented facts, no deliberation on the page. Final check: gate count, both products, every status copied faithfully, lead risk present in the table, first line matching section 4.
