# Worked memo - invented example

Willow Quay Marine, a boatyard servicing commercial fleets. Register: one Critical row (hull-repair contract with the port authority, filed Resolved), one Manageable row (parts supplier concentration 38% of materials spend), two Informational rows. Narrative: reported revenue 11.2M including 1.8M from a terminated insurance-repair surge. Debt 4.0M, cash 0.9M supplied. Ask 38M. Peers: three service yards at 4.2-4.8x revenue; one shipbuilder at 7.0x (control purchase).

## The evidence check

Port-authority contract: filed Resolved, but the narrative records that the counterparty's signature page "remains in circulation" - the instrument is not evidenced. Under the open-means-open rule this row is **open today**, and the missing page is a named, obtainable cure.

All other rows sit below the blocking tier. Open gating rows: 1 - curable, so the committee defers signing pending the signature page.

**Recommendation: request more data** - the Critical contract's executed signature page remains outstanding; EV 38-44M, equity 35-41M.

## 1. Deal thesis

Willow Quay earns contracted repair revenue from commercial fleet operators at one estuary yard. The durable base is about 9.4M revenue once the ended surge work is stripped. The deciding exposure is the unevidenced port-authority contract - a defined cure away, but not cured today.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Standing | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Port-authority hull contract | Critical | Resolved | Open - signature page not evidenced | Required paper before signing |
| Parts supplier concentration 38% | Manageable | Open | Contracts run to term | Price placement, dual-source plan |
| Drydock permit renewal due next year | Informational | Open | Renewal window disclosed | 100-day plan item |
| Two minor injury claims | Informational | Resolved | Releases signed | No action |

Open gating rows: 1.

## 3. Valuation

Base: 11.2M reported less 1.8M ended surge = 9.4M sustainable revenue.

Comps: the three service yards (4.2x, 4.5x, 4.8x) retained - same trade, same size. The shipbuilder at 7.0x is dropped - a control acquisition of a different asset class.

Placement: stable contract mix but thin supplier resilience - 4.0x low, 4.7x high.

4.0 x 9.4M = 37.6M enterprise value
4.7 x 9.4M = 44.2M enterprise value

Equity bridge (debt 4.0M, cash 0.9M supplied): net debt 3.1M. Equity 34.5-41.1M. The 38M ask sits inside the defended equity range but above its midpoint; on concentration alone the committee should hold nearer the middle of the range.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. Today's record shows one open gate with a defined cure - the executed signature page from the port authority. Price permission is already set: up to roughly 41M equity. If the signature does not arrive, or the authority's terms change, the deal is repriced against the reduced contract base or declined.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Port-authority contract - deal counsel obtains the executed signature page before signing; failure re-opens the whole vote.
2. Supplier concentration - procurement lead drafts a dual-source plan for top materials spend within the 100-day plan.
3. Drydock permit - operations lead diarizes the renewal filing ahead of the disclosed window.
4. Price - negotiating range held at 34.5-41.1M equity given supplier dependence.

---

## Shortened ending - the page is signed

With the executed signature page in the packet: open gates fall to zero, verdict becomes `proceed`, conditions 2-4 stand unchanged.

## Shortened ending - refusal recorded

With the authority's written unconditional termination in the record and no alternative contract of comparable value: the gate is incurable and the verdict becomes `pass`.
