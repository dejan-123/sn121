# Worked buyer price: Willow Fixture Co.

Willow Fixture Co. builds shelving and display fixtures for regional retailers. This invented example demonstrates how the permission to buy and the price to pay are separate questions.

## Another full memo: a zero-gate price file

Morrow Tile Merchants' register has only Manageable and Informational rows. An $11.0M ask sits inside the supportable range; incomplete stock work has not been filed as a vote blocker.

**Recommendation: proceed** -- no blocking-tier row was filed; $9.6M-$12.0M enterprise value against an $11.0M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis
Morrow distributes tiles to repeat retail accounts. Ordinary sales of $8.0M, after removing a one-time clearance event, anchor the acquisition case. The primary risk is surplus stock not yet counted, a Manageable finding that may affect working capital. Price protection and a stock count answer that risk without turning it into a fictional Must-Address item.

## 2. Tiered risk table
| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Current treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surplus stock count | Manageable | In-Progress | Count outstanding; buyer and seller reconcile the stock value at close. |
| Delivery claims | Manageable | Unresolved | Request claim-level records; structure a specific protection for verified losses. |
| Slow collections | Informational | Unresolved | Monitor receivables in the working-capital schedule. |

No blocking-tier row appears in this file. Open Manageable matters do not become new voting gates.

## 3. Valuation
The $9.0M reported sales include a $1.0M clearance event, leaving $8.0M in ordinary sales. The peer file quotes two retained trading distributors: Aster Quay at 1.2x, a cautious lower end, and Ridgeland Trading at 1.5x, a stronger operator. Neither is a control transaction; no other peer is provided to exclude. The products are 1.2 x $8.0M = $9.6M and 1.5 x $8.0M = $12.0M enterprise value. The $11.0M ask implies 1.375x ordinary sales and sits inside the supported range. Stock is not subtracted without a count.

## 4. Recommendation
Proceed, with stock and delivery protections. Neither unresolved finding was filed as Must-Address, so a desire for documents cannot justify voting to defer. The price passes the corrected-base test at $11.0M, conditional on the usual verified working-capital adjustment.

## 5. Conditions and next actions
1. Finance obtains a physical stock count before closing; reduce the working-capital settlement for documented unusable inventory.
2. Counsel obtains the delivery-claims schedule before signing and negotiates an indemnity for measured exposure.
3. Finance reviews the aged receivables after close and pursues the documented collection plan.

The financial narrative gives $30.0M LTM revenue, $3.0M EBITDA, growth of 8%, and gross margin of 27%. Revenue includes $2.5M from a one-off store-refit project. The materials do not state an asking price. Debt of $5.0M and cash of $1.0M are reported. The independent stock count is scheduled, not delivered; the potential discrepancy has not been quantified. Warranty returns are still within a bounded, but as yet unpriced, service program.

The four comparables are Kerrow Displays at 1.6x EV/revenue (7% growth, 26% margin); Brighton Fixtures at 1.9x (10%, 28%); Northbay Fittings at 2.2x (14%, 32%); and Steelbridge Group at 3.4x, a whole-company strategic acquisition. Their earnings multiples are not all provided, so use EV/revenue.

**Recommendation: request more data** -- the independent raw-material count is still outstanding; the supported enterprise range is $44.0M-$52.3M and the packet provides no asking price.

## 1. Deal thesis

Willow has a repeat-order fixtures business, but reported sales include a $2.5M project unlikely to recur. Its ordinary $27.5M revenue and 8% growth are an intelligible acquisition base. The primary risk is the outstanding independent count of raw steel inventory: its result could change the asset value used to support any offer. The unresolved product-warranty matter deserves a bounded protection, not a new voting gate.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Evidence and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent raw-material count | Must-Address | Unresolved | The narrative calls it scheduled, not delivered. Open; obtain signed count and reconcile any material variance. |
| Bracket warranty returns | Manageable | In-Progress | Open operating exposure; obtain service-cost detail and a specific warranty indemnity if supported. |
| Freight insurance renewal | Informational | Resolved | No supplied document disputes the filed status; request policy evidence in ordinary closing diligence. |

One Must-Address row remains open. The lower-tier issues require terms but do not create another gate.

## 3. Valuation

Reported LTM revenue of $30.0M less $2.5M of one-off refit work leaves $27.5M ordinary revenue. The stock variance is not yet known, so no number for it is subtracted. Although EBITDA of $3.0M is reported, the peer file does not give a consistent earnings multiple; EV/revenue sets the range.

| Comparable | EV/Revenue | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Kerrow Displays | 1.6x | Included, similar growth and gross margin. |
| Brighton Fixtures | 1.9x | Included, somewhat stronger than Willow on both measures. |
| Northbay Fittings | 2.2x | Included, faster-growing and higher-margin; informs the upper context, not the selected high endpoint. |
| Steelbridge Group | 3.4x | Excluded from trading range: strategic purchase of control. |

An 8% grower at 27% margin belongs near the lower two trading peers. The selected 1.6x-1.9x range gives 1.6 x $27.5M = $44.0M and 1.9 x $27.5M = $52.25M, rounded to $52.3M enterprise value. Debt of $5.0M less $1.0M cash implies $4.0M net debt, giving $40.0M-$48.3M equity value. No asking price was supplied, so there is no seller multiple or ask-versus-range comparison.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data, specifically the independent stock count and the reconciliation of any variance. The count remains an open Must-Address row; the range is an enterprise-value view, not approval to sign or an invented seller ask. A clean signed count permits a return to committee to consider proceeding at a price consistent with $44.0M-$52.3M enterprise value; a material shortfall requires a fresh price and possibly different terms. The warranty matter can be carried into a focused protection.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Seller's finance lead delivers the signed stock count and reconciliation before signing; without it, signing remains unauthorized and an adverse variance returns the price to committee.
2. Buyer's operations lead obtains bracket return and repair-cost records before signing; document a specific indemnity or correctly sized holdback if an exposure is verified.
3. Buyer's insurance lead verifies the renewed freight policy before close; if coverage does not exist, require replacement coverage rather than asserting the filed status was false without evidence.
4. Deal lead obtains the seller's actual asking price before any offer is recommended; compare it with the corrected enterprise range and the separately shown equity bridge.

## Alternate result, same price test

If the independent count had already been signed and the narrative contained no later contradiction, the committee could **proceed**, subject to verifying that document and resolving the manageable warranty matter. The $44.0M-$52.3M enterprise range would not change merely because a risk was reclassified as closed. Conversely, a proved, nonreplaceable loss of an essential operating right would be a reason to **pass**; a stock count still pending is not such a loss.

For a separate case with only two filed rows, Manageable returns-processing work and Informational equipment scheduling, neither creates a blocking tier. Even if both remain unfinished, the permission answer is **proceed** with specific completion terms. There is no open Must-Address row to refer. Price the actual returned goods from documented loss, not by converting an unfinished procedure into a fictional gating finding.

Consider a second price file for Morrow Tile Merchants. Three rows were filed, none at the blocking tier: uncounted surplus stock (`Manageable | In-Progress`), unresolved delivery claims (`Manageable | Unresolved`), and a slow collection cycle (`Informational | Unresolved`). No other document gives any of them a new filed tier. Sales are $9.0M, including a $1.0M clearance sale; the ordinary base is $8.0M. Two trading peers at 1.2x and 1.5x support $9.6M-$12.0M EV; an $11.0M ask is inside. The IC memo begins "**Recommendation: proceed** -- no top-tier item was filed; $9.6M-$12.0M enterprise range against the $11.0M ask." Section 2 says "Zero open blocking rows; the uncounted stock remains Manageable." Section 4 repeats proceed. Section 5 calls for a physical count, verified delivery-loss records and a collections plan. The count's uncertainty calls for a price protection inside proceed, not a fabricated Must-Address row or a change of verdict.

## Open but not ended

A required logistics partner may warn that it would leave *if* the incoming owner lacks a required qualification. If the packet never establishes the buyer's qualification and offers no evidence that another qualified partner is unavailable, that is an open question. The permission answer is request more data: obtain the partner's buyer-specific position and the replacement market. It is not a pass based on a condition the record has not tested.

An operating approval that can still be sought is likewise an open gate, not a fixed prohibition. A rule that already excludes this buyer class is different: it is a decided barrier, and the memo prices only what the buyer can own before deciding whether the remaining thesis survives.
