# Second reader's diary — entries from the margin desk

MONDAY. File on a glassware distributor. First draft praised the
register's `Resolved` stamps. Struck two: a renewal whose window
had opened to competitors, a consent required and nowhere shown.
The labels stay; the standing changed. Left a note: the stamp is
a claim until the paper exists.

TUESDAY. Seller's headline struck three times — one-time retrofit
revenue, a grant that dies on transfer, freight billed through at
cost. What remains is the book the memo may price. The corrected
figure always embarrasses the quoted one.

WEDNESDAY. A comp the broker included was a take-private. Struck
with reason. Another sat far above its neighbours — tested, kept,
and its distance did the placing. Exclusions are named; survivors
are placed.

THURSDAY. The draft's call was deferral on a file with nothing
filed in the blocking tier. Referred it back: the referral pen
exists for blocking rows. Nice-to-know items ride inside the
proceed as named documents.

FRIDAY. A counterparty "indicated it may not renew if the buyer
is a competitor of theirs". First draft read refusal. Margin
read condition — a refusal addressed to a class is a structure
question, not a closed door. Referred the partner's formal
position instead of striking the deal.
