# Examination cases — invented deals, form only

One full examination, then the three remaining call shapes. These
facts are invented; copy the form only.

## Case A — a full memo (request more data)

# IC memo: Solmarch Foods Automation
**Recommendation: request more data** — row 2 (plant fire-safety
remediation, Must-Address, In-Progress) and row 4 (key-account
contract assignment, Must-Address, Unresolved) stayed unanswered;
$28M ask sits inside the $24.9–$31.7M range.
**Open questions**: two unanswered gating rows; row 1's filed
Resolved contradicted by the narrative (recall costs still accruing);
no ask-side debt schedule.

## 1. Deal thesis (≤120 words)
Solmarch builds packaging-line automation for regional food
producers: $19.7M LTM revenue, 62% recurring service, 38% gross
margin, EBITDA $1.8M. The thesis prices the installed service base;
two open gating items stand between the thesis and signing — the
half-complete plant remediation and the unconsented assignment of
the key account (27% of revenue). Primary risk: row 2's open
remediation.

## 2. Tiered risk table
| # | Finding | Tier | Status | State | Deciding fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product recall (last year) fully reserved | Must-Address | Resolved | unanswered | Narrative: recall costs still accruing |
| 2 | Plant fire-safety remediation | Must-Address | In-Progress | unanswered | Contractor engaged; completion certificate absent |
| 3 | Founder retains 20% of sales relationships | Manageable | Unresolved | open | Transition plan drafted, unsigned |
| 4 | Key-account assignment consent | Must-Address | Unresolved | unanswered | 27% of revenue; consent requested, not received |
| 5 | Spare-parts import lead times | Informational | Unresolved | noted | 9-week lead, industry-normal |

Unanswered gating questions: 3. Flag: row 1's Resolved does not hold
against the still-accruing recall costs — it answers open.

## 3. Defended valuation (≤260 words + comp table)
Base $19.7M LTM revenue; the key account is at risk, not lost — it
stays in the base and names the downside. EBITDA positive but thin;
the comp set prices EV/Revenue, which leads.

| Comparable | EV/Rev | Growth | In/Out | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PackLine Systems | 1.8x | 14% | In | Trading peer |
| FoodTech Automation | 1.5x | 9% | In | Trading peer |
| ServeRight (acquired) | 2.9x | 12% | Out | Control premium paid by acquirer |
| ConveyorWorks | 2.1x | 18% | In | Trading peer |

Included median 1.8x. Solmarch grows in line (11% vs 13% median) on
thinner margin (38% vs 44%) — net a step below: 1.4x–1.9x.
1.4x × $19.7M = $27.6M; 1.9x × $19.7M = $37.4M. Range: $24.9–$31.7M
net of the $3.2M remediation reserve the narrative quantifies.
The $28M ask is 1.4x the base — inside the range.

## 4. Recommendation and consistency check (≤120 words)
**Request more data.** Rows 1, 2 and 4 stay unanswered and each is
document-curable: the recall close-out confirmation, the completion
certificate, the executed consent. The ask prices inside the
defended range — price is not the question. Checks: primary risk =
row 2, first gating row by severity; range rebuilt above; every
figure identical across sections.

## 5. Conditions and asks
1. Row 2: fire-safety completion certificate before signing —
   failing it, re-price net of remediation cost or walk.
2. Row 4: executed assignment consent from the key account before
   signing; without it, re-price excluding that revenue.
3. Row 1: auditor's final recall-reserve letter before close; any
   shortfall posts to an escrow.
4. Row 3: signed founder transition plan by close — retention
   consideration against it.
5. Row 5: monitor — parts lead times, post-close.

## Case B — the pass answer (a date nobody moves)

Rows: Must-Address `Unresolved` — the target's core product runs on
a patent-crosslicence that terminates by its own clause on a fixed
date 9 months out, and the licensor's filed position is no renewal
to acquirers; a Manageable attrition row beside it. The licence
fails all three tests: unanswered; it is what the base prices; no
document this deal can produce extends a fixed term against a filed
refusal. §4 answers:

> **Pass.** Row 1's licence ends on a date no party can move, on a
> filed refusal to re-paper — the clearing record cannot exist on
> this deal's timeline. No escrow, indemnity or price cures it. The
> file reopens only if the licensor's position or the term changes.
> Checks: …

The attrition row is the near miss — alarming, curable: a retention
condition and a price step, never the decline.

## Case C — the proceed answer (no gating questions on the docket)

Rows: Manageable `Unresolved` (manual billing backlog), Manageable
`In-Progress` (platform re-certification), Informational
`Unresolved` (NRR 97%). Not one gating question on the docket. §4
answers:

> **Proceed, with conditions.** No finding here is a deal-breaker:
> the docket holds no gating-tier row, and every open question is
> curable or priced. The billing backlog takes a named systems plan
> (ask 1); the re-certification becomes a pre-close deliverable
> (ask 2); NRR prices inside the range. Ask $9.8M sits inside the
> $9.1–$11.6M range. Checks: unanswered gating questions: none; …

Reporting the absence of deal-breakers is required — a committee
reading only manageable rows must be told none blocks.

## Case D — proceed carrying a ceiling

Rows: every gating question answered on file documents; ask $88M
against a $69.3–$78.4M range. §4 answers:

> **Proceed, at a price inside $69.3–$78.4M.** The examination
> closes clean — every gating question answered on record. The $88M
> ask exceeds the defended range by $9.6M; the deal clears only at
> or under $78.4M. Checks: …

Above range is a price condition, never the decline.

## Case E — the conditional refusal (near miss, not pass)

Rows: Must-Address `Unresolved` — the platform's clearing partner
"has indicated it may not continue if the buyer is not a member
firm"; plus routine open consents. §4 answers:

> **Request more data.** Row 1's partner stated a position, not a
> refusal: the exit hangs on the buyer's membership status — a fact
> the record never answers — and the clearing seat is fillable by
> any member firm, not this one alone. The asks: the buyer's status,
> and a successor partner's commitment letter. The remaining
> consents are ordinary documents. Nothing answers incurable; the
> file declines only if no member firm will take the seat. Checks: …

The trap: 'indicated it may not continue' reads like a refusal but
answers nothing — the condition's trigger is an unasked fact.

## Case F — the statutory clock (this one IS the pass)

Rows: Must-Address `In-Progress` — the platform's engine sits under
a patent expiring by statute in 16 months; competitors' continuation
filings may control the successor landscape. Beside it:
Must-Address `Unresolved` — a lapsed data-handling certification,
curable by re-filing. §4 answers:

> **Pass.** Row 1's right ends on a date no party can move — no
> filing, consent or indemnity extends a statutory term, and the
> successor landscape is already contested. The thesis prices the
> protection that right affords; it is gone whether or not the deal
> signs. Time is not a cure: an expiry months out is as incurable
> as one tomorrow. Row 2 is curable — but a deal does not proceed
> to fix the fixable while the premise lapses. Checks: …

Contrast with Case E: there the seat could be refilled — a
provider exists. Here the thing itself expires — nothing replaces
a lapsed right.

## The re-examination table and the instruments on record

Seller or narrative claim → the examination's answer:

| They claim | The examination answers |
|---|---|
| "gross revenue $14.7M" | "net revenue $8.9M — the figure the comps price" |
| "adjusted EBITDA +$3.1M" | "EBITDA -$1.2M as stated; non-plain add-backs rejected" |
| "94% recurring" | a composition claim, not a restatement ground |
| "pipeline growing 40%" | unanswered — growth priced is growth delivered |
| "ask $88M" | "ask $88M = implied multiple on the answered base" |

Instruments the record supports: condition precedent, closing
deliverable, indemnity, escrow or holdback sized to a quantified
exposure, contingent consideration, retention or non-compete, price
reduction to a defended ceiling, long-stop date only where the
artifacts name a date.
