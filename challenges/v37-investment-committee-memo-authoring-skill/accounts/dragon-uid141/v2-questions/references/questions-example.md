# Worked example — the examination

A full run on a fictional deal. The shape transfers: every element
takes the stand as a question, answers from the record, and the
memo reports what stayed unanswered. Entities and figures invented;
the examination pattern is the deliverable.

## The input (abridged for illustration)

Risk table filed:

| # | Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tower-crane operator certifications lapsed for 3 of 14 operators; state recertification exams scheduled, results not yet issued | Must-Address | In-Progress |
| 2 | Anchor general contractor = 45% of revenue under a master services agreement with a change-of-control consent clause; consent never requested | Must-Address | Resolved |
| 3 | OSHA recordable incident rate runs 1.8x industry average over trailing 24 months | Manageable | Unresolved |
| 4 | Fleet average age 11 years; $3.1M replacement capex unbudgeted | Manageable | Unresolved |
| 5 | Yard lease runs 12 more years | Informational | Resolved |

Narrative: LTM revenue $16.8M including a completed $0.9M storm
mobilization; adjusted base $15.9M; EBITDA $2.1M; organic growth
12%. Ask $19M. Comps: LiftPro 7.8x, SiteHaul 5.9x, CraneCorp 6.7x
EV/EBITDA, ApexHeavy 10.4x (banker note: "acquired by a PE platform
at a consolidation premium").

## The memo

**request more data** — the consent question and the certification
question stand unanswered; ask $19M as stated.

## 1. Question before committee

Acquire Hollis Crane & Rigging for $19M — a route-density heavy-lift
business whose $15.9M adjusted base (the completed $0.9M storm
mobilization stripped) supports $2.1M EBITDA at 12% organic growth.
The deciding question is whether 45% of revenue survives the close:
the anchor contractor's master agreement consents to nothing on
record — filed Resolved, but no consent was ever requested. Three
of fourteen operators also await state recertification results.
Both questions have named records that answer them.

## 2. Sharpened findings

| # | Finding | Filed | The question | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3/14 operator certifications lapsed; exams scheduled | In-Progress | When do recertifications issue? | **blocks** — filed In-Progress and genuinely so; the state exam board holds the dates, the results letter answers it |
| 2 | Anchor GC 45% of revenue; CoC consent clause; filed Resolved | Resolved | Does the revenue transfer? | **blocks** — filed Resolved but the consent the clause requires was never requested; an unsigned clause is not a resolved one |
| 3 | Incident rate 1.8x industry | Unresolved | What does the safety record cost? | **open** — quantified safety program and insurance review carries it |
| 4 | $3.1M fleet capex unbudgeted | Unresolved | What keeps the fleet running? | **open** — capex nets against the range, the funding plan carries in asks |
| 5 | Yard lease, 12 years | Resolved | — | **moot** — outside the decision |

**Still open:** 2 blocks / 2 open / 0 settled / 1 moot

## 3. Valuation

Base answers: $15.9M recurs after the completed storm mobilization
strips out; EBITDA $2.1M stands, so EV/EBITDA answers this one.
Growth prices at the realized 12%, not the 17% projected off two
unsigned municipal bids.

Comp answers: ApexHeavy is excluded — the banker note calls 10.4x a
platform's consolidation premium, and a buyer's price is not a
trading multiple. Included: SiteHaul 5.9x, CraneCorp 6.7x, LiftPro
7.8x — a 5.9x–7.8x band, 6.7x median. Hollis's fleet age argues
mid-to-low band; the unbudgeted capex says the same.

Arithmetic: 5.9x × $2.1M = $12.4M; 7.8x × $2.1M = $16.4M. The range
answers $12.4M–$16.4M, less the unbudgeted $3.1M capex the committee
nets in its own math — call the honest band $12M–$15M.

Ask answer: $19M implies 9.0x the posted EBITDA — above the band's
top. The gap answers as a price condition, not a pass.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. Two gating questions stand unanswered, and each
has a named record that answers it: the anchor contractor's executed
consent (a signature nobody has asked for yet — askable, not
outside-held) and the state board's recertification results. Proceed
cannot answer while a gating question is open; pass does not fit —
one question sits with a counterparty who has refused nothing, the
other with an agency that decides on its own calendar inside the
deal's timeline. The $19M ask at 9.0x answers repriced as the
negotiation the request carries.

## 5. Asks

1. Anchor GC's written consent to assignment before signing —
   closing deliverable; the clause is either consented or it blocks.
2. State recertification results for the three lapsed operators
   before signing — closing deliverable; scheduled exams are not
   credentials.
3. Written safety-improvement plan with insurer sign-off before
   close — indemnity posture priced at binding.
4. Fleet replacement schedule with funding source — $3.1M netted
   or escrowed in the price mechanism.
5. [to confirm: any crane-related liability claims in the trailing
   36 months — the artifacts are silent.]

The read — a real route business priced $3M–$7M above its band on
one unsigned consent and three unissued certificates. The
examination asks for both records before the committee prices
anything further.

## When nothing blocks — the answered shape

Same file with the consent signed and the certifications issued:

**proceed** — no blocking question; the ask answers inside the band
at 7.4x posted EBITDA.

## 5. Asks

None gating. Standing items: the safety plan's first insurer review,
the capex schedule filed for the first post-close quarter.

The questions run identically either way — an answered set changes
the answer, never the examination.

## When the record already ruled — the pass shape

If the anchor state's crane-operator licence were non-transferable
by statute — a rule on the books that bars the transfer itself —
the question is not unanswered, it is already answered against the
deal. The base restates net of the state-cohort revenue, the range
recomputes on what survives, and where the ask still prices the
dead revenue the answer is pass: name the statute, show the
arithmetic, name the amendment that would reopen the question.
