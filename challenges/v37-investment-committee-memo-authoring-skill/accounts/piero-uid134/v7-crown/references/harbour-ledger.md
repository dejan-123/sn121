# Worked memo - invented example

Harbour Fen Logistics, a regional cold-chain operator. The register tiers its findings Manageable and Informational only - no blocking tier exists. Reported revenue 18.0M includes 2.4M from a one-season harvest contract that ended. Asking price 52M. Comps quoted: three regional carriers at 3.0-3.6x revenue; a national platform bought for control at 6.1x; one distressed yard at 1.4x.

**Recommendation: proceed** - no gating rows filed; EV range 40-45M supports negotiation below the 52M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis

Harbour Fen sells contracted refrigerated haulage to grocery chains along two coastal routes. The durable base is recurring contract revenue of roughly 15.6M after removing the ended harvest work. The primary exposure is customer concentration: the largest shipper supplies about a third of contract revenue, which is a priced risk, not a barrier to signing.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Standing | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anchor shipper ~34% of revenue | Manageable | Open | Contracted through the term; renewal exposure remains | Retention covenant and price placement |
| Two drivers named in wage claim | Manageable | Resolved | Settlement deed referenced and executed | Monitor - no action |
| Fleet age averages nine years | Informational | Open | Capex schedule disclosed | 100-day plan item |
| Depot lease expires in 20 months | Informational | Open | Renewal option held by tenant | Confirm option exercised before close |

Open gating rows: 0 - the register contains no blocking tier, so nothing here can hold the vote.

## 3. Valuation

Corrected base: 18.0M reported less 2.4M ended harvest revenue = 15.6M sustainable revenue.

Comp decisions: the three regional carriers (3.0x, 3.2x, 3.6x revenue) are retained - same service model and size. The national platform at 6.1x is excluded as a control acquisition with platform synergies. The distressed yard at 1.4x is excluded - forced-sale pricing does not reflect a going concern.

Placement: Harbour Fen grows slower than the retained peers and carries higher concentration, so it prices below their midpoint. Chosen multiples 2.6x low, 2.9x high.

2.6 x 15.6M = 40.6M enterprise value
2.9 x 15.6M = 45.2M enterprise value

EV range: 40.6-45.2M. No debt or cash figures were supplied, so an equity bridge cannot be computed - the packet should be asked for both before the committee discusses headline price. The 52M ask implies 3.3x the corrected base, above the defended range by roughly 7-11M; the gap is a negotiation point, not a defect in the business.

## 4. Recommendation

Proceed. The committee may authorize signing a letter of intent at or below 45M enterprise value, subject to the conditions in section 5. Nothing in the register blocks the deal; the price ceiling matters because the ask sits above the defended range. Evidence that would change the call: the anchor shipper giving notice, or wage-claim exposure exceeding the settlement already executed.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Anchor shipper concentration - commercial lead obtains the shipper's renewal-intent letter before signing; failure moves the deal to a price reset or pass.
2. Depot lease - property counsel confirms the renewal option is exercised at close.
3. Balance sheet - finance lead requests year-end debt and cash figures so the equity bridge can be computed before the final price is set.
4. Fleet age - operations lead adds the replacement schedule to the 100-day plan.

## Variant ending A - an open blocking row with a cure

Same packet, but the wage claim is filed Critical and the settlement deed is referenced yet not executed. The closing event is defined and checkable: signed deed before signing. Verdict: `**Recommendation: request more data** - executed settlement deed outstanding for the Critical wage claim; EV 40.6-45.2M`. Section 5 leads with that deed, its issuer, and the consequence if the plaintiffs do not sign.

## Variant ending B - the packet already decided against

Same packet, but the anchor shipper's termination letter is in the record - unconditional, effective in 60 days, covering a third of contract revenue with no substitute customer identified. No buyer act restores that revenue. Verdict: `**Recommendation: pass** - anchor shipper departure unconditional and recorded; the priced base no longer exists`.
