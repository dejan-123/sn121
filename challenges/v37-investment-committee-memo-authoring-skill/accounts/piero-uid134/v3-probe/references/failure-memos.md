# Audit files — invented deals, form only

Three invented deals. Copy the shape; none of the facts are yours.

## File A — a full memo (request more data)

# Committee memo — Halvern Coldchain
**Recommendation: request more data** — row 1 (customer consent,
Must-Address, Unresolved) and row 3 (water-table permit, Must-Address,
In-Progress) gate the file; $34M ask sits below the $41.2–$48.9M range.
**Record exceptions**: two open gating rows; row 2 filed Resolved while the
narrative says the renewal is unsigned; ask given; no debt figures.

## 1. Deal thesis (≤120 words)
Halvern sells refrigerated-route scheduling to regional grocers:
$12.6M LTM revenue, 88% contracted, 64% gross margin, EBITDA $1.4M.
The economics support the ask, but the largest customer (31% of ARR)
has not consented to assignment and the coastal depot's discharge
permit is mid-renewal. Primary risk: row 1's unsigned consent.

## 2. Risk findings
| # | Finding | Tier | Status | Read | Deciding fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anchor customer consent to assignment | Must-Address | Unresolved | gates | No consent letter in file; customer is 31% of ARR |
| 2 | Fleet telematics contract renewal | Must-Address | Resolved | open | Narrative says renewal still unsigned |
| 3 | Depot discharge permit renewal | Must-Address | In-Progress | gates | Regulator decision pending, application filed |
| 4 | Driver overtime classification suit | Manageable | Unresolved | open | Quantified exposure $0.9M; escrow candidate |
| 5 | Two engineers hold key route IP knowledge | Informational | Unresolved | noted | Retention plan, post-close |

Open gating rows: 2. Flag: row 2's status contradicts
the narrative — treated open until the executed renewal arrives.

## 3. Defended valuation (≤260 words + comp table)
Base $12.6M LTM revenue; the anchor contract is at risk, not lost —
it stays in the base and names the downside. EV/EBITDA is usable
($1.4M positive); the comp set prices EV/Revenue, so revenue leads.

| Comparable | EV/Rev | EV/EBITDA | In/Out | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ColdRoute Systems | 3.2x | 19x | In | Trading peer, 21% growth |
| Frostline Logistics | 2.7x | 16x | In | Trading peer, 9% growth |
| ArcticFlow (acquired) | 4.9x | NM | Out | Control premium paid at close |
| ChillMetric | 3.6x | 24x | In | Trading peer, 26% growth |

Included median 3.2x. Halvern grows faster (24% vs 19% median) on a
thinner margin (64% vs 67%) — net roughly at the median: 3.0x–3.9x.
3.0x × $12.6M = $37.8M; 3.9x × $12.6M = $49.1M. Range: $37.8–$49.1M.
The $34M ask is 2.7x the base — below the range, $3.8M under the low end.

## 4. The call, audited (≤120 words)
**Request more data.** Rows 1 and 3 are open gating findings both
answerable by documents: the executed consent and the renewed permit.
Row 2's filed Resolved does not survive the narrative — its renewal
is unsigned. The ask prices below the defended range; price is not
the problem. Verified: primary risk = row 1, first gating row; range
$37.8–$49.1M rebuilt above; every figure identical across sections.

## 5. Conditions and next steps
1. Row 1: executed assignment consent from the anchor customer before
   signing — without it, walk or re-price to exclude that ARR.
2. Row 3: renewed discharge permit before close — failing it,
   re-price for depot relocation cost [to confirm].
3. Row 2: executed telematics renewal before signing; if terms
   change, restate the base.
4. Row 4: $0.9M escrow against the classification suit, released at
   dismissal or settlement.
5. Row 5: retention agreements for both engineers by close.

## File B — the pass shape (a clock nobody can move)

Row set: Must-Address `Unresolved` — the target's sole import
licence for its core component expires by statute in 5 months and
renewal requires domestic ownership the buyer cannot offer; plus a
Manageable customer-concentration row. The licence fails all three
tests: unresolved, it removes what the base prices (the component
the product is built on), and no document before signing cures a
statutory expiry with an eligibility bar. §4 reads:

> **Pass.** Row 1's licence lapses on a date no party can move, and
> the eligibility rule bars re-issue to this buyer — the clearing
> record cannot exist on this deal's timeline. No condition, escrow
> or price repairs it. The file reopens only if the statute or the
> buyer's eligibility changes. Verified: …

Note the near miss beside it: the concentration row is alarming
but curable — it takes a price step and a retention condition, never
the decline.

## File E — the conditional refusal (near miss, not pass)

Rows: Must-Address `Unresolved` — the platform's card-processing
sponsor "has indicated it may not continue if the buyer is not
FSC-licensed"; plus a second Must-Address `Unresolved` on ordinary
consent paperwork. §4 reads:

> **Request more data.** Row 1's sponsor has stated a position, not
> a refusal: the exit hangs on a condition — the buyer's licence
> status — the artifacts never settle, and a sponsorship seat is
> fillable by any qualifying provider, not only this one. The asks:
> the buyer's regulatory status, and a successor sponsor's term
> sheet. Row 2's consents are routine documents. Neither row proves
> incurable; the file declines only if no qualified provider will
> take the seat. Verified: …

The trap: 'indicated it may not continue' reads like a refusal but
answers nothing — the condition's trigger is an unasked fact.

## The translation table and the terms the memo may use

Seller or narrative framing → the memo's restatement:

| They say | The memo writes |
|---|---|
| "LTM revenue $14.7M" (gross volume) | "net revenue $8.9M — the figure the comps price" |
| "adjusted EBITDA +$3.1M" | "EBITDA -$1.2M as reported; $1.9M of add-backs not plainly one-time are rejected" |
| "94% recurring" | a composition claim, not a restatement ground |
| "pipeline growing 40%" | unrealized — growth priced is growth delivered |
| "ask $95M" | "ask $95M = 5.2x the restated base, above the defended range" |

Terms the memo may use without inventing facts: condition precedent,
closing deliverable, indemnity, escrow or holdback sized to a
quantified exposure, contingent consideration, retention or
non-compete, price reduction to a defended ceiling, long-stop date
only where the artifacts name a date.
