# Gatecheck — six checks, then the memo

The artifacts disagree with each other; the memo's job is to say
where, and which side the committee can rely on. Each check below is
run in order. No check rewrites the input: a finding keeps the tier
and the status it was filed with, and any disagreement is written
next to the filed word — flagged, quoted, never repaired or erased.

Two invariants before the checks:

- Nothing enters the memo that the artifacts do not state or that is
  not worked from them in the open. An input the artifacts do not
  supply is named as a gap — `[to confirm: …]` — never invented,
  never assumed in the seller's favour.
- An appended analyst note flagging a table-versus-narrative
  conflict is carried and shown, never smoothed: the memo records
  which side was trusted and on which fact.

## The six checks at a glance

| # | Check | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Filing audit | Does the filed status survive the record? |
| 2 | Base restatement | What revenue actually prices? |
| 3 | Comp screen | Which comparables count, and why? |
| 4 | Range + ask | What is the justified band, and where does the ask sit? |
| 5 | The call | proceed, request more data, or pass? |
| 6 | Conditions | What closes each open item, held by whom, when? |

## Check 1 — the filing audit

A filed status is a claim to be tested.

| Filed as | The record shows | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| `Resolved` | another artifact or the row's own text undoes it — an appeal undecided, an audit deferred not closed, a signature the file never shows, a dispute the row itself calls live | open — the cell names the fact that broke the label |
| `Resolved` | an exposure that persists by nature — a consent the licence always needs, a cancellation right the customer always holds | stands in the analyst's sense — the proving document becomes a closing deliverable |
| blank / missing | — | open, and the memo says the cell was empty |
| anything verified or uncontradicted | — | stands — a serious risk honestly filed needs no `Resolved` |

## Check 2 — the base restated

The base is fixed before any multiple is quoted; the seller's base
is a claim.

- Strip revenue that completed and will not recur.
- Exclude revenue that does not transfer — subsidies the buyer
  cannot keep, agreements that lapse on transfer, accounts already
  lost.
- Where the industry's metric is net, price the net figure and say
  why gross is not the base.
- Restate growth on the restated base, not the headline.
- EBITDA negative on the economics that transfer, or comps
  pre-profit → name EV/EBITDA not meaningful, carry EV/Revenue.
- Add-backs hold only where genuinely non-recurring; an evergreen
  cost in one-time costume is rejected in words, never subtracted
  twice; an accepted add-back lifts the base.
- Growth contingent on unsigned deals — prospects in study, pilots
  unconverted — is a claim: priced growth is realized growth.
- Each figure is computed once and reused unchanged; a figure
  computed twice drifts.

## Check 3 — the comp screen

Every comparable is named included or excluded, each with its
reason: a control or sponsor premium beside trading peers; a growth
or margin profile that prices a different business; an NM multiple
on the metric in use. Where the comp set's own note declares a
limit — mix undisclosed, a single viable multiple — the limit is
carried into the memo, not worked around. The target is adjusted
against the included median in words and a direction: size, margin,
growth.

## Check 4 — the range and the ask

Two products only: each an included comp's own multiple × base =
value, written in $M to one decimal. The range is those two
products, written as the valuation's last line. The ask becomes one
implied multiple on the priced base — written once per base where
the base restates (gross-to-net, blended-to-recurring): the two
readings are the comparison, not new arithmetic. These are the
memo's only calculations: no downside case, no implied margins.
Where net debt or cash moves the buyer's wiring, the bridge is
named — EV less net debt — in one line. With no ask stated, the
range is bid guidance; a missing ask is a finding, not a silence.

## Check 5 — the call

| Call | When it answers | What it names |
|---|---|---|
| `proceed` | every must-address row settled; open manageable rows carry named mitigations | the deciding rows, and why RMD and pass do not fit — holding back on a clean report is as wrong as proceeding over an open must-address |
| `request more data` | each open must-address is a question a document can answer — a consent, a closure letter, an executed renewal, a clearance, an audited figure | the record, and the party that issues it |
| `pass` | a gating finding the evidence has already answered against the deal | the deciding row, and what would reopen the file |

Pass fires only where: a counterparty is on record refusing; a term
or rule bars the transfer itself — the asset cannot legally move to
the buyer, and revenue that may not survive post-close is a base
question, never a bar; or the only clearing
record cannot exist on the deal's timeline — a live adjudication on
what the base prices, an expired right that cannot be re-papered;
not a record that merely sits on another party's calendar, because
a pending approval or a scheduled inspection is askable however
slow it looks.
A bar means a rule already on the books forbids the transfer; a
permission still pending — an application, an approval not yet
granted — is askable, not a bar. Only a must-address row carries a
file to pass; a manageable row always takes its mitigation instead.
An ask above the range is a price condition, never by itself a pass.

## Check 6 — the conditions

One condition per open or questioned row — one-to-one, numbered,
citing its row. Each picks an instrument and names the record, the
issuing party and the timing (before LOI, before signing, at close,
post-close):

- a closing deliverable for a document the deciding party can issue;
- a specific indemnity or escrow for a bounded liability;
- contingent consideration for revenue whose survival waits on an
  event the seller cannot settle before close;
- a price reduction for a quantified, unfunded need;
- retention or non-compete for people the value walks out with.

Timing is chosen from those four marks — never an invented period,
percentage, escrow size or earnout. A condition never predicts what
the document will say.

## Row vocabulary — a closed set

Each risk row takes exactly one verdict beside its filed tier —
no fifth label exists:

- `gates` — an open row filed at the gating tier
  (Must-Address/Critical): the filed tier decides. A Manageable row
  that looks deal-critical is flagged open with the reason, never
  re-tiered.
- `open` — a named step or record settles it; under proceed the cell
  carries the mitigation, so no row reads ignored.
- `stands` — verified or uncontradicted; carried as filed, its
  deliverable still named where a document proves the resolution.
- `noted` — informational or outside the decision; no spend.

A row verdict describes the finding's state — `proceed`, `request
more data` and `pass` are memo calls, never row labels. Every input
row carries a verdict — none dropped, none merged; rows sharing one
root cause keep separate lines and cross-reference ("compounds row
N"). A finding the record reveals but the table omits earns its own
row, grounded in the artifacts' words only. Then
`Gate tally: <n gates / n open / n stands / n noted>` re-foots to
the row count exactly.

## Output contract — the memo

First line: the call word alone, the deciding finding, the ask or
its absence — the verdict the committee reads before anything is
cut. Then the five sections, in this order:

1. `## 1. Deal thesis` — ≤150 words: what is bought, why it is
   worth owning on the restated base, the primary risk named in its
   row's words.
2. `## 2. Tiered risk table` — every finding in input order: #,
   finding, tier and status as filed, verdict cell ≤40 words stating
   the deciding fact. Tally line beneath.
3. `## 3. Defended valuation` — ≤300 words plus comp table: base,
   include/exclude reasons, directional adjustments, the ask's
   implied multiple, the two products, the range as the last line.
4. `## 4. Recommendation` — ≤140 words: the call as first words,
   the deciding rows, why the other two calls do not fit.
5. `## 5. Conditions and next steps` — one bullet per open or
   questioned row, ≤45 words each, instrument named.

Cut prose before rows, arithmetic, recommendation or conditions —
the tail is what a long memo loses. Every rationale is
non-interchangeable: a sentence that could sit under another finding
gets the deciding words instead.
