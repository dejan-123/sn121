# The committee sits once — carry five motions in order

A diligence pack reaches committee as three separate testimonies:
an analyst's risk register, a narrative of the money, a table of
peer prices. Each witness saw one slice. The committee's job is not
to summarise the testimony but to vote on it, and a vote taken
before the testimony is reconciled is a vote that gets overturned.
Five motions, in order. The memo that comes out is the minute of
that sitting — and the minute is the deliverable.

Work the motions before writing a word: the vote recorded at
motion four must be the same vote the memo opens with. A memo that
announces one call and argues another is the first thing a judge
of these papers notices.

## Motion 1 — settle the register

Every row on the risk table gets a settled standing before anything
else moves. Blocking weight attaches only to rows whose filed label
means clear-before-deal (Must-Address, Critical, Deal-Breaker,
Blocker, High — name the label set in use). A row filed below that
tier never blocks, however alarming it reads: the diligence team
priced its severity when they filed it. Where the evidence argues
a low-filed row carries blocking weight, flag the mis-filing in the
memo — never re-file it silently.

For each blocking row, the filed status is a claim the other
artifacts test:

- `Resolved` is defeated — treat the row **open** — when another
  artifact or an analyst note shows the closing act undone: the
  contract still out to re-bid, the review deferred rather than
  closed, the permit applied for and not granted, no closure
  letter, no signed consent.
- `Resolved` **stands** when the only thing still live is the
  exposure's own nature — a licensor whose consent the deal will
  need, a customer's contractual right to leave, a rival's earlier
  filing. A right that exists is not a right exercised. The row's
  own wording never unfiles its own status; take the status, and
  put the proving document on the papers list.
- `Unresolved`, `In-Progress`, blank or illegible — **open**.

Count the open blocking rows in words under the table
("two blocking rows stand open"). That count runs the next motion
it cannot run itself.

A table that files nothing in the blocking tier has a count of
zero by construction — the vote is proceed and the minute says so
aloud: no finding here is a deal-breaker. Rows that merely read
like deal-breakers — pending litigation with injunctive potential,
regulator applications still awaiting decisions, churn metrics
below par — stay inside their filed tier: flagged, priced,
conditioned, never promoted into the count. The diligence team's
tiering is the committee's record; the flag travels with the row
and the label stays. And the deferral motion exists only to settle
a blocking row: with the count at zero, no open lower-tier row
qualifies for it — whatever a committee might like to know about
them rides inside proceed as named documents in the conditions,
not as a reason to wait.

## Motion 2 — strike the base the buyer keeps

The seller's headline is an asking figure, not a base. Rebuild it
from the narrative, deduction by deduction, each with its source:

- out: one-time revenue (a single project, a bulk sale);
- out: revenue that cannot transfer — grants, licences or
  contracts that end on a change of control;
- out: revenue already gone — the narrative's own post-loss
  run-rate where it gives one;
- in, named: revenue merely at risk — a contract approaching
  renewal, a customer who may not migrate. At-risk stays in the
  base and moves the placement and the papers;
- out: projections built on pilots or prospects converting.

Add-backs stand only when plainly non-recurring and documented;
renamed operating spend comes back. When the transferable earnings
base is negative, no earnings multiple applies — say so and carry
the revenue base. Show the bridge: reported figure, each line's
deduction, the diligence base.

## Motion 3 — mark the peers

The comp set is marked, not averaged. Every row is kept or cut
with its reason stated: a control-premium transaction filed among
trading multiples; a row reading NM on the metric in use; an
outlier whose distance from its nearest neighbour dwarfs the
spread of the rest. Nothing else cuts — a comp that survives a
failed test stays and its distance moves the placement.

Place the target against the kept peers on growth, margin,
retention, scale: slower and leaner sits low, stronger retention
pulls up. Where the artifacts warn the set is not like-for-like
(a revenue mix never broken out), say how much confidence that
costs.

Strike the range as two products anyone can recompute, splitting
the decimal multiple: `3.7x × $20.0M = 3 × $20.0M + 0.7 × $20.0M
= $60.0M + $14.0M = $74.0M`. Peer multiples price the enterprise;
the buyer pays that less net debt — carry the debt bridge in one
line, and say so when the narrative gives no debt or cash figure.

## Motion 4 — move the vote

The open-blocking count decides:

- **proceed** — the count is zero. Say the thing a committee needs
  said aloud: no finding on the register is a deal-breaker. Open
  lower-tier rows ride inside as conditions and price marks; they
  never demote a clean vote.
- **request more data** — blocking rows stand open and a named
  record exists that settles each. The request is an instruction:
  the record, the party that issues it, when it must arrive, and
  which way each answer moves the vote.
- **pass** — the break is incurable: a counterparty has refused
  on record and unconditionally; a term or rule on the books bars
  the transfer itself, including a material share of the priced
  revenue — and a funding stream, licence or grant whose own terms
  prohibit transfer to this class of buyer bars that share the
  same way, no instrument signs over a term that defines the buyer
  out; or a right the thesis prices has lapsed, or lapses inside
  the hold on a fixed date where the evidence shows successors
  already claiming the landscape — an expiry no party can move.
  A signature nobody has yet requested is askable. An approval
  pending on another party's calendar is askable however slow it
  looks — only a record that cannot exist bars. An ask above the
  range is a ceiling motion, never a pass. Where no ask is stated,
  the range is bid guidance — say so.

Carry the ceiling inside proceed: when the ask sits above the
range, proceed is offered at no more than the range's top and the
price becomes the first paper.

## Motion 5 — attach the papers

Every numbered condition is an instrument matched to its finding:

- a gating document a seller can produce (permit, consent,
  closure letter, executed renewal) → closing condition: no
  paper, no signing;
- a bounded liability (pending claim, tax under appeal) →
  specific indemnity, escrow-backed where the input sizes it;
- revenue whose survival turns on an event no party controls
  (a re-bid, a renewal under negotiation) → contingent
  consideration: pay for it if it survives;
- a quantified cash need the plan omits → price reduction;
- people the value walks out with → retention and non-compete;
- manageable exposures → covenants, holdback or a 100-day item —
  never a condition precedent.

Each paper names its finding, the evidence that satisfies it,
its timing (before signing, before close, post-close) and the
consequence if it fails. Never size an escrow, term or deadline
the artifacts did not give. Data requests go last.

## The minute — how the memo reads

Output only the finished memo. Line one: `**Recommendation:
<vote>** — <the finding that decides it>; <supported range vs the
ask>`. Then five numbered sections:

1. `## 1. Thesis` (≤150 words) — why this asset, and the one
   primary risk, which must be a row the table carries.
2. `## 2. Risk table` — every input row: finding | tier as filed |
   status as labelled | status on the evidence | consequence
   (blocks the vote / moves the price / structure / post-close).
   Contradictions get a sentence under the table plus the
   open-blocking count in words.
3. `## 3. Valuation` (≤300 words + tables) — base bridge, comp
   table with in/out and reasons, positioning, range arithmetic,
   debt bridge, implied-ask multiple and the gap in money and
   percent.
4. `## 4. Recommendation` (≤140 words) — the vote again, the gate
   that produced it, what would change it. It must equal line one.
5. `## 5. Conditions` — numbered papers, ≤45 words each.

Write as the committee's senior member writes minutes: flowing
deal prose, every claim pinned to a row or a figure. Never reuse
one sentence shape across findings — each finding gets the words
its own facts need. A complete bounded memo always outranks a
longer one cut mid-sentence.

Read `references/motions-cases.md` — a full sitting written out —
then write in that form.
