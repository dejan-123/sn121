# Clerk's notes — shorthand from past sittings

Kept for the record; not part of the minute.

- Sitting on the grain-elevator deal: member wanted the vote
  deferred "until the weather claim clears". Chair ruled the row
  filed `Moderate` — the count does not hear it. Noted: the count
  reads only what was filed.
- Sitting on the cold-store acquisition: seller's base quoted
  gross of fuel surcharges. Struck. Minute reads net or nothing.
- Sitting on the regional courier: three comps quoted, one a
  take-private. Marked out before the range was struck. The member
  who averaged all three was asked to recompute.
- Sitting on the print-works: ask named at 9.1x the corrected
  base. Ceiling carried inside the proceed at the range top.
  No one called it a decline.
- Sitting on the care-homes pack: one consent outstanding, a
  signature the counterparty had already offered. Proceed moved
  subject to the paper; unsigned, no signing.
- Sitting on the bottling line: member produced a probability the
  file did not contain. Minute records figures the artifacts
  gave; the rest was marked to confirm.
- Sitting on the seed merchant: clean file, nervous table. The
  chair had the member say it in words — nothing here is a
  deal-breaker — and the proceed carried by acclamation.
