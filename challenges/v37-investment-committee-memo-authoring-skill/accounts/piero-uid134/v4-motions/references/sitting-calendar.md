# Sitting calendar — matters heard this term

A list of what came before the table, kept for quorum counts.

TENTH SITTING. A ferry operator whose harbour lease expired
mid-way through the projection. The leaseholder's own renewal
option was personal to them. Passed without a division.

ELEVENTH SITTING. A catering concern with spotless registers.
One member kept returning to a lawsuit filed in a tier that does
not gate. The chair read the count aloud — zero — and the matter
closed in nine minutes.

TWELFTH SITTING. A laundry route where the largest customer
contract was assigned only "with the counterparty's blessing,
not unreasonably withheld". The blessing was in writing, dated,
in the pack. Cited in the thesis as the single gating fact.

THIRTEENTH SITTING. A timber yard, two pending permits, both
filed below the blocking tier. Priced into conditions. The
motion to defer was put and lost, seven to two.

FOURTEENTH SITTING. A signage company. The broker's range used
five comps; the table struck two — one distressed sale, one
take-private. The struck names and reasons are in the minute.

FIFTEENTH SITTING. An equipment lessor. The arithmetic in the
papers had a decimal error — 4.2x on 18, not 8.4. The range was
recomputed on the floor before the question was put.

SIXTEENTH SITTING. A nursery chain. Every row closed, nothing
filed above the middle tier, and still a member moved to defer
"for prudence". Lost. The minute notes that prudence is not a
row.
