# Record — Fenwick Agricultural Inputs, entered in full

The pack on **Fenwick Agricultural Inputs**, a crop-inputs merchant
and agronomy services business (seed, fertiliser, advisory), came
before the board. Below: the record as settled, then the minute
as filed. This one resolved to proceed — a record that earns a
clean resolution is as much a discipline as one that catches a
break.

## The pack (compressed)

Entries as filed:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Seed-supply agreement with the region's largest genetics licensor (29% of seed margin) requires licensor's written consent on change of control; executed consent held in the data room | Must-Address | Resolved |
| Elevated nitrate readings at one depot; remediation plan agreed with the environmental authority and halfway complete | Manageable | In-Progress |
| Two agronomists who manage 40% of advisory revenue have employment contracts but no non-solicit clauses | Manageable | Unresolved |

Narrative: LTM revenue $61.3M including $7.8M of fertiliser sold
through at cost-plus-zero on a supply-short contract (pass-through
volume). EBITDA $5.5M. Cash $3.4M; debt $8.9M. Growth 13%.
Asking price $48.0M.

Peer entries: PlainField Agronomy 7.9x EV/EBITDA (15% growth) ·
SeedVault Merchants 6.6x (9%) · CropLine Inputs 8.6x (17%) ·
AgriGiant Consolidated 12.4x (26%) — acquired in a take-private ·
SoilStart NM (pre-profit).

## The record as settled

**Entries.** Blocking tier = Must-Address. The sole gating entry
carries `Resolved` — and the record corroborates it: the executed
consent is held in the data room. A consent that exists is not a
consent outstanding; the licensor's right to require one persists
by nature, and that persistence is priced, not treated as open.
Below the tier: the nitrate remediation (on plan, on record) and
the missing non-solicits (a real exposure — flagged into the
instructions, never promoted). **No blocking entry outstanding.**

**Money.** $61.3M less the $7.8M pass-through fertiliser — volume
carried for customers at no margin — = **$53.5M** the record
delivers. EBITDA $5.5M clean. The agronomists' 40% of advisory
revenue is exposed, not gone: retained in the base, flagged to
the instructions.

**Peers.** AgriGiant is **excluded** — a control-premium
take-private. SoilStart is **excluded** on the earnings metric
(NM). Admitted: 6.6x–8.6x. Fenwick's customer retention and
service mix sit near the admitted middle: 7.0x–8.0x.

- 7.0x × $5.5M = 7 × $5.5M = **$38.5M**
- 8.0x × $5.5M = 8 × $5.5M = **$44.0M**

Enterprise $38.5M–$44.0M, less net debt $5.5M → equity
**$33.0M–$38.5M**. The $48.0M ask implies 8.7x — above the range
top by ~23%: a pricing instruction, not a resolution.

**Resolution.** The record holds no outstanding blocking entry:
**proceed**, entered in words — and the price instruction rides
inside it.

## The minute as filed

**Recommendation: proceed** — the record holds no outstanding
blocking entry; supported equity range $33.0M–$38.5M against a
$48.0M ask.

## 1. Thesis
Fenwick's case is recurring merchant-and-advisory income anchored
by a consented supply agreement and a service book that keeps
growers renewing; at a mid-to-high single-digit multiple it is a
sound acquisition. The primary risk is advisory-revenue
concentration: two agronomists carry 40% of it with no
non-solicits — an exposure to be papered, and it is real enough
to name first, but it does not gate the deal.

## 2. Risk table

| Finding | Tier filed | Status labelled | Standing on evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Licensor change-of-control consent (29% of seed margin) | Must-Address | Resolved | Settled — executed consent held | Instruction only |
| Depot nitrate remediation | Manageable | In-Progress | As filed — halfway complete on an agreed plan | Instruction |
| Agronomist non-solicits absent (40% of advisory revenue) | Manageable | Unresolved | As filed — real exposure, below gating weight | Instruction |

No blocking entry stands outstanding. The consent entry is noted
for the record: its label is corroborated by the data room, and
the residual exposure is priced rather than re-opened.

## 3. Valuation
The delivered base is $53.5M — reported $61.3M less $7.8M of
pass-through fertiliser volume carried at no margin — with clean
EBITDA of $5.5M. Of five peer entries, AgriGiant is excluded as
a control premium and SoilStart on the earnings metric. The
admitted set runs 6.6x–8.6x EV/EBITDA; retention and service mix
place Fenwick mid-set: 7.0x–8.0x.

- 7.0x × $5.5M = **$38.5M**
- 8.0x × $5.5M = **$44.0M**

Enterprise $38.5M–$44.0M, less net debt of $5.5M → equity
**$33.0M–$38.5M**. The $48.0M ask implies 8.7x the corrected base
— roughly 23% above the range top. That is a pricing instruction,
and the resolution does not bend to it.

## 4. Recommendation
**Proceed.** Nothing on this file is a deal-breaker — the board
says so plainly because a clean record deserves a clean
resolution, not a manufactured doubt. The gating entry is settled
on the evidence; the lighter entries are instructions, not
obstacles. The price, however, is instructed at no more than
$38.5M of equity — the ask sits ~23% above the supported range,
and closing that gap is the negotiation this resolution
authorises.

## 5. Conditions
1. Price: equity at no more than $38.5M; the premium the ask
   carries is instructed down, not deferred.
2. Executed non-solicit and retention agreements with the two
   named agronomists, before signing — their book is 40% of
   advisory revenue and it is the deal's thinnest wall.
3. Contingent consideration on advisory revenue: the
   agronomist-served book is paid for as it is retained through
   the first year.
4. Nitrate remediation completion sign-off from the
   environmental authority, post-close, with a holdback sized
   only on the remediation plan's own figures.
5. Licensor supply terms confirmed as continuing on current
   terms post-close — recorded as instruction, not reopened as
   a gate.

## Other records — how the same desk resolves differently

**If the consent were unsigned but offered** — one outstanding
blocking entry, closed by a signature the licensor can simply
execute: **proceed, subject to the deliverable** — the executed
consent is a condition precedent; unsigned, no signing; refused,
the resolution converts to pass.

**If the authority had opened a formal contamination
investigation** (a second blocking entry, needing an outcome, not
a signature): **request more data** — the instruction names the
investigation's findings, its issuer, its expected date, and the
answer that changes the resolution.

**If the licensor had refused in writing, unconditionally** — the
consent cannot exist: 29% of margin has no legal path to the
buyer. **Pass** — a term on the books bars the transfer of a
material share of the priced base.

## Records that get sent back

1. **A clean record resolved timidly.** "Request more data" on a
   file with nothing outstanding manufactures a problem the
   board was not asked to invent — the committee needs to hear
   *no finding is a deal-breaker*, in words.
2. **A settled entry re-opened by suspicion alone.** The executed
   consent is evidence; treating it as open because consents are
   "generally risky" second-guesses the record.
3. **A price that sets the resolution.** The ask is 23% above
   the range; in this record that instructs the negotiation.
   Declining on price alone confuses the money section with the
   gating entries.
