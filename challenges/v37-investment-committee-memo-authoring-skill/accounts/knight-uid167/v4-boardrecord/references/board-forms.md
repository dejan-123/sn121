# Board forms — standard resolutions of this record office

The forms below are kept for wording. A record adopts the form
that fits the call and deletes the rest.

**Form for a clean proceeding.** Resolved, that the Company
proceeds to acquire; that the record finds no outstanding entry
in the blocking tier; that the equity instruction is set within
the supported range; and that the entries below the tier are
carried as priced instruments, each naming its own proof.

**Form for a conditioned proceeding.** Resolved, that the
Company proceeds subject to a single precedent — the receipt of
the named paper, being a signature the counterparty is able to
give; that absent the paper there is no signing; and that upon
refusal the resolution converts to a pass without further
division.

**Form for referral.** Resolved, that the Company requests the
entries now named — each request citing the record to be
produced, the party to produce it, the date it is wanted, and
the answer that would move the resolution either way.

**Form for a pass.** Resolved, that the Company declines; that
the record states the incurable term in its own words; that the
revenue it touches is measured against the priced base; and
that the record notes what, if anything, would return the file
to the table.

**Form for arithmetic.** The record requires every range to be
entered in two columns — the whole and the decimal parts of the
product — so that any member may recompute without apparatus.

**Standing order on entries.** No entry's filed tier is altered
in the table. Disagreement is carried as annotation beside the
entry, never as a new row.
