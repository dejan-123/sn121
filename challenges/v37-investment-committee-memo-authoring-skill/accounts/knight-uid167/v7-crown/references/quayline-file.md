# Worked file - invented example

Quayline Packaging, a converter of food-grade films. The register's top filed tier is Manageable - one open row covering a single-plant site dependence, one Resolved row covering a minor product recall. Two Informational rows. Narrative: reported revenue 26.5M with a 3.0M tolling arrangement that is pass-through. Debt 12.0M and cash 2.5M supplied. Ask 74M. Quoted peers: four converters at 3.4-4.1x revenue; a global films group taken control at 5.8x.

## Re-rating

Blocking tier present: none - Manageable is the top filed tier, so no exclusions exist on this file. Both Manageable rows get subjectivities; the verdict is terms.

**Recommendation: proceed** - no filed gating rows; rated EV range 61-71M, equity 51-61M, against a 74M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis

Quayline converts food-grade film for regional brands under multi-year supply agreements. The rated base is about 23.5M of owned revenue once the pass-through tolling stream is removed. The lead exposure is single-site dependence - one plant produces everything - which the terms must address through continuity planning rather than a decline.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Standing after re-rating | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single production site | Manageable | Open | Exposure stands; business-interruption continuity plan warranted | Subjectivity before signing |
| Minor product recall (prior year) | Manageable | Resolved | Closed - regulator's closure letter on file | Monitor only |
| Key operator near retirement | Informational | Open | Succession gap | 100-day plan |
| Two patents filed, pending grant | Informational | Open | Applications in process | Track status |

Open gating rows: 0 - nothing filed at a blocking tier.

## 3. Valuation

Rated base: 26.5M reported less 3.0M pass-through tolling = 23.5M revenue.

Comparables: the four converters (3.4x, 3.6x, 3.9x, 4.1x) retained - same conversion model. The global films group at 5.8x is excluded - control transaction with scale synergies this file cannot claim.

Positioning: mid-pack margins, slower growth, elevated site risk - 2.6x low, 3.0x high.

2.6 x 23.5M = 61.1M enterprise value
3.0 x 23.5M = 70.5M enterprise value

Equity bridge (debt 12.0M, cash 2.5M): net debt 9.5M. Equity value 51.6-61.0M. The 74M ask implies 3.15x the rated base - above the defended range on an enterprise basis and roughly 13-22M above on equity. That is a premium to negotiate down, not a defect.

## 4. Recommendation

Proceed. The file supports issuing a letter of intent up to about 61M equity, subject to the subjectivities in section 5. The ask stands above the defended range; negotiation is expected. Facts that would re-rate the file: loss of a top brand account, or the single-site exposure becoming uninsured.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Site dependence - risk engineer obtains a business-continuity plan with recovery-time estimate before signing; failure re-opens the rating.
2. Recall history - quality lead files the regulator's closure letter in the data room for the record.
3. Operator succession - operations lead schedules a documented handover inside the 100-day plan.
4. Price - negotiation mandate capped at 61.0M equity; movement above requires fresh evidence on the site exposure.

## Alternate declination - an open exclusion

Were the recall row filed Critical with the closure letter still outstanding - referenced but absent from the file - the exclusion stands open with a named cure: `request more data`, leading conditions with the regulator's closure letter.

## Alternate declination - the door already shut

Were the top brand account's exit recorded unconditionally with a third of revenue departing and no replacement on file, the rated base no longer supports any defensible range: `pass`.
