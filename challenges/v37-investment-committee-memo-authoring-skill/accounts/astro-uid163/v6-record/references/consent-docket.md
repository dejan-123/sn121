# Closing record: Bramble Cove Marine

Bramble Cove Marine services harbour ferries under contract to a coastal transit authority. The fictional packet below feeds the worked memo only; it is unrelated to any real transaction.

The register:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| The transit authority's operating concession requires its written consent before any change of control; correspondence shows the consent request was filed but no signed consent exists | Must-Address | In-Progress |
| Dock lease has 14 months remaining with a renewal option | Manageable | Resolved |
| Two seasonal engineer roles are unfilled | Manageable | Unresolved |

The narrative: $6.8M LTM revenue, of which $0.8M was a one-time dredging contract; ordinary service revenue $6.0M. Growth 12%, gross margin 38%. No debt or cash supplied. Ask $15.5M enterprise value.

Comparables: Harborline Services 2.2x EV/revenue (14% growth, 40% margin), Quaypoint Group 2.6x (19%, 44%), Tideworks 1.8x (9%, 35%), and BeaconPort at 3.9x - a completed sale to the port operator itself at a control premium.

**Recommendation: request more data** -- the authority's signed change-of-control consent is filed but unsigned; $10.8M-$13.2M enterprise value on ordinary revenue against a $15.5M ask.

## 1. Deal thesis

Bramble Cove earns $6.0M of recurring service revenue under a concession that already operates. The primary risk is the change-of-control consent: the concession is the entire revenue base, and its transfer instrument is filed but not signed. The dock lease renewal and open roles are ordinary closing matters.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Instrument mark and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority consent to change of control | Must-Address | In-Progress | Instrument missing, obtainable: the signed consent is a document the authority can issue. Request it; a signed consent reopens proceed, a refusal ends the deal. |
| Dock lease, 14 months + renewal option | Manageable | Resolved | Uncontested; obtain the exercised renewal as a closing deliverable. |
| Two unfilled engineer roles | Manageable | Unresolved | No instrument needed; staffing plan and cost reserve in conditions. |

1 open gating row: the authority consent.

## 3. Valuation

Reported $6.8M less the $0.8M one-time dredging contract gives a $6.0M transferable base. Harborline (2.2x), Quaypoint (2.6x) and Tideworks (1.8x) are retained; BeaconPort (3.9x) is excluded as a completed control sale. Bramble Cove's 12% growth and 38% margin sit between Tideworks and Harborline, supporting 1.8x-2.2x:

- 1.8x x $6.0M = $10.8M enterprise value
- 2.2x x $6.0M = $13.2M enterprise value

Range $10.8M-$13.2M. The $15.5M ask is 2.6x the base and $2.3M over the ceiling. No equity bridge is possible without debt and cash.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. The consent is a named, obtainable instrument from a party that has not refused; proceed is unavailable while the one gating row lacks its signature, and pass is premature because nothing in the packet says the authority will not sign. The price view is separate: the deal returns at no more than $13.2M.

## 5. Conditions / next steps

1. Counsel obtains the authority's signed change-of-control consent before signing; a written refusal ends the deal.
2. Operations delivers the executed dock-lease renewal at close.
3. Deal lead counters the ask within $10.8M-$13.2M before signing.
4. HR files the engineering staffing plan and costed hiring reserve before close.

## Same record, different instruments

If the packet instead showed the authority had *signed* the consent, N falls to zero and the identical rows produce **proceed** at the same range - the consent becomes a confirmatory deliverable, not a gate.

If the packet showed the authority *refused* consent to this buyer class and the concession names no permitted assignee, the instrument is impossible: **pass** regardless of price, and no amount of diligence changes it.
