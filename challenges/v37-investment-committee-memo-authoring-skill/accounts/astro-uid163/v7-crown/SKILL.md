# No label closes until the proof is on the docket

The packet arrives pre-digested - somebody else tiered the findings, somebody else marked them resolved, somebody else chose the reported earnings. This skill treats every one of those choices as a claim to verify against independent evidence elsewhere in the packet. Apply the six disciplines below in sequence, then write the decision. Consult `references/consent-docket-v7.md` once - it walks a complete memo where labels and proof disagree, with two alternate endings. Its companies and figures are invented.

## 1. Establish which findings can hold the vote

Only a filed blocking tier - Must-Address, Critical or whatever the register itself declares sign-blocking - can gate the committee. The topmost label on the page is not automatically blocking: a register of Manageable and Informational rows alone contains zero gates - pending or In-Progress lower-tier items are conditions, never gates - and the present call is proceed with conditions. Suspected under-tiering is noted beside the row, never counted upward silently.

## 2. Match each resolved row to its instrument

For every blocking-tier row filed as Resolved, the packet must contain or clearly reference the instrument that resolved it - the executed deed, the signed consent, the regulator's letter, the issued license, the awarded contract. **A resolution asserted without its instrument evidenced anywhere in the packet is treated as open** for the purpose of the vote; the row keeps its filed status in the table and the missing instrument is named beside it. Where the packet says outright the closing act is undone, the label fails outright; cite the conflict. A historical exposure recited in the row - an earlier competing filing, a continuing termination right, a consent obligation - is what the instrument answers, and its absence leaves the question open rather than decided either way. Rows filed Unresolved, In-Progress or blank are open by definition.

Print the count: `N open gating rows`, where N = blocking tier and open-under-the-proof-rule. N=0 produces proceed; N>0 with a defined instrument produces request more data; N>0 that no instrument can cure produces pass.

## 3. Pick the vote

- **proceed** once the proof rule leaves no blocking row open; each lower-tier open item converts into a named condition, price protection or monitoring line.
- **request more data** while open blocking rows still have a specific obtainable cure - list each required paper, its issuer and the closing outcome, plus the adverse outcome that reprices or kills. A gap resting only on a signature or consent still needs that signature or consent - name the deliverable.
- **pass** where the packet itself has foreclosed a material gate for this buyer: a recorded unconditional refusal, an express bar on owning the priced asset, or a terminating exclusive right without a successor on the record.

Watch for condition words in adverse statements - `if`, `unless`, `would`, `provided`. A refusal that operates only when the buyer lacks a stated qualification is a question, not a refusal: ask whether this buyer qualifies and whether a qualified substitute stands in. Buyer failure of a stated condition must never be assumed. An ask above the supported range is repricing, not a gate.

## 4. Reprice only the business being bought

Rebuild the earnings base: strip stated one-time revenue, departed customers and pass-through volume; reject add-backs that recur. Revenue that persists under a cancellation right or heavy single-customer share remains in the base - but the memo must say what fraction of the priced book depends on it, and attach that dependence to placement, conditions or protection. Negative or unsupported earnings switch the metric to revenue and the memo explains why.

Each named comp is retained or dropped by a stated rule (trading only, metric-compatible, no control-premium outliers). Position the target on growth, margin, retention, scale. Print exactly:

`low multiple x corrected base = low enterprise value`
`high multiple x corrected base = high enterprise value`

These products are the range wherever the memo states it. **When the packet supplies debt and cash figures, show the equity bridge - EV less net debt - before commenting on the ask:** that bridge is required content, because price fairness is judged on equity, not enterprise value. Restate any ask on the corrected base and compare; no supplied ask means none is invented. No fabricated deadlines, holdbacks, retention figures or thresholds anywhere.

## 5. Conditions carry paper

Every condition cites its finding, the closing instrument by name, the issuer or owner role, the deadline relative to signing, and the remedy if unmet - walk-away, price reset, or protection sized only from packet figures. Regulatory responses pending an outcome, unsigned consents, unassigned patents and pending licenses each get their deliverable named verbatim.

## 6. Deliver a finished decision, not notes

First line: `**Recommendation: <proceed | request more data | pass>** - <deciding finding>; <defended EV range, plus the equity range where debt and cash are given>.` Then exactly five numbered sections:

1. `## 1. Deal thesis` - the asset bought, the durable base, the lead risk also in the table (under 150 words).
2. `## 2. Tiered risk table` - all rows in source order: finding, filed tier, filed status, standing under the proof rule, consequence; open-gate count beneath.
3. `## 3. Valuation` - the base bridge, every comp decision, target placement, the two products, the equity bridge when supplied, ask comparison (under 300 words plus table).
4. `## 4. Recommendation` - the first line's verdict verbatim; what proceeds now, what evidence changes it; signing permission distinct from price permission (under 140 words).
5. `## 5. Conditions and next actions` - numbered: finding, owner, instrument, timing, consequence.

Professional finished prose under 900 words: no brackets, no invented numbers, no working notes. Final pass checks the gate count, both products, copied statuses, the lead risk's row and line-one/section-4 agreement.
