# Worked decision letter: Alder Grounds

This is a fictional commercial landscape-maintenance company. Its risk scale, numerical facts and peers are invented for demonstration, not a source of facts for another deal.

The diligence table says:

| Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Municipal contract invoice reconciliation completed by outside accountant | Must-Address | Resolved |
| Foreman recruitment and route handover remain unfinished | Manageable | In-Progress |
| Aging mower fleet has planned maintenance expense | Manageable | Unresolved |
| Office relocation is complete | Informational | Resolved |

The narrative says the outside accountant delivered the reconciliation and found no adjustment to reported revenue. Alder generated $18.0M in last-twelve-month service revenue and $2.4M EBITDA. A $0.6M winter storm cleanup contract was nonrepeatable; ordinary revenue is $17.4M. Regular contracted accounts renewed as expected, though crew shortages could constrain next year's capacity. The owner asks $47.0M enterprise value. No cash or debt figures are given.

The comparable dataset lists Elmfield Services at 2.2x revenue, 7% growth, 29% margin; Tern Property Care at 2.6x, 11%, 31%; Vale Route at 2.9x, 16%, 34%; and GreenArc at 4.5x, a price paid to buy control of an entire regional network rather than a trading quote. Alder's ordinary-service growth is 9% with a 30% margin.

**Recommendation: proceed** -- no Must-Address finding remains open; the $47.0M ask exceeds a $38.3M-$45.2M enterprise range on ordinary revenue.

## 1. Deal thesis

Alder Grounds has a contracted maintenance book and an established route network with $17.4M in ordinary revenue. The acquisition case rests on converting that book into reliable service capacity. The primary risk is the unfinished foreman recruitment and route handover: staff coverage could constrain the next season even though the largest reported accounting issue has been resolved. A capped price and a retention plan keep the committee from paying for capacity not yet in place.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Evidence and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Municipal invoice reconciliation | Must-Address | Resolved | The narrative says the outside accountant delivered it without a revenue adjustment. Status stands; obtain the signed report at signing. |
| Foreman recruitment and handover | Manageable | In-Progress | Open staffing matter; secure a named transition schedule and retention terms. Not a filed voting gate. |
| Mower fleet maintenance | Manageable | Unresolved | Get the existing maintenance budget and service records; price a verified shortfall, not a guessed one. |
| Office relocation | Informational | Resolved | No contrary evidence; no new closing gate. |

No Must-Address finding remains open. The two manageable items require terms, not a change in the vote.

## 3. Valuation

$18.0M in reported revenue includes $0.6M of one-off storm work, giving $17.4M of ordinary revenue. The $2.4M EBITDA is reported without enough detail to recast it, so revenue sets this range.

| Comparable | EV/Revenue | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Elmfield Services | 2.2x | Included; its growth and margin are near Alder's. |
| Tern Property Care | 2.6x | Included; somewhat stronger growth. |
| Vale Route | 2.9x | Included; faster growth and higher margin. |
| GreenArc | 4.5x | Excluded from trading range; completed control acquisition. |

Alder's 9% growth and 30% margin support the bottom half of the trading group, or 2.2x-2.6x. At 2.2 x $17.4M = $38.28M, rounded to $38.3M; at 2.6 x $17.4M = $45.24M, rounded to $45.2M enterprise value. The $47.0M ask implies 2.70x ordinary revenue and is $1.8M above the rounded ceiling. No debt or cash bridge is possible on the supplied figures.

## 4. Recommendation

Proceed at no more than $45.2M enterprise value on the present evidence. The only Must-Address row is filed Resolved and is corroborated by the accountant's completed work; this vote does not assume that the open manageable items are closed. Reprice the ask, obtain the accountant's signed report at signing, and make the staffing handover a specific closing obligation. A failed handover changes the agreed terms before close, rather than retroactively inventing a new blocking tier.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Buyer's finance lead receives the outside accountant's signed reconciliation report before signing; any variance returns the financial base for review.
2. Deal lead counters the $47.0M ask at no more than $45.2M enterprise value before signing; no approval exists for the excess.
3. Operations lead and seller document the foreman recruitment and route handover plan before close; missing coverage requires a revised operating plan and price discussion.
4. Operations lead reviews mower service records and the maintenance budget before close; a documented shortfall is addressed in price or a targeted holdback.

## Short counterfactuals

If an independent later report instead stated that the reconciliation was still unfinished, the filed Resolved label would be disputed: the call would be **request more data** and the committee would await the final signed report. If a binding rule made the contracted service book unavailable to this buyer and the record offered no way to replace it, a priced core of the thesis would fail and the call would be **pass**, not an indefinite request for documents describing the rule.

Suppose a different pack contains no Must-Address or equivalent row at all: a warehouse racking repair filed Manageable remains incomplete, and a utilization survey filed Informational remains pending. The open gating count is zero. The decision letter says **proceed**, carries the racking work into a documented closing schedule, and leaves the survey in ordinary diligence. Calling those filings a reason to postpone the committee vote would secretly change their tiers.

Another complete decision letter would start: "**Recommendation: proceed** -- no source finding is filed as a blocking risk; the price ceiling remains subject to the peer set." Its only findings are a new storefront sign still awaiting municipal approval (`Manageable | In-Progress`), a stock-loss claim with an insurer still to respond (`Manageable | Unresolved`) and weak midweek traffic (`Informational | Unresolved`). In section 2 the three rows retain those exact tiers and statuses. The sentence under the table is "There are zero open gating rows because no gating-tier finding is filed." Section 4 repeats proceed, while section 5 requires the sign permit as a pre-opening deliverable, the claim under a properly bounded indemnity and a traffic recovery plan. None of those ordinary questions changes the present vote into a request for more data.

## Two decided endings and one open question

A dated end to the exclusive operating licence that supports the whole price is not a question for more diligence. If the packet says the licence cannot be renewed or replaced, the decision letter passes even while another row still awaits an answer. A document describing the expiry cannot make the right last longer.

A funding or operating stream that the stated rules make unavailable to this buyer class is also decided. Remove it from the acquired base; if the remaining business cannot support the ask, pass. Do not write "request more data" merely to ask whether an expressly forbidden stream might transfer.

By contrast, when a required partner says it *would* withdraw if a buyer lacks a qualification, and the packet does not establish that this buyer lacks it or that no qualified replacement exists, the committee requests the partner's position and the replacement market. A conditional warning is an open gate, not a proved refusal.
