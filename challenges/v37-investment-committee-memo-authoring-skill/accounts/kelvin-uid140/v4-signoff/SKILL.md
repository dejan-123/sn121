# Write the risk like a policy, not a summary

Three diligence artifacts arrive the way a broker's submission
arrives: a schedule of exposures, a story about the money, a table
of what similar books fetch. An underwriter does not paraphrase the
submission — the underwriter decides whether to write it, on what
terms, at what price. This skill prices an acquisition the same
way: survey the exposures, fix the book being bought, mark the
peer market, then sign, refer, or decline — and set the
endorsements that complete the cover.

Decide before drafting. The decision reached at the survey desk is
the decision the memo opens with; a memo that recommends one thing
and argues another gets referred back.

## The survey — what the schedule actually shows

First pass, before any counting: screen every row for a fact
already decided against the deal — a dated expiry with filed
successors, a refusal on record, a term or rule barring transfer
to this buyer, a counterparty holding an at-will right over a
material share of the book. A decided fact is not an open
exposure: no document extends a term date, no consent undoes a
stated refusal — and no knowable question sits beside it: the
exclusivity ends on that date whether the successors win or
lose; a counsel opinion on who dominates afterwards is
arithmetic on a protection already gone. Such a row exits the
count entirely; where it touches the priced book or the thesis,
it is the declination, and no other row converts it back into a
referral. Only rows whose fact is still *unknown* — unsigned,
unstated, unproduced — enter the count below.

Blocking weight attaches to exposures filed as clear-before-deal
(Must-Address, Critical, Deal-Breaker, Blocker, High — name the
schedule's own label set). A row filed below that band never
blocks on its own, however loudly it reads; where facts argue
mis-filing, flag it — never re-file it yourself.

Each blocking exposure's status is a representation the other
artifacts can void:

- `Resolved` is **voided** — the exposure stands open — where the
  narrative or an appended note shows the closing act undone:
  renewal invited to competitive bid, a review deferred not
  decided, an application filed and ungranted, no closure letter,
  no signed consent on file.
- `Resolved` **holds** where the exposure persists by nature — a
  licensor whose consent is always needed, a contract right that
  was always there, a filing that predates the deal. A right
  existing is not a right exercised; the exposure's own text never
  voids its own status. Take the label; put the proving document
  on the endorsement list; let what remains move the price.
- `Unresolved`, `In-Progress`, blank — **open**.

Declare the count plainly under the schedule — "two blocking
exposures stand open" — it signs or refers the risk.

A schedule filing nothing in the blocking band has a count of
zero by construction — the risk writes proceed, and the memo says
it aloud: nothing on this schedule is a deal-breaker. Exposures
that merely read hard — pending claims with injunctive potential,
regulator applications awaiting decisions, retention metrics below
par — stay inside their filed band: flagged, priced into terms,
never promoted into the count. The flag travels with the row; the
label stays; the count reads only what was filed.

## The book — what the premium actually covers

The submission's headline figure is the seller's base, not the
buyer's. Rebuild it, deduction by deduction, each with its source:

- strip one-time revenue — a single project, a bulk sale;
- strip revenue the policy cannot cover — grants, licences or
  contracts ending on a change of control;
- strip revenue already lost — the narrative's own post-loss
  run-rate where it gives one;
- keep, named, revenue merely exposed — a renewal approaching, a
  customer who may leave. Exposed stays in the book and moves the
  placement and the endorsements;
- strip projections that need pilots or prospects to convert.

Add-backs stand only when plainly non-recurring and evidenced;
"one-off" costs that recur annually are operating costs. Where the
coverable earnings base is negative, earnings multiples do not
apply — carry the revenue base and say why. Show the bridge:
reported figure, each deduction, the coverable base.

## The market — which comparables earn a place

Every quoted comp is placed **in** or **out** with its reason: a
control-premium acquisition quoted among trading prices; a row
reading NM on the multiple in use; an outlier whose gap to its
nearest neighbour exceeds the spread of the rest. A comp that
survives its exclusion test stays — its distance becomes a
positioning input, not a cut.

Position the target on growth, margin, retention and scale among
the kept set; a weakness the kept peers do not share pushes low.
Where the artifacts warn the set is not comparable (a revenue mix
never broken out), say the confidence cost in one sentence.

Quote the range as two products anyone can verify, splitting the
multiple: `3.7x × $20.0M = 3 × $20.0M + 0.7 × $20.0M = $60.0M +
$14.0M = $74.0M`. Multiples price the enterprise; the buyer's
equity is that less net debt — show the debt bridge in one line,
or say the narrative gives none. Then state the ask as an implied
multiple of the coverable base with the gap in money and percent;
where no ask is stated the range is the guidance, not a gap.

## The decision

- **Proceed** — no blocking exposure stands open. Say it plainly:
  nothing on the schedule is a deal-breaker. Open sub-blocking
  exposures ride as endorsements and price marks; they never
  reopen a written risk. An ask above range caps the price at its
  top — an endorsement, not a declination.
- **Request more data** — blocking exposures stand open and a
  named document settles each. Referral is an instruction, not a
  stall: the record, its issuer, its due date, and which way each
  answer moves the decision.
- **Pass** — an incurable bar stands per the first-pass screen:
  a refusal on record and unconditional; a term or rule barring
  the transfer itself, including a material share of the priced
  book; a priced right already lapsed; a funding stream, licence
  or grant whose own terms prohibit transfer to this buyer class.
  A fifth sits at the desk: a documented termination-for-
  convenience — walk without cause on short notice — over ~40%+
  of the priced book through a channel the timeline cannot
  replace, paired with a priced asset the file cannot show owned:
  a base that can leave after the premium is paid.
  Ordering: a bar standing makes open exposures beside it moot —
  priced or waived, never a referral waiting on the bar.
  Against the bars: a signature nobody has requested is a
  referral, not a refusal; an approval pending on someone else's
  calendar is a referral however slow it looks — only a record
  that cannot exist declines; revenue merely at risk is a
  placement input, never a declination; an ask above range is a
  ceiling, never a pass.

## The endorsements

Match the instrument to the exposure, one per numbered line:

- a gating document the seller can obtain → condition precedent:
  no document, no signing;
- a bounded, known liability → specific indemnity, escrow-backed
  where the artifacts size it;
- revenue contingent on an outside event → contingent
  consideration: the book pays as it survives;
- a quantified cash need the plan omits → price reduction;
- people the book depends on → retention and non-compete;
- sub-blocking exposures → covenants, holdback or 100-day terms —
  never conditions precedent.

Every endorsement names its exposure, the evidence that satisfies
it, its timing and the consequence if it fails. Size nothing the
artifacts do not size. Information requests go last.

## The memo as issued

Only the finished memo. Line one: `**Recommendation: <decision>**
— <the exposure that decides it>; <supported range vs the ask>`.

1. `## 1. Thesis` (≤150 words) — the case for the book and its
   primary exposure, which must be a row the schedule carries.
2. `## 2. Risk table` — every row: exposure | tier filed | status
   labelled | status on the evidence | consequence (blocks the
   decision / moves the price / terms / post-close); open-blocking
   count in words underneath.
3. `## 3. Valuation` (≤300 words + tables) — the base bridge, the
   comp placement with reasons, the two range products, the debt
   bridge, the implied ask and gap.
4. `## 4. Recommendation` (≤140 words) — the decision, the survey
   result behind it, what changes it. Equals line one.
5. `## 5. Conditions` — numbered endorsements, ≤45 words each.

Write like the covering note on a well-priced slip: precise,
book-specific, every claim pinned to a row or figure.
Never reuse one sentence frame across exposures. A complete memo
inside the bounds outscores a longer one truncated.
