# Slip margins — what senior underwriters write in the edge

Collected marginalia. Not part of the cover.

- "Show me the document that closes it, or show me the referral."
- A label resolved and a thing resolved are different files;
  price the second.
- The book is what transfers. Everything else is the seller's
  brochure.
- Never decline what an endorsement covers. Never endorse what
  the schedule already declined.
- A walk clause over half the book is not an exposure; it is the
  book, conditionally.
- Comp tables are arguments, not averages. Every row kept or cut
  gets its reason in the margin.
- The ask is a multiple waiting to be divided. Divide it before
  it flatters anyone.
- Bounded fine, bounded window, quantified estimate — that is an
  indemnity asking a price, not a bar asking a vote.
- If the slip says proceed, say why plainly. A clean risk
  underwritten apologetically is still a clean risk.
- Write the range so the actuary can recompute it. Splits or it
  did not happen.
- Referrals are instructions: the record, the issuer, the date,
  the answer that moves it. Anything less is a stall.
