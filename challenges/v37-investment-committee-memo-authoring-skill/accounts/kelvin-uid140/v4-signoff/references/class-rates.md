# Class rates — the desk's standing guidance

How this desk prices recurring deal hazards. Rates are posture,
not law; each slip still stands on its own file.

| Hazard class | Posture | Why |
|---|---|---|
| signable paper missing | closing condition, never a decline | a signature is the cheapest instrument in the market |
| bounded fine / estimate | indemnity or escrow sized to the figure | it is already a number; make it someone else's number |
| event-bound revenue | contingency — pay only if it arrives | the seller believes the projection more than we do |
| quantified shortfall | straight price item | the math is done; just move it to their side |
| single key person | retention tied to dates | people are not indemnifiable, only paid to stay |
| at-will right over a material share | referral with a deadline, or walk | the counterparty holds a lever they can pull on day 91 |
| unconditional refusal from the source | decline | there is no instrument; instruments need a counterparty |
| term barring our buyer class | decline on that revenue share | you cannot underwrite what cannot transfer to the insured |
| litigation below the gate tier | priced, watched, never a gate | courts are slow; referrals are slower; premiums are fast |
| grant / subsidy non-transferable | strike from the base, then re-price | the money was never ours; quoting it flatters the ask |
| personal contract (key client) | survival confirmation or price cut | the revenue belongs to the relationship, not the entity |
| missing filing / approval pending | precedent with deadline and fail-state | bureaucracies answer to calendars, not to concern |
