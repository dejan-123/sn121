# Ledger cases — invented deals, form only

One full posting, then the three remaining call shapes. None of
these facts are yours.

## Case A — full posting (request more data)

# IC memo: Brightstack Field Services
**Recommendation: request more data** — row 1 (master service
agreement novation, Must-Address, Unresolved) posts open and
document-curable; the $47M ask posts inside the $44.6–$55.4M range.
**Open postings**: one open must-clear row; row 4 filed Resolved
while the narrative says the insurer has not bound cover; no debt
schedule provided.

## 1. Deal thesis (≤120 words)
Brightstack runs utility-line maintenance crews on multi-year
municipal contracts: $16.2M LTM revenue, 79% contracted, 41% gross
margin, EBITDA $2.1M. The book prices the contract base; the anchor
MSA (44% of revenue) requires the counterparty's novation consent
and none is on file. Primary risk: row 1's missing consent.

## 2. Risk ledger
| # | Finding | Tier | Status | Posting | Deciding fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anchor MSA requires novation consent | Must-Address | Unresolved | gates | 44% of revenue; no consent letter in file |
| 2 | CDL-driver shortage in two regions | Manageable | Unresolved | open | Hiring plan stated; quantified shortfall absent |
| 3 | Equipment fleet avg age 11 years | Manageable | Resolved | stands | Capex schedule verified in narrative |
| 4 | Pollution liability cover renewed | Manageable | Resolved | open | Narrative: insurer has not bound the renewal |
| 5 | Owner-operator contracts, 60-day exit | Informational | Unresolved | noted | Market-standard terms |

Open must-clear: 1. Flag: row 4 posts open — filed Resolved but the
narrative shows the renewal unbound.

## 3. Defended valuation (≤260 words + comp table)
Base $16.2M LTM revenue — the MSA is at risk, not lost, so it posts
in full and names the downside. EBITDA positive; the comp set prices
EV/EBITDA, which leads.

| Comparable | EV/EBITDA | Growth | In/Out | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GridCrew Services | 8.4x | 12% | In | Trading peer |
| LineWorks Group | 7.6x | 8% | In | Trading peer |
| PoleStar (acquired) | 11.2x | 15% | Out | Control premium at close |
| UtilServe Co. | 9.0x | 17% | In | Trading peer |

Included median 8.4x. Brightstack grows 9% vs the 12% median → one
step down; top customer at 44% > 20% → one step down.
`8.4x − 0.5x concentration − 0.5x growth = 7.4x`… steps post at
0.25x revenue / 1.0x EBITDA: `8.4x − 1.0x − 1.0x = 6.4x` low, 7.4x
high. 6.4x × $2.1M = $13.4M …

(The range products, the range as the last line, and the ask's
implied multiple follow the same postings.)

## 4. Settlement and check (≤120 words)
**Request more data.** Row 1 is an open must-clear posting curable
by a document: the executed novation. Row 4's filed Resolved does
not hold — the binder is unsigned; it posts open and takes a
pre-signing deliverable. Price is not the problem: $47M posts inside
the defended range. Checks: primary risk = row 1, first must-clear
posting; range rebuilt above; every figure identical in all
sections.

## 5. Open postings
1. Row 1: executed novation consent before signing — failing it,
   walk or re-price excluding that contract's revenue.
2. Row 4: bound insurance certificate before signing; lapsed cover
   re-prices the liability.
3. Row 2: regional hiring pipeline report at close; quantified
   shortfall posts to the capex reserve [to confirm].
4. Row 5: monitor — 60-day exit terms post-close.

## Case E — the conditional refusal (near miss, not pass)

Rows: Must-Address `Unresolved` — the target's bonding carrier
"has indicated it may not renew if the incoming owner lacks the
trade certification"; plus routine open consents. §4 posts:

> **Request more data.** Row 1 posts a position, not a refusal: the
> exit hangs on the buyer's certification status — a fact the
> artifacts never post — and the bonding seat is fillable by any
> qualified carrier. The asks: the buyer's status, and a successor
> carrier's commitment. The remaining consents are ordinary
> documents. Nothing yet posts incurable; the file declines only
> when no qualified provider will take the seat. Checks: …

The trap: 'indicated it may not renew' reads like a refusal but
posts nothing settled — the condition's trigger is an unasked fact.

## Case F — the statutory clock (this one IS the pass)

Rows: Must-Address `In-Progress` — the platform's core algorithm
sits under a utility patent expiring by statute in 15 months; two
competitors have continuation filings that may control the
successor landscape. Beside it: Must-Address `Unresolved` —
a data-residency certification lapsed, curable by re-filing. §4
posts:

> **Pass.** Row 1's right ends on a date no party can move — no
> filing, consent or indemnity extends a statutory term, and the
> successor landscape is already being contested by others. The
> thesis prices the protection that right affords; the base loses
> it whether or not the deal signs. Time is not a cure: an expiry
> months out is as incurable as one tomorrow. Row 2 is curable —
> but a deal does not proceed to fix the fixable while the premise
> lapses. The file reopens only if the statute or the landscape
> changes. Checks: …

Contrast with Case E: there the seat could be refilled — a
provider exists. Here the thing itself expires — nothing replaces
a lapsed right.

## The reposting table and the instruments the ledger knows

Seller or narrative entry → the ledger's reposting:

| They post | The ledger reposts |
|---|---|
| "gross revenue $14.7M" | "net revenue $8.9M — the figure the comps price" |
| "adjusted EBITDA +$3.1M" | "EBITDA -$1.2M as posted; non-plain add-backs rejected" |
| "94% recurring" | a composition note, not a reposting ground |
| "pipeline growing 40%" | unposted — growth priced is growth delivered |
| "ask $62M" | "ask $62M = implied multiple on the posted base" |

Instruments the ledger settles with: condition precedent, closing
deliverable, indemnity, escrow or holdback sized to a quantified
exposure, contingent consideration, retention or non-compete, price
reduction to a defended ceiling, long-stop date only where the
artifacts name a date.
