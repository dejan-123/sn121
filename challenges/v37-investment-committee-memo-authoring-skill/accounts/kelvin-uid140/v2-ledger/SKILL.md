# Ledger policy — every deal posts by the same rules

Three artifacts arrive as opening entries, filed by different hands
at different times. A memo that inherits their balances without
posting the differences has reconciled nothing. Every entry keeps
the label it was filed under — the ledger never re-tiers an entry,
never rewrites a status; where the record disagrees with the filing,
the standing cell carries the disagreement next to the filed word,
in view.

Every figure posts from the inputs or is worked from them in view.
What the inputs do not contain posts as a named gap — never assumed,
never estimated in the seller's favour. `[to confirm: …]` is a valid
posting.

## The standing vocabulary — closed set

Each row posts exactly one standing beside its filed tier. No fifth
standing exists; `proceed`, `request more data` and `pass` are memo
standings, never row standings.

| Standing | When it posts |
|---|---|
| `breach` | an open posting filed at the gating tier (Must-Address/Critical): filed open, or Resolved undone by another artifact or the row's own words — a live re-bid, a review deferred not closed, a consent the text never documents, a claim the row calls pending. The filed tier decides; a Manageable posting that looks material is flagged unsettled with the reason, never re-tiered. Unsettles the ledger. |
| `unsettled` | a named posting closes it; the step posts in the cell, and under a proceed it stays visible there |
| `carried` | verified or uncontradicted, stands as filed — including a Resolved whose exposure persists by nature (a consent the licence will need, a right to cancel): the label holds while the proving document posts as a closing deliverable |
| `struck` | informational or outside the decision; no settlement spent |

Every input row posts — none dropped, none merged; rows sharing one
root cause keep their own lines and cross-reference ("compounds row
N"). A finding the record reveals but the table omits posts its own
line, grounded in the artifacts' words. An analyst note flagging a
contradiction posts as its own evidence, never smoothed.

## Entry 1 — status reconciliation

Filed statuses are opening entries to be tested against the record:

- `Resolved` undone by another artifact or the row's own words posts
  unsettled or breach — the cell names the fact that broke the
  label.
- `Resolved` beside an exposure that persists by nature posts
  carried — the label holds; the proving document posts as a closing
  deliverable.
- A blank or missing status posts unsettled, and the memo says the
  cell was empty.
- What reconciles stands — a serious risk honestly filed posts with
  its mitigation; posting `Resolved` is not required for restraint.

## Entry 2 — the base posting

The seller's revenue and EBITDA are opening entries to be restated,
not balances to carry. Post the adjusted base: strip revenue that
completed and will not recur; exclude revenue that does not transfer
at close — grants a for-profit cannot hold, contracts that terminate,
customers already gone; post the net figure where the industry's
metric is net and say why gross is not the base. Restate growth on
the adjusted base. Where EBITDA is negative on the economics that
transfer, or the comps are pre-profitability, post EV/EBITDA as not
meaningful and carry EV/Revenue. Add-backs post only when genuinely
non-recurring — evergreen items relabeled are rejected in words,
and a rejected add-back is never subtracted twice; an accepted one
lifts the base. Growth contingent on unsigned deals — prospects in
study, unconverted pilots — posts as a claim: priced growth is
realized growth. Each figure is computed once and reused unchanged;
a figure posted twice drifts.

## Entry 3 — the comp posting

Every comparable posts included or excluded with its reason: a
control or sponsor premium beside trading peers; growth or margin
so far from the target it prices another business; an NM multiple
on the metric in use. A limitation the comp set's own note declares
posts into the memo, not around it. The target posts against the
included median in words with a direction — size, margin, growth.

## Entry 4 — the range and the ask

Two products only: an included comp's own multiple × base = value,
written in $M to one decimal. The range is those two products, the
valuation's last line. The ask posts as one implied multiple on the
priced base — once per base where the base restates (gross-to-net,
blended-to-recurring): the two postings are the comparison, not new
arithmetic. These are the ledger's only calculations. Where net
debt or cash is material, the bridge posts in one line — EV less
net debt. With no ask stated, the range is bid guidance and the
absent ask posts as a finding.

## Entry 5 — settlement

The recommendation posts `proceed`, `request more data` or `pass`,
naming the deciding postings and why the other two standings do not
fit.

- `proceed` — no breach posting stands. An unsettled manageable
  posting never unbalances the books: its named settlement posts
  among the open postings. Refusing to settle a clean ledger is as
  false an entry as settling over a breach.
- `request more data` — each breach posting has a named closing
  entry: a consent, a closure letter, an executed renewal, a
  clearance, an audited figure — the record and the party that posts
  it. Contract and regulatory lapses are asks; a named record
  settles each.
- `pass` — a breach posting the ledger already shows broken: a
  counterparty on record refusing, a term or rule that bars the
  transfer itself — the asset cannot legally move to the buyer;
  revenue that may not survive post-close is a base question,
  never a bar — or the only settling record cannot be
  posted on the deal's own timeline — a live adjudication on what
  the base prices, an expired right that cannot be re-papered; not
  a record that merely sits on another party's calendar, because a
  pending approval is askable however slow it looks. A
  bar means a rule already on the books forbids the transfer; a
  permission still pending is an unsettled posting, not a bar.
  Only a must-address posting carries the books to pass; a
  manageable posting always takes its settlement instead. A pass
  names the postings that would reopen the books.

An ask posted above the range is a price condition, never by itself
a pass.

## Entry 6 — open postings

One posting per unsettled row — one-to-one, numbered, citing its
row. Each names its settlement instrument, the record, the posting
party and the timing (before LOI, before signing, at close,
post-close):

- a closing deliverable for a document the deciding party can post;
- a specific indemnity or escrow for a bounded liability;
- contingent consideration for revenue whose survival waits on an
  event the seller cannot settle before close;
- a price reduction for a quantified, unfunded need;
- retention or non-compete for people the balance walks out with.

Timing posts from those four marks — never an invented period,
percentage, escrow size or earnout. A posting never predicts what
the record will say. The section exists under every standing —
under proceed it carries the settlements named in the cells; zero
open postings is a complete statement, not a missing section.

## Output contract — the reconciliation

First line: the standing word alone, the deciding posting, the ask
or its absence — the entry the committee reads before anything is
cut. Then the five sections, in this order:

1. `## 1. Ledger context` — ≤150 words: what is being acquired, why
   the restated base justifies owning it, the deciding posting named
   in its row's words.
2. `## 2. Postings` — every finding in input order: #, finding, tier
   and status as filed, standing cell ≤40 words stating the deciding
   fact. `Tally: <n breach / n unsettled / n carried / n struck>`
   beneath, re-footing to the row count exactly.
3. `## 3. Valuation ledger` — ≤300 words plus comp posting: adjusted
   base, include/exclude postings, directional adjustments, the
   ask's implied multiple, the two products, the range as the last
   line.
4. `## 4. Recommendation` — ≤140 words: the standing as first
   words, the deciding postings, why the other two standings do not
   fit.
5. `## 5. Open postings` — one posting per unsettled row, ≤45 words
   each, instrument named.

Cut prose before postings, arithmetic, recommendation or the open
postings — the tail is what a long memo loses. Every entry is
non-interchangeable: a line that could post under another finding
gets the deciding words instead.
