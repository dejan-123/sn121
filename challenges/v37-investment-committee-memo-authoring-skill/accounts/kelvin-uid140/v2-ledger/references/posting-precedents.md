# Posting precedents — journal entries that carry the rulings

Precedent entries posted in earlier files. When a row sits between
rules, find its entry here and post the same way. Format: the
situation posts on the left, the entry that settles it on the right.

## Status reconciliation entries

- `Resolved` beside a live re-bid → posts **unsettled/breach**; the
  entry names the running re-bid as the fact that broke the label.
- `Resolved` beside an undocumented consent → posts unsettled; the
  entry names the missing signature, not the missing goodwill.
- `Resolved` beside a deferred review → posts unsettled; a deferral
  is a date change, not a closure.
- `Resolved` beside an exposure that persists by nature — a consent
  a licence will always need, a customer's right to cancel → posts
  **carried**; the label holds and the proving document posts as a
  closing deliverable.
- Blank status → posts unsettled; the memo notes the cell was
  empty.
- Appended analyst note flagging a table-versus-narrative
  contradiction → posts as its own evidence line; the memo names
  which side it trusted.

## Base-posting entries

- Completed one-time revenue inside the quoted base → posts out
  before any multiple is quoted.
- Revenue that does not transfer — a grant a for-profit cannot
  hold, a contract that terminates, a customer already gone → posts
  out; the surviving base is what prices.
- An industry that prices on net → the net figure is the base;
  gross posts as the rejected denominator with its reason.
- A churn-out cohort shrinking the run-rate → the run-rate posts as
  a second base; the ask's implied multiple posts on each.
- EBITDA-negative base or pre-profit comps → EV/EBITDA posts not
  meaningful; EV/Revenue carries.
- A recurring item dressed as an add-back → rejected in words; a
  rejected add-back is never subtracted twice.
- Growth quoted off unsigned prospects → posts as a claim; realized
  growth prices.

## Comp-posting entries

- A comp acquired at a control or sponsor premium → excluded in the
  note's own words.
- A comp whose growth or margin prices a different business →
  excluded, direction named.
- An NM multiple on the metric in use → excluded.
- A set note declaring a limit → the limit posts into the memo.

## Settlement entries

- Every must-address settled; a manageable posting still unsettled
  → **proceed**; the open posting carries its named settlement.
- Open must-address postings, each answerable by a record →
  **request more data**; the record and the posting party are
  named.
- A counterparty on record refusing → **pass**.
- A term or rule that bars the transfer itself → pass; the
  surviving base posts and the arithmetic shows.
- The only settling record cannot exist on the deal's timeline —
  a live adjudication on the priced asset, an expired right →
  pass; the memo names the postings that would reopen the books.
- A permission still pending → an unsettled posting, never a bar.
- An ask above the range → a price condition; posted against the
  band, never declined on price alone.
- A clean ledger → proceed; refusing to settle a balanced book is
  as false an entry as settling over a breach.

## Instrument entries

- A document a named party can post → closing deliverable.
- A bounded liability → specific indemnity or escrow.
- Revenue whose survival waits on a post-close event → contingent
  consideration.
- A quantified, unfunded need → price reduction.
- People the balance walks out with → retention or non-compete.

Timing posts from four marks only — before LOI, before signing, at
close, post-close. Nothing posts an invented period, percentage,
escrow size or earnout.
