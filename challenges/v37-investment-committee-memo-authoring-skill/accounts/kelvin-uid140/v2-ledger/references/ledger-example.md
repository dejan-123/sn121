# Worked example — a ledger reconciliation

A complete posting on a fictional deal. The entry order is the
deliverable: standings first, base second, comps third, the range,
the settlement, the open postings. Entities and figures are
invented; the posting pattern is what transfers.

## The input (abridged for illustration)

Risk table filed:

| # | Finding | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hospital-group master service agreement carries 38% of route revenue; the group holds an unexercised renewal option through December | Must-Address | Unresolved |
| 2 | Boiler emissions permit renewal filed complete; narrative shows the application submitted, permit not yet issued | Manageable | Resolved |
| 3 | Route drivers' union CBA expires in 8 months; negotiations not opened | Manageable | Unresolved |
| 4 | Depot lease runs seven more years at below-market rent | Informational | Resolved |
| 5 | Delivery fleet 60% owned, 40% under operating leases | Informational | Resolved |

Narrative: LTM revenue $21.4M includes a completed $1.8M emergency
disaster-relief contract; adjusted base $19.6M. EBITDA $2.4M on the
adjusted base (the relief contract ran at breakeven). Route revenue
recurring at 91% with 9% organic growth. Ask $22M. Comps with a
banker note: LinenWorks 8.1x, RouteTextile 6.2x, UniformPro 7.4x
EV/EBITDA, MetroClean 11.8x "taken out by a roll-up platform at a
consolidation premium."

## The memo

**request more data** — the anchor contract's unexercised option
posts open; ask $22M as stated.

## 1. Ledger context

Grindle Industrial Laundry is a route-density business: recurring
linen contracts, 91% recurring revenue, positive $2.4M EBITDA on the
adjusted $19.6M base — the completed $1.8M relief contract stripped
as non-recurring. The deciding posting is the hospital-group
agreement carrying 38% of route revenue: the renewal option sits
unexercised in the customer's hands, filed Unresolved and honestly
so — the ledger posts it as the breach that gates a clean
settlement. The balance sheet posts clean; the route economics post
real.

## 2. Postings

| # | Finding | Filed | Standing | Tier carried |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hospital-group MSA, 38% of revenue, unexercised option | Unresolved | **breach** — the deciding signature belongs to the customer; an executed renewal posts the books closed | Must-Address |
| 2 | Boiler permit renewal filed Resolved | Resolved | **unsettled** — the narrative dates an application, not an issued permit; submitted is not issued. The issued permit settles it | Manageable |
| 3 | Drivers' CBA expires in 8 months | Unresolved | **unsettled** — dated event with runway; opened negotiations and a signed term sheet carry it | Manageable |
| 4 | Depot lease, 7 years, below-market | Resolved | **carried** — uncontradicted and favourable; stands as filed | Informational |
| 5 | Fleet 60/40 owned-leased | Resolved | **struck** — a balance-structure fact, outside the decision | Informational |

**Tally:** 1 breach / 2 unsettled / 1 carried / 1 struck

## 3. Valuation ledger

Base posting: $19.6M revenue after the completed $1.8M relief
contract posts out; EBITDA of $2.4M stands on the adjusted base, so
EV/EBITDA is meaningful here — the first time this week the ledger
can say so. Growth posts at the realized 9%, not the 14% the
seller projects off unsigned hospital prospects.

Comp posting: MetroClean is excluded — the banker note itself calls
the 11.8x a consolidation premium paid by a roll-up; a platform's
transaction price is not a trading multiple. Included: RouteTextile
6.2x, UniformPro 7.4x, LinenWorks 8.1x EV/EBITDA — a 6.2x–8.1x band,
7.4x median. Grindle's route density and EBITDA margin post at the
set's strong end; no further adjustment posts.

Arithmetic: 6.2x × $2.4M = $14.9M; 8.1x × $2.4M = $19.4M. The
justified range posts $14.9M–$19.4M, defensible band ~$17M–$19M.

Ask posting: $22M implies 9.2x on the posted base — above the band's
top. The gap posts as a price condition, not a broken premise.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. One breach posting stands: the anchor contract's
unexercised renewal option, settled by the executed renewal the
hospital group can post. Proceed cannot post over a breach; pass
does not post because the option is a signature, not a refusal —
the customer has declined nothing and the option runs through
December inside the deal's own timeline. The ask at 9.2x posts
repriced against the band as the negotiation the request carries.

## 5. Open postings

1. Hospital-group renewal option exercised in writing before
   signing — closing deliverable; unexercised is not renewable on
   the buyer's word.
2. Boiler emissions permit issued before close — closing
   deliverable; an application is a filing, not a permit.
3. CBA negotiations opened with a signed bargaining timetable
   before close — closing deliverable; a strike contingency is
   priced, not assumed away.
4. Fleet lease schedules delivered with expirations and renewal
   terms — standing diligence item.
5. [to confirm: workers' comp claim frequency across the route
   fleet, trailing 36 months — the artifacts do not state it.]

Reconciliation — a real route business priced $3M above the band on
one signature that has not posted. The ledger asks for the renewal,
posts the permit as a deliverable, and pays for the $19.6M base.

---

## When the books balance — the settled shape

Had the option posted exercised and the permit issued, the same
skeleton compresses to:

**proceed** — no breach posting stands; the ask posts inside the
band at 8.1x adjusted EBITDA.

## 5. Open postings

None gating. Standing items only: the CBA timetable tracked on its
negotiation calendar, the fleet lease schedules filed.

Postings, tally and valuation run identically either way — the
recommendation reads the ledger, it does not write it.

---

## When the ledger cannot close — the pass posting

Worked note for the structural case. If the anchor contract were a
state corrections laundry agreement that by statute cannot be
assigned or subcontracted on change of control, the posting is not
unsettled — it is voidable revenue: a rule on the books bars the
transfer itself. The base posts net of the 38% cohort, the range
recomputes on what survives, and where the ask still prices the
dead revenue the settlement is a pass that names the statute and
shows the arithmetic. A posting is not offered where the deciding
record cannot exist on the deal's timeline.
