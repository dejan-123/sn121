# Minuted resolution - invented example

Marchbank Adhesives, a maker of industrial bonding agents. Register: two Critical rows - a plant-emissions permit renewal filed Resolved, and the founding chemist's patent licence filed Resolved. Narrative notes the permit authority has not yet issued the renewed certificate and the licensor's change-of-control signature page is "with the founder". Revenue 21.8M including 4.6M from one dominant distributor contract terminable on 120 days' notice. No debt or cash supplied. Ask 59M. Peers: three specialty chemical firms at 2.4-2.9x revenue; one diversified group at 4.5x (control deal).

## What the record establishes

Permit renewal: filed Resolved, but the renewed certificate is absent and the authority's issue is still pending - the claimed closure fails on the record: **open**.

Patent licence: filed Resolved; the signature page is in circulation, not in the packet - nothing in the record contradicts the closure, so the entry stands and the signed page is minuted as a pre-signing deliverable.

Open gating rows: 1 - the permit; the licence consent proceeds as a named condition.

**Recommendation: request more data** - the renewed emissions certificate is outstanding on a Critical row; the licensor's signed consent is required before signing; EV 46-55M (equity bridge unavailable - no debt or cash supplied).

## 1. Deal thesis

Marchbank sells formulated bonding agents to converters through a distribution network. The durable base is roughly 17.2M of revenue outside the dominant terminable contract - though the full 21.8M remains in the base with that dependence quantified below. The deciding items are the two Critical closures awaiting their instruments.

## 2. Tiered risk table

| Finding | Filed tier | Filed status | Standing on the record | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emissions permit renewal | Critical | Resolved | Open - renewed certificate not issued | Deliverable before signing |
| Founder patent licence consent | Critical | Resolved | Stands - consent page in circulation | Signed page minuted before signing |
| Distributor contract, 21% of revenue, terminable at will | Manageable | Open | Right subsists | Placed down the range; continuation letter sought |
| Warehouse flood repairs | Informational | Resolved | Works certified complete | None |

Open gating rows: 2.

## 3. Valuation

Base: 21.8M revenue; no stated one-time items. The dominant distributor's share - 21% of revenue under a 120-day termination right - is recorded and reflected in placement rather than removed.

Comparables: the three specialty firms (2.4x, 2.6x, 2.9x) admitted; the diversified group at 4.5x excluded as a control transaction.

Placement: concentration and permit exposure pull below the admitted midpoint - 2.1x low, 2.5x high.

2.1 x 21.8M = 45.8M enterprise value
2.5 x 21.8M = 54.5M enterprise value

EV range 45.8-54.5M. No debt or cash figures were supplied; the equity bridge is therefore unminuted and the packet should be asked for both before the price vote. The 59M ask implies 2.7x - above the defended range.

## 4. Recommendation

Request more data. The meeting authorizes continued work, not signing: two named instruments must enter the record - the renewed emissions certificate and the founder's signed consent. Price authority stands at 45.8-54.5M enterprise value. Should either instrument fail to materialize, or the distributor exercise its termination right meanwhile, the resolution reopens.

## 5. Conditions and next actions

1. Emissions permit - plant director obtains the renewed certificate from the authority before signing; denial ends the resolution.
2. Patent consent - general counsel enters the founder's signed change-of-control page before signing; refusal ends the resolution.
3. Distributor - commercial director requests a continuation-intent letter; an adverse reply moves placement to the range floor.
4. Balance sheet - finance lead requests closing debt and cash so the equity bridge can be minuted.

---

## Recorded alternate - instruments entered

Were the certificate and consent page both in the record, the tally falls to zero and the resolution reads `proceed` on the same valuation, the distributor letter remaining a condition.

## Recorded alternate - refusal minuted

Were the founder's refusal unconditional and entered - "no consent will be given to any transfer" - no instrument cures it and the resolution reads `pass`.
