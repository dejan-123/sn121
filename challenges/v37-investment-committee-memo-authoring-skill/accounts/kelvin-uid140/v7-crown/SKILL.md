# The meeting resolves only what the papers establish

Committee records read as though every assertion in the packet were already fact. This skill writes the resolution the way a careful chair would: first settle what the record shows, then resolve. Six standing orders govern every memo; `references/marchbank-resolution.md` minutes one full meeting on invented facts with two alternate outcomes recorded. Nothing in it supplies facts for a real transaction.

## 1. The chair counts the blocking items

A finding stands between the committee and signing only when filed at a tier the register itself treats as blocking - Must-Address, Critical or the source's own equivalent. Severity of wording is not the test; the filed tier is. A register topped by Manageable or Informational rows presents nothing that blocks - pending or In-Progress lower-tier items are tabled as conditions, never as blockers: the resolution is to proceed, with each open lower-tier item minuted as a condition or protection. Suspected under-tiering is noted for the record beside the row; the count is not altered.

## 2. The record tests every claimed resolution

For each blocking-tier finding, the filed status is preserved in the table and checked against the remainder of the packet. A packet statement that the closing act remains undone defeats the Resolved entry - the conflict is cited verbatim beside it. An exposure recited inside the row - a rival's earlier filing, a subsisting cancellation right, a consent the licence has always demanded - is what the claimed resolution addressed; where the record does not contradict the closure, that exposure is minuted as a pre-signing deliverable, not as a reopened item. **Where the papers neither prove nor contradict a filed closure, the entry stands and the missing instrument is minuted as a pre-signing deliverable** - absent evidence is tabled for production, not reopened. Unresolved and In-Progress entries stand open.

The tally is entered as `N open gating rows`: blocking-tier and open on this test. N=0 obliges proceed - no lower-tier entry may be minuted as the deciding finding.

## 3. The resolution follows the tally

- **proceed** - no open blocking entry; all residual items carried as named conditions, price protections or monitoring.
- **request more data** - open entries each curable by a defined, checkable paper: executed deed, signed consent, regulatory outcome, granted licence, awarded contract. Where a single signature or consent is the sole outstanding item before signing, the resolution may note approval conditional on its arrival - the minuted verdict remains one of the three formal words, chosen by whether any blocking row stands open.
- **pass** - the record itself has decided against: unconditional refusal minuted, express bar on the priced asset, terminal exclusive right with no successor in the record.

Any adverse clause carrying `if`, `unless`, `provided` or `would` is a question tabled, not a refusal recorded: the meeting asks whether this buyer holds the qualification or whether a qualified substitute exists, and never assumes the buyer fails it. An asking price above the supported range is tabled for negotiation, not treated as a defect.

## 4. The valuation annex prices what transfers

The base is restated from the narrative: stated one-time revenue, departed customers, gross pass-through volume and recurring items disguised as add-backs all come out. Revenue persisting under a cancellation right or inside a dominant account remains in the base, but the minutes record the share dependent on it and carry that share into placement or protection. Where earnings are negative or unsupported, the annex values on revenue and says why.

Each quoted comparable is admitted or excluded by stated rule - trading rows matching the chosen metric; control transactions and NM rows excluded with reasons. The target is placed against the admitted set on growth, margin, retention and scale. Two products are entered verbatim:

`low multiple x corrected base = low enterprise value`
`high multiple x corrected base = high enterprise value`

Those products are the range wherever the memo states it. **Where the papers supply debt and cash, the equity bridge must be minuted** - EV less net debt, arithmetic shown - because the ask is judged on equity and an unminuted bridge leaves it untested. Any ask is restated on the corrected base and its gap recorded; absent a stated ask, none is invented. No figure, date, holdback or threshold outside the packet may be entered.

## 5. Each outstanding item carries a named deliverable

A condition minuted without its paper is a wish, not a condition. Every item names the finding, the deliverable (executed deed, written consent, outcome letter, granted licence, assignment instrument), the owner by role, the deadline relative to signing, and the consequence on failure - the resolution lapses, the price is reset, or a protection sized only from recorded figures attaches.

## 6. The minutes issue a finished resolution

First line: `**Recommendation: <proceed | request more data | pass>** - <deciding finding>; <defended EV range, and equity range where debt and cash are supplied>.` Exactly five numbered sections follow:

1. `## 1. Deal thesis` - the asset acquired, the durable base, the lead finding also tabled (under 150 words).
2. `## 2. Tiered risk table` - every filed row in order: finding, filed tier, filed status, standing on the record, consequence; the open-blocking tally beneath.
3. `## 3. Valuation` - restated base, comparable admissions and exclusions, placement, the two products, the equity bridge when supplied, ask comparison (under 300 words plus table).
4. `## 4. Recommendation` - the first-line verdict restated; what the meeting now authorizes, what would reopen it; signing authority separate from price authority (under 140 words).
5. `## 5. Conditions and next actions` - numbered entries: finding, owner, deliverable, timing, consequence.

A finished resolution under 900 words: no placeholders, no invented particulars, no debate left on the record. The chair verifies before issuing - tally, both products, every status as filed, the lead finding's presence in the table, and line one agreeing with section 4.
