# The ledger sheet

The sheet is the first message of a benchmark reply: the working shown in plain text, in view, before any verdict is filed. It opens with the floor, every row's date set against it, the line printed above the table, every row's notes, the company's own offers and the rows by family; it takes the families one at a time in snapshot order; it closes with the pool's totals and the verdicts. Dockline, its IDs and its figures are invented - take the working, never the numbers.

## What decides a line

- **The floor is quoted, then applied.** The window's own words set the first month that counts; that month is in, the month before is out, and an undated row is out.
- **Kinds fail before families.** A contractor rate, a total or OTE figure with no split, a grant, an undated or unnamed survey, and spoken hearsay are out wherever they sit - they are dropped under their family and never weighed. A supplied component converts once, the sum shown with its direction. A foreign currency with no supplied rate is out on basis: nothing is converted on invented arithmetic.
- **One near miss is partial; two are out.** A different named metro, one stage off, a slash title joining the seat's work to another function, a volunteered range, and remote-no-city while the input leaves roles unanchored unnamed are each a near miss; a slash title that names the seat counts, and a range silent on its posting counts - silence is not voluntariness; only the row's own note or the over-table line makes a range volunteered. Any two together, or any out, drops the row. A partial corroborates by its lean - an earlier round usually pays less, a volunteered range runs high - and never anchors; a partial owed only to an unstated split also says what counting it would move.
- **The over-table line can classify a column.** Copied word for word, it tells every range silent on its posting that it volunteered.
- **Anchors never blend.** Offers alone, or range centres alone, and internals, partials and drops never join either.
- **The gray zone is settled by own evidence.** Inside 3% either way is competitive, past 5% the anchor decides. Between them on the lagging side, own offers within ~5% of the band's top or past it - or a decline citing pay - tip lagging; own offers lower in the band leave competitive; none, or the above-market side, leaves the anchor's reading. The deciding signal is named and the gap called close.
- **The sheet files the call.** The zone test runs on the gap line and the verdict is filed there; the memo copies it unchanged and relitigates nothing. A verdict that appears first in the memo is a defect.
- **Internals test, and inside the zone settle.** Own offers are compared with the verdict on the sheet - agree or trouble - and reach the memo only as corroboration and the named zone signal; no own-offer figure sits inside a verdict's arithmetic and no derived percentage beside a gap; decline reasons are hearsay, named as such.
- **Opposing partials mean wait.** Partial points pulling opposite directions are a reason to defer, not a figure to average.
