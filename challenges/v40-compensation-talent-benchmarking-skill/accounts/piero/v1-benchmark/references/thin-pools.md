# Thin pools

Read when any family ends the sheet with fewer than two `P` points. Thin pools are where benchmarks go wrong quietly: a single advertisement is allowed to carry a Low verdict, and nothing is allowed to carry more.

## The lone-range rule

A posted range that survives the funnel is a counted point, and a counted point anchors. A lone range reads Low and no higher - say it rests on one posting. Only `P=0` defers. A volunteered or slash-titled advertisement can never carry a verdict, because it is a partial, not a counted point.

## Low is a verdict, not a shrug

A family called on a lone range still gets its label, its worked gap, its falsifier and its named lean - the tier says how far to trust it, and the memo says it rests on one posting. Never inflate a thin call's confidence, and never let a thin pool borrow strength from internals: own offers corroborate, they never rescue.

## Deferred is a state, not an absence

A deferred family keeps its table row, its funnel and its paragraph - verdict `Deferred`, confidence not a tier. Say what came closest by ID, which way each partial leans and why none binds, and exactly what would settle it: kind, metro, stage, level. Two partials pulling opposite ways are a reason to wait; an open P1 or P2 role makes the wait worth a clause, not a call.

## The thin family's reads still run

- **Premium** stands where own accepted offers exist - their position against the band and the external anchor, never a derived percentage; "no own offers on record" is said as a named gap, never skipped silently.
- **Falsifier** is the deferred family's whole point: name the cheapest evidence that settles it first - kind, metro, stage, level - no price invented.
- **Spread and Decoy** run as usual; in a thin family the loudest failure is often the only figure a reader will remember, so its reason is said plainly.
