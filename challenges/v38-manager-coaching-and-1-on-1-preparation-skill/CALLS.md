## Suite-level plants (2nd pass — the recurring patterns)

1. **Manager-owes items — 4 of 8 scenarios** (S-003 benchmarking deck,
   S-009 self-review feedback, S-010 deck template, S-004 both Yuki
   items due). The brief serves the MANAGER — their own deliverables and
   slippage belong in the prep. Almost certainly a scored dimension.
2. **External-dependency status items — 3 of 8** (S-001 Finance access,
   S-005 observability team, S-008 Haskell compiler team). In each, the
   "status" item's real content is an upstream block the MANAGER must
   unstick — the recurring correct call is manager-owned escalation,
   not report-owned progress.
3. **Report's own submitted topic must appear** — explicit in S-001 and
   S-003 ("submitted agenda topic"), present in S-008 (promotion). It's
   the highest-salience item to the report; burying it fails judgment.
4. **Recognition: warranted in 7 of 8** — named public praise or hard
   numbers everywhere except S-007 (no signals at all). The ONLY
   absent-recognition scenario is the thin-input one; S-004's
   numbers-only evidence is the borderline.
5. **Sensitive routing — exactly 2** (S-004 family health, S-007
   salary). Both = named note + different venue, zero talking points.
6. **Reclassifies — 4 strong** (S-001 feedback→dependency, S-003
   coaching→single-incident, S-005 disengaged→blocked-engaged, S-008
   feedback/low→org-escalation/high) + 2 reframes (S-009 scope-flag
   timing, S-010 process-not-character). The manager's label is wrong
   or mis-weighted in 6 of 8 scenarios — classification-by-signals is
   the scored core skill.
