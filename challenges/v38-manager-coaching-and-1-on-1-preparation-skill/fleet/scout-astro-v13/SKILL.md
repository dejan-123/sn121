# Rules of the page

There is a quarter hour before the meeting. The first part belongs to the record, not the draft — the draft was written from memory, and memory recalls the subject but not the substance. The checking is written down where the manager can see it: reasoning printed is reasoning trusted.

## Reading rules

- **If** a row is open, overdue or NOT STARTED **→** it counts open.
- **If** a row is resolved **→** it stays closed unless something newer directly contradicts it; the old complaint never reopens it.
- **If** the manager owns an open row **→** it is preparation, collected before anything is asked of the report.
- **If** praise landed by name **→** note the exact words.
- **If** a number lives only in the manager's head **→** it does not exist; compute current versus plan from its operands, points never percent.

## Calling rules — each draft item, in the draft's own order

- **If** written praise exists — a note, a thanks, work named **→** **Recognition**: opens the meeting in the giver's own words, the figure attached.
- **If** another desk holds it — approval not given, dependency gone quiet **→** **Blocked**: the manager leaves carrying the move. Sharp messages after ignored chases are the hold-up talking, not a tone problem. And **if** the trail shows it wider than the draft admits — other teams held up, leadership already aware **→** say so; the item climbs whatever priority was written.
- **If** one behaviour shows on two or more separate dated occasions, or the report set the goal **→** **Coaching**: questions, never lectures. **If** the report already found the cause and tracks it **→** coach the step before it — spot it sooner — on the real figures.
- **If** the report themselves raised growth, a course, scope or review timing **→** **Career**: one sincere question, a keepable promise.
- **If** nothing needs repair, praise or settling **→** **Status**: last, squeezed to a line or two.
- **If** its room is elsewhere — pay, HR, a dispute a different team owns, next quarter **→** **Parked**: sent there with a line saying why. Personal matters stay "the personal matter", elaborated never.

## Correction rules

- **If** a tone complaint traces to another desk's hold-up **→** Blocked, not Coaching.
- **If** several messages concern one problem **→** one issue; Coaching wants separate dated occasions.
- **If** an impression has nothing underneath it **→** dropped, the signals named.
- **If** a slip was self-caught and fixed **→** a quick word, never a coaching slot.
- **If** praise has no quotable instance **→** no opener; a line above item one admits it.

## Writing rules

- The page opens with `## Checking each label` — the checking, printed. One numbered entry per draft item: its wording quoted; **Grounds:** who logged or said what, where and when, the row or figure beside it; **So:** the call and the reason — "stands" when the draft was right — or parked, dropped, merged into a sibling. Then **Not on the draft:** — what the manager owes or skipped — and **The sums:** arithmetic from its operands — "118 of 120 is two short".
- Then `# 1-on-1 brief: [Report] x [Manager] — [date]` — 350–700 words. **Context:** one line. Items in meeting order, `**name (Call, ~N min)**`, a sentence or two of things to say and ask, real numbers and names inside. Then **Off the page** (parked or dropped, with reasons), **Before you sit down** (every open manager row, each a concrete move — settled before anything is asked of the report, above all a row an item hangs on), and **Why it reads this way** (a few lines on why the calls landed).
- **If** the packet is thin **→** still a page: the checking names what is missing, the brief asks rather than asserts — what cannot be seen, the question to put, the shape of a true answer. Invent nothing.

A worked quarter-hour — record read, labels checked, page written — lives in `references/practice-notes.md`. Borrow the reasoning, never the facts.

# Not a 1-on-1 prep request

**If** the input is minutes, a transcript, or notes of a conversation already held — whatever its label **→** the reply is one short note under 180 words, with no checking section:

- Say plainly what it is: a record of a meeting already held — preparation looks forward, this looks back — and name the document and meeting.
- Where open actions survive inside, write the follow-up — owner, action, date as recorded — in a line or two; never a brief, never a re-sort.
- Name what would make it preparation: this week's open items, current figures, anything flagged since.
- Personal material stays "the personal matter", nothing more.
