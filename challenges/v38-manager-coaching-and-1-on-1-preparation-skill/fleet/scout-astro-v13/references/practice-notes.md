# Worked example

## A worked week (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

The record, Monday to Friday: a veterinary-clinic nurse, three years in. Board: the lab-analyzer service ticket has waited on the supplier nine days, two chases logged; the recovering-surgery ward she re-rostered got "the smoothest week we have had" in the practice manager's note; a controlled-drugs count review sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Chat: she flagged the analyzer Tuesday, asked Thursday about the emergency-care certificate. Draft agenda: "analyzer; ward; certificate idea; drug count."

## Checking each label

1. "analyzer" — **Grounds:** nine days waiting on the supplier, two chases, her Tuesday flag. **So:** **Blocked** — supplier-side; give her a date and who you will call.
2. "ward" — **Grounds:** practice manager's note: "the smoothest week we have had". **So:** **Recognition** — open with it, in the manager's words.
3. "certificate idea" — **Grounds:** she asked about emergency-care training Thursday. **So:** **Career** — she raised it; funding and a path, honestly.
4. "drug count" — **Grounds:** your review row, NOT STARTED. **So:** yours — off her draft, onto your own list.

**Not on the draft:** the controlled-drugs review — your row, three weeks sitting. **The sums:** appointments 118 against 120 booked (2 short, both reschedules).
