# Worked example

## A full batch (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

A coffee-roastery production hand, two years in. Board: the destoner service is a week overdue, two engineer callbacks unanswered; a café account emailed "the cleanest single-origin we have poured all season"; the green-bean stocktake sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Log: he flagged the destoner Monday, chased Thursday, asked Friday about the Q-grader course — and he was late back from break twice, the third week running. The draft says: "destoner; café account; the lates; Q course; stocktake."

## Reading each label

1. "destoner" — **Found:** service a week past its service date, two callbacks unanswered, his Monday flag. **Call:** **Blocked** — engineer-side; put a date in his hand and name your next call.
2. "café account" — **Found:** the email: "the cleanest single-origin we have poured all season". **Call:** **Recognition** — opens the meeting, in the café's words.
3. "the lates" — **Found:** late back twice this week, same the two weeks before. **Call:** **Coaching** — a recurring pattern — ask what the afternoons are costing
4. "Q course" — **Found:** he asked Friday about the Q-grader course. **Call:** **Career** — he raised it; a straight answer on cost and where it leads.
5. "stocktake" — **Found:** your stocktake row, NOT STARTED. **Call:** yours — off his draft, onto your own list.

**Missed:** the green-bean stocktake — your row. **Worked out:** batches roasted 58 against 55 planned (+3); defects 1 (target under 4).

# 1-on-1 brief: Sam Whitfield x his manager — Friday

**Context:** Two years on the roaster; a café account praised his single-origin.

**1. The café — Recognition, ~5 min**
They wrote "the cleanest single-origin we have poured all season" — say the words, ask what he changed in the curve.

**2. Destoner — Blocked, ~7 min**
A week overdue, two callbacks unanswered. Name the date the engineer is being held to and when the next call lands.

**3. The lates — Coaching, ~10 min**
Late back twice this week, same the two weeks before. Ask what is behind it before opening the log — a real reason may exist, and three consecutive weeks merits one either way.

**4. Q-grader — Career, ~8 min**
He asked about the course Friday. A straight answer on cost and where it leads — or an honest "written answer by Wednesday".

**5. Numbers — Status, ~2 min**
Batches 58 against 55 (+3), defects 1 under the ceiling — logged, then closed.

**Off the list:** "stocktake" came off the draft. **Before the meeting:** call the engineer today; finish the green-bean stocktake before close — the destoner the meeting leans on sits on your side. **The reasoning:** the draft filed an engineer wait as "destoner" and a written compliment as "café account" — the log re-labelled both — and the row it skipped is the oldest on the board.
