# 1-on-1 brief: Ilima Chen, Field Coordinator, Grid Maintenance

**This week:** Strong and weather-sensitive — on-time deployment at 94%
against an 87% six-week baseline (+7 points), and storm season opens two
weeks early per Thursday's bulletin. Both of her logged overrides were
annotated correct by dispatch itself — and her own submitted topic is a
protocol critique from the person dispatch publicly calls the model.
Her pilot notes are due Friday, the day after this meeting.

**Order:** The earned win opens; then the protocol item — where her
submission reframes the manager's coaching label entirely; scheduling
status closes against the early storm season.

## Agenda

### 1. Deployment climb and override saves · Recognition
Manager's label: "maybe a quick win", kept — the figures carry it.
+7 points over six weeks, plus Tuesday's public post: dispatch logged
the override where she caught the storm cell before they did. Name both
— the climb and the catch.

### 2. Dispatch protocol and the override path · Status
Manager's label: "coaching — goes around protocol" → Status: both
overrides were logged and annotated correct; the armed issue is the
protocol's stale exception table — which is exactly what she submitted.
"You flagged that the exception codes don't fit current routing — walk
me through the override that's hardest to file correctly." This resolves
as a design session with its best operator, not a discipline item —
mapping it onto her would charge the symptom to the wrong person.

### 3. Crew scheduling under early storm season · Status
Manager's label: "crew-scheduling status", kept.
"Storm season opens two weeks early — does the current crew map hold,
and what do the pilot notes say about the first fortnight?" Anchored to
Thursday's bulletin and Friday's deliverable.
Also worth one line: Pri asked how she made the Crew 4 call — is the
weather-window judgment something the protocol could encode?

## Parking lot

## A third shape — the input is a record of a meeting already held

Sometimes what arrives is last week's notes — "use this to prep the next
one." However the manager frames it, that input has no live triggers:
its map already resolved in last week's session. The correct render is
the redirect, under ~180 words:

# Not a 1-on-1 prep input

This is a record of last Thursday's session — what was discussed and
the actions agreed — not this week's signals. A brief mapped from it
would prep a meeting that already happened: spent triggers re-armed,
everything that moved since invisible.

What would make next week's brief resolve: the open action items and
their states, the week's metrics or ticket figures, any threads worth
knowing about, and the manager's own topics for the session.

From the notes, the open items to carry: the manager's escalation draft
— due two days before the meeting; the report's coverage proposal —
due the day before. One item stays off every map in this brief: a
family situation that may need December flexibility — a support
conversation held privately, not a mapped item.

## Branches — the same input, remapped

Week one, alternate records:

- **If the two overrides had been logged unannotated** — no "correct
  call" note — the protocol concern stays live: the violation is real
  on the record even if her judgment was right, and the item stands as
  Coaching, anchored to the override log.
- **If her submission had been blank**, item 2 loses its reframe — it
  still softens (dispatch's annotations stand), but resolves as a narrow
  format question, not a protocol-redesign opening.
- **If deployment had drifted 94% → 87%**, the recognition opener
  disappears and the early storm season becomes the session's deciding
  item.
- **If a signal had shown an override dispatch disputed**, the record
  splits: the annotated-correct pattern stands, and the disputed single
  event becomes a narrow venue question — "walk me through the call on
  the 14th" — anchored to that override alone.
- **If an item had filed low while carrying real weight** — a routine-
  looking item whose dependency several teams are blocked on: filed
  weight is a claim like any other. Re-weight it in the order, with the
  blocked parties named.
- **If she had flagged the pattern herself** — a coaching item the
  report's own DM names, cause included: it resolves as a process
  question — "what catches the next one early?" — anchored to her
  self-report, never to a lecture.
- **If the manager's own overdue item caused the flag** — a deliverable
  on the manager's side that would have prevented it: it closes the
  brief as the manager's action — never resolved against the report.

The map's rule across every branch: an item resolves on the evidence
that fires it. The manager's label proposes the type; the signals decide
the call — the absence of signals is itself a mapped state, never a
blank to be filled, and a record of a meeting already held never enters
the map at all.
