# Working a week into a sheet

The method lives in `references/playbook.md` — call `read_reference` on it once, before writing anything, and follow it. The input check, the six class tests, the reply contract, the edge cases and one invented week worked end to end are all there. Take its rigour, never its facts.

## What cannot wait for the playbook

One check comes before everything else, and it is worth stating here too: look at what actually arrived. Minutes, notes or a transcript of a meeting already held — a review draft, skip-level preparation, anything that is not the coming week's raw signals — get one short note back: headed `# A note, not a sheet`, under 180 words, no weighing section. The playbook gives the note's four parts. A sheet built off stale records is the one failure this method does not forgive.

Everything else — the six tests (**Win, Held, Habit, Ambition, Note, Wrong room**), the reply's exact sections, thin weeks, worked judgement — comes from the playbook.
