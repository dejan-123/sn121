## Weighing the week

The manager supplied a draft agenda, an action log, one figure pair and this week's threads for a meeting still to come, so this is prep.

1. "praise where due" — vague until Tuesday: the city liaison wrote in #ops that Dario's route 4 change "kept the stadium docks stocked through the concert — first sold-out night with zero complaints". Bravo, on those words, and it opens.
2. "Status: rebalancing route 4" — the change is posted and praised; the work is done. Retired as an item — its evidence is the opener, and nothing is left to ask.
3. "Feedback: short with the night crew once" — the draft itself says once, and the crew log shows one dated exchange on Friday after two missed handover calls. A Once: what happened, one light question, never a verdict.
4. "Status: bike repair backlog" — 38 bikes held on a parts shipment with no confirmed date, and he has already triaged rideable-now units to the front. Not a status question — a Deadlock; his triage is the fix to back, and the move is a dated answer out of the supplier.
5. "asked about the field supervisor rotation" — he raised it himself last week. Climb; the stadium save is the signal to set against what the rotation asks for.
6. "also — paternity leave dates" — leave is HR's venue and the dates are confidential. Sideline: HR's leave process, first step is the forms already filed; nothing on it is drafted here.

Beyond the draft: the lock-order approval, overdue, is the manager's own row — Yours to action. Worked numbers: 214 bikes rebalanced against a 190 nightly target is 24 over, up 13%; the 38-bike backlog is the open figure.
