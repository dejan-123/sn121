# One invented week answered in this voice — borrow the shape, never the facts

Everyone below is made up. What transfers is the form: a reading that walks the draft in order, items phrased like a colleague who did the homework, questions written as words the manager can speak. In a live answer the facts all come from the packet supplied.

## The answer

## The week's reading

A draft, a log, one figure pair and the week's threads arrived for a session still to come — this is prep.

1. "credit where due" — vague until Tuesday: the visitor director posted publicly that her crew replanted the storm-damaged border overnight, and the photo made the front page. Plaudit on that signal; it leads.
2. "Status: glasshouse heating" — the engineer visit slipped to next week and she has already moved the tender orchids to the warm annex. Not a status question: an Impasse on the engineer, her relocation is the remedy to back, the move a confirmed visit.
3. "Coaching: short with the volunteers" — one dated exchange Thursday, after a double-booked potting bench. A Nick: recount it, ask once, no verdict.
4. "Status: member evenue nights" — 2,340 tickets on a 2,500 target and the evenings question sits inside the gap, yet nothing says who owns the programming or whether it repeats. Thin evidence on a real question: a Flow pinned to the 160-ticket shortfall.
5. "she asked about the programmes manager post" — her own ask last month. Path; the overnight replant is the signal to set beside what the post wants.
6. "personal — her eldercare rota" — the rota is private and she has already told HR what she needs. Elsewhere: the leave process, starting with confirming dates at HR; nothing gets written on it in this room.

Not drafted: the overdue marquee-contract approval is the manager's own row. Figures run: 2,340 on a 2,500 target leaves 160 short, and the evenings question lives inside that gap.

# Lena Torres with her manager — the overnight border and the drifting boiler

The garden sold 2,340 tickets on a 2,500 target (160 short), and Tuesday gave the week its headline: the storm-damaged border replanted overnight before the press visit — the visitor director's photo made the front page. The glasshouse boiler keeps cycling off on a slipped engineer — her orchid relocation is already holding — and the marquee-contract approval waiting on you is overdue.

## The agenda

**1. The overnight border — Plaudit**
Open on the visitor director's public words — a front-page photo from damage that could have been a front-page problem is the rare kind of save worth naming whole. Then ask how the crew turned it around overnight; a save that fast is a method worth writing down before it fades.

**2. Glasshouse heating — Impasse**
The engineer slipped to next week and her orchid move to the warm annex is holding meanwhile — the workaround is hers, what remains is yours. Pin the visit to a confirmed date and tell her plainly the relocation is approved; she should not carry the gap quietly.

**3. Member evenings — Flow**
160 short and the member nights sit unowned inside it — one question: "where do the evening tickets actually lag, and whose desk catches the programming?" If the same gap shows next week it earns its own item.

**4. Thursday — Nick**
A curt word after a double-booked potting bench — one dated moment in a week she otherwise ran clean. One gentle question covers it: "has the bench stopped double-booking since Thursday?" A single moment never earns a verdict.

**5. Programmes manager — Path**
- "The overnight replant is the clearest thing on your record this quarter — which parts of the manager post does it already prove, and which does it leave open?"
- "You asked about the post yourself — where would you want it to take you?"

## Not this room

Her eldercare rota — private, and HR already has what they need; the step is confirming dates so the schedule can plan around it. Nothing on it is drafted here.

## Before the meeting

Approve the marquee hire contract — overdue, and the events calendar she just built prices off it. Bring it or set the day.

**Why these calls.** The credit stays, anchored — the director named the save in public. The boiler is a hold on an outside engineer, not a status, and her orchid move is the remedy to back. "Short" is one dated instance after a double-booked bench, so a Nick rather than a Refrain. The evenings gap is live but unowned, so it stays a question until the record says more. The post is hers and stands on the week's clearest signal. The eldercare rota belongs to HR, not this room.
