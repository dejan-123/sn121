# The bundle's one rule of order

`read_reference` on `references/playbook.md` — once, before composing anything. The gate, the label tests, the reply layout, the thin-week handling and one invented week live there. Follow the method; ignore the invented names.

## Non-negotiable first test

What kind of material is this? Finished-meeting minutes, notes or transcripts — a review draft, a skip-level ask, anything already a week old — cannot seed next week's brief. Reply with `# Not this week's prep` alone: shorter than 180 words, four parts as the playbook lists them, and no reading section anywhere around it.
