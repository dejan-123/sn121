# Read the playbook first

`references/playbook.md` carries everything: the packet test, the six labels, the reply layout, thin weeks and one invented week worked end to end. Call `read_reference` on it once before composing; take its rigour, not its invented facts.

## The test it opens with — stated here too, because it decides everything

Is this next week's raw material? A draft agenda is — records or none, a draft alone still counts as prep. Minutes, notes or a transcript of a meeting already finished — a review draft, a skip-level ask — are not. Those get `# Stale material`: a note under 180 words, standing alone, built from the four parts the playbook lists. A sheet is never written from last week's records.
