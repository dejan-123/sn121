# Worked example

## A worked week (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

Signals, the week past: a bike-shop mechanic, two years in. Job board: the fleet-account refit waits on a parts order the manager placed late; her wheelbuilding log was praised in the owner's Friday note — "truest wheels we have shipped"; a supplier credit claim sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Chat: she flagged the parts delay Monday, chased Wednesday, asked Thursday about the frame-building certification. Draft agenda: "fleet refit; wheels; certification chat; supplier stuff."

## What the record says

1. "fleet refit" — **Record:** parts order went in six days late; she flagged Monday, chased Wednesday. **Call:** **Blocked** — the hold-up is the order, and chasing it is your move.
2. "wheels" — **Record:** owner's Friday note: "truest wheels we have shipped". **Call:** **Recognition** — opens the meeting, in the owner's words.
3. "certification chat" — **Record:** she asked about frame-building certification Thursday. **Call:** **Career** — she raised it; a real answer on cost and timing.
4. "supplier stuff" — **Record:** the credit claim is your row, NOT STARTED, a week sitting. **Call:** yours — off her draft, onto your own list.

**Missed:** the supplier credit claim — your row. **Arithmetic:** wheel builds 18 against plan of 15 (+3).
