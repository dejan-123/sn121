# Worked example

## A worked week (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

Say the week looks like this: a garden-centre assistant, two years in. Task board: the irrigation-timer replacement is a week overdue, two supplier callbacks unanswered; the rose-bed display she re-planted got "best the front has ever looked" on a customer comment card; a fertilizer order sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Log: she flagged the timer Monday, chased Thursday, asked Friday about the horticulture certificate — and she was late back from lunch twice, the third week running. The draft says: "watering system; rose display; the lates; course chat; fertilizer."

## Item by item

1. "watering system" — **On the record:** timer replacement a week overdue, two supplier callbacks unanswered, her Monday flag. **The call:** **Blocked** — supplier-side; give her a date and say when you will chase.
2. "rose display" — **On the record:** the comment card: "best the front has ever looked". **The call:** **Recognition** — opens the meeting, in the customer's words.
3. "the lates" — **On the record:** late back from lunch twice this week, and the two weeks before show the same. **The call:** **Coaching** — real, recurring, worth a question: what is pulling on the afternoons?
4. "course chat" — **On the record:** she asked Friday about the horticulture certificate. **The call:** **Career** — she raised it; a real answer on cost and where it leads.
5. "fertilizer" — **On the record:** your order row, NOT STARTED. **The call:** yours — off her draft, onto your own list.

**The draft missed:** the fertilizer order — your row. **Figures:** trays potted 186 against 180 planned (+6); plant losses 2 (target under 4).

# 1-on-1 brief: Dana Okafor x her manager — Friday

**Context:** Two years on the floor; a customer card named her rose beds.

**1. Rose display — Recognition, ~3 min**
A customer wrote "best the front has ever looked" — say the words, ask what she changed in the planting.

**2. Watering system — Blocked, ~7 min**
A week overdue, two callbacks unanswered. Give her the date you are holding the supplier to and when you will call.

**3. The lates — Coaching, ~9 min**
Late back twice this week, same the two weeks before. Ask what is going on rather than reading her the log — there may be a real reason, and the pattern matters either way.

**4. Course — Career, ~10 min**
She asked about the certificate Friday. A real answer on cost and what it builds toward — or an honest "I will find out by Tuesday".

**5. Numbers — Status, ~2 min**
Trays 186 against 180 (+6), losses 2 under the ceiling — read out and done.

**Set aside:** "fertilizer" moved to the Before the meeting list. **Before the meeting:** call the irrigation supplier today; place the fertilizer order before close — the watering fix the meeting depends on sits on your side. **Thinking:** the draft filed a supplier wait as "watering system" and a written compliment as a routine line — the record re-labelled both, and the row it skipped is the one most overdue.
