# One invented week, answered end to end — form only

Everything below is made up: the workplace, the people, the numbers. Copy the shape — a record check that takes the draft line by line in plain sentences, a title built from the week's signals, sections that always print, questions pinned to named evidence — and copy nothing else. In a real reply every name, day and figure comes from the inputs supplied.

## The record check

The manager supplied a draft agenda, an action log, one figure pair and this week's threads for a meeting still to come, so this is prep.

1. "well done this week" — real but unanchored until Monday: the depot coordinator wrote in #ops that Ola "caught the van 3 coolant leak before it stranded anyone — that's the second breakdown she's headed off this month". Cheers, on those words, and it opens the meeting.
2. "Status: van 3 servicing" — the garage gives Thursday at the earliest and she has already reshuffled the six bookings onto van 1. Not a status question — a Gridlock, and her workaround is the fix to back: the move is a confirmed collection time out of the garage.
3. "Coaching: curt with the receptionist" — one dated instance, Friday's diary note, after three unanswered calls about a double-booking. One means one: a Single, asked lightly, not a coaching habit.
4. "Status: appointment no-shows" — the diary shows five missed bookings this week but nothing says who owns the follow-up or whether it repeats earlier weeks. A legitimate ask with thin evidence stays on as a question: a Heartbeat, pinned to the five misses.
5. "Development: she mentioned the senior groomer cert" — she raised it herself last week. Ascent; the coolant save is the signal to set beside what the cert asks for.
6. "one more thing — her holiday request" — leave sits with HR and her unread form. Annex: the leave process, first step is opening the form, and nothing on it is drafted here.

Not on the draft: the winter route plan sign-off, overdue, is the manager's own row — On your desk. The maths: 47 appointments completed against 52 booked is 5 missed, and the five show up in the diary the same week.

# Ola Brennan x her manager — the coolant save and the van that is still in the garage

**The picture.** The week ran 47 appointments of 52 booked (5 missed), with a public save when she caught the van 3 coolant leak — her second headed-off breakdown this month. Van 3 sits at the garage until Thursday at the earliest; she has already moved the six affected bookings onto van 1. Your winter route sign-off is overdue; her holiday form sits unread.

**On your desk.** Sign off the winter route plan — overdue, and it gates the schedule she works to. Bring it or set the day.

## The agenda

**1. The coolant catch — Cheers**
The depot coordinator's words to her, not a summary: "the second breakdown she's headed off this month". Then ask what told her to look — that instinct is the process worth naming.

**2. Van 3 — Gridlock**
At the garage, parts Thursday at the earliest, six bookings already reshuffled onto van 1 by her. Your move: pin the garage to a collection time this week, and confirm her reshuffle covers the gap if it slips again.

**3. No-shows — Heartbeat**
Five missed bookings against 52 — who owns the confirmation calls on those, and do the same clients appear in earlier weeks? One question; if a pattern shows, it earns its own item next time.

**4. Friday's exchange — Single**
A sharp word after three unanswered calls about a double-booking — one dated instance, in a week she otherwise held together. One light question: "has anything been said since?" — never a verdict on a single moment.

**5. Senior groomer cert — Ascent**
- "The coolant catch is the second time this month you saw a problem before it happened — which parts of the senior cert does that already cover, and which does it leave open?"
- "You asked whether it is still worth doing this year — what would you want it to unlock?"

## Off the table

The holiday request — her form is unread; that goes to the leave process with HR, first step is opening it today. Nothing on it is drafted here.

**The reasoning.** The well-done stays but gains its anchor — the depot coordinator named the save; the van is a hold on a garage, not a status, and her reshuffle is the fix to back; "curt" is one dated instance after three ignored calls, so it stays a Single and does not become a habit; the no-shows are live but unowned, so they stay a question until the record says more; the cert is hers and stands on the week's clearest signal; the leave belongs to HR, not this room.
