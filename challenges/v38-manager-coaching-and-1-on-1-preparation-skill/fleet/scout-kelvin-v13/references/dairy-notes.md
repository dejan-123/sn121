# Worked example

## A week's walk (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

A creamery operator, three years in. Board: the pasteurizer calibration is a week overdue, two engineer callbacks unanswered; the wholesale buyer emailed "the cleanest batch we have tested all year"; the hygiene-audit paperwork sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Log: she flagged the calibration Monday, chased Thursday, asked Friday about the dairy-technology course — and she was late back from break twice, the third week running. The draft says: "pasteurizer; buyer call; the lates; course; hygiene audit."

## What the week proves

1. "pasteurizer" — **Proof:** calibration seven days behind, two callbacks ignored, her Monday flag. **Judgment:** **Blocked** — engineer-side; put a date in her hand and name your follow-up.
2. "buyer call" — **Proof:** the email: "the cleanest batch we have tested all year". **Judgment:** **Recognition** — opens the meeting, in the buyer's words.
3. "the lates" — **Proof:** late back twice this week, same the two weeks before. **Judgment:** **Coaching** — a proven pattern — ask what is pulling on the afternoons
4. "course" — **Proof:** she asked Friday about the dairy-technology course. **Judgment:** **Career** — she raised it; a straight answer on cost and where it leads.
5. "hygiene audit" — **Proof:** your audit row, NOT STARTED. **Judgment:** yours — off her draft, onto your own list.

**Missed:** the hygiene audit — your row. **The sums:** batches 41 of 50 planned (9 behind, one line down); rejects 1 (target under 3).

# 1-on-1 brief: Ines Marsh x her manager — Friday

**Context:** Three years on the floor; a wholesale buyer praised her batch.

**1. Buyer call — Recognition, ~2 min**
The buyer wrote "the cleanest batch we have tested all year" — say the words, ask what she changed in the run.

**2. Pasteurizer — Blocked, ~6 min**
A week overdue, two callbacks unanswered. Name the date the engineer is being held to and when the next call goes out.

**3. The lates — Coaching, ~10 min**
Late back twice this week, same the two weeks before. Ask what sits behind it rather than reciting the log — a real reason may exist, and three straight weeks needs one either way.

**4. Course — Career, ~9 min**
She asked about the dairy-technology course Friday. A straight answer on cost and what it builds toward — or an honest "an answer by Wednesday".

**5. Numbers — Status, ~2 min**
Batches 41 of 50 (9 behind, one line down), rejects 1 under the ceiling — said once, then closed.

**Off the list:** "hygiene audit" came off the draft. **Settle first:** call the engineer today; complete the hygiene audit before close — the calibration the meeting leans on sits on your side. **Why it reads this way:** the draft filed an engineer wait as "pasteurizer" and a written compliment as "buyer call" — the record re-labelled both — and the row it skipped is the longest-standing one.
