## The answer

## Reading it through

A draft, a log, one figure pair and the week's threads arrived for a session still to come — this is prep.

1. "credit for the week" — vague until Wednesday: the resort director posted publicly that his crew had every lift turning by first tracks after the ice storm, and that guests never knew what the night took. Tribute on that signal; it leads.
2. "Status: lift 7 gearbox" — the parts courier slipped to next week and he has already cut the lift to half capacity. Not a status question: a Holdup on the courier, his load-cut is the remedy to back, the move a confirmed delivery.
3. "Coaching: curt with the ski school" — one dated exchange Thursday, after a double-booked lesson slot. A Graze: recount it, ask once, no verdict.
4. "Status: season-pass refunds" — 18,600 rides on a 20,000 forecast and the refund question sits inside the gap, yet nothing says who owns the queue or whether it repeats. Thin evidence on a real question: a Current pinned to the 1,400-ride shortfall.
5. "he asked about the mountain-ops deputy role" — his own ask last month. Frontier; the ice-storm recovery is the signal to set beside what the role wants.
6. "personal — his physiotherapy sessions" — the sessions are private and he has already told HR what he needs. Separate lane: the leave process, starting with confirming times at HR; nothing gets written on it in this room.

Not drafted: the overdue snowmaking-contract approval is the manager's own row. Figures run: 18,600 on a 20,000 forecast leaves 1,400 short, and the refund question lives inside that gap.

# Dario Fontaine with his manager — the ice-storm save and the noisy gearbox

The mountain ran 18,600 rides on a 20,000 forecast (1,400 short), and Wednesday gave the week its headline: every lift turning by first tracks after the ice storm — the resort director said guests never knew what the night took. Lift 7's gearbox runs noisy on a slipped courier — his half-capacity cut is already holding — and the snowmaking-contract approval waiting on you is overdue.

## What to run

**1. The ice-storm recovery — Tribute**
Open on the resort director's public words — a recovery invisible to the guests it was for is the rarest kind of save, so having it named in the open is the recognition itself. Then ask how his crew sequenced the night; a turn-around that fast is a method worth writing down before it fades.

**2. Lift 7 gearbox — Holdup**
The courier slipped to next week and his half-capacity cut is holding meanwhile — the workaround is his, what remains is yours. Pin the delivery to a confirmed date and tell him plainly the cut is approved; he should not carry the gap quietly.

**3. Refund queue — Current**
1,400 short and the refunds sit unowned inside it — one question: "where does the refund queue actually stall, and whose desk catches it?" If the same gap shows next week it earns its own item.

**4. Thursday — Graze**
A curt word after a double-booked lesson slot — one dated moment in a week he otherwise ran clean. One gentle question covers it: "have the lesson slots stopped overlapping since Thursday?" A single moment never earns a verdict.

**5. Mountain-ops deputy — Frontier**
- "The storm recovery is the clearest thing on your record this season — which parts of the deputy role does it already prove, and which does it leave open?"
- "You asked about the role yourself — where would you want it to take you?"

## Other doors

His physiotherapy sessions — private, and HR already has what they need; the step is confirming times so the rota can plan around them. Nothing on it is drafted here.

## Before you sit down

Approve the snowmaking contract — overdue, and the staffing grid he just published leans on it. Bring it or set the day.
