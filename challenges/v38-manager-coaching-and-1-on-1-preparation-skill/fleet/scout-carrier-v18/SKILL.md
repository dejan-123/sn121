# Where the method lives

Everything is in `references/playbook.md` — open it once with `read_reference` before any writing. It carries the input test, the six labels, the reply's exact sections, the edge cases and one invented week worked end to end. Follow its method; never its invented facts.

## The check that runs ahead of everything

First read what the packet *is*, before what it says. Old minutes, notes or a transcript of a finished meeting — a review draft, a skip-level ask — are not the coming week's signals. They get `# Not this week's prep`: a note under 180 words, no reading printed on it, built from the four parts the playbook lists. A brief assembled from stale records is the one result this method refuses to produce.
