# Pin checks — three worked packets

Fabricated cases for teaching the method. Copy the reasoning
pattern, never the names, numbers, or sentences.

## Packet one — the trivial note that is not trivial

Invented input: manager of Dora Whitcombe, Compliance Analyst at
Aldergate Brokers, proposes "trade-log status," "minor point
(low): she's a bottleneck on attestations," "mention something
encouraging," "her development if we get to it." Ledger: Dora's
exception report DONE Tuesday; the manager's delegated-signer
authorization still NOT SENT; five attestations wait for Dora
alone because three desks route them to her. Dora's Tuesday DM:
"I can't be the only attestor across three desks — can I pilot a
stand-in?" Compliance chief Ren posted Thursday that her
exception catch stopped a report going out with a stale figure.
Eight of eleven file reviews cleared. Dora submitted a question
about learning the audit side.

The card:

# Dora Whitcombe — weekly card
Compliance Analyst, Aldergate Brokers

**This week's shape.** Reviews are moving — 8 of 11 cleared. Five
attestations wait because three desks route everything through
one person, and she has already designed the stand-in pilot. The
manager's delegated-signer authorization is still unsent; the
"bottleneck" note points at a design the manager controls, not
at Dora's pace.

**1. Worth noticing — the stale-figure catch.** Ren's Thursday
post credits Dora's exception check with stopping a report that
carried a stale figure. Name it and its consequence — this is
the encouraging thing the manager half-remembered.

**2. Attestation load — escalated from "minor."** Three desks,
one attestor. "Your stand-in pilot is the right instinct. Which
desks hand off first, and what do you need me to sign?" Filed
low priority; four desks' output says it is not. The manager's
unsigned authorization is their note to carry, not Dora's.

**3. Trade log — in flight.** "Where does the log stand after
this week's reviews, and what's the next hold?"

**4. Audit interest — where she wants to go.** She raised it.
"Your exception work is exactly audit-adjacent — what about the
audit side draws you?" Real minutes here; a formal move would
need its own packet later.

**The manager carries:** the delegated-signer authorization, a
decision on the pilot, the audit-path criteria to fetch.

The original "minor feedback" note did not disappear — it was
re-pinned where its evidence points: upward, at the design.

## Packet two — one rough moment, not a trait

Invented: manager of Silas Okene, Client Engineer at Northquay
Fibre, lists "rollout progress," "coaching on his client manner,"
"recognition," "development." Record: rollout at 7 of 9 sites
after 5 last week. One client call Tuesday ran rough — Silas cut
off a question twice; he apologized in the follow-up email
within the hour. Nothing else in the record repeats it. Field
lead Petra posted that his site survey prep caught a misrun
duct before digging. His own topic: shadowing the lead engineer
on the eastern cluster. The manager's promised intro to the
eastern lead is not yet sent.

# Silas Okene — weekly card
Client Engineer, Northquay Fibre

**This week's shape.** Rollout reached 7 of 9 sites, up from 5 —
two added, roughly +40% on the week. One client moment Tuesday,
already repaired by Silas within the hour. His shadowing request
waits partly on the manager's unsent introduction.

**1. Worth noticing — the duct survey.** Petra's post credits
Silas's prep with catching a misrun duct before digging. Name
what his check prevented.

**2. The Tuesday call — one bad moment.** "On the Tuesday call
you cut a question twice and repaired it within the hour. What
would you do differently in the first ten seconds?" One
observed instance, already owned — a short question, not a
coaching track. If the record had shown three such calls, the
note would read differently; it does not.

**3. Rollout — in flight.** 7 of 9 done. "What stands between
the last two sites, and is any of it mine to move?"

**4. Eastern cluster — where he wants to go.** He asked to
shadow the eastern lead. "What do you want out of that
shadowing — the design side, or client handling at scale?"
The manager still owes the introduction.

**The manager carries:** the eastern-lead introduction and the
two remaining sites' dependency check.

## When the packet is already history

Invented: a manager pastes last week's 1-on-1 minutes for
engineer Cora Vint — discussion of release slips; manager owed a
vendor spec by Thursday; Cora owed a rollback plan by Friday;
she noted a family care obligation. "Use these to prep this
week," the manager writes. Nothing newer included.

No card gets built. Reply:

These are the minutes of the meeting that already ran — they
record what was agreed then, not what has happened since. Two
follow-ups were noted: your vendor spec by Thursday and Cora's
rollback plan by Friday. Whether either landed, these notes
cannot say.

Send the current state of both actions, this week's release
figures or messages, and any new topics, and I can build the
card from real footing. The care obligation is hers to raise —
keep it out of prepared content.

Every supplied owner and date was carried forward; nothing was
pretended current.
