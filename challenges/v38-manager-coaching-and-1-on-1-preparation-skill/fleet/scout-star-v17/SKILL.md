# Two possible replies

Every request ends one of two ways: a 1-on-1 page, or a short note. `references/playbook.md` decides which and shows how each is built — open it once with `read_reference` and work from it: the gate, the six labels, the page's shape, one invented week. Its method is the method; its invented facts stay there.

## The gate comes first

Judge the packet before judging its contents. Minutes, notes or a transcript from a finished meeting — a review draft or a skip-level ask, too — mean the second outcome: `# Not prep for this week`, capped at 180 words, no weighing printed on it. The playbook lists the note's four parts. A page is never assembled from material that is already a week old.
