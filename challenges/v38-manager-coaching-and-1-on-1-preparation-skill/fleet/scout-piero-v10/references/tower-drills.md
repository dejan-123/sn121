# Tower drills — case specs

Five invented packets worked end to end. Names, figures and
products are invented; the clearances are the part to carry.

---

## Case A — normal window, one mis-filed plan

**Report:** Sofia, senior mobile engineer.
**Filed:** (a) "Sofia's attitude — argued with QA in the
release channel"; (b) "why is the payments migration late";
(c) "recognise the dark-mode launch".
**Conditions:** QA lead posted public thanks naming Sofia for
catching a regression pre-release; her objection — blunt, on
substance — was to a build that failed its own checks;
migration 3 of 8 endpoints done, stalled on a security
sign-off filed 9 days ago; dark mode shipped, crash-free
sessions 97.8 → 99.1%.

**Clearances:**
- (a) Re-routed → one-time note, one narrow channel question:
  "The QA exchange was on the merits — they were already
  thanking you for the catch. One thing: it ran in the
  release channel rather than on the ticket. Does moving it
  there cost anything?" Substance stands; channel takes one
  question.
- (b) Re-routed → another field's traffic. 3 of 8 done,
  stalled on a 9-day sign-off: "I'll chase security today
  for a date; who do I escalate to if it slips again?"
- (c) Cleared first. Public thanks plus 97.8 → 99.1% — two
  attributable credits depart first.
- Every filed plan: a strip line and a clearance. Nothing
  filed disappears.

**Board:** `# Strip board: Sofia, senior mobile engineer` —
picture line, worked figures, departure order, vectors,
pre-flight, diversions, controller's log.

## Case B — the self-diagnosed pattern

**Report:** Flor, product designer.
**Filed:** (a) "Flor keeps blowing estimates — 2 days became
4, then 3 became 5"; (b) "usability study came back 3 of 5".
**Conditions:** both sprints slipped — a real pattern — but
her own note shows she diagnosed it: scope grew 14 → 23
screens mid-sprint; "tracking scope for next time." Eng
lead publicly praised her "cleanest handoff this quarter."
Manager's self-review feedback for Flor: NOT STARTED.

**Clearances:**
- (a) Re-routed → upstream behaviour. Pattern real, cause
  already found — the strip coaches what precedes the slip:
  "Your scope log is the right instinct. 14 → 23 screens —
  when did you first see it growing, and what would
  flagging it then have changed for the estimate?" One
  anchored question on earlier flagging — never a lecture
  about estimates she already explained.
- (b) Cleared — light status. 3 of 5 with a rescheduled
  session is a status line, not a coaching plan.
- Recognition: Cleared first — "cleanest handoff this
  quarter" is attributable credit.
- Pre-flight: the NOT STARTED self-review appears plainly —
  "I owe you the review write-up; I'll have it before
  Friday" — a manager-owned item with a date, never omitted
  for comfort.

## Case C — thin packet, worked honestly

**Report:** Ari, staff engineer.
**Filed:** "prep my weekly with Ari — his route to senior
staff, and whether he can own the ingest rebuild." No action
log, figures, or excerpts. One note: Ari asked in passing
about pay bands.

Thin is not invalid — the packet aims at the coming window,
so it is still this window's traffic. Only a wrong-kind
document (Case D) turns around. First line:
`# Strip board: Ari, staff engineer`.

**Clearances:**
- Route to senior staff: Cleared — split by field. Ari's
  direction and the readiness question stay in the window
  with real time: "You asked about the next step — here's
  the gap evidence I see and what I'd need documented; what
  scope would you take to build it?" The pay-band question
  is Diverted — bands sit with the comp review; destination
  and owner named, one line, no vectors.
- Rebuild readiness: Holding. No delivery record; it stands
  as the honest question: "I don't have your recent record
  in front of me — walk me through the last few weeks:
  shipped, stuck, and what you'd take on."
- Nothing invented to fill the board. A thin board of honest
  questions beats a padded one.

## Case D — old traffic, turned around whole

**Packet:** "Here are the minutes from this week's 1-on-1
with Devon — please prep next week's agenda from them."
Three open actions: Devon's benchmarking doc (due Thursday),
the manager's budget confirmation, a mentoring follow-up.

**Whole packet turned around — no strips.** Under 150
words, titled `# Not this window's traffic`:

Minutes of a meeting already held — they record what was
agreed, not what has moved since. This supports a follow-up
on the open actions, not a board: Devon's benchmarking doc
(due Thursday), your budget confirmation, the mentoring
follow-up — owners and dates exactly as filed. For a board,
send this week's action items, any figures, and whatever
Devon raised or you want addressed. Nothing in the material
is dated after that window, so a board would replay old
positions or invent new ones.

Personal matters in the minutes stay private — not
repeated, not summarized, not hinted at.

## Case E — the queue that is the manager's own plan

**Report:** Ruth, senior platform engineer.
**Filed:** (a) "Ruth must clear her review queue — seven
docs wait on her, two teams blocked"; (b) "Ruth seemed
disengaged in planning".
**Conditions:** the seven docs sit with her because the
process names her sole approver for cross-team work; she
shipped two features this sprint and raised the queue in
retro herself, proposing a rotation; "disengaged" appears
once, in the manager's note.

**Clearances:**
- (a) Re-routed → manager's capacity call. A sole-approver
  rule stalling two teams is owned by the manager, early on
  the board: "Your rotation proposal is the right starting
  point — I'll approve it this week and take it to the
  team leads. What load should each reviewer hold so
  nothing queues behind one person again?"
- (b) Grounded. One unsourced observation; the sprint shows
  two shipped features and a raised process fix. Its
  absence is logged plainly.
- The manager's own open item — acting on the retro
  proposal — sits in pre-flight with whom and when.

---

## Constants across all five

- The conditions decide; the filing is only a draft.
- Attributable credit clears first.
- A self-diagnosed pattern coaches the upstream behaviour,
  never the symptom.
- Justified substance keeps its slot; the channel takes one
  question.
- Another field's traffic diverts there.
- One occurrence is never a pattern.
- Missing readings stand `Holding` — the honest question —
  never covered over.
- Manager-owned items appear even when marked not begun.
- Every figure shows its arithmetic; every filed plan gets
  a strip line and a clearance.
- A thin packet still gets a board. The turnaround is for
  the wrong document only — under 150 words, no board
  structure.
