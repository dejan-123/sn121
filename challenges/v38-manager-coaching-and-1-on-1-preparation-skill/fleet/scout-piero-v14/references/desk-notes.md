# Worked example

## A duty week (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

A hotel front-desk agent, two years in. Board: the group-booking rate file is a week overdue, two revenue-team callbacks unanswered; a guest comment card says "she sorted our flooded room at midnight like it was nothing"; the loyalty-report reconciliation sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Log: she flagged the rate file Monday, chased Wednesday, asked Friday about a reservations course — and her Tuesday reply to a guest came back clipped, the only mark of its kind all season. The draft says: "rate file; guest comment; sharp reply; course idea; loyalty report."

## Checking the list

1. "rate file" — **Shows:** a week outstanding, two callbacks ignored, her Monday flag. **Call:** **Blocked** — revenue-side; hand her the date and name your next chase.
2. "guest comment" — **Shows:** the card: "she sorted our flooded room at midnight like it was nothing". **Call:** **Recognition** — opens the meeting, in the guest's words.
3. "sharp reply" — **Shows:** one clipped note to a guest Tuesday, nothing like it before or since. **Call:** a passing word — one rough moment on a clean record is a touchpoint, not a coaching slot.
4. "course idea" — **Shows:** she asked Friday about the reservations course. **Call:** **Career** — she raised it; a straight answer on cost and path.
5. "loyalty report" — **Shows:** your reconciliation row, NOT STARTED. **Call:** yours — off her draft, onto your own list.

**Missed:** the loyalty reconciliation — your row. **Arithmetic:** check-ins 412 against 430 booked (18 short, group cancellation).

# 1-on-1 brief: Ana Costa x her manager — Friday

**Context:** Two years on desk; a guest card named her midnight fix.

**1. Guest comment — Recognition, ~4 min**
A guest wrote "she sorted our flooded room at midnight like it was nothing" — say the words, ask what she did differently.

**2. Rate file — Blocked, ~8 min**
A week overdue, two callbacks unanswered. Tell her the date revenue is being held to and when the chase lands.

**3. The reply — Status, ~3 min**
One clipped note in an otherwise clean season — a passing word and on, not a coaching session.

**4. Course — Career, ~11 min**
She asked about the reservations course Friday. A straight answer on cost and what it builds toward — or an honest "she hears it Wednesday".

**5. Numbers — Status, ~2 min**
Check-ins 412 against 430 (18 short, one group cancelled) — stated once, then done.

**Left off:** "loyalty report" came off the draft. **Manager's own list:** call revenue today; finish the loyalty reconciliation before Friday close — the rate file the meeting needs sits on your side. **The reasoning:** the draft filed a revenue wait as "rate file" and a written compliment as a comment — the record re-labelled both — and the row it skipped has been waiting longest.
