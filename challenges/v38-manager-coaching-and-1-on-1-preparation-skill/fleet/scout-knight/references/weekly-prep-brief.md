## A third shape — the input fails the zeroth guard

Sometimes what arrives is last week's notes — "use this to prep the
next one." However the manager frames it, there is nothing to defend:
the notes document a conversation that already happened. The defended
answer is the redirect, under ~180 words:

# Not a 1-on-1 prep input

This is a record of last Thursday's session — what was discussed and
the actions agreed — not this week's signals. A brief defended on it
would prep a meeting that already happened: settled items argued again,
everything that moved since invisible.

What would make next week's brief defensible: the open action items and
their states, the week's figures or ticket counts, any threads worth
knowing about, and the manager's own topics for the session.

From the notes, the open actions to carry: the manager's escalation
draft — due two days before the meeting; the report's coverage
proposal — due the day before. One item stays out of every defended
brief: a family situation that may need December flexibility — a
support conversation held privately, never an agenda item.

## Branches — the same input, defended differently

Week one, alternate records:

- **If the legal-hold stamps were absent** — the two misses just late —
  the item stands as Coaching: a real pattern, anchored to the dates,
  and the question becomes time management framed around the two
  specific misses.
- **If Anselm's "my queue" post were missing**, the upstream claim is
  weaker — the stamps alone still reframe the misses as blocked, but
  the item survives in narrower form: "what's the early-escalation
  path when a hold lands?" — anchored to her Wednesday DM.
- **If her submission had been blank**, item 2 loses its submitted fix —
  the SLA point still surfaces (the stamps demand it), but as the
  manager's follow-up, not her agenda slot.
- **If a signal had shown a sharp public exchange** — "legal kills
  another deadline" in #publishing — a narrow venue item appears:
  correct escalation of a real problem, wrong channel. What gets
  coached is the venue, never the underlying correctness.
- **If an item filed low while carrying real weight** — a "quick
  question" whose dependency other teams are waiting on: filed weight
  is a claim like any other label. Re-weight it in the order, with the
  waiting parties named.
- **If she had flagged the pattern herself in a coaching area** — a
  coaching item the report's own DM already names, cause included: it
  stands as a process question — "what catches the next one early?" —
  never as a lecture she doesn't need.
- **If the manager's own overdue item caused the miss** — a deliverable
  on the manager's side that would have prevented the flag: the charge
  stays on the manager's side, named plainly — never defended against
  the report.

The invariant across every branch: each item defends on its own record.
The manager's label proposes the charge; the signals decide what
actually stands — where the record is silent, the honest brief names
the silence, and where the input is a record of a meeting already held,
the defended answer is to send it back.
