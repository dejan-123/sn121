# Desk drills — full pages on invented packets

Four invented packets, assigned the way the page should assign them.
The names, figures and products are invented; the assignments are the
part to carry.

## Drill 1 — a normal week with a mis-slugged pitch

Packet: report is "Marta, senior platform engineer". Manager's draft:
(a) "discipline issue — Marta snapped at QA in the team channel",
(b) "why is the payments migration late", (c) "quick win — mention the
dashboard launch". The reporting: QA's lead posted a public thank-you
naming Marta for unblocking their release by building the replay tool
early; the channel exchange shows Marta objecting — sharply but on the
substance — to QA shipping a build that failed its own gate; the
migration is 3 of 8 endpoints done, waiting on a security sign-off
requested 9 days ago; the dashboard launched and the support lead
messaged that ticket volume on it halved in week one.

Assignments:

- Pitch (a): **Re-slugged**. One substantive objection to a build that
  failed its own checks — filed as a discipline issue, run as a
  one-time note with one narrow question. Copy: "The QA exchange was
  on the merits — QA was already thanking you for the release help.
  One thing to keep in view: that disagreement ran in the team channel
  rather than the ticket. Does moving it there cost you anything?"
  The objection stands; the channel takes one question and no more.
- Pitch (b): **Re-slugged — the story belongs to another hand.**
  3 of 8 done, and the stall is a 9-day-old sign-off with security,
  not Marta's pace. Copy: "Migration is waiting on security's
  sign-off — nine days outstanding. I'll chase the reviewer today and
  get a date; who should I escalate to if it slips again?"
- Pitch (c): **Runs as the lead.** Two attributable credits — the QA
  lead's public thanks and the support figure. Open with them before
  any process story; credit lands first.
- Every filed pitch appears on the page; nothing filed disappears.

Page: `# Desk page: Marta, senior platform engineer` — shape line
("a strong week with one decision waiting on another team"), figures
shown as work, then the run order with all three filed slugs, the
reporting behind each, and each assignment — then copy, before-the-
meeting items, sent-over list, why these assignments.

## Drill 2 — a thin packet logged honestly

Packet: report is "Ilya, staff engineer". Manager's note: "prep my
weekly with Ilya — want to talk about his promo timeline, and whether
he's ready to own the ingest rewrite." No action log, no figures, no
channel excerpts. One calendar item: Ilya asked in passing about
salary bands for the next level.

Thin is not invalid: the packet aims at the coming meeting, so the
desk still logs it — assigned pitch by pitch. Only the wrong kind of
document (drill 3) is sent back whole. The page opens
`# Desk page: Ilya, staff engineer` — never a send-back preface.

Assignments:

- Promo timeline: **Runs — split by desk.** Ilya's direction and the
  manager's readiness question belong in this meeting, with real
  time: "You asked about the next level — here's the gap evidence I
  see and what I'd need documented; what scope would you take to
  build it?" The pay-band question is **Sent over**: bands live in
  the compensation review, not a weekly — the page names the venue
  (the comp review with the comp partner) and the owner, one line, no
  copy.
- Readiness for the ingest rewrite: **Held**. The packet has no
  delivery record to weigh. It runs as the honest question, not an
  assessment invented for the slot: "I don't have your recent
  delivery record in front of me — walk me through the last few
  weeks: what shipped, what's stuck, what you'd take on."
- Nothing else is fabricated to fill the page. A thin page of honest
  questions beats a padded one; the masthead names what the packet
  lacks ("no action log, figures or channel record this week") and
  before-the-meeting lists what to pull.

## Drill 3 — a packet that is not this week's reporting

Packet: "Here are the minutes from this week's 1-on-1 with Dana —
please prep next week's agenda from them." The minutes list three
actions: Dana to send the benchmarking doc (due Thursday), the
manager to confirm the conference budget, a follow-up on the
mentoring pilot.

Assignment: **whole packet sent back — nothing logged.** The reply,
under 180 words, titled `# Not this week's reporting`:

- What it is: minutes of a meeting already held — they record what
  was agreed, not what has moved since.
- Why a page misleads: it would replay logged items as if live and
  invent status the reporting does not have.
- What the record does support: a follow-up on the three open
  actions, listed with owners and dates exactly as filed — Dana's
  doc (due Thursday), the budget confirmation, the mentoring pilot.
- What to send instead: this week's action log, any figures, and
  whatever Dana raised or the manager wants on the page.
- Any personal matter in the minutes stays private — not repeated,
  not summarized, not hinted at.

## Drill 4 — the queue that is the manager's own story

Packet: report is "Sana, senior backend engineer". Manager's draft:
(a) "remind Sana to clear her review queue — five design docs are
waiting on her and two partner teams are blocked"; (b) "Sana seems
quiet in standup lately". The reporting: the five docs are assigned
because the process names her sole approver for cross-team changes;
she shipped two features this sprint and flagged the queue in the
last retro, proposing a rotation; "quiet" appears once, in the
manager's note.

Assignments:

- Pitch (a): **Re-slugged — the editor who can move it is the
  manager.** A sole-approver rule stalling two teams is a capacity
  story the manager owns, not a note to work faster. It runs high on
  the page as the manager's own decision: "Your rotation proposal is
  the right starting point — I want to approve it this week and take
  it to the partner leads. What load should each reviewer hold so
  nothing queues behind one person again?"
- Pitch (b): **Killed.** One manager observation with no reporting
  behind it; the sprint shows two shipped features and a raised
  process fix. The page logs its absence plainly rather than
  inventing a wellbeing segment.
- The manager's own open item — acting on Sana's retro proposal — is
  listed under before-the-meeting as the manager's commitment, with
  whom and when.

## What every drill shares

- The reporting assigns; the slug is only the filing.
- Attributable credit leads — a named person, a concrete result.
- A justified matter raised in the wrong channel keeps its substance
  and takes exactly one channel question.
- What another hand controls is logged to that hand.
- One occurrence is a single source, never a trend.
- Missing records run as `Held` — the honest question in the slot —
  never covered over.
- Other-desk stories get a venue and an owner, never copy.
- Every figure shows its own working; every filed pitch gets a desk
  line and an assignment.
- A thin packet still gets a page. The send-back is for the wrong
  kind of document only.
