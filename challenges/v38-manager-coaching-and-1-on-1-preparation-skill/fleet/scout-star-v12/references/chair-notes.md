# Worked example

## A worked week (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

Signals across the week: a dental-clinic hygienist, four years in. Book: the sterilizer service is overdue, two supplier callbacks unanswered; a patient letter names her — "the calmest hands I have sat in"; the manager's fire-safety walkthrough sits NOT STARTED. Messages: she flagged the sterilizer Monday, re-flagged Thursday, asked Wednesday about the sedation-assist qualification. Draft agenda: "sterilizer update; patient feedback; qualification; rota."

## Weighing the draft

1. "sterilizer update" — **Signals:** service overdue, two supplier callbacks unanswered, her Monday flag. **Call:** **Blocked** — supplier and your diary hold it; commit a date.
2. "patient feedback" — **Signals:** a letter naming her: "the calmest hands I have sat in". **Call:** **Recognition** — open with the words.
3. "qualification" — **Signals:** she asked about sedation-assist training Wednesday. **Call:** **Career** — she raised it; give a real answer on funding and cover.
4. "rota" — **Signals:** next week's rota posted, no gaps flagged. **Call:** **Status** — one line.

**Missed by the draft:** your fire-safety walkthrough, NOT STARTED three weeks. **Figures:** patients seen 96 against 100 booked (4 short, two were cancellations).

# 1-on-1 brief: Priya Nair x her manager — Friday

**Context:** Four years in; a patient wrote in about her this week.

**1. The letter — Recognition, ~3 min**
A patient called her "the calmest hands I have sat in" — read the words aloud, ask what she does to settle people.

**2. Sterilizer — Blocked, ~7 min**
Two supplier callbacks unanswered and service overdue. Tell her when you will chase it and the date you are holding them to.

**3. Qualification — Career, ~10 min**
She raised sedation-assist training. Say what the practice can fund and when cover allows.

**4. Rota and numbers — Status, ~3 min**
96 of 100 booked; rota clean. One line.

**Set aside:** "rota" stayed, compressed; nothing parked beyond that. **Before the meeting:** call the supplier today; book the fire-safety walkthrough before month's end. **Thinking:** two labels were memory softening the record — a supplier hold-up is not an update, and a named letter is not "feedback". Your own walkthrough was the real fourth item.
