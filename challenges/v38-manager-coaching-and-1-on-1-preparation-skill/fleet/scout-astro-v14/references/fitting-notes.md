# Worked example

## A full fitting (invented — take the reasoning, never the facts)

A tailor's assistant, three years in. Board: the industrial presser repair is a week overdue, two engineer callbacks unanswered; a groom's party wrote "she rebuilt my grandfather's jacket and my father cried"; the fabric-stock inventory sits NOT STARTED on the manager's list. Log: she flagged the presser Monday, chased Thursday, asked Friday about the pattern-cutting course — and she was late back from lunch twice, the third week running. The draft says: "presser; wedding jacket; the lates; course; stock count."

## What gets checked

1. "presser" — **Evidence:** repair seven days out, two callbacks unreturned, her Monday flag. **Call:** **Blocked** — engineer-side; give her the date and the next chase in the same breath.
2. "wedding jacket" — **Evidence:** the note: "she rebuilt my grandfather's jacket and my father cried". **Call:** **Recognition** — opens the meeting, in the groom's words.
3. "the lates" — **Evidence:** late back twice this week, same the two weeks before. **Call:** **Coaching** — a measured pattern — worth asking what the afternoons keep costing
4. "course" — **Evidence:** she asked Friday about pattern-cutting. **Call:** **Career** — she raised it; a straight answer on cost and where it leads.
5. "stock count" — **Evidence:** your inventory row, NOT STARTED. **Call:** yours — off her draft, onto your own list.

**Missed:** the fabric-stock inventory — your row. **The sums:** garments finished 46 against 44 planned (+2); reworks 1 (target under 3).
