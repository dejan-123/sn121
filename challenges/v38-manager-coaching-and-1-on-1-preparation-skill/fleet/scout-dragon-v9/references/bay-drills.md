# Bay drills — case specs

Five invented bookings worked end to end. Names, figures and
products are invented; the diagnoses are the part to carry.

---

## Case A — normal week, one mis-described job

**Report:** Lena, senior platform engineer.
**Booked:** (a) "Lena's friction with security — disputed
their findings in the shared channel"; (b) "why is the
identity migration behind"; (c) "call out the backup test".
**Record:** security lead posted public thanks naming Lena
for flagging an auth bypass pre-release; her objection —
blunt, on substance — was to a finding she had shown was a
false positive; migration 4 of 11 services done, stalled on
a vendor amendment filed 13 days ago; restore test ran in
22 minutes against a 90-minute target.

**Diagnoses:**
- (a) Re-diagnosed → one-time note, one narrow channel
  question: "The security exchange was on the merits —
  they were already thanking you for the bypass catch. One
  thing: it ran in the shared channel rather than on the
  finding ticket. Does moving it there cost anything?"
  Substance stands; channel takes one question.
- (b) Re-diagnosed → another shop's job. 4 of 11 done,
  stalled on a 13-day amendment: "I'll chase the vendor
  today for a date; who do I escalate to if it slips
  again?"
- (c) Fixed first. Public thanks plus 22 vs 90 minutes —
  two attributable credits lead.
- Every booked job: a booking line and a diagnosis. Nothing
  booked disappears.

**Sheet:** `# Job sheet: Lena, senior platform engineer` —
picture line, worked figures, work order, service notes,
manager's own jobs, referred list, foreman's close.

## Case B — the self-diagnosed pattern

**Report:** Priya, product designer.
**Booked:** (a) "Priya keeps blowing estimates — 2 days
became 4, then 3 became 5"; (b) "usability study came back
3 of 5".
**Record:** both sprints slipped — a real pattern — but her
own note shows she diagnosed it: scope grew 14 → 23 screens
mid-sprint; "tracking scope for next time." Eng lead
publicly praised her "cleanest handoff this quarter."
Manager's self-review feedback for Priya: NOT STARTED.

**Diagnoses:**
- (a) Re-diagnosed → upstream behaviour. Pattern real,
  cause already found — the job coaches what precedes the
  slip: "Your scope log is the right instinct. 14 → 23
  screens — when did you first see it growing, and what
  would flagging it then have changed for the estimate?"
  One anchored question on earlier flagging — never a
  lecture about estimates she already explained.
- (b) Fixed — light status. 3 of 5 with a rescheduled
  session is a status line, not a coaching job.
- Recognition: Fixed first — "cleanest handoff this
  quarter" is attributable credit.
- Manager's own jobs: the NOT STARTED self-review appears
  plainly — "I owe you the review write-up; I'll have it
  before Friday" — a manager-owned item with a date, never
  omitted for comfort.

## Case C — thin booking, worked honestly

**Report:** Omar, staff engineer.
**Booked:** "prep my weekly with Omar — his path to
principal, and whether he can own the search rebuild." No
action log, figures, or excerpts. One note: Omar asked in
passing about the bonus cycle.

Thin is not invalid — the booking aims at the coming
meeting, so it is still this week's work. Only a wrong-kind
document (Case D) goes back. First line:
`# Job sheet: Omar, staff engineer`.

**Diagnoses:**
- Path to principal: Fixed — split by shop. Omar's
  direction and the readiness question stay in the meeting
  with real time: "You asked about the next level — here's
  the gap evidence I see and what I'd need documented; what
  scope would you take to build it?" The bonus question is
  Referred — it sits with the comp review; destination and
  owner named, one line, no service notes.
- Rebuild readiness: Awaiting parts. No delivery record; it
  stands as the honest question: "I don't have your recent
  record in front of me — walk me through the last few
  weeks: shipped, stuck, and what you'd take on."
- Nothing invented to fill the sheet. A thin sheet of
  honest questions beats a padded one.

## Case D — old notes, returned whole

**Booking:** "Here are the minutes from this week's 1-on-1
with Grace — please prep next week's agenda from them."
Three open actions: Grace's deprecation plan (due Friday),
the manager's budget confirmation, a rota follow-up.

**Whole booking returned — no jobs.** Under 150 words,
titled `# Not this week's work`:

Minutes of a meeting already held — they record what was
agreed, not what has moved since. This supports a follow-up
on the open actions, not a sheet: Grace's deprecation plan
(due Friday), your budget confirmation, the rota follow-up —
owners and dates exactly as written. For a sheet, send this
week's action items, any figures, and whatever Grace raised
or you want addressed. Nothing in the material is logged
after that meeting, so a sheet would replay finished jobs
or invent unbooked ones.

Personal matters in the minutes stay private — not
repeated, not summarized, not hinted at.

## Case E — the queue that is the manager's own job

**Report:** Sana, senior backend engineer.
**Booked:** (a) "Sana must clear her review queue — six
specs wait on her, two teams blocked"; (b) "Sana seemed
flat in demos".
**Record:** the six specs sit with her because the process
names her sole approver for cross-team work; she shipped
two features this sprint and raised the queue in retro
herself, proposing a rotation; "flat" appears once, in the
manager's note.

**Diagnoses:**
- (a) Re-diagnosed → manager's capacity job. A
  sole-approver rule stalling two teams is owned by the
  manager, early on the sheet: "Your rotation proposal is
  the right starting point — I'll approve it this week and
  take it to the team leads. What load should each reviewer
  hold so nothing queues behind one person again?"
- (b) Declined. One unsourced observation; the sprint
  shows two shipped features and a raised process fix. Its
  absence is logged plainly.
- The manager's own open item — acting on the retro
  proposal — sits in manager's own jobs with whom and when.

---

## Constants across all five

- The record decides; the booking is only a description.
- Attributable credit is fixed first.
- A self-diagnosed pattern coaches the upstream behaviour,
  never the symptom.
- Justified substance keeps its slot; the channel takes one
  question.
- Another shop's job refers there.
- One occurrence is never a pattern.
- Missing records stand `Awaiting parts` — the honest
  question — never covered over.
- Manager-owned items appear even when marked not begun.
- Every figure shows its arithmetic; every booked job gets
  a booking line and a diagnosis.
- A thin booking still gets a sheet. The return is for the
  wrong document only — under 150 words, no garage
  structure.
