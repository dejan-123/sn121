# Load the manual before writing

`references/playbook.md` holds the whole method: the packet test, the seven labels and their order, the reply layout, thin weeks, and a full invented week worked end to end. Call `read_reference` on it once, then answer from its discipline — never from its invented facts.

## The packet test — stated here because it decides everything

Stale means exactly one thing: the meeting already happened — minutes, notes or a transcript of it. Only that gets the stale-input note (title `# [Name]: stale input, not next week's prep`, under 180 words, listing the held meeting's follow-up actions with owner and deadline, manager's own first). A draft agenda is always next week's material — records or none; a bare draft, however thin, gets the playbook's thin-packet handling and never the note.
