# Inspection drills — full manifests on invented shipments

Four invented filings, cleared the way the manifest should clear
them. The names, figures and products are invented; the clearances
are the part to carry.

## Drill 1 — a normal shipment with a mis-declared item

Packet: report is "Marta, senior platform engineer". Manager's draft:
(a) "discipline issue — Marta snapped at QA in the team channel",
(b) "why is the payments migration late", (c) "quick win — mention
the dashboard launch". The findings: QA's lead posted a public
thank-you naming Marta for unblocking their release by building the
replay tool early; the channel exchange shows Marta objecting —
sharply but on the substance — to QA shipping a build that failed
its own gate; the migration is 3 of 8 endpoints done, waiting on a
security sign-off requested 9 days ago; the dashboard launched and
the support lead messaged that ticket volume on it halved in week
one.

Clearances:

- Item (a): **Re-declared**. One substantive objection to a build
  that failed its own checks — declared as a discipline issue,
  released as a one-time note with one narrow question. Entry note:
  "The QA exchange was on the merits — QA was already thanking you
  for the release help. One thing to keep in view: that disagreement
  ran in the team channel rather than the ticket. Does moving it
  there cost you anything?" The objection stands; the channel takes
  one question and no more.
- Item (b): **Re-declared — another authority's cargo.** 3 of 8
  done, and the stall is a 9-day-old sign-off with security, not
  Marta's pace. Entry note: "Migration is waiting on security's
  sign-off — nine days outstanding. I'll chase the reviewer today
  and get a date; who should I escalate to if it slips again?"
- Item (c): **Released to lead.** Two attributable credits — the QA
  lead's public thanks and the support figure. Open with them before
  any process item; credit clears first.
- Every filed declaration gets a manifest line and a clearance;
  nothing filed disappears.

Manifest: `# Manifest: Marta, senior platform engineer` — picture
line ("a strong week with one clearance waiting on another
authority"), figures shown as work, then clearance order with all
three declarations, the finding behind each, and each clearance —
then entry notes, pre-clearance, bonded list, officer's note.

## Drill 2 — a thin filing processed honestly

Packet: report is "Ilya, staff engineer". Manager's note: "prep my
weekly with Ilya — want to talk about his promo timeline, and whether
he's ready to own the ingest rewrite." No action log, no figures, no
channel excerpts. One calendar item: Ilya asked in passing about
salary bands for the next level.

Thin is not invalid: the filing aims at the coming meeting, so the
manifest still logs it — cleared declaration by declaration. Only
the wrong kind of filing (drill 3) is turned back whole. The reply
opens `# Manifest: Ilya, staff engineer` — never a turn-back
preface.

Clearances:

- Promo timeline: **Released — split by authority.** Ilya's
  direction and the manager's readiness question belong in this
  meeting, with real time: "You asked about the next level — here's
  the gap evidence I see and what I'd need documented; what scope
  would you take to build it?" The pay-band question is **Bonded
  onward**: bands are decided in the compensation review — the
  manifest names the office (the comp review with the comp partner)
  and the owner, one line, no entry notes.
- Readiness for the ingest rewrite: **Inspection**. The filing has
  no delivery record to weigh. It clears as the honest question, not
  an assessment invented for the slot: "I don't have your recent
  delivery record in front of me — walk me through the last few
  weeks: what shipped, what's stuck, what you'd take on."
- Nothing else is fabricated to fill the manifest. A thin manifest
  of honest questions beats a padded one; the manifest head logs
  what the filing lacks and pre-clearance lists what to pull.

## Drill 3 — a filing that is not this week's shipment

Packet: "Here are the minutes from this week's 1-on-1 with Dana —
please prep next week's agenda from them." The minutes list three
actions: Dana to send the benchmarking doc (due Thursday), the
manager to confirm the conference budget, a follow-up on the
mentoring pilot.

Clearance: **whole filing turned back — nothing declared.** The
reply, under 150 words, titled `# Not this week's shipment`:

Minutes of a meeting already held — they record what was agreed, not
what has moved since. The document this supports is a follow-up on
the open actions, not a manifest: Dana's benchmarking doc (due
Thursday), your budget confirmation, the mentoring pilot follow-up —
owners and dates exactly as filed. For a weekly manifest, send this
week's action log, any figures, and whatever Dana raised or you want
declared. Nothing in the material is dated after that meeting, so a
manifest would replay old declarations or invent new ones.

Any personal matter in the minutes stays private — not repeated, not
summarized, not hinted at.

## Drill 4 — the queue that is the manager's own cargo

Packet: report is "Sana, senior backend engineer". Manager's draft:
(a) "remind Sana to clear her review queue — five design docs are
waiting on her and two partner teams are blocked"; (b) "Sana seems
quiet in standup lately". The findings: the five docs are assigned
because the process names her sole approver for cross-team changes;
she shipped two features this sprint and flagged the queue in the
last retro, proposing a rotation; "quiet" appears once, in the
manager's note.

Clearances:

- Item (a): **Re-declared — the authority that can clear it is the
  manager.** A sole-approver rule stalling two teams is a capacity
  matter the manager owns, not a declaration to work faster. It is
  released early as the manager's own decision: "Your rotation
  proposal is the right starting point — I want to approve it this
  week and take it to the partner leads. What load should each
  reviewer hold so nothing queues behind one person again?"
- Item (b): **Refused.** One manager observation with no findings
  behind it; the sprint shows two shipped features and a raised
  process fix. The manifest logs its absence plainly rather than
  inventing a wellbeing segment.
- The manager's own open item — acting on Sana's retro proposal —
  is listed under pre-clearance as the manager's commitment, with
  whom and when.

## What every drill shares

- The findings clear; the declaration is only the filing.
- Attributable credit clears first — a named person, a concrete
  result.
- A justified matter raised in the wrong channel keeps its substance
  and takes exactly one channel question.
- What another authority clears is logged to that authority.
- One occurrence is a single finding, never a pattern.
- Missing findings clear as `Inspection` — the honest question in
  the slot — never covered over.
- Other-authority items get an office and an owner, never entry
  notes.
- Every figure shows its own working; every filed declaration gets a
  manifest line and a clearance.
- A thin filing still gets a manifest. The turn-back is for the
  wrong kind of filing only — and it stays under 150 words.
