# Run this list in order

Draft agendas get three ordinary things wrong. Memory **flattens** — the held-up job comes out as "status". Memory **softens** — the client's written compliment comes out as "went well". Memory **blurs** — one bad afternoon comes out as "a pattern". The list below corrects all three.

## The list

**Step 1 — Read the week's material, not the draft.** Tickets, messages, reviews, figures, praise. Anything open, overdue or NOT STARTED counts open; anything closed stays closed unless something newer directly contradicts it — the old complaint never reopens it. Collect every open row the manager owns. Collect every piece of praise that landed by name. A figure that exists only in the manager's head does not exist.

**Step 2 — Take each draft item in turn and pick its call:**

- **Recognition** — written praise exists: a note, a thanks, work named and praised. Opens the meeting in the giver's own words, the figure attached.
- **Blocked** — another desk holds it: an approval not given, a dependency gone quiet, more requests than one pair of hands. The manager leaves carrying the move. If the trail shows it wider than the draft admits — other teams queued, an exec already copied — say so and move it up, whatever the draft's priority.
- **Coaching** — one behaviour on two or more separate dated occasions, or a goal the report chose. Questions, never lectures. When the report already found the cause and tracks it, coach the step before it — spot it sooner — anchored to real figures, not the symptom.
- **Career** — the report themselves raised growth, training, scope or review timing. One sincere question; promise only what is keepable.
- **Status** — nothing to repair, praise or settle. Last, squeezed to a line or two.
- **Parked** — its room is elsewhere: pay, HR, a dispute a different team owns, next quarter. Sent there with a line saying why. Personal matters stay private — "the personal matter", elaborated never.

**Step 3 — Apply the finer corrections.** A tone complaint tracing to another desk's dependency is the hold-up talking — Blocked, not Coaching. Repeated flags on one problem count once. A vibe with nothing under it is dropped, signals named. A slip the report found and fixed themselves is a quick word, not a coaching slot. Recognition opens only on a quotable instance; when none exists, a line above item one admits it.

**Step 4 — Print the sorting.** `## Sorting the items` — one numbered entry per draft item, in the draft's own order: its wording quoted; **Shown:** who said it and where it was logged, when, and the row or figure beside it; **Call:** the call and the reason — "stands" when the draft was right — or parked, dropped, merged into a sibling. Then **Missed:** — what the manager owes or the draft forgot — and **Worked out:** any arithmetic, from its operands — "97.1 against a 96 floor is +1.1 points" — points, never percent.

**Step 5 — Write the page.** `# 1-on-1 brief: [Report] x [Manager] — [date]` — 350–700 words. **Context:** one line — report, tenure, the week in a phrase. Items in meeting order, `**name — Call, ~N min**`, a sentence or two of things to say and ask, real numbers and names inside. Then **Left off** (parked or dropped, with reasons), **Before the meeting** (every open manager row, each a concrete move — done before the report hears a single ask, most of all a row an item stands on), and **Why this order** (a few lines on why the calls landed).

**Step 6 — Thin packet? Still a page.** The sorting names what never arrived; the brief asks rather than asserts — what cannot be seen, the question to put, what to listen for. Invent nothing.

A complete week run through the list — draft, sorting, page — sits in `references/depot-notes.md`. Same three lies, same corrections; the reasoning transfers, the facts do not.

# Not a 1-on-1 prep request

When what arrives is minutes, a transcript, or notes of a conversation already had — whatever its label — the reply is one short note, under 180 words, no sorting section:

- Say it straight: this looks backward at a meeting, not forward to one — name the document and the meeting it records.
- Where open actions survive inside, write the follow-up — owner, action, due date as given — in a line or two; no brief, no re-sort of old items.
- Preparation wants the present week — open items, current figures, flags raised since.
- Personal material stays "the personal matter", nothing more.
