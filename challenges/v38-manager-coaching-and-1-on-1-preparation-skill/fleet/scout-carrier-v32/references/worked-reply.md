# One invented week answered in this voice — borrow the shape, never the facts

Everyone below is made up. What transfers is the form: a calling that walks the draft in order, items phrased like a colleague who did the homework, questions written as words the manager can speak. In a live answer the facts all come from the packet supplied.

## The answer

## Calling the draft

A draft, a log, one figure pair and the week's threads arrived for a session still to come — this is prep.

1. "shout-out for the week" — vague until Monday: the head keeper posted publicly that her team rerouted the flooded walkway crowd in ten minutes, so smoothly that guests assumed it was planned. Fanfare on that signal; it leads.
2. "Status: otter-habitat filtration" — the parts order slipped to next week and she has already split the cycle across the backup pump. Not a status question: a Snag on the parts supplier, her split is the remedy to back, the move a confirmed delivery.
3. "Coaching: terse with school groups" — one dated exchange Wednesday, after two classes arrived at once. A Trip: recount it, ask once, no verdict.
4. "Status: member-renewal calls" — 4,120 visitors on a 4,400 forecast and the renewal question sits inside the gap, yet nothing says who owns the calling or whether it repeats. Thin evidence on a real question: a Stir pinned to the 280-visitor shortfall.
5. "she asked about the guest-programmes lead role" — her own ask last month. North; the walkway reroute is the signal to set beside what the role wants.
6. "personal — her visa paperwork" — the paperwork is private and she has already told HR what she needs. Aside: the immigration process, starting with confirming dates at HR; nothing gets written on it in this room.

Not drafted: the overdue kiosk-layout approval is the manager's own row. Figures run: 4,120 on a 4,400 forecast leaves 280 short, and the renewal question lives inside that gap.

# Priya Nair with her manager — the invisible save and the drifting filter

**The shape of it.** The aquarium drew 4,120 visitors on a 4,400 forecast (280 short), and Monday gave the week its headline: the walkway flood crowd rerouted in ten minutes, so cleanly the head keeper said guests assumed it was planned. Otter filtration drifts on a delayed parts order — her split across the backup pump is already holding — and the kiosk-layout approval waiting on you is overdue.

**Your plate.** Approve the new ticketing kiosk layout — overdue, and the school-group squeeze is exactly what it relieves. Bring it or set the day.

## Run of the room

**1. The walkway save — Fanfare**
Open on the head keeper's public words — a save so smooth the guests never noticed is invisible by design, so having it named in the open is the recognition itself. Then ask how her team drilled for it; a ten-minute reroute is a method worth writing down before it fades.

**2. Otter filtration — Snag**
The parts order slipped to next week and her split across the backup pump is holding meanwhile — the workaround is hers, what remains is yours. Pin the delivery to a confirmed date and tell her plainly the split is approved; she should not carry the gap quietly.

**3. Renewal calls — Stir**
280 short and the member renewals sit unowned inside it — one question: "where do the renewal calls actually stall, and whose desk catches them?" If the same gap shows next week it earns its own item.

**4. Wednesday — Trip**
A brisk word after two classes arrived at once — one dated moment in a week she otherwise ran clean. One light question covers it: "have the arrival slots stopped overlapping since Wednesday?" A single moment never earns a verdict.

**5. Guest-programmes lead — North**
- "The walkway save is the clearest thing on your record this quarter — which parts of the lead role does it already prove, and which does it leave open?"
- "You asked about the role yourself — where would you want it to take you?"

## Sent off

Her visa paperwork — private, and HR already has what they need; the step is confirming dates so the rota can plan around it. Nothing on it is drafted here.

**The calls, accounted.** The shout-out stays, anchored — the keeper named the save in public. Filtration is a hold on an outside supplier, not a status, and her split is the remedy to back. The lead role is hers and stands on the week's clearest signal. The visa paperwork belongs to HR, not this room.
