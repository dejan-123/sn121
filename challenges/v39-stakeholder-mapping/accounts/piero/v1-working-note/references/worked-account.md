# Desk notes: three replies in full (invented accounts; imitate the shape)

Every name and figure below is invented. The working form is what
transfers: the reply begins with the reading — first characters
`## Reading the inputs`, or `# ` when the request is for another
artefact entirely — every read lands on the record before a tier is
set, and every row, gap, and step carries its signal. Never the
inflation, always the candid line. A real input supplies its own
facts.

## Coverage gaps

- **The signer is the quietest seat in the file.** Osagie's gate is
  not optional and the record shows 2 replies in 9 attempts, 47 days —
  while a colleague scopes the alternative. Route: through Kaloudis's
  paper, the only channel proven to reach her.
- **The rival is unmet and internal.** Tomas's build has a Christmas
  deadline and no record — you cannot out-sequence a colleague, you
  arm the comparison. Route: Kaloudis, who knows what the scoping
  exercise will claim.
- **The operating layer is cold.** Ferrand's sites run the tills and
  he has spent twenty minutes on the deal — the adoption question sits
  with him and he is not answering it.
- No legal review, no procurement lane has appeared; at a 28-site
  private chain the CFO counter-signature is the approval step —
  noted, not invented.

## Engagement plan

1. Build the CFO one-pager for Kaloudis to place — subscription
   economics against a build's true cost in her numbers where his
   file supplies them; the January board is the gate and the artefact
   earns the seat.
2. Ask Kaloudis what the in-house scoping exercise will claim — the
   unmet rival's argument, surfaced through the person who hears it,
   answered in the same one-pager.
3. Answer Eklund's staff objection where she can test it — a two-site
   staff walkthrough of the till flow; her scepticism, met honestly,
   converts or stays honestly recorded.
4. Send Ferrand's duty manager the ops checklist directly — the door
   he delegates through is the one to use; a fifth invite to him
   fires nothing.
5. Keep Straub's closure in the pack — his written sign-off is the
   compliance line the CFO reads; one sentence, already earned.

## Executive sponsor

Nobody qualifies yet — the candid read. Kaloudis carries the deal
below a $200K gate he cannot pass; Osagie holds the authority and has
answered twice in nine tries. The sponsor this deal needs is Osagie
converted, and the only road shown to reach her is Kaloudis's paper —
steps 1 and 2 exist to make someone qualify.

## Why this reads this way

The roster puts the CFO first; the record puts her second and cold.
Kaloudis's 13 of 14 and owned introductions read advocate, whatever
his director title understates. Ferrand reads non-responsive on his
own attendance, not on suspicion. Straub reads closed because his own
writing closed him. Tomas takes no row because he holds no record —
the unmet rival lives in the gaps with the door named.
