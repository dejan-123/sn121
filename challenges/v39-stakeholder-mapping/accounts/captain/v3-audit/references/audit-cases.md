# Audit cases — one full audit, then the trigger library

The account below is invented; the form is what transfers — every
name, number, title and date in a real reply comes from the record in
front of you, never from here. After the audit, the trigger library:
fragments of record with the disposition each one fires, covering the
situations a real record throws at you.

## Coverage gaps

- **The signer has never engaged.** $510K sits above Whitlock's
  $300K line — Vail signs, and he has no record. Route: Kamara's
  decision pack, which Whitlock herself commissioned — the pack
  earns the room.
- **The estates director is a stated stakeholder with no contact.**
  Whitlock named the dependency; a rollout-plan review is the door.
- **The deal is single-threaded.** Every live line runs through
  Kamara — Whitlock's endorsement is real but the working
  relationship is all his. If he stalls, the deal stalls.

## Engagement plan

1. Build the decision pack Whitlock asked for — the
   maintenance-backlog audit, the rollout plan, the signature
   requirement. Her own commission is the vehicle; it answers her
   March ask and opens the CFO door in one move.
2. Ask Kamara to put the rollout plan in front of his director — he
   owns that relationship; the plan already exists in step 1.
3. Deliver Trask's onboarding review — his is open and it gates any
   PO; confirm nothing sits unreturned on our side first.
4. When the pack lands, ask Whitlock — through Kamara — for the CFO
   introduction. Not a cold note to Vail: mail he never expected
   asks a stranger for the deal, and it tells Kamara we walked past
   him.

## Executive sponsor

No active sponsor today. Diana Whitlock is the candidate — a lapsed
sponsor: the endorsement is on record but second-hand now that
Kamara owns the work; his decision pack is the route back to her
attention. Vail is the sponsor the deal will eventually need, and he
is nobody to us yet. On our side: if Whitlock re-engages, a peer
path could open — none exists today.

## Calls worth a note

- Whitlock's title outranks Kamara's; the counts reverse the seat —
  the delegation call, not the org-chart assumption.
- Vail has no record — he enters through the gap, not the table.
- Marsh's check reads done because her own writing closed it — a
  closed gatekeeper is resting, not quiet; nothing re-confirms it.
- Still open: whether Whitlock still intends to appear when the
  pack lands. The pack itself is the test — her response to it
  settles the question.

## The trigger library

Signal on top, the firing underneath — grouped by what it settles.

### Champion or not

- **7/9 replies; set up the pilot; forwards internals unasked** →
  champion / high. Owned acts make the call, not warmth.
- **Answers everything, starts nothing, three months on** →
  evaluator / rising. A friend, not a champion — champions spend
  first; the note says so plainly.
- **"He'll take it to the board," three weeks, no introduction** →
  champion / faint — untested on access. One test fires with it:
  ask who it reached; if it went nowhere, plan around him rather
  than through him.
- **Champion quiet since the budget meeting** → champion / gone
  quiet — the silence is a fact, the reason is not. Write it as an
  open question, not a story.

### Who signs

- **"Anything under $25K lands on my desk," deal is $18K** → she
  fires champion AND economic buyer — one chair, no executive gap,
  no procurement layer invented; the gap section says coverage fits.
- **"The CFO signs anything above $300K," deal $510K, CFO never
  met** → he is the buyer; no row — `never met` is a sentence and
  the access gap, with the brokered route written.
- **VP signed last year's $90K pilot; silent 90 days** → the seat
  is still his on record — the signature proves authority, not
  warmth. `gone quiet`; the plan routes back through the champion,
  never direct.
- **Endorsed in March, work handed to her director** →
  `economic buyer / lapsed` — the candidate. The director fires
  champion and is the working owner. "Endorsed — delegated to
  <name>" in the role cell; never "the one who signs," and the
  champion is never the candidate.
- **"Send me the business case," an executive never met** → a
  request, not an endorsement. `never met` fires alone — nothing
  was handed down, no candidate exists; the seat needs earning and
  the document still travels through the champion who brokered it.

### Checks and gates

- **"Satisfied for now," in the reviewer's own writing** →
  gatekeeper / closed. A finished check is a resting relationship —
  done, not quiet — and no move re-confirms what nothing doubts.
- **Procurement asked for the vendor packet; we haven't sent it** →
  our debt — first among the gaps, first among the moves. A gate
  holds an order; it cannot choose you — report what it demanded,
  nothing more.
- **Reviewer stopped answering** → gatekeeper / faint — reach the
  colleague who works beside them, not a third unanswered email
  into the same inbox.

### Reading the exchange

- **Warm meeting notes, silent inbox** → both post: acts over
  words, recent over old. Strength drops a grade; the next
  scheduled touch is the settling action.
- **No reply in 58 days, seven sends** → gone cold or gone quiet —
  check who wrote last: their unanswered message is our lapse and
  leads the plan; ours is their cooling.
- **"We're pausing vendor conversations"** → gone cold — stated
  plainly, facts only, no invented hostility in the telling.
- **Zero touches, or a name held only in others' words** → never
  met — a sentence, never a row: "the COO," "the team," "demo
  attendees" are not names.
- **A stage label a newer note disagrees with** → post the note,
  flag the stale label — never average them into a stage the record
  never said.

### Gaps and their scale

- **$18K deal, signer in hand, one quiet colleague** → `no material
  gaps`. Not single-thread — no decision sits elsewhere. The quiet
  colleague is a note, not a gap; no move is spent confirming what
  nothing doubts.
- **$510K, every live line through one champion** →
  `single-thread`, first gap — her warmth is real but the working
  route is all hers.
- **Amount above the engaged authority, no route to the holder** →
  the signature gap, named plainly with the route that exists.
- **A named competitor still in evaluation** → urgency context —
  it outranks tidy admin in the order, and it is not a missing
  person.

### The order of moves

- **We owe documents, they owe nothing** → the debt leads. Nothing
  is asked of them while our side is open.
- **Dormant signer, live champion** → the re-warm fires through
  her: a one-page brief in the executive's own language she can
  carry upward. The cold note is never the move.
- **Unnamed attendees at the demo** → ask the champion for names —
  widening starts with knowing who was in the room.
- **Two moves both look first** → whatever unblocks the most ranks
  higher; say what the top move waits on.
- **A faint or ambiguous firing still standing** → its settling
  action becomes a step, stated as a test not a task.

### The sponsor call

- **Endorsed then handed down** → no active sponsor; the endorser
  is the candidate, the route is her delegate.
- **Buyer never met, champion below the line** → the seat needs
  earning — who it takes and the step that earns it.
- **$18K inside the buyer's own authority** → the buyer is the
  sponsor; nobody above is named.
- **Our side** → an executive of ours goes in only when a peer on
  their side and a path both show in the record; otherwise the void
  fires with what is missing.

### Thin and wrong inputs

- **Activity described but not supplied** → the evidence opens with
  what is missing, and each thin contact keeps the trigger
  that would settle it plus one askable line.
- **"Sketch me their org structure" on a contact record** → the
  redirect fires alone: a ~180-word note at `# <Account>: org
  chart — not what this record holds` — asked against arrived;
  what a chart needs and where it lives (their directory, not our
  inbox); what this record can build instead, categories only; and
  the precise paste that would let the map exist. No name, title or
  count from the record appears, and no map is produced.
