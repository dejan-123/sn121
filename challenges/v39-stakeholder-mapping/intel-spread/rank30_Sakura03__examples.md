# Four worked cases (invented; form only)

Every company, person, date and number below is invented. The setting is one vendor selling crew-rostering software to container shipping lines. Copy the shape of the reasoning, never a fact: in a real reply every name, count and date comes from the input in front of us.

## Case A: large late-stage deal, dormant approver, open gate

**Input (abridged).** Tasmar Ocean Carriers, 40 vessels. Deal: $1.6M ARR, fleet-wide crew rostering and rest-hour compliance. Stage: final evaluation, contract targeted next quarter. Contacts: Ingrid Vahl, Director Crew Operations (we sent 14, she replied 12, last two-way 5 days ago); Marten Solberg, Finance Director (cc'd on the first intro email in May, no reply, no meeting, 118 days); Joaquim Reis, Fleet Personnel Analyst (sent 6, replies 5, last 9 days ago); Petra Lindqvist, Vendor Risk Manager (sent 3, replies 2, last 16 days ago); Hanne Brekke, Chief Operating Officer (sent 5, replied once, a thank-you on 19 Jul after a briefing Ingrid arranged, saying she was "keen to see this through"; silent 64 days; CRM tag: "Executive sponsor"). Notes: 3 Aug, Ingrid set up discovery, sent last season's overtime data unprompted. 24 Aug, Ingrid said she approves crewing spend "up to $350K; above that it goes to Marten." 15 Sep, Ingrid ran an internal briefing using our deck and introduced Joaquim. 6 Oct, Petra said a vendor security assessment must be completed before any contract is signed; it has not been returned. 2 Nov, Ingrid: "our CTO will want to see the integration plan." No CTO contact exists.

**Reply.**

The vendor security assessment is still open and blocks the contract, and the CTO Ingrid mentioned has never been introduced. Input is rich on Ingrid and thin on everyone above her, and the record on Hanne Brekke contradicts itself.

## Coverage gaps

Needed for this deal: at $1.6M late stage, the budget holder above $350K, the champion, the data evaluator, the vendor-risk gate, and the technical executive Ingrid says will review integration.

- **Single-threaded.** Only Ingrid can reach Marten Solberg, the one person who can say yes at this size. Joaquim and Petra answer us, but neither touches the budget; if Ingrid stalls or leaves, we have no route to the approver.
- **Open gate.** The vendor security assessment Petra requested on 6 Oct has not been returned; no contract until it is.
- **Unintroduced technical executive.** "Our CTO" (Ingrid, 2 Nov) will review the integration plan; no name or contact on record.
- **Unsettled sponsor.** Hanne Brekke's tag and her silence disagree; see step 3.

## Engagement plan

1. **Marten Solberg, through Ingrid Vahl (single-thread repair):** ask Ingrid to introduce us to Marten in a 30-minute business-case review she hosts, built on her own overtime data. Signal: her $350K ceiling (24 Aug) and Marten's 118 days of silence. Dependency: none; this comes first. No direct email to Marten.
2. **Petra Lindqvist (open gate):** complete and return the vendor security assessment, then confirm with Petra in writing that nothing else is outstanding. Signal: her 6 Oct condition. Dependency: none; run alongside step 1.
3. **Hanne Brekke, through Ingrid (settles the conflict):** ask Ingrid one direct question: is Hanne still backing this, and will she sit in on the review with Marten? A yes and her attendance move her to sponsor; a no or no answer drops the CRM tag. Signal: the "Executive sponsor" tag against 1 reply in 5 sends and 64 days of silence. Dependency: in the same conversation as step 1; no direct email to Hanne.
4. **CTO (gap), through Ingrid:** ask Ingrid for the CTO's name and a slot to walk through the integration plan with Joaquim present. Signal: her 2 Nov remark. Dependency: after step 1 is booked, so Ingrid is not asked for two favours at once.
5. **Joaquim Reis:** give him the integration plan draft to check against his data before the CTO session. Signal: 5 of 6 replies, data role. Dependency: before step 4.

## Executive sponsor recommendation

Marten Solberg is the right sponsor on authority, since every amount above $350K goes to him, but nothing on record shows he knows the deal exists. Ingrid Vahl is our sponsor in practice and the only route to him. Hanne Brekke is not yet a sponsor, whatever the CRM says, until step 3 shows she is still engaged. If Ingrid cannot get Marten into a review this month, the deal has no approver engaged and the close date should move.
