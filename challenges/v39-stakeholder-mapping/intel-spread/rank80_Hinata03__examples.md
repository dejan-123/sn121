# Worked examples: stakeholder maps for a maintenance-software vendor

Three invented cases. Case 1 shows a conflict about one person and a single thread to a dormant approver; Case 2 a small deal kept small; Case 3 a thin packet with one contact. The seller offers asset-maintenance software (work orders, condition tracking, capital planning) to regional water utilities. Each case shows the pasted input, then the full document. The names, utilities and figures are made up; compare shapes, not facts.

---

## Coverage gaps

Needed for this deal: an $860K late-stage purchase at a 410-staff public utility needs the approver who takes it to the board, finance sign-off on cost, a champion, the operations users, and the security and purchasing gates. Against that roster, the approver is dormant and finance is missing.

- **Single-threaded on the approver.** Gideon has not answered 4 emails since 2026-08-29, and the only person who still reaches him is Ines. Rosalind, Kwabena and Thelma answer us, but none has shown a line to him. This is a structural risk, not one more missing seat: if Ines leaves or stalls, we lose the route to the decision.
- **Finance Director (unnamed, not introduced).** Ines says this person must see lifecycle-cost numbers before Gideon goes to the board (2026-11-13). No name, no contact, no record of their view.
- **The board** approves the deal on 2026-12-09; we have no direct line and should not seek one; Gideon carries it.
- **Open gates:** Kwabena's four network questions (unanswered by us since 2026-11-04) and Thelma's review of our 2026-11-06 redlines.

## Engagement plan

1. **Ines Marrow, for Gideon (repairs the single thread and settles the conflict):** reply to her unanswered 2026-11-13 note with the lifecycle-cost model, and in that reply ask her to set up a short joint briefing for Gideon (Ines presents, we support) on the cost model, security status and the board paper, ending with a direct ask that he put the deal on the 2026-12-09 agenda. Signal: our own silence on her note, and Gideon's "run the numbers" hand-off to her (2026-08-29) after which he answered none of our 4 emails. His answer to the agenda ask tells us whether the 2026-08-27 support still holds. No dependency.
2. **Kwabena Stroud:** answer his four control-system network questions this week, with our network diagram attached. Signal: his 2026-11-04 email is unanswered for 13 days and it is the only open security item. Parallel with step 1; the briefing should report it closed.
3. **Ines Marrow, for the Finance Director gap:** in the same thread, ask Ines to introduce us to the Finance Director or carry our cost model to them, with a 30-minute walk-through she attends. Signal: her 2026-11-13 note names this as the step before the board. Before the step 1 briefing if possible, so it can report finance's view.
4. **Thelma Quist:** confirm she has our 2026-11-06 redlines and agree a date for her response that lands before 2026-12-09. Signal: terms are in her hands with no reply. Parallel with steps 1 to 3.
5. **Rosalind Ebbe:** ask her for a two-line statement of crew support that Ines can put in the board paper. Signal: her crew leads' 2026-09-18 feedback. After step 1 sets the board paper.

## Executive sponsor recommendation

Gideon Achterberg should sponsor this deal: he is the only person on record who takes deals of this size to the board, and he backed the direction in person on 2026-08-27. He has not acted as sponsor since; his single reply handed the work to Ines. The way to secure his sponsorship is the Ines-led briefing in step 1, ending with a direct ask that he put the deal on the 2026-12-09 agenda. If he declines the briefing, the deal is not yet board-ready, and the forecast should say so.

## Case 2: small, early deal, right-sized buying group

### Input (as of 2026-11-17)

Account: Hollin Creek Water District (small district utility, 38 staff)
Deal: $24K per year, work-order and leak-repair module for 9 field technicians. Stage: early, one discovery call and one demo done.

Contacts:
- Mateo Rusk, Distribution Supervisor: came in through our pricing page on 2026-10-27, booked the discovery call, sent three emails with questions about mobile offline mode and leak logging. Said he can approve software under $30K from his operations budget.
- Odessa Kint, GIS Technician: joined the 2026-11-10 demo for the map section, asked whether we import their pipe-network layers, said "fine if it reads our files", left early. We sent one follow-up; she answered with a sample file the same day.

Notes:
- 2026-10-30, discovery with Mateo: crews log repairs on paper; two missed valve inspections this year. Engaged throughout.
- 2026-11-10, demo with Mateo and Odessa: Mateo asked for a two-week trial for three technicians. No procurement or legal raised.

## Executive sponsor recommendation

Mateo Rusk is the sponsor: he found us, owns the budget at this size and is driving the evaluation. No more senior sponsor is needed for a $24K purchase, and adding one would slow a deal he can sign himself.
