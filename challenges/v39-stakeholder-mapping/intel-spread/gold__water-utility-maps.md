# Water-utility maps, illustrated

All organisations, people, amounts and counts below are invented for illustration and appear nowhere else. They show the reasoning and the page shape; they are not facts to reuse.

## Illustrated map: a sponsor who does qualify

The record: Fernhollow Valley Water Authority, a municipal utility serving about 210,000 residents. Deal: $360K ARR for leak-detection analytics. Stage: late evaluation; purchases above $100K need approval at the monthly board meeting, where the general manager presents. Four contacts with counts, plus notes. A reply of about this size is the target.

```
## Evidence on file: Fernhollow Valley Water Authority
- $360K ARR, late evaluation; Rosalind says anything above $100K goes to the monthly
  board meeting, presented by the general manager.
- Security gate cleared: Philippa confirmed in writing that the review is complete.
- Open condition: finance must fit the spend into the capital plan (Rosalind's words).
- Referenced, never introduced: the board members.
- We owe Rosalind the data-retention statement she asked for three days ago.
- Evidence is strong for Rosalind and Cornelius, complete for Philippa, absent for Augustin.

## Stakeholder table
| Contact | Title (as given) | Tier and the behaviour behind it | Grade and the counts behind it | Role in this deal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rosalind Achebe-Marsh | Senior Network Engineer | Champion: requested the pilot, shares pressure-zone data unasked, briefed the GM herself | A: 16 sent / 14 replied, last exchange 1 day ago, she starts most threads; rising | Builds the case; no approval rights at this size |
| Cornelius Vantongeren | General Manager | Authority: presents purchases above $100K to the board; asked for his own meeting after Rosalind's briefing | B: 5 sent / 4 replied, last 9 days ago, substantive questions on the pilot summary; rising | Takes the deal to the board |
| Philippa Strand | IT Security Officer | Gate owner (cleared): confirmed in writing the review is complete | B: 3 sent / 3 replied, last 20 days ago; flat now her work is done | No further role unless scope changes |
| Augustin Kowalczyk | Finance Director | Observer on behaviour; owns the capital-plan fit Rosalind mentioned | 0: 2 sent / 0 replied | Whether the spend fits this year's plan |

## Gaps the deal shape exposes
- Capital-plan fit: a named condition whose owner has never replied to us.
- Board members: the approving body is known only collectively; no individual is on record.
- Nothing else. Security is closed and the approval route is stated; adding procurement or
  legal personas without a signal would be invention.

## Plan of engagement
1. Send Rosalind the data-retention statement today - it is the only thing we owe.
2. Ask Cornelius what the board will want to see, and prepare it with Rosalind - he presents,
   so his questions set the agenda. Depends on 1.
3. Ask Cornelius or Rosalind to bring Augustin into a short capital-plan conversation - he
   has never answered us, so the introduction must come from inside.
4. Ask Cornelius whether any board member has raised concerns, and how he wants them handled.

## Sponsor recommendation
Cornelius qualifies. Authority: he takes purchases of this size to the board. Engagement:
he asked for his own meeting and has answered 4 of 5 messages, most recently 9 days ago.
Path: direct, and Rosalind briefs him too. Remaining risk sits with finance and the board,
not with him.

## Why some calls overrule the title
Rosalind outranks every title but one because she initiates, shares data and briefed the GM
herself. Philippa keeps her gate-owner tier but drops out of the plan because her
requirement is closed in writing. Augustin's title implies budget power, yet with zero
replies he is graded 0 and reached only through someone he already works with.
```

## Illustrated redirect

The request: "Draw me a power map of Brackenmoor Water with percentages of influence for each person and lines for the chain of command." The record holds five contacts and their job titles, plus a sentence saying "we've had about ten meetings with them this year". No notes, counts or reporting lines are included.

```
## Evidence on file: Brackenmoor Water
## What the record cannot support
Influence percentages and a chain of command: the paste records no behaviour, no counts
and no reporting relationships, and job titles cannot establish either one.
## What it does support
Five contacts by name, each with the job title supplied; the deal value and its stage.
## What to paste
Notes from the meetings mentioned, message and reply totals for each contact, and the
chain of command if anyone has confirmed it (an HR export or a contact's own account).
```

No tier, grade, percentage or line appears. Once the notes arrive, the full map follows; a reporting diagram follows only if reporting lines are part of what arrives.

## Illustrated small deal: the restraint is the answer

The record: the laboratory at a small district utility is buying a sample-tracking module, $14K per year, early evaluation after one demo. Contacts: Marguerite Olawale, Laboratory Manager (asked for the demo, sent her current chain-of-custody form, requested a quote); Emrys Calloway, Water Quality Technician (attended part of the demo, asked whether results export to the state reporting format, then wrote "that covers it"). Marguerite says she approves lab purchases up to $25K. No procurement, IT or legal mentioned.

```
## Gaps the deal shape exposes
Coverage fits this deal. Marguerite holds the authority, and the one functional question
(state reporting export) was raised and answered. No finance, IT, legal or executive
contact is needed on this record. Trigger: if the price moved above $25K, approval would
leave Marguerite's hands and this section would change.

## Sponsor recommendation
No separate sponsor is needed; Marguerite is the buyer.
```

Emrys is an Influencer graded C: one narrow, positive exchange. The plan opens with the quote Marguerite asked for, because that is what we owe.

## Illustrated single thread

The record: a rural water cooperative, $88K ARR, mid-evaluation. One contact, Thaddeus Ironside, Operations Superintendent: 8 sent / 7 replied, initiated contact, ran a demo for "two of the crew leads", wrote that "the co-op board signs anything over our discretionary budget", and last week asked whether we could provide a one-page summary for the board packet. We have not replied.

What the page does: one table row; the board and the crew leads appear as gaps without invented names; the plan opens with the reply and the one-page summary we owe; the next step asks Thaddeus how the board packet is assembled and whether he can bring us into a board question session; the single-thread warning names Thaddeus; the sponsor section says nobody qualifies yet, because the board holds authority with no direct engagement on record.

## What to copy from these

The disclosure and the restraint. Each grade quotes its counts and each tier names the behaviour behind it, so a reader can audit every call. A cleared gate is stated once and leaves the plan. A role without a name stays a gap. A sponsor is named when authority, engagement and a path all appear, and the page says plainly when they do not.
